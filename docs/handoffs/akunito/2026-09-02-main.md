## Why (user intent)

## Start here (for the next agent)
`system/app/vram-sampler.nix` — the only source of honest numbers here; every hand-taken figure in this repo is a stationary-desktop snapshot and understates the peak by ~25%.

## What it connects to
- `swayUseSwayfx` is read in **THREE** places that must agree: `system/wm/sway.nix`, `user/wm/sway/swayfx-config.nix` (the one SDDM launches), `user/wm/sway/extras.nix` (`home.packages`)
- `llama-evict` depends on `/run/llama-gaming/lock` (gamemode) and ollama's `/api/ps`; `amdgpuGttSizeMiB = 10240` bounds what it may do
- DESK + LAPTOP_X13 both at `swayUseSwayfx = false`; X13 **not deployed** (machine was off)

## What's fragile / don't touch without discussion
- `llama-evict` guard "a model is resident": GTT is 10240 MiB vs a ~12.4 GiB model — evicting with one loaded asks TTM for more than GTT holds
- The knob **livelocks on a busy desktop**: 245 s at 98% GPU, VRAM oscillating. `TimeoutStartSec` does NOT bound it. Hence no timer, no ollama hook
- Missing the `extras.nix` package gate breaks HM with a `bin/swaybar` buildEnv collision
- The #478 patch must cover **both** compositors — the crash is upstream sway's too; dropping it re-arms the gamescope session-killer
- Overlay asymmetry (pre-existing): HM's pkgs is patched, `nixosConfigurations.*.pkgs` is not

## What's intentionally minimal — and why
- `ollamaServerEvictTimerSec = 0` and `ollamaServerEvictOnOllamaStart = false`: the livelock above makes every automatic path unsafe; manual command only
- GUARD 5 (`gpu_busy_percent`): documented as weak — vkcube read 13% and an eviction at that level livelocked anyway. Kept because it catches a genuinely pinned card
- `nixosConfigurations.*.pkgs` left unpatched: pre-existing, harmless today (compositor comes from HM profile), out of scope for this session
- Q3_K_XL not wired as a second `ollamaServerCustomModels` entry: user has not decided yet whether to switch quant
