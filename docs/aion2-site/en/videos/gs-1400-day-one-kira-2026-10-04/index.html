1,400 GS on day one with no Odyle spent (KiraSaveTheWorld)

Region: global (Advanced Access, South America server) · Version: video from 2026-10-04 · Reviewed: 2026-10-05 · Confidence: medium. It is among the first sources recorded on the Global client. What the video says carries its minute, and what we add is always said as ours. The video is in Portuguese.

The thesis, in one line

Section titled “The thesis, in one line”

Do not open a single Exploration cube while levelling. Bank all your Odyle for Conquest, which pays yellow (Unique) pieces with far more GS, and reach the 1,000 GS the story demands using horizontal content alone 9:08. With that, the author says he ended day one at close to 1,400 GS without having spent any energy 0:00.

What counts as gear score 0:39

Section titled “What counts as gear score ”
  • GS adds up the gear, the Daevanion board and Transcendence cards (Arcana) 0:51. It is what unlocks content.
  • The Expedition menu has Exploration (the campaign dungeons) and Conquest (the star-tier ones, with far more in the guaranteed chest and in drops) 1:16.
  • Without VIP, opening the chest costs 40 Odyle; with VIP it costs 80 and pays double: the same loot in half the runs 1:30. It matches our Odyle table.

Map, quests and Daevanion 1:57

Section titled “Map, quests and Daevanion ”
  • Quest colours: yellow = main story, green = regional (side), red = Ascension 1:57.
  • The map’s question marks reveal themselves as you approach, either a sealed dungeon (ring icon: enhancement stones and Daevanion crystals) or a hideout/stronghold (campfire-with-shield icon) 2:22.
  • Every Daevanion board node adds GS by its colour: grey/white 1, green 2, blue 3, orange 4 2:47. White = flat stat, green = passive level, blue = active-skill level, orange = big effects like combat speed or cooldown reduction.
  • Route: go straight for an orange, then branch out toward two passives and two or three skills from your rotation. Do not try to fill the whole board early 3:36.

What this clears up

Our Daevanion page had a row with “blue 3, green 2, orange 4” as node cost, from one early English source, against “1 node = 1 point”. The video shows on the Global client that those numbers are the GS each node adds, not its cost. The two readings were compatible.

Belt, Amulet and what to enhance 4:14

Section titled “Belt, Amulet and what to enhance ”
  • Level 13: Noble Belt, from the red Ascension quest. The game looks like it has stalled; hold Alt to free the cursor and click the teleport next to the red quest’s distance marker 4:14.
  • Level ~17: Revelation Amulet 4:39.
  • Enhance only the 7 attack pieces to +5, and only once they are green: weapon, guard (off-hand), the two earrings, the necklace and the two rings 4:52. These are the ones that deal damage in the campaign.
  • Hideouts (campfire with shield) have bandits and a chest that gives the Belt upgrade scroll 5:30.
  • Feathers are turned in at the pink Monolith in the main cities; your home Monolith caps at level 30, after which feathers pile up unused 5:55.
  • Do not enhance Belt or Amulet until everything is green and the 7 attack pieces are at +5. Then take Belt to +10 and Amulet to +10 and transmute them: Esc → Transmutation → Equipment, unequipping the piece first so it shows up as a material. Out comes the Epic (blue) version 6:21.

This clashes with what we said

Our levelling page said “push Belt and Amulet from the start”. The video gives the opposite order by resource priority: the attack pieces first. Both agree on the destination (+10 and grade conversion); what changes is the timing. We have added his order as an alternative on that page.

Extraction and Crow Cave in a private room 7:24

Section titled “Extraction and Crow Cave in a private room ”
  • Extraction (flask icon) → Quick Select: the game only flags what you have already outgrown. It refunds every enhancement stone, not the Kinah, and carries the +5 over to the new piece 7:24.
  • Do not use Quick Join for Crow Cave. Create room → Private → Start: monsters and bosses scale to a single player and fall like campaign mobs 8:17.
  • There is a cube at the end: do not open it, leave and continue the main quest. The same goes for every Exploration dungeon 9:08.

The limit the video does not mention

Skipping the chest is not free: it burns an “unobtained cube” allowance, capped at 10 a week, and if you run it out you cannot enter any dungeon until the weekly reset (dungeons’ shared rules). The campaign only runs you through a few Explorations and you should not reach ten, but do not repeat Exploration dungeons without opening their chest.

Alts and the 1,000 wall 9:59

Section titled “Alts and the 1,000 wall ”
  • Odyle starts banking once you reach the Crow Cave step. At that point, create your alts, take them to 22 to unlock that part (you do not need to run Crow Cave on them) and go back to the main 9:59.
  • At the end of the campaign, a quest sends you to an NPC in the Abyss who only appears at 1,000 GS 10:25. It is the Global confirmation of the wall we had from Korean data.
  • To get there: side quests, sealed dungeons, Belt and Amulet in yellow +0, regional-quest gear (about 40 GS per piece), the quest that gives the Clash Rune, and board points 10:52.

The Rift 11:04

Section titled “The Rift ”
  • It opens at level 45, every 3 hours: 2, 5, 8 and 11, morning and evening, in the author’s local time (South America server) 11:04.
  • The portal is a purple, sun-shaped ring with roots. On the Asmodian side it appears at one of 4 points around the Moslan forest 11:44.
  • It does not show on the map until you have entered once. A regional quest with a blue symbol leads you to the spot 11:56.
  • On the enemy map (Verteron for an Asmodian) there are more question marks: sealed dungeons with plenty of enhancement stones and Abyss Points, and hideouts that pay Abyss Points 12:22.
  • The portal stays open 10 minutes and you can stay inside for 1 hour. If you die, you return to your own map and cannot re-enter until the next opening 13:39.
  • According to the author it is not PvP inside: deaths come from the guards. The shop-bag icon on the minimap marks a settlement with guards that shoot you down. If you see it while flying, steer away 13:53.
  • Doing the Rift quest once gives a summoning stone: a respawn point (kisk) you place on enemy territory. The goods NPC sells it for 100,000 Kinah 14:18.

Bracelet and manastones 15:10

Section titled “Bracelet and manastones ”
  • The campaign’s last red Ascension quest gives the yellow Ascension Bracelet. With that you sit around 1,200 GS 15:22.
  • Final state: everything at +5 except the Clash Rune (it breaks) and Belt and Amulet (yellow, at +0); weapon and off-hand are the quest’s yellow pieces, at +5 15:36.
  • Manastones (manastone binding): each line adds GS by its colour: white 1, green 2, blue 3, yellow 4. Aim for at least 2 green lines on a blue piece and 3 on a yellow piece, and do not reroll: material is scarce on day one. It gives about 5-7 GS per piece 15:48.

Abyss 16:41

Section titled “Abyss ”
  • The Abyss has a total time budget and a separate timer counting down to the big battle (~200 versus 200, heavy lag) 16:41.
  • There are feathers turned in at the Abyss Monolith, at your faction’s base: it caps at level 20, and with the feathers available you reach about 16 17:19.
  • It is a PvP zone, but dying costs nothing 17:31.
  • Flight rings recharge flight stamina: zigzag through them. Open the map (M), check where every feather is and fly straight there, so you do not burn Abyss time 17:44.
  • The Abyss Monolith pays points for the Asphel board: about 36 GS, no skill points, only PvP damage and defence bonuses 18:11.
  • Spending Abyss Points on stigmas or on the PvP set “didn’t feel like much” in early access, according to him 12:48. It is a few-days-old opinion.
  • Daily dungeon: the more mobs you kill, the bigger the reward; run it one or two days before the weekly reset, once you are already stronger 13:01.
