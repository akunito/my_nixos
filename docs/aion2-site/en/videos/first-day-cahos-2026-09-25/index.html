The thesis, in one line

Section titled “The thesis, in one line”

Two things never come back: the Odyle Energy you failed to bank and the materials you spent on gear you are going to throw away 0:00. Everything else follows: unlock the things that generate on a clock as early as you can, and do not burn what is permanent.

Sponsorship

From 0:40 to 2:23 the video is an ad for a ping optimiser (GearUP Booster) with no game information in it; the content starts at 2:28. One useful thing slips out there: the author plays on the Taiwan server 1:20, so the numbers on screen are TW, not Global.

The four principles 2:28

Section titled “The four principles ”
  1. Odyle Energy is spent claiming a dungeon’s final chest, not entering it 2:35. With no energy you can still clear the content, you just cannot collect. It unlocks at level 22 with expeditions, and from then on it banks itself up to the cap: 840 with membership, 560 free-to-play 3:00. The earlier you unlock it, the more you carry into the endgame.
  2. Every alt banks its own energy 3:12. Three alts plus the main are four reservoirs, that is four times the dungeon chests to open at endgame.
  3. Combat power rewards levelling everything evenly 3:25, not dumping resources into one piece. Within that, prioritise weapon and accessories, the pieces that turn into damage and therefore into speed toward the endgame.
  4. Horizontal content is half the game 3:50: sealed dungeons, strongholds, feathers, rifts, events. It sits outside the story, plenty of players skip all of it, and doing it in the first days is what adds most.

All four match what we have in levelling and routine: the site uses the same Odyle numbers (15 per 3 h, cap 560/840) and also splits power into a vertical and a horizontal track.

Organising the account 4:13

Section titled “Organising the account ”

How many characters

Section titled “How many characters”
  • You can play with no alts; what changes is the pace, not access to content 4:15.
  • His recommended point is three alts plus the main, four in total 4:40, and he explicitly frames it as optimal time versus quality of life, not as the maximum possible.
  • He qualifies it in the mistakes section: if you play an hour a day, do not build four characters 13:58. Start with the main and add one when you have time to spare.

What is shared and what is not 5:06

Section titled “What is shared and what is not ”
Account-wide (warehouse) Per character
Enhancement materials: amplification stones and soul stones → send them to the main Abyss Points: each alt farms its own for its PvP gear 5:44
Wardrobe appearances, and each one adds passive stats to the whole account → have alts buy skins with their nightmare currency 5:19 Daevanion fragments: they do not transfer, but the fused piece does → fuse on the alt and send the finished piece 5:44
Pets and wings → have alts open pet chests so the main saves points for high-grade wings 5:32 Daily login rewards: log in for 10 seconds on every alt, they are individual and they stack 6:11

The trick so you never have to guess: left-click the item and look at the bottom; if the server-warehouse icon is there, that item can move between characters 5:58.

Gear and resources 6:18

Section titled “Gear and resources ”
  • Level up in passes: every piece to +1, then every piece to +2, and so on 6:23. Each level gained that way gives more combat power than concentrating it in one piece.
  • But do not spend on pieces you will throw away 6:37. While levelling, only upgrade what gives you damage; once gold pieces start dropping, take those to +10.
  • Amulet and Belt are the exception: to +10, then push their tier in the Substance Morph up to yellow rarity 6:49. Same rule we give in gear: they are unique, permanent and cannot be farmed.
  • Pantheon statues have to be placed. They pile up in your inventory without you noticing, and each one adds stats 7:01.

The skill-point trick 7:13

Section titled “The skill-point trick ”

This is the most concrete part of the video and it is worth reading slowly:

  • At level 8, each active skill opens a passive slot.
  • The cost per level scales: from level 4 it asks 2 points per level, and from 8 it asks 4 7:25.
  • The trick: leave the skill one level short of the breakpoint and cover that last level with a Daevanion board point instead of skill points. You save 4 points per skill, which go into the passive tree, which is what gives the most damage 8:03. The same applies at higher breakpoints such as 12 7:50.
  • Where those points come from: sealed dungeons and side quests 7:52.

This is the lowest-confidence claim for Global

The PvE Daevanion board was not in the Global test build and is still unconfirmed for 10-05, so the 4-point saving per skill depends on a system that may not exist on day one; see Daevanion. Our levelling plans use the same breakpoints (8, 12, 16, 20) but spend ordinary skill points, which is the version that works either way. If the board is there, the trick is free; if it is not, you lose nothing by having levelled the skill by hand.

The day-one and first-week route 8:20

Section titled “The day-one and first-week route ”
  1. Main to 22 on the story plus a few side quests 8:27.
  2. Every alt you intend to use, also to 22 8:41, because from there they bank energy on their own with no daily login needed.
  3. Back to the main and take it to 45 on the main story 9:05. Taking the main to 45 first and the alts afterwards is also fine; it is less efficient, not a mistake 8:53.
  4. Always prioritise the main quest (yellow), and among side quests the regional ones (green), because they give jewellery and fill the ascension bar 9:17.
  5. Ascensions are mandatory: four across the game, tied to the story, and if the bar is not at 100% the main quest jams 9:29. It is the gate we explain in levelling.

Three things to watch on the map as you go 9:56

Section titled “Three things to watch on the map as you go ”
What How it looks What it gives
Sealed dungeons 9:56 yellow question mark 2 skill points + 2 Daevanion points each
Strongholds 10:09 campfire icon enhancement materials, Quna, mana stones and the Belt scrolls
Feathers 10:22 on the minimap handed in at the Monolith (quest at level 16) for permanent upgrades and the Amulet scrolls 10:36

On feathers the video already warns that with the Global changes picking them up is not mandatory, and that most of them are on your way anyway 10:22. Our levelling page closes it: in Global one pickup counts as 3, the Monolith no longer gives skill points, and the sensible target is just enough to push the Amulet.

And the rarity ladder in the Substance Morph: a green piece at +10 converts to blue, and it goes on from there 10:49.

At level 40: crossing to the enemy map 11:02

Section titled “At level 40: crossing to the enemy map ”

Rifts open every 3 hours, stay open about 10 minutes to enter, and leave you a full hour on the enemy faction’s map 11:15. That hour is enough to clear sealed dungeons and strongholds on the other side and farm pets 11:27.

Here the video is TW

He says level 40 to start crossing. In the Global build all PvP, rifts included, opens at 45; see Abyss. The cadence (every 3 h, 10-minute window, 1 hour inside) does match what we have in routine.

Two events not to skip 11:39

Section titled “Two events not to skip ”
  • Shugo Festa: minigames on the hour; you pick one and claim the reward with a key 11:44.
  • Dimensional Invasion: killing a boss with a few other players, short and co-operative 11:51.
  • Both give Daevanion and skill points 11:51.

The point of the whole route: the main clears horizontal content and gains the most power it can in the first week, while the alts bank energy that is later spent pushing the main 12:04.

The five expensive mistakes 12:20

Section titled “The five expensive mistakes ”
  1. Spending Odyle Energy too early 12:28. The story walks you through beginner versions whose final chests hold tempting gold items. Do not open them: the campaign hands you similar gear for free, and every energy point is worth more at endgame.
  2. Chasing perfect stats too soon 13:07. Early on you only need combat power to open the next content; the build comes later.
  3. Wasting materials while levelling 13:33. Only what it takes to move forward, the rest banked. Belt and Amulet are the only permanent pieces.
  4. Ignoring horizontal content 13:45, thinking it is minor and leaving it until the vertical track is done.
  5. Making more alts than your play time supports 13:58.

The first two are literally the ones that open our own mistake lists in routine and levelling, which is a good sign: they are consensus, not one channel’s opinion.

Before the weekly reset 14:16

Section titled “Before the weekly reset ”
  • With membership: buy everything you can from the Bliss Merchant special tab 14:23.
  • Craft the extra Odyle Energy in the Substance Morph, with or without membership 14:35.
  • Daily dungeons done, so they start generating materials 14:40.
  • 10 nightmare levels per boss, and you can go straight to the highest level you can clear because the reward is retroactive. Tickets are per character, so this is alt activity too 14:47.
  • Weekly contracts and the daily duty missions 15:00.
  • If your faction holds an Abyss corridor after the siege, use it 15:05.
  • Log in on every alt for the login rewards 15:13.

That list is the same as our night before the reset, with two things the video puts better: nightmare rewards being retroactive, and its tickets being per character.
