Playing on KR/TW, the playtest and the anti-cheat

Region: mixed (KR, TW and Global) · Version: checked 2026-09-15 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium. The network checks are first-hand; the registration procedure comes from transcribing four video guides, and the real scope of the anti-cheat is undocumented by NCsoft.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • The Launch Scale Test runs in a separate Steam app, “AION 2 Playtest” (appid 4972320), not on the game’s own store page. It does not come up in search, because Playtest-type apps are hidden from the store search: the only way in is a direct link.
  • The message saying the developer will notify you when they accept more participants is not a rejection. It is Steam’s standard system text. NC’s announcement says literally “Open to everyone. No keys, no invites, no registration list”, so access is granted in bulk when the predownload opens.
  • Predownload on 2026-09-16 at 15:00 CEST, and it appears in your Steam library, not in the store. Look for it there and not on the game page.
  • Six hours a day, twelve in total, with a level cap of 37. No shop, Quna, market, trading, Founder’s Pack items or wardrobe: only the default sets.
  • Progress carries from day 1 to day 2 and is wiped at the end. NC puts it without ambiguity: “Nothing transfers to Advanced Access or launch”.
  • After the test you must uninstall the Playtest App and download AION 2 separately (appid 3393110) for early access on the 30th. They are two distinct clients, on the order of 60-80 GB each. PURPLE users have nothing to do.
  • Korea and Taiwan have been playing since 2025-11-19, but around 2025-11-22 NCsoft switched on an IP whitelist: only connections from Korea and Taiwan get through. What is geofenced is the account and login layer, not the informational pages.
  • The procedure for playing on TW from Europe works and is free if you want it to be, but it is five steps where the first one decides everything: register by email and only by email, country Taiwan, SMS with your own number, log into PURPLE choosing Global, and a TW route before the first login.
  • Real latency from Western Europe is 170-180 ms, not the 250-300 usually quoted, measured by a player in France who describes it as playable.
  • Fifteen days from the Global launch it is not worth it. Progress does not transfer, it is a ~100 GB download, and the Launch Scale Test gives you twelve hours on European servers with the launch balance and every class. As preparation the test is better in everything except duration.
  • A HWID is not a number, it is a composite weighted fingerprint. Changing one part does not give you a new identity, and if the TPM is in the fingerprint it cannot be masked at all.
  • Sunshine and Moonlight do not work for Aion 2. The anti-cheat detects keyboard and mouse arriving over streaming as injected input and rejects them. You have to play sitting in front of the machine.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

The geoblock and what exactly it blocks

Section titled “The geoblock and what exactly it blocks”

Checked on 2026-09-15 from a European IP: www.plaync.com and tw.ncsoft.com answer with a 302 to www.nc.com, the western portal, and the Taiwanese registration is unreachable because the browser ends on “The page cannot be loaded temporarily”. The Korean content site aion2.plaync.com/ko-kr/ by contrast loads with a normal 200. The conclusion is useful: what is geofenced is the account and login layer, not the informational pages. The download CDN (gs-purple-inst.download.ncupdate.com, on CloudFront) is not blocked either, so the PURPLE installer downloads fine from Europe. What stops you is not getting the client, it is creating the account and logging in.

What a HWID actually is

Section titled “What a HWID actually is”

Not a number, but a composite weighted fingerprint: several identifiers are read, combined, the hash is stored server-side and re-compared at every boot. Because the comparison is fuzzy, changing one part does not give you a new identity. It is worth separating what is genuinely unique per machine from what half the internet repeats without it being true.

Unique per machine: the System UUID (SMBIOS type 1), the motherboard serial (type 2), the chassis serial (type 3), the serial of each disk (ATA or NVMe), the MAC of each NIC, the TPM Endorsement Key and sometimes the monitor serial over EDID. Also the Windows registry MachineGuid, which is software rather than hardware. Not unique, whatever is commonly claimed, is the CPUID: it identifies family, model and stepping, that is “a 7800X3D” and not your 7800X3D, because x86 has exposed no per-unit serial since Intel withdrew the PSN from the Pentium III in 1999. And the GPU device ID is vendor plus device over PCI, which is to say the card model.

Masking it is another matter. If the TPM is in the fingerprint, you cannot: the key signs from inside the chip and never leaves it. So-called spoofers are unsigned ring-0 drivers that need Secure Boot off or signature verification disabled, which is itself a signal, breaks the attestation the anti-cheat checks, and on top of that the market is functionally malware. The only robust separation is physical: another machine.

Why streaming does not work

Section titled “Why streaming does not work”

The anti-cheat is VIOLET plus NCGuard, distributed through PURPLE. It detects keyboard and mouse arriving over streaming as virtual input (Windows’ LLMHF_INJECTED flag) and rejects them, so the game is unplayable over Sunshine or Apollo. It is reported as Apollo issue #1258, opened on 2025-11-24 and still unresolved. Playing from another room over Moonlight is not an option: with Aion 2 you have to be in front of the machine running the game.

Procedure for playing on Taiwan from Europe

Section titled “Procedure for playing on Taiwan from Europe”
Step What to do Why
1 Register by email and only by email This is the step that decides everything. No Google, Facebook, Apple, Steam or X: those accounts already carry your regional information and leave you locked out of the region. With a social login, Aion 2 never appears in the PURPLE store.
2 Country = Taiwan It does not matter where you live. Korea demands a genuine Korean phone number and that is the real blocker; Taiwan does not.
3 SMS verification with your own number Taiwan does ask for SMS but accepts any number in the world, a European one included. Inside the game there is a second SMS to register the device.
4 In PURPLE, log in choosing Global Counterintuitive: the account is created as Taiwan but the login is always done as Global. If Aion 2 does not show up in the PURPLE store, the registration was done wrong.
5 VPN or booster with a TW route before the first login Without it you get “disconnection from login server”. This is the part the 2025 guides do not carry, because they were recorded before the block.

What NCsoft has not said, and what is advertising

The only thing NCsoft has stated officially about its measures is one sentence, in the 2026-01-28 release: blocking specific overseas VPN connections, enhancing the in-game reporting system, and implementing hardware-based restrictions. No components, no scope, no duration. Everything else in circulation comes from HWID spoofer shops that make a living selling the bypass: it is advertising, not published reverse engineering. The same applies to the choice of VPN: the two 2026 videos documenting the procedure push GearUP Booster, one of them sponsored by GearUP and the other handing out affiliate codes, and the blogs that surface in search (ExitLag, GearUP, LagoFast, thebestvpn) all sell the alternative they recommend. The method is corroborated; the recommended brand is paid advertising. The January measures were framed as anti-bot, and in ten months of videos and comments not a single case appears of an ordinary foreign player sanctioned by HWID for bypassing the geoblock. The mechanism risk is real, because automatic enforcement does not read intent and a hardware restriction lands on the PC, which would be the same one you use at the Global launch, but observed enforcement against normal players appears to be nil.

Plan

Section titled “Plan”

For the playtest (17 and 18 September). Request access from the direct link to app 4972320 and do not worry about the waiting message. On the 16th at 15:00 CEST look in your Steam library, not the store, and leave the predownload running: it is tens of gigabytes and you only get twelve hours of play. Since progress is wiped and the level is capped at 37, treat the test as a test bench rather than a race: settings, controls, macros on a dummy, and the feel of the classes you are considering. On the 19th uninstall the Playtest App and start downloading the real client, appid 3393110, with time to spare before early access on the 30th.

If you still want to see TW. Follow the five steps in order and do not skip the first: email registration, country Taiwan, SMS with your number, login as Global in PURPLE, and a TW route before you connect. The free option is SoftEther VPN Gate, the academic public-relay project from the University of Tsukuba, with one detail that appears in only one video: you have to connect to a Korean relay, not a Taiwanese one, because the servers are physically in Korea. The relay list rotates constantly, which is exactly why an IP block cannot hold against it. Not all of them let you connect, so you have to try until you find a fast one. Expect 170-180 ms and a dodge that responds in around a second or less.

What you will not be able to do. No Sunshine, Moonlight or Apollo. Leaving the game on one machine and playing from another is not a configuration problem: the anti-cheat rejects the input by design and the issue has been open since November 2025.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Searching for “AION 2 Playtest” in the Steam store and concluding it does not exist: Playtest-type apps are hidden from store search and only a direct link gets you there.
  • Reading the “the developer will notify you” message as a rejection. It is standard text and access is granted in bulk.
  • Looking for the predownload on the game’s store page instead of in the library.
  • Assuming the Playtest App works for early access: they are two distinct clients and you have to uninstall and download again.
  • Registering on PURPLE with Google, Facebook, Apple, Steam or X. Those accounts already carry your region and the game will never appear in your PURPLE store.
  • Choosing Korea as the country. It demands a genuine Korean phone number, and that is the block you cannot get past.
  • Logging into PURPLE as Taiwan: the account is created as Taiwan but the login is always done as Global.
  • Pointing the VPN Gate relay at Taiwan instead of Korea, when the servers are in Korea.
  • Attempting the first login without a TW route: you get “disconnection from login server” and it looks like an account fault when it is not.
  • Buying a HWID spoofer. It is an unsigned driver that requires Secure Boot off, breaks the attestation the anti-cheat checks, and that market is functionally malware.
  • Counting on a new disk or NIC to give you a new identity: the comparison is fuzzy and weighted.
  • Planning sessions over Sunshine, Apollo or Moonlight. They do not work and there is no fix in sight.
  • Downloading ~100 GB to see TW fifteen days before the Global launch, with progress that does not transfer.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Which components exactly go into the VIOLET and NCGuard hardware fingerprint, and in particular whether the TPM is among them, which would decide whether masking it is impossible or merely a bad idea. How long a hardware restriction lasts, since NCsoft has published neither scope nor duration. Whether the KR/TW IP block stays on after the Global launch or NC relaxes it. Whether a hardware restriction applied to a PC on TW would carry any consequence to the Global account on the same machine, which would be the one risk with real consequences. Whether Apollo issue #1258 will ever be resolved, and whether any form of streaming would pass the injected-input filter. And how many hours of predownload are actually needed, because the exact size of the playtest client is unpublished and the window between predownload and start is short.
