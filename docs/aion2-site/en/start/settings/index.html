Settings, controls and ping

Region: Korea and Taiwan (current client menus) · Version: September 2026; the Global client should match, verify in the playtest · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium.

The essentials

Section titled “The essentials”

Aion 2 drops gear constantly and gives you three ways to destroy items by accident (extraction, sinking, dismantling). The usual “inventory full of things I don’t understand” complaint is solved in the settings menu, not in the inventory: Auto Extract with filters on rarity, equippability and base item level turns trash into enhancement stones the moment it drops, and item locking protects what matters in every destructive menu. No auto-combat, no auto-attack, no autoplay: all manual input.

Settings to change in the first ten minutes

Section titled “Settings to change in the first ten minutes”
Setting Why
Control scheme: Aion 2 mode (hybrid action camera) over Aion 1 mode (mouse-hold camera, click-to-move) Fits the actual combat; try Aion 1 mode once if you played the original
Disable auto-target pursuit and hard target lock Both misposition you in PvP and multi-pulls
Bind “target hostile players” (Options → Key Settings → General) to a spare key such as middle mouse; keep Tab for cycling Stops you tab-targeting neutrals in crowded PvP
Move Defiance off spacebar/jump Otherwise a knockdown forces a glide instead of the CC break
Enable mass combat quality and identical player appearance Large FPS gain in sieges and 10v10, at the cost of visual detail
Motion blur off; damage-direction indicators on Aerial combat legibility; knowing where hits come from in group fights
Disable other players’ skill effects (keep self and party) Removes most visual noise
Configure the Auto Extract filters The main answer to inventory clutter
Lock every item you care about Extraction, sinking and dismantling all respect the lock
Life Potion auto-use at ~60% HP Carry potions and serums both: separate cooldowns
Map → “content” overlay Shows collected feathers, strongholds and sealed dungeons
Market: sort by unit price, not stack total Large stacks look cheap and are not
Controller: check field of view and dead zones before any PvP Customisation is limited
A preset swap hotkey Flips gear, skills, titles, wings, Pantheon and Genus between PvE and PvP in one press; blocked while any Stigma is on cooldown

Default keys worth knowing

Section titled “Default keys worth knowing”
Key Action
V Open/close wings (flight); tap again mid-air to glide on momentum
Shift Flight dash / mid-glide twirl (extra distance)
Space Land
F Interact; also auto-paths to a selected Daevanion node
M World map (yellow “?” are sealed dungeons and garrisons)
Alt Free cursor; hover an enemy name on the minimap to read their gear score
Alt+H Material conversion (green → blue → yellow)
Esc Menu: Growth Support, morphing, presets
Hold 1-8 The skill re-fires the moment its cooldown ends

Left click: normal attack, restores mana. Right click: strong attack, consumes mana. Flight is travel and combat; it burns a flight gauge that costs half inside the Abyss.

Class by ping (Europe to an EU server)

Section titled “Class by ping (Europe to an EU server)”
Class Stated tolerance
Assassin under 50 ms; above 60 ms costs significant DPS
Gladiator 0-50 ms; about 50% damage loss at high ping
Templar 50-80 ms; forgiving
Cleric under 100 ms; no ping optimisation needed
Ranger less ping-dependent than Assassin and Gladiator
Spiritmaster summons are ping-independent
Sorcerer punishes positioning errors; avoid at high ping

These tiers come from a single sponsored source selling a ping product; the “Templar and Cleric are forgiving” half is corroborated by three independent sources, and both classes get instant dungeon queues. The hardware floor quoted in the same video (32 GB RAM, RTX 4000 series, wired Ethernet) is opinion, not a published requirement. Measure latency under real combat load, not standing in town.

What to do in the playtest (September 17-18, 12 h)

Section titled “What to do in the playtest (September 17-18, 12 h)”
  1. First 20 minutes: control scheme, keybinds, targeting, graphics. Do it once and note it for the 30th.
  2. Benchmark ping under combat load.
  3. Play 2-3 classes to an actual rotation, not all eight.
  4. One or two Expeditions (Crow Cave at 20, Uruguru Canyon at 28, Fire Temple at 35 if you get there) to feel matchmaking and party flow.
  5. Verify what the guides are unsure about: is Heroic gear absent, does Aion 1 mode exist, what is the pet cap, what does the Daevanion board look like.
  6. Do not chase level 37, do not farm materials, do not collect anything.

Available: story to 37, the 5-player Expeditions, crafting and gathering, pets, Genus Insight, wings, Legion, Pantheon, achievements, titles, the early Daevanion boards, both control schemes and all eight classes. Locked: season content, Conquest-mode Crow Cave (level 45), Draupnir, Transcendent dungeons, Nightmare, Ascension Trial, the Abyss, arenas, the 10v10 battlefield, raids, the shop, Quna, membership, the Daeva Pass, Founder items and the transmog Closet.
