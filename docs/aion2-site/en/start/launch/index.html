Launch, servers and what to buy

Region: Global · Version: Season 1 per NCsoft’s announcements to 2026-09-19 plus what the Launch Scale Test (09-17/18) showed first-hand; KR details are cited only where Global has said nothing · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. NCsoft still has not published a what’s-in / what’s-out list for Season 1, and test-build numbers can still change before 09-30.

Calendar

Section titled “Calendar”
Date (2026) What happens
09-16, 15:00 CEST Playtest pre-download (Steam library, not the store)
09-17 and 09-18 Launch Scale Test: two 6 h blocks (15:00-21:00 and 21:00-03:00 CEST), level cap 37, everything wiped. Closing notice 09-19
09-30 to 10-04 Advanced Access (early access), Founder’s Pack holders only. Advanced Access is NCsoft’s own name for the window
10-05, 06:00 PDT / 13:00 UTC / 15:00 CEST Season 1 global launch, free-to-play. The time is the official Steam page’s, relayed twice on r/Aion2 (GLOBAL, 2026-09-22)

PC only: Steam (app 3393110) and NCsoft’s PURPLE launcher. No mobile client for Global (KR and TW have one). Ten languages, Spanish included. DLSS 4.5, already selectable in the Launch Scale Test client rather than an Advanced Access addition.

The playtest was a separate app: uninstall it

“AION 2 Playtest” (app 4972320) was hidden from store search, direct link only, and it is not the launch client. Uninstall it and download AION 2 (app 3393110) before Advanced Access on 09-30; they are 60 to 80 GB each. Playtest progress carried from day 1 to day 2 and was then erased: “nothing transfers to Advanced Access or launch”.

Servers: the least reversible decision

Section titled “Servers: the least reversible decision”
  • Advanced Access servers open on 09-30 for Founder’s Pack holders only. Launch servers open on 10-05 for everyone. Non-Founders can join an Advanced Access server later only “if those servers remain open and available at launch”. Advanced Access progress is not wiped: it carries into the 10-05 launch, and a Founder can also roll a second character on a launch server for friends who did not buy in, with Arena and group dungeons matchmaking across both sets (GLOBAL, 2026-09-21, r/Aion2, community-sourced, no NCsoft wording).
  • Dungeon matchmaking is region-wide the whole time: Advanced Access servers only during Advanced Access, merged with Launch servers from launch.
  • Abyss PvP during Advanced Access pairs Advanced Access servers with each other; afterwards NCsoft pairs servers “based on how far along and how strong each server is”. Progression-based, not a permanent split.
  • The per-server Market opens with Advanced Access. The Cross-Server Market does not open at launch; it comes “once participating servers have reached a similar stage”.
  • Server transfers: committed to, no mechanism, no date.
  • Servers come in faction pairs. In the test each server was one faction’s: the EU pairs added mid-test were Vaizel (Elyos) / Triniel (Asmodian) and Kaisinel (Elyos) / Lumiel (Asmodian). NCsoft opened them on demand as the population grew, so capacity is elastic but your faction choice and your server choice are the same decision.
  • Queues are not first-come-first-served. When a server is full, members “in good standing” enter a priority queue and everyone else the general one; both move as capacity frees. It is explicitly revocable: an account under disciplinary action or suspected of a ToS violation loses the privilege. A separate Trust score, built by normal play, also gates Marketplace access: it is aimed at bots and gold sellers, and normal play raises it on its own.

Five regions: NA West, NA East, South America, Central Europe and Japan. Servers are not region-locked, so you pick by ping or by where your group is. If you play with a fixed group, agree the server before 09-30, not on the day.

Faction: matters less than it looks, more than KR guides say

Section titled “Faction: matters less than it looks, more than KR guides say”

Elyos and Asmodians have separate continents, stories and capitals. On KR all PvE except the marketplace is shared between factions, and Global goes further: cross-faction and cross-server matchmaking confirmed for Expeditions and probably Sanctuaries. Friends on different factions can run dungeons together. What stays split: the open world, Rift crossings, the Abyss and sieges, and a faction that loses sieges every week progresses slower.

Open-world PvP can be switched off: War Mode (KR since 2025-12-03) changes only inside your own territory with a 70-minute cooldown between switches. The Global team’s “no-PvP flag” is that same system, seen first-hand in the Launch Scale Test: a manual PvE/PvP switch inside your own faction territory, same 70-minute cooldown. Attacking an enemy player or a kiosk, or partying with a flagged player, forces PvP on you for about 10 minutes.

Character: class is permanent

Section titled “Character: class is permanent”
  • Four character slots per server: one main and up to three alts on the server you pick. Whether the account total across servers is higher is unverified (one tester reports eight).
  • Eight classes: Gladiator, Templar, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Cleric, Chanter. Brawler (KR’s ninth, July 2026) is not in Global.
  • No class change. A “Class Change Ticket” exists (~2,000 Quna, level-gated, unverified) and is explicitly “not a respec button”. Switching means starting from zero.
  • The character creator is the feature the Korean community praises most: 200+ parameters and 40 presets per gender.

What to buy

Section titled “What to buy”

Founder’s Pack (one per account, platform-locked)

Section titled “Founder’s Pack (one per account, platform-locked)”
Tier Price Adds on top of the tier below
Standard $24.99 5-day Advanced Access, 30-day Membership, supply chest (bound), title “Vanguard of Atreia”
Deluxe $49.99 “Ascended Daeva” armor skin, “Eternal Sun” weapon skin
Ultimate $99.99 styling chest, “Moonlit Aria” skin, “Black Dragon” pet, “Blazing Sun” wings

Everything above Standard is cosmetic. The 30-day membership was added to all three tiers on 08-07, retroactively. Founder cosmetics will never be sold again. Bought on Steam, played on Steam: no crossover with PURPLE. No upgrading later.

Membership ($15/month, single tier)

Section titled “Membership ($15/month, single tier)”

Not a WoW-style subscription but a licence to take part in the economy. Without it you can level, run every dungeon, craft and PvP in the Abyss with no daily limit. What you cannot do: buy or sell on the Market, exchange Quna for Kinah, use the remote warehouse or the members’ shop, and your Odyle energy caps at 560 instead of 840. Direct player-to-player trading is a separate question: it stayed switched off for the whole Launch Scale Test and NCsoft presented that as an anti-bot policy rather than a test restriction, leaving the Market as the only trading channel. Energy regenerates at 15 per 3 hours for everyone now (two Global-facing guides still describe the old KR split of 10 free and 15 with membership): membership raises the ceiling, not the rate. In a game where crafted gear is a primary endgame source and Kinah is the bottleneck, that is not a convenience. KR pays 25,000 KRW since July 2026 (down from 45,000; stacked tiers used to reach $40-50 a month).

Daeva Pass

Section titled “Daeva Pass”

Per character, not per account: buying it on alts multiplies the cost. Free track buyable with Kinah, premium with Quna. It contains upgrade materials, not only cosmetics, so “the shop is cosmetic-only” needs a caveat. KR/TW veterans say its rewards are outgrown fast and the membership is the better buy.

How much to spend

Membership $15/month Buy: it is the economy, not a convenience
Daeva Pass premium Quna, per character Skip at launch

Founder Standard once, then the monthly membership; anything beyond that is optional. Whatever is left over, hold it for now: the Quna→Kinah exchange is the real pay-to-win vector, and for the first 3-4 days the market has nothing to sell and prices are broken.

Monetization: the shape

Section titled “Monetization: the shape”

Free base game. Revenue: shop (cosmetics and consumables), optional membership, optional per-character Daeva Pass, Founder’s Packs. Two currencies: Kinah (earned, for the Market) and Quna (premium; also written “Kuna” or “Cuna”). Cosmetics are stat-free and sold directly: no gacha, no boxes. Wings at Global are purely cosmetic.

What differs from KR and makes old guides dangerous: Global does not get Chapter 1 (July 2026), so no level 50, no Stigma 25, no pendant slot, no Brawler, no new zones. Bound and unbound Kinah are both live: Exploration dungeons drop bound Kinah and Conquest dungeons drop unbound, and dungeon farming of unbound Kinah is capped near a million a day per character. The test build also pulled most of the levers whales used on Taiwan: the tradable PvE Daevanion board is absent from the build, crafted gear lost its built-in PvE damage line and cannot be sold, a wing dismantles for about 10,000 enhancement stones instead of 90,000, and Daevanion node values and title bonuses read around 1.5% where Taiwan had 2.5% to 5%. None of this is in published patch notes: it is what testers measured in the 09-17/18 build, and NCsoft can still move it before 09-30.

Advanced Access day one (09-30): the account first

Section titled “Advanced Access day one (09-30): the account first”
  1. On the main: yellow (campaign) and green (regional) quests, plus every sealed dungeon that sits on your path. On Global the Monolith no longer gives skill points: a sealed dungeon gives 2 skill points and 2 Daevanion crystals, a green side quest gives 1 of each plus +3 permanent stat points. Feathers still matter, but only for the belt and amulet enhancement scrolls and the collection title, and one pickup now counts as 3 (Taiwan: 4). Collect enough to take the amulet to blue, then stop.
  2. Stop the main at level 22. That is where Expedition (Odyle) energy unlocks, and it accrues passively per character. Create the alts and bring each to 22 before pushing the main to cap.
  3. Four characters on your server. The weekly dungeon entries are capped per character (about 25 a week with membership, plus a server bonus), so alts still multiply your weekly runs, but each one also needs its own Odyle energy to be worth anything, and energy accrues per character from level 22.
  4. Clear sealed dungeons and strongholds as you pass them: the main Daevanion crystal source, and they scale if you come back later.
  5. Reach gathering level 5 while questing: ten minutes now versus an hour later when everyone competes for the same nodes.
  6. Kill mobs along the path for soul fragments: first pet and a permanent account-wide stat bonus.
  7. Buy nothing on the Market for the first 3-4 days.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Still having the Playtest App installed on the 30th. App 4972320 is not the launch client: uninstall it and download 3393110 in time for Advanced Access.
  • Buying the Founder’s Pack on the wrong platform. Locked to Steam or PURPLE; one per account; no later upgrade.
  • Picking a server alone. No transfers announced and no Cross-Server Market at launch.
  • Enhancing or getting attached to levelling gear. Disposable: a weapon to +5 in the 20-35 range is the ceiling worth paying for. Save Kinah, stones and shards for level-cap gear.
  • Selling gear to merchants. Always extract: extraction refunds 100% of the enhancement stones spent (not the Kinah).
  • Leaving the belt and amulet unenhanced. Unique, cannot be warehoused or extracted, scrolls come from side quests, sealed dungeons and Monoliths. Enhance on sight, then convert (Alt+H) green → blue → yellow.
  • Morphing accessories with the belt and amulet equipped, or doing it only on the first gear preset. Unequip on every preset or you feed your own gear into the morph.
  • Enhancing the Clash Rune casually. Destroyed outright on a failure, from as low as +2, and the warning above the button is easy to miss.
  • Skipping dungeon reward chests “to save runs”. Unclaimed runs cap at 10 per week, then dungeon entry locks entirely.
  • Treating Stigma Shards like skill points. Normal respec is free; Stigma resets cost currency and shards are scarce (about 20 by level 45).
  • Spending general Daevanion essence expecting it to work on the endgame boards. Ariel (PvE) and Asphel (PvP) need their own essence, earned only after cap.
  • Leaving Logitech or Razer software running. KR sanction waves caught legitimate players with it in the background.
  • Expecting remote play to work. The anti-cheat rejects injected input from Sunshine, Moonlight or Apollo (issue open since November 2025).
  • Reading KR guides dated July 2026 or later as if they described Global. Growth Support, Eltnen and Morheim, the pendant slot, cap 50 and Stigma 25 are all out.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

The Launch Scale Test closed four of these: the cap is 45, there are 4 stigma slots (unlocked at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37), both control schemes (“Aion 1” tab targeting and “Aion 2” action) are in the Global client, and the no-PvP flag is the War Mode switch described above. What is still open: the exact Season 1 content cutoff (NCsoft has still never listed it). Whether direct player-to-player trading returns at all after launch, or the Market stays the only channel for good. Whether the premium Daeva Pass track carries power. The server transfer mechanism and date. How the Trust score is actually earned, and what losing it costs you beyond the Marketplace and the priority queue. And one client bug is open: the game exits with no error window during the loading screen, the opening cutscene or the first 30 seconds in the world, on the Global build only, while the same PC runs the Taiwan client fine; it is filed with NC support with no confirmed fix (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19, r/Aion2).
