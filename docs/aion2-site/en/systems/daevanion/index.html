Daevanion

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The Global test build carried five boards, not six: the PvE board was absent from it, and the node values below were cut by a third to a half.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Daevanion (데바니온) is a node board you fill with Daevanion Points or Crystals. It is the biggest free power source of the first weeks: every point spent raises item level and combat power by 1, whatever the node. It is not gear; nobody can buy it.
  • It opens at level 12 and the boards unlock at 12 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 45 / 45, confirmed on the Global test. That test build had only the four base boards plus the PvP board (Asphel): the PvE board (Ariel) was absent from the Global LST build, though its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text, so treat it as unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch rather than removed.
  • Points come almost entirely from side quests, sealed dungeons and strongholds, on both factions’ maps: over 100 instances on Global, and they are per character, not account-wide. Do them as you pass; they scale up if you come back over-level.
  • The blue nodes break the level-10 skill cap: skill points cap a Mastery skill at 10; the four base boards give +1 each (+4, to 14); Arcana cards and a soul binding take you to 16 and 20. Only rings and weapons can roll a soul binding for an active skill; every other slot rolls passives. The specialty ladder is 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 and the level-20 step exists at Global launch; what each step grants is not settled.
  • Templar: accuracy early so taunts do not miss (KR groups wiped on the Uruguru boss from lost aggro), then attack and attack/cooldown speed; split damage vs tolerance depending on whether you have a static.
  • Cleric: cooldown reduction first, fund Earth’s Retribution, Judgment Lightning and your core actives, and avoid max Spirit, crit resistance, Holy Aura, Warfare Spirit and Recovery Block: the 5 nodes the KR top-100 route around.
  • Budget: 503 points unlocks a class’s full offensive spread. Expect ~300 by level 45 and 450-500 after a few weeks of sealed dungeons and Rifts.
  • Respeccing is cheap: 500 Kinah per node, ~13,500 per branch. Do not hoard points out of caution; do not buy points with premium currency.
  • Noise you will hit: nodes only unlock from an already-active neighbour (up, down, left, right); select your target node and press F to auto-path rather than clicking your way there.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • A grid, not a tree: each activated node unlocks only its four orthogonal neighbours, so reaching a distant node means buying every node on the path, including the ones you do not want. That is the whole game of it.
  • Colours: blue = +1 level to a named active skill, green = +1 to a named passive, orange or gold = a high-value % stat (the “core” at a branch end), grey or white = small flat stats (HP, attack, defense, crit).
  • Each board carries exactly one blue and one green node per skill, never duplicated: find the board that holds your skill before routing.
  • Six boards in the KR launch build, by character level; the last two at 45 with their own currencies. The four base boards share one point pool; Ariel and Asphel use separate crystals. The Global test build had five: the four base boards and the PvP board (Asphel).
  • Skill-level math: skill points to 10 → base boards +4 (14) → Arcana cards or a “+N level to skill” soul binding on a ring or weapon to 16 and 20. Specialty effects unlock at 8, 12, 16 and 20, and sources disagree on what each step grants: those steps, not raw damage, are what routing chases. An Arcana’s “+1 level” lands on a random skill from that card’s pool, not on the one you wanted.
  • Points are item level: demonstrated on screen with 462 points = 462 item level. Consequence: once your priority nodes are covered, any node will do.
  • At 45 the system forks: the four shared boards keep taking general points; Ariel and Asphel each demand their own crystal, earned only from PvE endgame (Ascension Trials, Nightmare shop, sealed-dungeon crystals) and PvP endgame (Abyss feathers, arena and Abyss shops). On Global the fork is one-sided for now: the PvE board (Ariel) was absent from the test build, and Asphel’s crystals no longer appear in Substance Morph, so that board is farmed, never bought.

Routes

Section titled “Routes”

Leveling, any class

Route the shortest path to the blue node of whichever core skill sits closest to its next specialty breakpoint: pushing a level-7 skill to 8, or a 10 to 12, beats any stat node at this stage; orange end nodes have low values early.

Templar (dungeons, Global): Nezekan: attack, then attack and cooldown speed (favour speed; Judgment is a static cast). Accuracy early and unconditionally so Taunt and aggro skills land. Then defense, and the fork: with a static, damage; without one, tolerance.

Cleric (dungeons, Global): cooldown reduction first; fund the never-removed actives (Earth’s Retribution, Judgment Lightning, Sovereign’s Grace) then plain stat nodes (attack, HP, defense, crit). Avoid the five unpopular top-100 nodes.

PvP (both): the Asphel board is a separate currency PvE cannot feed, and on Global money cannot feed it either: its crystals are gone from Substance Morph, so there is no craft or market path. Abyss feathers are collected by flying the zone and turned in at the Monolith; arena and Abyss shops and battlefield entries (30 + 3 extra per week) supply the rest.

Other classes: Ranger, attack and combat speed on Nezekan, damage on Zikel, then 4 crit damage and 4 multi-hit; skip defense and cooldown reduction. Gladiator, orange damage nodes (speed, attack, reduction) before any defense; blues on Keen Strike, Ankle Slice, Mocking Blade and Ruinous Blow. Spiritmaster: do not buy casting speed, nearly everything is instant.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Skipping regional quests and sealed dungeons while leveling: the main source, and they scale if you return.
  • Assuming general points work on Ariel and Asphel.
  • Hoarding points “until you know the meta”: a node refunds for 500 and a branch for ~13,500; unspent points are item level you do not have.
  • Spending points near a node instead of on it: adjacency does nothing.
  • Chasing an orange before your rotation’s skills.
  • Buying points with premium currency (event tokens are fine).
  • Disenchanting gear that rolls “+level to skill”: the only way past 14.
  • Investing in Spirit (except the displayed combat-power quirk).
  • Templar: neglecting accuracy. Spiritmaster: buying casting speed.
  • Treating Stigma and Daevanion as the same system.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Whether the PvE board (Ariel) ships at the 10-05 launch: it was absent from the Global LST build, yet the Nightmare shop still prices its points and a client-extracted board simulator still lists it, so it is unconfirmed for launch rather than removed. Global board layouts are still unphotographed: screenshot all five in Advanced Access (early access), from 09-30. Cost model (flat 1 point or by colour). Whether the July routing UI comes with the Korean-launch board Global uses. Exact reset rules on Global: one source says node refunds get more expensive with each reset, against KR’s flat 500 Kinah. English names for the boards and for Cleric’s nodes. Whether Arcana’s “+4 per card” is uniform across classes, and how random its “+1 level” roll really is. Whether the board’s diagonal mirroring is universal or Cleric-specific. Whether feathers still feed the board at all, now that the Monolith has stopped paying skill points.
