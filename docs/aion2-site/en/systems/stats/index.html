Stats and combat formulas

Region: Korea and Taiwan · Version: up to 2026-09-15 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium. Mechanics corroborated by two creators who tested them live; every number is pre-Global and several caps changed mid-service.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Offense is king, even on Templar and Cleric. Every PvE boss is a gear check with an enrage timer, so the meta for all eight classes, tanks and healers included, is a near-glass-cannon build. Defensive stats matter only enough not to be one-shot.
  • Smite (강타, a chance to deal double damage) is the single highest-value PvE stat, fed by Wisdom. Chase it first. Caveat: raid bosses carry ~30% Smite resist and Season 2 cut its PvP multiplier to 1.5, so ~90% Smite yields 60-70% effective against bosses: push past 100%.
  • Combat speed (전투속도) is number two and the one you feel. It shortens the whole cast and animation cycle and, since 2025-12-17, charge speed; it rolls mainly on weapon, guard and gloves.
  • Accuracy is a threshold, not a curve. Miss the raid threshold and the whole build is wasted: 2,350 was the KR raid gate in March 2026; ~2,500 combined for PvP, ~3,000 for Assassin and Ranger. Hit it, then stop.
  • Cooldown reduction has one number for Cleric: ~34%. It drops Earth’s Punishment from 30 s to 20 s and gives permanent uptime. Most classes 33-36%; Assassin no ceiling.
  • Combat power (전투력) is not damage. Two KR videos with 150,000+ views exist purely to say so: it scores the systems you have filled, not the damage you deal. Use it as a content gate only.
  • Templar and Cleric on stats is nearly the same conversation: both stack Might and Precision, combat speed, Smite and damage. Templar adds Block Penetration and front damage; Cleric adds Healing Power Enhancement and received healing on helmet and accessories.
  • Do not build two gear sets at launch: one hybrid PvX set (PvE weapon plus PvP off-hand and accessories), because two sets halve your progression.
  • Global is roughly KR pre-Chapter 1, so most numbers should hold; May’s 250 caps and 130% are out; Genus Insight caps at 3.

The three layers

Section titled “The three layers”
  1. Six base stats: Might, Dexterity, Precision, Willpower, Intelligence, Constitution. Conversion value ÷ 10 = %, confirmed by two self-testing creators.
  2. Ten god or Pantheon stats named after the Empyrean Lords: Justice, Freedom, Illusion, Life, Time, Space, Destiny, Wisdom, Death, Destruction. Conversion (value × 2) ÷ 10 = % (30 Justice → 6%).
  3. Sub-stats on gear, soulbinds, manastones, Genus Insight, titles, wings, Arcana and Pantheon.

Mechanics

Section titled “Mechanics”
  • Hit: accuracy is checked against the target’s evasion and block. Monsters cannot evade, only block, so against bosses accuracy is purely anti-block, and attacking from behind bypasses block entirely. A blocked hit deals half.
  • Damage: damage boost is offset 1:1 by the target’s tolerance (64% vs 64% = 0). Tolerance is a flat multiplicative reduction against every source, which is why it beats flat defense. Weapon damage boost is calculated later in the formula and is strictly better than plain damage boost (9.4 vs 3.5 power per point).
  • Procs: multi-hit fires 4 extra hits proportional to the attack (10-15%) and does not scale down with boss defense. Smite is ×2 on final damage, after crit, Perfect and every multiplier; that is why it scales with everything.
  • Percentage beats flat. A governing rule per the TW tier-list host and the Cleric ranker analysis.
  • Combat speed is the top gear-roll priority on weapon, guard and gloves across KR, TW, Spanish and English sources. Since December 2025 it also raises charge speed, so Sorcerer and Ranger value it even more.

Combat power lies

It scores filled systems (breakthroughs, enhancement, skill levels, stigmas, closet extraction) with imperfect weights and bugs NCsoft acknowledged (Penetration over-valued, passives double-counted). A server number-one at 932 was doing less damage than peers. Use it as a content gate and nothing else.

Priorities

Section titled “Priorities”

Universal PvE, weapon and guard soulbind in order: combat speed → weapon damage → damage → Might → Precision → multi-hit → attack (Precision overtakes Might once your other caps are met).

Armour by slot: shoulders crit damage; chest damage amp; legs tolerance (plus Perfect and attack); gloves combat speed; boots move speed; helmet received healing; cloak crit damage resistance. Slot-locked stat first, then offensive passives, then attack, accuracy and crit, defense last. Consistent across KR, TW and Chinese sources.

By class: multi-hit is S for Assassin, Gladiator and Ranger and skipped by Cleric and slow casters, though it rises in value because it does not scale down with boss defense. Ranger hits a hard 100% attack cap from passives and gear: reach it and stop stacking. Penetration: essential even while leveling; its combat-power over-valuation was a scoring bug, not a damage change.

What is contested

Section titled “What is contested”

Base-stat cap 200 or 250 (250 since May on KR; Global forks earlier). Crit cap 80% or 50% (80: two independent testers). Plain or weapon damage amp (weapon). Smite’s real value after the PvP nerf and boss resist (still the best offensive stat). Regeneration: 25% or 50% of damage taken (unresolved). PvE or PvP gear for boss damage (in September a test showed PvP armour beating PvE against an Abyss boss via the set stat).

Chronology that matters: 2025-12-17 combat speed also charges; 2026-03-18 new combat power with two acknowledged bugs; 2026-03-25 PvE and PvP coefficients split, formula rebalanced, charge bonus 20% → 40%; 2026-05-27 250 and 130% caps, brooch and wings (out); 2026-07-01 level 50 and stigma 25 (out).
