Seasons

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW season-change rules from January to July 2026 + what the Global LST of 2026-09-17/19 taught us · Reviewed: 2026-10-05 · Confidence: medium. NCsoft has not published Global’s season rules; almost everything on this page is Korean and Taiwanese precedent, and Global season 1’s end date is a reading of item expiry, not an announcement.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • A season is not a wipe. On Korea and Taiwan a season change only resets the PvE and PvP rankings. Character, gear, Kinah, Arcana, Daevanion and Abyss Points stay (KR/TW, 2026-06-09).
  • What a season brings is content: new dungeons, new Arcana sets (sometimes one more slot), a new Nightmare floor, and a season currency earned from season quests and spent in a season shop.
  • The only thing that is actually lost is the season shop and its currency (Oath Coins). On the season 1 to 2 transition the shop closed entirely and the currency expired; the Abyss, Nightmare, Shugo and pass shops stayed open with their purchase history intact (KR/TW, 2026-01-19).
  • Season rankings pay out by best score within your class, not cumulative score, since Korean season 2 (KR/TW, 2026-01-21). Ranking titles are season-bound: they disappear from your gear when they expire (KR, 2026-07-01).
  • Korean seasons ran 9, 11 and 12 weeks, and Chapter 1 ran about 18. Season 2 was extended by two weeks because “90% of players weren’t ready” (KR/TW, 2026-03-04).
  • Global: season 1 starts 2026-10-05. Three Launch Scale Test sources read 2026-12-16 as the end date from energy-item expiry (GLOBAL, 2026-09-18/19): about 10 weeks. NCsoft has not announced it. Global season 2 has no date and no content.
  • What to do: spend your season currency before it ends; don’t be afraid to invest in gear, Arcana or Daevanion at the end of a season, because none of that resets.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • What resets: the PvE (Nightmare, etc.) and PvP rankings. Nothing else. The creator who explains it with 900 hours on KR/TW insists that “it’s not even a soft reset” (KR/TW, 2026-06-09).
  • What stays: level, gear and its enhancement, Kinah, Arcana, Abyss Points, the Daevanion board, stigmas and their levels (Stigma), pets and wings. Daevanion points are bought with Nightmare and Shugo currencies, and both of those currencies carried over whole from season 1 to 2 (KR/TW, 2026-01-19).
  • What arrives: in Korea every season brought new expedition dungeons, a transcendence dungeon with new Arcana sets, a new Nightmare final boss (Zikel in season 1, Kaisinel in season 2, Tyrant in season 3) and season quests. Old content isn’t deleted: season 1’s Nightmare bosses stayed on their own floor (KR/TW, 2026-01-21).
  • Season quests: completed once per season (plus a weekly part) and give Daevanion crystals, AP, PvP medals, Arcana materials and season points for the shop (KR/TW, 2026-07-06). The Supply Requests board has a season tab with its own reset (KR, 2026-01-14).
  • Season shop: sells crafting materials, wings, pets, resurrection stones and cosmetics for season points. Every season opens a new shop and closes the previous one.
  • Global: one LST source describes the same model, season quests roughly every ~3 months that give Oath Coins, amplification stones and medals, and a season shop with crafting materials, wings, pet souls and skins (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19). It’s a single source, and season content was locked during the test, so Global’s catalog is still unseen.
  • Gear across seasons: inheritance carries a piece’s enhancement over to its replacement. Korea capped it at about 6 transfers per season, with a chance of failure, until Chapter 1 made it unlimited and 100% (KR/TW, 2026-06-16). Global has it at 100%; whether it has a per-season cap is not stated.

What happens to each thing at a season change

Section titled “What happens to each thing at a season change”

Precedent: the season 1 to 2 transition, maintenance of 2026-01-21, per NCsoft’s plan as summarized by the community (KR/TW, 2026-01-19).

What At the season change
PvE and PvP rankings reset
Oath Coins (season currency) expire: season 1’s were only valid until 2026-01-21; new ones are handed out for season 2
Season shop closes entirely; a new one opens for the next season
Pass shop (Daeva Pass) stays open; the old pass shop coexists with the new one
Nightmare, Abyss and Shugo Festival shops stay open, with purchase history not reset (some items change)
Abyss Points stay
Abyss Rift Stones stay; new season ones expire at the end of the following season, but ones bought on the Abyss exchange don’t expire
Phantasmal Fragments (Nightmare) stay
Century Root Emblems (Shugo) stay
Expedition and transcendence tickets stay, no expiry; new season tickets are also handed out and those do expire
Ascension Trial and conquest tickets stay; the new season ones expire
Gear, Kinah, Arcana, Daevanion stay (KR/TW, 2026-06-09)
Inheritance transfers the per-season counter used to reset (KR/TW, 2026-01-21); since Chapter 1 there is no counter

On Global the tickets no longer exist: Odyle Energy replaces them. What expires on Global at the end of season 1 is not published.

Per-season AP caps

Section titled “Per-season AP caps”

AP splits into capped (open-world PvP kills and mob farming in the Abyss) and uncapped (Abyss Corridors, Shugo, invasions, Battlefield, arenas, Supply Requests, quests). The cap is the part shaped by season. How Korea got to its current form:

Date Rule Region
2025-12-03 200,000 per week of hunting + 200,000 of PvP; 2,500,000 per season combined TW
2025-12-24 400,000 combined per week; open-world PvP AP stops counting toward the cap, only AP earned inside the Abyss counts KR/TW
2026-01-07 weekly cap removed: a season cap that starts low and rises each week (what you don’t use accumulates) TW
2026-03-11 no longer a wall: past the cap a reduced amount still drops KR/TW

Global: no reliable figure. One tester gave 5,000,000 for the first week without being able to show it (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17); others estimated 200,000-500,000, and the Abyss was closed for the whole test. What every source agrees on is that it accumulates week by week. AP detail and sources in Abyss.

Before the season ends

Section titled “Before the season ends”

Assuming Global copies the Korean rules, which is all there is to go on. Revisit the week of 2026-12-09 once NCsoft publishes the change notes.

  1. Spend your season points. It’s the only thing that’s lost. Buy order per Korean guides: Magical Breath (guaranteed craft, on your main) → Mysterious Crystals for your first Arcana Unique card → shop wings if your class lives on cooldown reduction → everything else. Resurrection stones from this shop are expensive.
  2. Finish the season quests you have left: they give season currency, medals and crystals that don’t come back.
  3. Lock in your ranking score if you’re going for a reward: it counts your best score within your class, so one good attempt is enough, you don’t need to grind volume.
  4. Don’t save season currency “for next season.” It doesn’t carry over.
  5. Everything else you can save without worry: AP, Nightmare fragments, Shugo emblems and Kinah all carried over whole in Korea. If you’re torn between spending AP on a piece now or waiting, a season change is no reason to rush.
  6. Don’t hold back investment in gear, Arcana, Daevanion or stigmas out of fear of a reset: there isn’t one.
  7. Check your equipped titles on changeover day: if you’re wearing a season ranking title, it can disappear and take its stats with it.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Thinking a season is a wipe and stopping progression in the last weeks. Only the rankings reset.
  • Hoarding Oath Coins “just in case” and losing them when the shop closes.
  • Taking 2026-04-01 as Korean season 3’s start: that was the preceding patch; the season started 2026-04-08.
  • Reading a Korean “what to do in season X” guide as a plan for Global: Global season 1 doesn’t match Korean season 1; it’s a mix with the March rework and without Heroic.
  • Rationing inheritance transfers per season by following guides written before July 2026: Korea removed the cap and Global ships at 100%.
  • Assuming season ranking titles are permanent.
  • Buying account-bound items (Magical Breath) from the season shop on an alt: the result stays stuck on that alt.

Section titled “Open questions”
  • Does Global season 1 really end on 2026-12-16? It’s a date read from item expiry, not an announcement.
  • What does Global season 2 contain? Stigma to 25 is announced in the client as “a later season”; Heroic, Brawler, level 50 and the pet dungeon are unconfirmed candidates.
  • Does Global have the season shop and Oath Coins under the same model, and what does it sell? A single LST source, with season content locked.
