Account and character utilities

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 + Global client 2026-10-01 · Reviewed: 2026-10-05 · Confidence: medium. Almost everything on this page was seen first in Korea and Taiwan; what is confirmed on the Global client is marked GLOBAL. What matters most from Global: membership applies per server, not per account, and the Twitch Drops have three fixed windows (Oct 2-4, 7-9 and 12-14).

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Membership applies per server, not per account. The Global client says it outright (“Applied per server”) (GLOBAL, 2026-10-01). If you play on two servers, you pay twice.
  • There are two storage tabs: character storage (that character only) and server storage (every character you have on that server). Server storage is the only path to move things to your alts (KR/TW, 2026-01-28).
  • Character slots: 4 per server on Global (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17). On Korea and Taiwan it was 4 free plus 4 more for Kinah; Chapter 1 gave a fifth for free (TW, 2026-06-15). Whether Global sells the extra slots the same way is unverified.
  • Don’t count on a class-change ticket. It was announced in Korea in February 2026, and by September Korean players were still waiting on it. Pick your class as if it were permanent.
  • Gear-exchange vouchers convert dungeon tickets plus Kinah into a box where you choose the piece. They exist on Global, and are the way to get pieces that don’t drop directly (GLOBAL, 2026-09-18).
  • Feathers and pets sync across your characters on the same server; sealed dungeons do not (KR/TW, 2026-05/07).
  • The official DPS meter is private: you only see your own damage, on purpose, so it can’t be used to kick people from the party (KR, 2026-04).
  • Combat Power and item level are two different numbers: item level is the one that gates content. Detail in stats.
  • War for Atreia: three Twitch Drops windows, with the exclusive mount in the Oct 7-9 one at 4 hours of watch time (GLOBAL, 2026-10-03).

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

What is account-wide, server-wide and per character

Section titled “What is account-wide, server-wide and per character”

The full table (materials, Closet, pets and wings account-wide; AP, Daevanion fragments and login rewards per character) is already in the first-day video. What this page adds:

  • “Account-wide” in practice means “account-and-server-wide.” Server storage, feathers, pet bonuses, Duty Missions (5 a day) and membership all stay on one server (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19 and 2026-10-01; KR/TW, 2026-06).
  • The trick to tell which is which: left-click the item; if the server-storage icon shows up, it can go to another character (TW, 2026-09-25, a tip aimed at Global).
  • The Daeva Pass and the Season 1 starter pack are per character (GLOBAL, 2026-10-01). Pricing details are in monetization.

Character storage and server storage

Section titled “Character storage and server storage”
  • Character storage: that character only, and it’s the tab that opens by default. What’s inside is used directly for crafting and conversion, no need to move it to your bag first (KR/TW, 2026-01-28).
  • Server storage: shared by every character you have on that server. It’s what moves materials, unbound Kinah and gear between your main and your alts (KR/TW, 2026-01 to 2026-08).
  • Remote storage (opening it from anywhere) is a membership perk; without it you need to visit the NPC (GLOBAL, 2026-10-01).
  • Merge-duplicates button: joins a stack that got split between the two storages. From the gear icon you can set up quick-send rules by category, for example sending all food to one storage (TW, 2026-09-17).
  • Expansions: the character-storage ticket cost 200 Quna in the shop (TW, 2026-01-28); the server one, around 200 Quna with a cap of 20 purchases per server (TW, 2026-01-14, the creator wasn’t sure of the price). Global pricing is unseen.
  • Bound Kinah: doesn’t go through storage. How much is bound on Global is covered in monetization.
  • The bound-Kinah workaround (KR, 2026-08): send a piece from your main to your alt through server storage, upgrade it there with the alt’s Kinah (the alt can’t equip it, but can still upgrade it), then send it back to the main.

Character slots

Section titled “Character slots”
  1. You start with 4 slots per server: one main and up to three alts (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17).
  2. On Korea and Taiwan there were 4 more unlockable with Kinah, which is where Global testers’ “8 per account” comes from (KR, 2026-09-27; GLOBAL, 2026-09).
  3. Korea’s Chapter 1 raised the total to 9 and left 5 free (TW, 2026-06-15). That’s content that came after Global’s current build.
  4. Deleting a character in Korea leaves the slot locked for ~1 week (KR, unconfirmed for Global). Don’t delete a character if you need the slot that same week.

How many alts are worth keeping is covered in the first-day video and in routine.

Class-change ticket

Section titled “Class-change ticket”
  • Announced in Korea on 2026-02-04 for level 45 or above, no date given (KR, 2026-02-04).
  • By late September 2026 it was still just a rumor in Korea: Inven forums and creators talked about “if it ships” (KR, 2026-09-22).
  • A pre-launch Global guide gave ~2,000 Quna, with a minimum level and “it’s not a respec button,” and flags it itself as unconfirmed (Global, 2026-09-05). We haven’t seen it in the Global client’s shop (GLOBAL, 2026-10-01).
  • Practical takeaway: changing class means making another character. See launch.

Gear-exchange vouchers and selection boxes

Section titled “Gear-exchange vouchers and selection boxes”
  • What it is: you collect tickets that drop from dungeons, pay Kinah, and get a box where you choose the piece, instead of waiting for the exact drop.
  • Korean launch recipe (KR/TW, 2025-12-17): 2 tickets + 1,000,000 Kinah → a dungeon weapon box to choose from.
  • Rudra version (KR/TW, 2025-12-17): 2 Rudra tickets + 1 Rudra weapon + 10,000,000 Kinah → a weapon box to choose from; armor: 1 ticket + 1 armor piece + 10,000,000. In April 2026 the Rudra recipe changed to 2 tickets + 20,000,000 (KR, 2026-04-08).
  • On Global (GLOBAL, 2026-09-18/19): pieces that were Heroic in Korea don’t drop directly and are obtained through this exchange system instead; the Rudra-type weapon is Unique, not Heroic. Raid weapons that aren’t for your class can be exchanged for yours: check the exchange before dismantling them.
  • For alts (KR, 2026-03-09): if a Sanctuary reward lets you choose between a bracelet and a Rudra voucher, take the bracelet on the alt; the voucher is a gamble worth making on the main.

Feathers and pets across your characters

Section titled “Feathers and pets across your characters”
  • Feathers: collected once per account and server and synced to your alts through the Monolith (“inheritance”) (KR/TW, 2025-12-17; TW, 2026-05-11). What they pay out on Global is in character systems.
  • Pets: owning a pet gives flat stats, and those bonuses (and Genus Insight’s) count for every character on your server, regardless of its level (KR/TW, 2026-03 to 2026-06).
  • Paid pets, mounts and outfits: Chapter 1 made them account-wide; before that only the free ones were (TW, 2026-06-15). On Global, shop appearances “do not grant Collection points” (GLOBAL, 2026-10-01).
  • What doesn’t sync: sealed dungeons are once per character (KR, 2026-07-31; TW, 2026-05-12). Every alt does them again from scratch.

Auto-evade (mobile only)

Section titled “Auto-evade (mobile only)”

Korea added a mobile-only option in December 2025 that tries to dodge ground-marked attacks on its own, with a warning that it isn’t 100% reliable (KR/TW, 2025-12-24). Global is PC-only (Steam and PURPLE), so this doesn’t affect you.

Showing the weapon out of combat

Section titled “Showing the weapon out of combat”
  • The weapon used to only appear briefly every 40-60 seconds while idle; now there’s a toggle to keep it visible all the time (KR, 2026-05-06).
  • Route in the Korean client: Settings > Graphics > Camera Direction Display > “show weapon out of combat.” It hides itself automatically while flying (KR, 2026-05-06). The setting exists on Global (GLOBAL, 2026-09-21); the exact label in the Spanish client hasn’t been captured.
  • It’s purely cosmetic, for showing off a weapon skin from the Closet. The rest of the settings worth touching are in settings.

Trying on another player’s appearance

Section titled “Trying on another player’s appearance”

On another player’s profile there’s a “try on” button that overlays their visible gear on your character so you can see it (TW, 2026-01-28). Since March 2026 you can also inspect characters from the opposing faction (TW, 2026-03-17). Unconfirmed on Global.

Personal DPS meter

Section titled “Personal DPS meter”
  • How it works: a breakdown of your own damage by skill, visible only to you. It landed in Korea in late March or early April 2026 (KR, 2026-03-24/31).
  • Why it’s private: NCsoft built it this way to stop parties from kicking whoever is pulling the least weight (KR, 2026-04-06). Templar, Cleric and Chanter land at the bottom of any meter, and their value doesn’t show in it (KR/TW, 2025-12; GLOBAL, 2026-09-20).
  • Global: NC America said Global ships the meter from day one (KR/TW, 2026-06-28, citing NC America). In the Launch Scale Test, creators used third-party meters sold by their sponsors (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17/20).
  • Use it to compare two rotations or macros against the training dummy, not to judge other people. The method is in macros and combat.

Reporting a macro user

Section titled “Reporting a macro user”

The target-info panel has a button to report a macro or bot user (KR/TW, 2025-12-17). The game’s official macro is allowed; the button is for external programs and bots (macros and combat). In the same month, Korea blocked a flagged player from looting in sealed dungeons, strongholds, Transcendence and Expedition even while grouped (KR/TW, 2025-12-24). The Global Market’s Trust system is covered in monetization.

Combat Power versus item level

Section titled “Combat Power versus item level”
  • Item level is the gate for content and can be inflated with mana stones and Arcana without making you any stronger. Combat Power is the number that tries to measure your real strength. Full detail, weights and gates: stats and gear.
  • Combat Power gets recalculated with patches. In Korea it started counting passive skills, and almost everyone dropped in number without losing any strength (KR, 2026-04-21). If your number drops after maintenance, check the patch notes before redoing anything.
  • Global recalculated the item-level-to-stats formula, so Korean figures don’t carry over (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19).
  • Global guides call figures in the thousands “CP” (Fire Temple conquest at 2,200 per the September Global stream). That’s the item-level scale, not the hundreds of thousands of Korean Combat Power. Check the scale before comparing yourself to anyone.

War for Atreia and Twitch Drops (Global)

Section titled “War for Atreia and Twitch Drops (Global)”
  • What it is: NC America’s launch event, 2 weeks, 60 streamers on two teams competing in PvP and PvE. Drops activate once the combined creator pool hits a points threshold (GLOBAL, 2026-09-28 and 2026-10-03).
  • How to get it: link your Twitch account and watch any of the 60 participating creators during a window. Watch time counts toward it.
  • Windows and rewards in the table below. None grant stats: event mounts and outfits are cosmetic (GLOBAL, 2026-10-03).

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Paying for membership thinking it covers all your servers. It’s per server (GLOBAL, 2026-10-01).
  • Storing crafting materials in server storage if you only craft on one character: character storage is used directly for crafting and saves you steps.
  • Dismantling a raid weapon from another class without checking the exchange first (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19).
  • Picking a class “because you can change it later.” The ticket hasn’t even shipped in Korea.
  • Deleting an alt to make another one the same day: the slot can stay locked for a week.
  • Doing sealed dungeons only on the main expecting it to count for alts. They’re per character; feathers are the ones that are shared.
  • Panicking because your Combat Power drops after a patch. It can be a recalculation, not a loss.
  • Installing a third-party meter just because a sponsored creator advertises it: it’s an external program running alongside an anticheat. We haven’t found any source saying whether it’s allowed.
  • Watching drops on a channel that isn’t participating. Only the 60 event creators count, and only within the three windows.
