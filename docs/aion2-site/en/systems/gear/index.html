Gear

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The built-in +5% PvE damage on crafted gear is gone on Global: crafted and dungeon gear both take that power from Potential stones now.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Gear is not the whole build. A large share of power lives in permanent systems that survive every gear swap: Daevanion boards, the belt (strongholds), the Revelation Amulet (feathers and Monolith), runes, Pantheon, Arcana cards, wings, the pet’s Genus Insight and the skin Collection Effect. Every guide from November 2025 to September 2026 puts these before gear enhancement for a fresh 45.
  • Global Season 1 ships without Heroic (orange) gear: no drops, no crafting, no Heroic amplify stones. Unique (gold) is best-in-slot at launch, confirmed hands-on in the September playtest and not just read off a tooltip. That flattens the power curve and kills the biggest whale lever.
  • Enhancement is safe but not free. +1 to +10 is 100% and never destroys or regresses the item; from +11 it drops to ~65% per attempt, and a failure eats materials but builds pity for the next try. The risk is Kinah, not bricking.
  • Spread, don’t spike. Five items at +1 cost less in total than one at +5 for the same score. Standard launch-week advice: armour to +5, weapon and guard to +10.
  • As a Templar or Cleric you need accuracy, not just attack. Accuracy is measured against the target’s block: every 10 points of block over your accuracy is a 1% chance to be blocked, and a blocked hit deals half. A KR raid tier asked for 2,350.
  • Two different “power” numbers exist and guides mix them up. Before the March overhaul one ~900 to 3,000 score was called both combat power and item level. After it, KR shows a new combat power in the hundreds of thousands next to an item level in the thousands. Date-check every threshold.
  • PvP and PvE gear are separate ladders with a real penalty for crossing. A PvP set carries about -15 to -20% PvE damage outside the Abyss.
  • Soulbind (영혼각인) is where late power comes from, and it is fed by Unique junk. Never sell or disenchant a Unique drop: it is Sync fuel for rerolling a line on a keeper. A fully engraved 2,200-power character out-performs an un-engraved 2,500.
  • Plan for a casual schedule: main story → every sealed dungeon and stronghold on both maps → belt, amulet and Daevanion → cheap Unique armour from the Market enhanced evenly → Abyss accessories (rings first) → Transcendence for Arcana → crafted weapon last.
  • Do not chase “orange gear at launch” content: any KR guide about Heroic Succession, Heroic bonding, 5,000-tier Abyss sets or Legion-tier gear describes things Global does not have.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Rarities: Common → Rare (blue) → Epic → Unique (gold, 유일) → Heroic (orange, 영웅). Gold caps at +15, orange at +20. English sites sometimes render it Blue → Purple → Gold → Legendary.
  • Four sources, by long-term value: 1) dungeon gear (Expeditions in Exploration and Conquest, 1★, 2★, 3★ tiers; one dungeon per tier drops weapon and accessories, the other armour); 2) crafted, better per tier, but on Global the built-in +5% PvE damage is gone: crafted and dungeon gear now both take that power from the Potential system, and crafted keeps only its extra soulbind line (5 vs 4, or 3 on extended-reach dungeon weapons). On KR/TW the +5% is still built in; 3) Abyss and PvP with points and medals (Decanus → Centurion → Tribunus → High Commander); 4) the Sanctuary raid, the only source of certain slots (bracelets, brooches, seals).
  • Weapon first, always. Every guide puts weapon (then guard, 가더) ahead of armour for enhancement, crafting and replacement.
  • Enhancement: consumable stones; guaranteed to +10; failure never destroys or de-levels, it only wastes stones and raises the next attempt’s odds (a +11 at 50% that fails goes to 55%). Disenchanting an old piece refunds the stones spent (not the Kinah). Breakthrough (돌파): 5 stages past max enhancement, Unique and up only.
  • Manastones (마석) and spirit stones (영석): socketable stat gems; manastones for weapon and armour, spirit stones for accessories; fixed slot counts per item; higher grade, bigger stat. High-grade stones become mandatory from item level 67.
  • Soulbind is three actions: engrave raises an already-rolled line toward 100% with books; sync destroys a Unique item to reroll exactly one line on a keeper (Rare is neither fuel nor target; once per item); reset.
  • Godstones (신석): add a proc to weapon or guard; duplicate effects do not stack; replacing one destroys the old one.
  • Inheritance: enhancement level, breakthrough stage and soulbind lines carry from an old item to its same-grade replacement. Investment in an early piece is not lost when you move up a tier.
  • Offense over defense in PvE: bosses have enrage timers; you only need enough defense not to be one-shot. Templar is the stated exception where survivability has its own party value.
  • Smite (강타) is the standout stat: a chance to deal double damage, from the Wisdom god stat, gear rolls and Genus Insight slots 3 and 9 on KR/TW. On Global the client renames it Double Chance and it rolls only on slot 4 of the Special board, capped around 3%.

Arcana

Section titled “Arcana”

5 slots in Season 1 on KR/TW. Common card ~+20 item level, Rare ~+40, blue ~+60, Unique ~+80; full contribution ~+320. Crafting a Unique: 5 Mysterious Crystals + 5M Kinah. One Global endgame guide describes only 4 slots (parchment, compass, bell, mirror), unconfirmed, since the playtest capped at level 37 and nobody reached Transcendence.

Routes

Section titled “Routes”

Leveling → 45

Do not enhance leveling gear: it is replaced within hours. Finish the entire main story (it continues past 45) for its exclusive Unique weapon and bracelet, which are not sold. Get every alt to 22 immediately.

Fresh 45 → first gates (the “free gear” phase): 1) clear every sealed dungeon and stronghold on your map and the enemy’s (100+ instances, via the Rift); 2) feathers → Monolith → Revelation Amulet to Unique +10; 3) class runes at +1 and stop; 4) only then buy the 7 non-weapon Unique armour pieces at the cheapest Market listing and enhance armour to +5, weapon and guard to +10; 5) mid-grade manastones everywhere, no fishing for the 0.07% rolls.

PvE dungeons (crafted track): Conquest 1★ → 2★ → 3★; in each tier farm the weapon-and-accessory dungeon and the armour dungeon before moving up. Save guaranteed-reward tokens for the last set pieces. Crafted replacement order: weapon → guard → accessories → armour.

Soulbind by slot (PvE): weapon and guard, combat speed, then weapon damage amp, Might and precision (the last two roll only here); gloves combat speed; chest damage amp; shoulders crit damage; legs damage resistance or move speed; boots move speed; helmet received healing; cloak crit damage resistance; necklace and earrings attack then crit or accuracy (Healing Power Enhancement for a healer); ring active skills only (on Global, weapons can carry them too; breakthrough the ring first, given the ~1%). Engrave order: weapon and guard, then gloves, legs and chest, then boots, then the rest. This table is KR/TW-derived. Global reshuffled several slot-to-stat assignments: attack speed moved from gloves to the necklace, shoulders gained crit-damage amplification, and necklace, earrings and rings gained Might, Precision, combat speed and move speed, which testers call best-in-slot on every class.

PvP and Abyss: accessories first (rings are the cheapest slot in points), then earrings and necklace. Full Decanus set minus weapon ~715,000 AP. Enchant weapon → guard → accessories to +11 everywhere before pushing anything to +17. Set mixing is legal and stronger (5 Centurion + 8 Tribunus), but that is KR/TW. Global Season 1 only reaches Decanus and Centurion, with silver medals and no Tribunus, so there is nothing to mix yet. Do not wear PvP gear in dungeons: the PvE penalty is heavy; the one documented exception is Abyss bosses and artifacts, where Abyss damage amplification dominates.

Templar and Cleric: Templar’s parry mechanic degrades on low accuracy: keep an accuracy roll on the guard until the Pantheon covers it. Cleric prioritises Healing Power Enhancement on helmet and accessories.

What is contested

Section titled “What is contested”
  • Which number gates content: one score before March; two after. Unknown for Global.
  • Damage amp vs weapon damage amp: the community preferred plain because it scales everything; measured, weapon amp is worth 9.4 power per point vs 3.5 and applies later in the formula. Weapon amp is better.
  • Smite always good: Season 2 cut its PvP multiplier to 1.5 and new raid bosses carry ~30% resist. Still the best offensive stat, but push past 100%.
  • Accuracy and crit: crashed in the combat-power formula (13 per point vs 97 for Might), flagged as a probable bug alongside a confirmed Penetration bug; official advice was “do not reroll anything yet”.
  • PvP gear in PvE: reversed twice in six weeks; in September a test showed PvP armour out-damaging PvE against an Abyss boss via the set stat. KR’s 16 September patch then raised the 12-piece Abyss PvE-damage-amp bonus across all five tiers, with the top tier now at 55%, so the Abyss set stat matters more on KR, not less. None of this is reachable on Global, which launches with only two Abyss tiers.
  • The “십부/백부/천부” Abyss tier shorthand: KR-only and changed between February and April in the same creator’s videos. Do not port it to Global.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Enhancing leveling gear. Selling or disenchanting Unique drops (Sync fuel). Spiking enhancement on one piece. Fishing for 0.07% manastone rolls. Two identical godstones. Wearing PvP gear in dungeons. Runes above +1. Morphing accessories with belt and amulet equipped. Ignoring accuracy on Templar and Cleric. Believing KR guides about Heroic. Pouring Potential into gear you will replace: Potential is the one layer that does not transfer to the next tier, and neither do manastones, spirit stones or the Theo stone. Porting any KR/TW item-level gate, stat percentage or Genus slot number to Global: the playtest moved almost all of them.
