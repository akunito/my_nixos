Character systems: pets, Genus Insight, the Closet and titles

Region: Korea and Taiwan, with the known Global adjustments · Version: up to 2026-09-15 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium. The mechanics are well cross-checked; Global’s pet cuts are disputed and the Closet cannot be verified before Early Access.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • This is where your real power lives, and none of it shows in Combat Power. A fully soul-engraved 2,200 CP character beats an un-engraved 2,500 CP one. A player in +20 PvP gear with zero pet and Genus investment still loses fights on the accuracy and evasion swing alone. Budget time for this from week 1.
  • Priority order on a casual schedule: (1) Soul Engraving on your real gear, (2) pet levels and Genus Insight, (3) feathers and the Monolith while levelling, (4) Closet skins passively via auto-extract, (5) the titles you get free from content you already run. Wings and the 700-skin title are month-2 projects.
  • The single highest-leverage stat in the game is Smite (강타), a double-damage proc. It only rolls on Genus Insight slots 3 and 9, nowhere else. Lock nothing before it lands there.
  • Almost all of this is account-wide or server-wide, not per character. Skins, the Closet’s Collection Effect, pets, race understanding and their achievements are shared across every character on your server since the KR patch of 2026-01-07. Alts multiply your Black Cloud Merchant rotations, not your grind.
  • Global nerfs the pet grind but nobody agrees how. Four sources say “cap 3 instead of 5”: two mean the individual pet’s level, two mean the Genus board. Separately confirmed: the KR/TW fragment-acquisition boost does not carry to Global.
  • This is the casual-friendly half of the game. Almost all of it is weekly-capped or uncapped-but-passive; the 5 Duty Missions (~10 min) are the only genuinely daily thing. It fits a casual evening schedule far better than the raid and siege calendar does.
  • Templar and Cleric note: the one class-dependent call here is the Genus defensive lean. Cleric and Templar are told to take Defense where a DPS takes Attack, because their passives reward staying alive. Everything else on this page is class-agnostic.
  • Do not buy cosmetics for power. Cash-shop skins, mounts and pets carry zero stats and are excluded from the Collection Effect. The Ultimate Founder’s Pack’s Black Dragon pet and Blazing Sun wings are cosmetic only.
  • Two traps that cost real Kinah: never extract rings or gaiters hunting for a skin (they never yield one), and never use a Reset book on a Unique-grade piece.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

What trips up every new player is that Combat Power is a gear score, not a power score. Soul Engraving, the Genus Insight boards, wings’ retention effects, title possession effects and the Closet’s Collection Effect all feed real damage and survivability while contributing little or nothing to the number over your head. There is even a documented inverse exploit: an Arcana loadout tuned purely to inflate the displayed number by about 40 CP to pass a party entry check, swapped out for the real build once inside. Party leaders know this, which is why the gate-keeping moved to “show me your soul engraves” from Transcendence stage 9 onward.

The pet system has three nested layers and most confusion comes from collapsing them. Layer one is the individual creature: kill a mob type until you have ~10 fragments to own it, then keep killing to push it 1 to 5, each level paying a flat stat. Layer two is the category board: once any pet in a category hits 5, that mob’s souls start dropping as category crystals, which level the board 0 to 10, unlocking one of nine stat slots per level. Layer three is the reroll: spend those crystals plus Kinah to reroll individual slots, locking the ones you want, with every lock raising the price of the next roll. Layer one is a chore you finish; layers two and three are the endgame. That is why the standard advice is to farm one mob until its pet caps and then keep farming that same mob: the spot never stops paying, it just changes currency.

Smite is why the board matters at all. It is a flat ×2 on a hit, it is the stat every endgame Korean guide converges on, and it is structurally scarce: two slots out of nine can roll it, at roughly a fifth of a percent per attempt, and only once the board is level 9 or higher for Heroic tier. One anecdote lands it: a player with 20% Smite and 50,000 enchant investment did less damage than a Spiritmaster alt with 40%+ Smite and 30,000 investment.

Soul Engraving is three verbs and one hard rule. Engrave (각인) pushes a line you already have toward 100% of its potential, using books from achievements, login and the battle pass. Tune / Sync (조율) burns a Unique-rarity duplicate to reroll one line. Reset (초기화) rerolls every line at once. The hard rule is that Unique items are both the fuel and the floor: you cannot sync onto Rare gear and you cannot feed Rare gear in. That makes your Unique drop stream, not Kinah, the real bottleneck, which is why every guide tells you to stop vendoring Uniques. Inheritance / Transfer crafting compensates: enchant and soulbind progress carry onto a same-grade successor piece. Manastones do not carry, and that asymmetry is the single most-repeated gearing warning in the whole corpus.

The Closet turns your loot stream into a stat. Every piece you outgrow can be extracted for Enhance Stones plus a roll at its skin, and owning more skins of a type raises a flat bonus for that type server-wide across every character. Duplicates are not waste either: four of a rarity combine into one roll at the next rarity. Because the effect is server-wide and because each alt gets its own hourly Black Cloud rotation, the recommended trick is alts of your own class: drops are weighted toward the class you are playing.

Flight is deliberately not a taxi. The gauge drains while you flap, so the whole skill ceiling is about not flapping: climb, fold, coast on gravity, re-open, repeat, and the same gauge carries you three times as far. Acceleration rings top it back up mid-flight, which is what makes the Abyss ones an escape tool and not scenery. Mounts do not fly at all, they glide, and only from pet level 5. Wings sit on top as a gear slot with the unusual property that owning one pays a retention bonus even when you are wearing a different pair, so wing collection is itself horizontal progression, with a cosmetic override so you never have to look at the one you are actually wearing.

Long term, the “perfect page” minimums are 2 Smite rolls, 14 attack-bonus rolls and 25 crit or 35 accuracy on the defensive lines, and the right structure is to split the three available pages into one PvP page, one short-term PvE page for reaching caps and one long-term perfect PvE page.

Transformations: three unrelated things with one name

Section titled “Transformations: three unrelated things with one name”
  1. Faction military rank (총사 신장). Abyss ranking resets hourly on points earned and feeds a military rank; high ranks unlock a transform. At Commander-in-Chief it gives +20% PvP damage amplification, enough to break through otherwise-immune tank-line targets. It is the only one that matters for a competitive PvP goal.
  2. A pet-linked transformation at Pet Understanding level 5. Named as a threshold; its effect is undocumented in every source read.
  3. An anti-bot penalty state: failing the in-game CAPTCHA-style challenge forces a punitive transform with reduced move speed and disabled skills.

Contested or changed by patch

Global’s “cap 3” is two different claims wearing one coat. Four sources say Global reduces a pet cap from 5 to 3: two mean the Pet Insight level and two mean the individual pet’s level. Two more treat it as a guess. Since every KR/TW source puts the Genus board cap at 10 rather than 5, the likeliest reading for Global S1 is that the individual pet caps at 3, cutting souls per pet by roughly 60%, while the board keeps its 0-10 range but is gated by the reduced crystal flow. Unresolved: verify it on day 1. Alt farming of pet souls died between January and August 2026: the pet dungeon moved to 14 runs a week per account and alt fragment farming is named as a removed feature. Skin extraction rates contradict each other and the axis may be item source rather than rarity alone; all three sources agree on 25% for cheap market gear and 100% for Abyss gear. On cash-shop skins: every source agrees they give no Collection Effect, but two claim they do count toward the 700-item achievement while not appearing in the Wardrobe (two different counters). No patch note confirms it, so do not buy skins on the strength of it. Command Quests are quoted as a flat 12 scrolls a week for Global and as 12 plus 12 Abyss (24 per account) on KR/TW: the Abyss half is probably Season 2 content absent from Global S1. With no Heroic tier on Global, the 10% extraction bracket is empty and Unique becomes best-in-slot and therefore the only rarity that works as both sync fuel and sync target. The KR/TW fragment-acquisition boost does not carry to Global, so any Korean video’s pet-farming time estimate is optimistic by an unknown margin. Wings only became enhanceable on 2026-05-27 and race understanding and pet progress became server-wide on 2026-01-07: anything written before those dates is stale on those points. And the crit cap is quoted as 50% in 2025-12 and 80% in 2026-08, probably a patch change with no note confirming it.

Plan

Section titled “Plan”

While levelling (1 to 45)

Section titled “While levelling (1 to 45)”

Collect every feather you pass and turn them in at each region’s Monolith: it is account-wide, there is no decision to make, and it is free god stats. Turn on auto-extraction with filters excluding Unique, Heroic and quest items, so the Closet fills itself. Do not soulbind or manastone levelling gear. Pick up whatever pets drop in your path but do not detour to farm them yet.

PvE dungeons (post-cap)

Section titled “PvE dungeons (post-cap)”

Soul-engrave weapon and guard first, following the per-slot main option table above. On Genus, get Accuracy to 1,800-2,200 before anything else; then Smite on slots 3 and 9; then the PvE priority list. For Templar and Cleric, take Defense where a DPS would take Attack. Once you have crystals to spare, set up the three-page split: one PvP page, one PvE page for reaching caps and one long-term perfect PvE page.

PvP and the Abyss

Section titled “PvP and the Abyss”

Swap to the PvP Genus page: Smite, then Accuracy, Evasion, PvP Attack, PvP Damage Boost, Damage Tolerance, PvP Defense. Target around 2,500 combined accuracy (roughly 3,000 for Assassin and Ranger). Extract Abyss-shop tier-10 gear for guaranteed skins and an 80% AP refund, and keep flight-ring locations in mind for disengages.

A realistic weekly loop for a casual evening schedule

Section titled “A realistic weekly loop for a casual evening schedule”

Daily: 5 Duty Missions (~10 min, crystals) and a look at the Black Cloud Merchant on every character you log into. Weekly: burn the 14 pet-dungeon tickets, the 12 Command Scrolls and the 15 level-45 merchant contracts. Passive: keep auto-extract on and open yellow field cubes on sight (no key needed; red ones need keys). Everything else on this page is uncapped grind you do when you feel like it.

Preset hygiene

Section titled “Preset hygiene”

Set up the equipment-preset quick-swap hotkey early: one combat preset, one Flight Power accessory preset for long cross-territory hops. A single preset swap flips gear, skills, titles, wings, Pantheon and Genus page together, but it is blocked while any stigma is on cooldown.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Extracting rings or gaiters hunting for a skin. They never yield one. Gaiters look like a bargain on the market precisely because they are extraction-dead.
  • Locking a Genus slot before Smite lands on 3 or 9. Every lock raises the cost of every later reroll, from ~55 crystals to 75 at three locks to 405 near-max.
  • Locking a roll sitting at the bottom of its range, or rerolling a board below level 8: check the listed minimum and maximum before committing, and remember Heroic tier needs board level 9.
  • Spending rainbow crystals on a farmable category. Every other crystal type is infinitely farmable; the rainbow ones are the only genuinely scarce currency and belong to the Special board.
  • Using a Reset book on Unique gear. Unique tuning material is too scarce to recover from a bad reset: Heroic only.
  • Syncing away a rolled passive or active skill line, or selling and disenchanting Unique drops. Those lines are keepers, and Uniques are your only sync fuel.
  • Soulbinding or manastoning gear you will replace. Soulbinds transfer; manastones are destroyed.
  • Paying for Soul Engraving rerolls. Success rates on low-efficiency slots are too poor and the reroll stone is free via Material Conversion.
  • Batch-combining skins with one Lucky Aura in your bag. It is consumed per combination: one Aura boosts exactly one of ten combines. Skin-synthesis chance-up items are explicitly called not worth it.
  • Joining speed-clear parties that skip loot cubes. Boss achievement counters only increment when the cube is actually opened.
  • Comparing a title’s equip effect instead of its possession effect. You will already be wearing something better; the possession line is the one that stacks for free.
  • Pet-farming near enemy guard NPCs across the rift. They auto-aggro and flag you for PvP even in PvE mode.
  • Building an alt army for pet fragments. That path was removed: the pet dungeon is 14 runs a week per account.
  • Chasing every system. Aion 2 has more optional horizontal content than anyone can finish. Do the handful you will actually feel and skip the rest: on a casual schedule that is the correct instruction.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Which of the two “3”s is Global’s cap, the individual pet level or the Genus board: check it in Early Access (from 09-30); the September test ran pets at a reduced cap and did not settle it. The Closet was unavailable in that test, so extraction rates, the Collection Effect and the 700-skin title stay unverified until Early Access on 09-30. With no Heroic rarity on Global, it is unknown whether the 10% extraction bracket vanishes or whether Unique rates move. What the Pet Understanding level 5 transformation does is named in one low-quality source and nowhere else, and of the military-rank ladder we only have the top. Nobody quantifies the per-Monolith payoff of the 500+ feathers. And the named farming zones (Isle of Spirits, Immortal Island, Elun River Midstream, Kantas Valley East) are KR/TW map names that may not survive Global localisation: the method (split a spawn into three or four cycles and rotate, on a low-population channel) transfers, the coordinates may not.
