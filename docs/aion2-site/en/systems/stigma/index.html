Stigma

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: high. Global Season 1 equips four stigmas, not six, and they arrive at character levels 22, 27, 32 and 37.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Stigma is Aion 2’s second skill track: about 13 class-specific skills, of which only a handful are equipped at once, levelled with Stigma Shards (not skill points). It is a different system from Daevanion, the node board.
  • Shards are the scarcest progression currency in the game and the main reason to do PvP: the Abyss shop is the primary source. Questing to 45 gives about 20 shards; one skill to level 20 costs ~75.
  • So: budget for 2-3 maxed stigmas, not six. Every recent class guide levels 2-4 high and leaves the rest at 1-5.
  • Equip slots went 4 → 5 (2026-03-25) → 6 (TW by July) on KR and TW. Global Season 1 ships 4, and they unlock one at a time at character levels 22, 27, 32 and 37: the Global Launch Scale Test of 2026-09-17/19 settled it, a dozen sources agreeing.
  • Global’s stigma level cap is 20, not KR’s 25: the 21-25 “advanced shard” tier is a Chapter 1 feature, and the Global client states outright that 25 comes in a later season. No longer inferred: seen in-client in September.
  • Specialization options unlock automatically every 5 levels, so 5, 10, 15 and 20 are the only breakpoints that matter; stopping at 13 wastes shards.
  • Templar: the PvE damage stigmas (Doom Shield, Battlefield Banner) outrank the tanking ones in every 2026 guide; Taunt is a 1-point utility pick. The “Templar can DPS” thesis in one system.
  • Cleric: Earth’s Punishment is the single mandatory stigma: the debuff engine, not a heal, and it wants cooldown reduction for 100% uptime. Confirms the “debuff maintainer, not healer” framing.
  • Resets: 20,000 gold per level per skill on TW at launch; free on KR by mid-2026. Do not assume free on Global: treat early spending as semi-permanent.
  • Stigma-level presets only shipped on KR on September 9 after repeated delays. Until Global gets them, switching between a PvE and a PvP stigma build means re-levelling.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Two separate tracks. Mastery (normal) skills level with skill points, cap at 10 from points alone, with specialty sockets at 8, 12, 16 and 20; stigma skills level only with shards.
  • Stigmas are the class’s strongest abilities and are build-defining. Bar slots 1-4 are fixed class skills; stigmas occupy the free slots.
  • Pool of ~12 per class at the KR launch, 13 after the March 25 overhaul (one new per class); only a subset can be socketed.
  • Specialization unlocks every 5 levels, automatically and in a fixed order, unlike Mastery specialties, which you choose.
  • Costs scale in 5-level bands, doubling each band: 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 shards per level for 1-5 / 6-10 / 11-15 / 16-20.
  • Abyss Points are the main shard faucet. Shards are bought in the Abyss and arena shops; the Abyss is a progression requirement, not an optional PvP hobby.
  • They are overwhelmingly instant-cast with 30 s to 2 min 30 cooldowns: buffs, CC and burst, not filler damage.
  • The March 25 overhaul converted many buff-strip and bind stigmas, across every class, from resistible checks into guaranteed hard CC that ignores block and evade: the change that made stigmas PvP-defining.

Sources expanded over time on KR: Transcendence dungeons drop unstable shards since 2025-12-17; the March patch added Awakening War, Hidden Cube keys and Abyss directives, and doubled the shards on entry tickets. Global rebuilt the list. The gambling-based shard morph, roughly a 25% craft and resource-hungry, is gone, and shards now come from the Abyss, the Shugo Festival shop, Nightmare, Arena, Battlefield and the Ascension Trial. One source also reports shards removed from Transcendence and cube rewards on Global; nobody else repeats it, so treat that part as single-sourced. The practical effect is that shards no longer come from one grind, but the Abyss is still the only faucet without a cap.

That test settled a question that had been open since July: Global Season 1 equips four stigmas of the thirteen, and unlike KR and TW, which hand you a flat six, the slots arrive one at a time on the way to 37. A dozen independent testers agree; two hedged guesses of “5 or 6” are the only dissent, and neither shows a slot count. KR and TW stay on 6, so a KR/TW build has to be cut down by two before it fits.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Spreading shards evenly: six at 10 cost the same as two at 20 and are far weaker, because the effects change at 15 and 20.
  • Stopping on a non-multiple of 5.
  • Resetting casually: TW charged 20,000 per level per skill; KR is free; Global is unknown.
  • Skipping the Abyss because you want to PvE: it throttles shards, which throttles your best skills.
  • Judging a class before stigma 20: the investment is a huge power spike for some classes.
  • Assuming KR numbers. Skill and stigma effects do transfer, since the Global test build already carried a KR balance patch from two weeks earlier, but slot count, level cap and shard sources do not. Cut a KR or TW build from six stigmas to four before you use it.
  • Confusing Stigma with Daevanion.
  • Treating Taunt as mandatory on Templar: the 2026 damage builds cut it or leave it at 1.
  • One outlier: a TW guide says Abyss-shop Daevanion points “unlock stigma slots”; nobody else repeats it and it contradicts patch-driven slot progression. Probably a mistranslation.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Global’s stigma reset cost: nobody has seen the screen. Skill-point resets are free on Global, but that is a different system. Whether the level-1/2/3 loadout presets, which do save skill level on Global, also save stigma level. Shard drop rates: the Abyss, arenas and battlegrounds were all locked during the September test, so every Global figure so far is a shop price, not a farm rate. The week-one Abyss Point cap is part of that, with no reliable figure: see the shard economy table above. Whether the four-slot cap is a Season 1 squeeze that lifts to six later, as one pre-test source claimed.
