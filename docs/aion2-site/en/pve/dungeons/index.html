Dungeons and raids

Region: Korea and Taiwan · Version: up to 2026-09-15 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium. Real gaps exist: Draupnir and Vakron have no mechanics guide in any source, and the one English dungeon article lists instances that do not exist.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Group PvE has exactly three shapes: Expedition (원정, the gearing treadmill), Transcendence (초월, Arcana cards) and Sanctuary (성역, the raid). Everything else labelled “dungeon” is solo or semi-solo chore content.
  • On Global parties are 5 (not 4) and the raid is 10 (not 8); item-level gates are higher, so you cannot skip the 1-star dungeons.
  • As Templar you are the puller and the solo tank of every “only the aggro holder may be hit” mechanic: that pattern exists in Krao Cave, Ferocious Horn Den, Ludra and both Sanctuary 4 bosses.
  • Almost nothing is daily. The real budget is weekly: 14 daily-dungeon runs per account, 3 Ascension Trials, 1 rewarded Sanctuary clear, 14 Nightmare entries banked. 3-4 h a day is far more than the content needs.
  • Loot is gated twice: a rare ground drop, then a cube you open with Odyle energy (40 per open, regen 10 per 3 h, 15 with membership).
  • Anti-alt rules are strict: 1★ and 2★ Kinah is 100% bound, 3★ 50%, only 4★ and 5★ free. Daily-dungeon entries are shared server-wide: an alt cannot re-run what your main did.
  • The Season 1 raid is contested. One source says Abyssal Forge: Ludra ships; two others say the Sanctuary is absent at launch. Plan for no raid in week one.
  • Sanctuary 3 (Chalice of Muspel) and 4 (Snowfield of Sorrow) are KR-only. Read them as a preview of raid design, not as homework.
  • Noise warning: the loop is built on duplicate gear you must keep (Substance Morph, Potential stones). Budget bag space on day one.
  • The playtest (cap 37) only exposes Exploration-mode Krao Cave (20), Uruguru Canyon (28) and Fire Temple (35).

Shared rules

Section titled “Shared rules”
  • Expedition difficulty bands: Exploration (탐험) → Conquest Normal (정복 보통) → Conquest Hard. Exploration pays bound Kinah, Conquest unbound.
  • Star tiers in Conquest: 1★ Krao Cave and Draupnir · 2★ Uruguru Canyon and Vakron’s Sky Island · 3★ Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den. In each tier one drops weapon and accessories and the other armour.
  • Two loot rolls per clear: a low-odds tradeable ground drop and a guaranteed end-boss cube opened with Odyle energy (membership doubles the open).
  • Pity exists: entry-tier Expeditions guarantee a chosen piece within 3 runs; Conquest pity needs 14 chests (7 with membership).
  • Anti-carry gate: skipping the end chest still burns an “unobtained cube” allowance, capped at 10 a week, after which you cannot enter dungeons at all.
  • Transcendence differs: a monster-kill quota inside before the end boss unlocks, and each of its 4 difficulty levels adds a debuff.
  • Sanctuary is the only source of certain slots: bracelets (S1-2), brooches (S3), seals (Chapter 1).
  • Crafted gear beats dungeon gear at equal tier (+5% PvE damage, more stat freedom); dungeon gear closes half the gap with Potential stones (4 applications).
  • The Rift is on a clock: the Rift of Time and Space opens every 3 h (400 capacity since December 2025) and is the only way to the enemy faction’s sealed dungeons and strongholds.
  • Field-boss loot goes to the party with the highest damage contribution; a single hit is enough for the title.
  • The entry tickets (1 per 8 h, cap 21) of late 2025 died with Chapter 1: on Global only Odyle energy applies.

Expeditions

Section titled “Expeditions”

Krao Cave (크라오 동굴, Crow Cave, Kraal Cave) · 1★

Exploration at level ~20-22; Conquest at 45. Recommended item level 1,300+ (1,400-1,600 in practice). The first Conquest and the easiest hero-item source; unbound Kinah farm. Proven comps: Templar + Cleric + 2 DPS, or Cleric + Cleric + Ranger + DPS with no tank. Mechanic: the final boss forces a groggy check at 50%: the whole party must land a CC or wipe; every member slots at least one groggy skill. “Pizza” positional patterns. The cheapest place to learn the “block the tank-only hit” pattern.

Draupnir (드라웁니르) · 1★. Krao Cave’s pair; out of reach in the playtest. The Draupnir story chapter cannot be completed below gear score 1,000, and finishing it grants your first gold weapon. Explicitly unskippable on Global. No source has a boss guide: a real hole in one of the two dungeons you must clear first.

Uruguru Canyon (우루구구 협곡) · 2★. Exploration at 28. The accessory half of the 2★ pair. Its Exploration needed a ticket, energy and a horn item: worse per run than Fire Temple. No mechanics guide; run occasionally for accessories while Vakron gives the rest.

Vakron’s Sky Island (바크론의 공중섬) · 2★. Official requirement ~1,600, pugs 1,800+; farm window from ~1,800 to ~2,200. Drops the level-76 Unique armour set, and its cube drops wing tablets, the reason to spend Odyle here. “The key content” per several KR guides. No extractable mechanics guide.

Fire Temple (불의 신전) · 3★. Exploration at 35. Conquest gate 2,200 stated, 2,500 demanded by pugs after a nerf, ~2,400 commonly. Boss Kromede. Signature drop: the “Enraged Kromede” growing weapon set. Its Exploration needs no ticket, only 30 energy: the cheapest farm in the game. 32 achievements grant a free Pantheon colossus. Hard mode needs a second healer: 1 Chanter + 1 Cleric + 3 DPS. Naming trap: Korean abbreviates it 불신 and auto-captions render it “Bulsin”, “Blesin Shrine” or “Unfallen”.

Ferocious Horn Den (사나운 뿔 암굴) · 3★. Fire Temple’s pair; final boss Ferocious Horn Nuakum; drops the “Nuacom” guard. Full mechanic list (Conquest Normal): at the entrance pick up a bomb crate (F) and throw it at the barricade. Boss 1: diagonal charge (dodge diagonally, not backwards); dance phase with purple orbs; a leap with a water-stream pattern. Totem wave: do not activate the totem until everyone is inside; DPS throw crates at the shaman while tank and healer hold centre. Boss 2: allied souls mechanic; on “Soul Call” spread to the sides then re-stack. Before the final boss, drink Darin’s Corilus Tea (more dodges). Final: blue orbs damage anyone close; on “the prey has been chosen” four group up and exactly one player blocks the shadow add (two blockers stack a debuff lethal at 2 stacks); pizza AoE to a side; triple charge sidestepped; black hole: the chosen player stands ~180° opposite to redirect the red orb; final “red hand”: one bound and the boss lands exactly two hits, block them, do not dodge. Cleric trap: a Salvation-type shield or immunity does not block this boss’s mechanic damage. The most Cleric-specific warning in the corpus.

Dead Dramata’s Nest (Season 2, January 2026) and Cradle of Nothingness / Nihility (무의 요람, January 2026, boss Afflicted Bakarma): inside the inferred Global cutoff, with no English coverage. Cradle is used as the difficulty benchmark: “you must actually learn the gimmicks”.

Corridor of Illusion (환영의 회랑, Season 3), Citadel of the Fallen Daeva (Chapter 1): outside Global. Corridor: Normal 3,000, Hard 3,500, three bosses; boss 1, lure the ghost chasing you onto a glowing floor patch or it explodes into a fear that throws you out of the arena; a gate needing 4 players; boss 3, a mirror puzzle with crystals (2 red orbs survivable, 3 not); on Hard, clones at 0% tagged 1 and 2.

Transcendence

Section titled “Transcendence”

Rules: 5 players on Global; 10 stages on KR, 4 levels on Global; an S-rank clear unlocks the next; entries 1 per 12 h, cap 14; weekly boss cap 28. Global improvement: level 1 drops Rare Arcana where KR dropped Common, so it is worth pushing early.

  • Deus Research Base (데우스 연구기지): one of the two at Global launch. Stage 9 is melee-unfriendly: a +2 m weapon cleared it ~40 s faster. The “research base” daily-dungeon entry is the best early enhancement-stone source.
  • Shattered Arkanis (조각난 아르카니스): the second at launch.
  • Sunken Temple of Life (February 2026), Mirror of Scarlet Desire (2-4 Arcana per run at difficulty 2; the Punishing Overture set), Abyssal Horn Den (Chapter 1; not the same as Ferocious Horn Den): KR.

Sanctuary (the raid)

Section titled “Sanctuary (the raid)”

Rules: 8 on KR (2 parties of 4), 10 on Global; 4 attempts a week but only 2 pay; a 2-revive limit per player per boss.

Abyssal Forge: Ludra (루드라, Sanctuary 1). Gate 2,700 power, minimum 8 to start; in practice ranged DPS 2,900-3,000 and melee ~3,200 because the patterns favour ranged. World-first comp: 2 Cleric, 1 Sorcerer, 1 Ranger, 2 Gladiator, 2 Assassin, all 3,500+. Drops: 2.5% full legendary set, 10% tier-1 Unique set; the “Siméon” bracelet guaranteed. Bosses 1 and 2 have no enrage; boss 3 does. Boss 1: tank front, everyone behind; one player drags a red tether to a map edge without crossing anyone. Boss 2: a large fan-shaped hit (tank front, rest opposite); at a third, an add phase with a laser sequence and killing the spirit before it reaches centre. Ludra: two squads; when it rears up, two laser rings emit from the floor: jump and fly to clear both. Ludra’s design was carried by 1-2 skilled players plus infinite Cleric revives; the developers moved away from it in later raids.

Erosion Purification Chamber (Sanctuary 2, February 2026): exclusive bracelets. Chalice of Muspel (Sanctuary 3, May, item level 4,500, boss Kaldrix, brooch slot): explicitly not in Global. Snowfield of Sorrow (비탄의 설원, Sanctuary 4, September): Easy, Normal and Hard; 4 weekly runs; a “physical skill check” design with fewer RNG wipes; a raid-wide Temperature system (below the midpoint it slows you; frozen, 10% HP every 5 s; standing near a warmer ally restores it); bosses Returner Triton and Deltras (two phases with an HP reset between). See the Templar PvE and Cleric PvE guides for the exact role in this raid.

What is contested

Section titled “What is contested”

Whether the Sanctuary ships in Season 1 (three sources, three answers). Fire Temple’s gate: the official and the social number never matched. Sanctuary 4’s boss count (two, the second with two phases). Subjugation War: removed in KR Season 3; unknown for Global. aion2kina’s dungeon article lists “Abyss Citadel”, “Sanctum Library”, “Dark Poeta Expedition”, a 12-player raid and a level-50 floor: none exists in any Korean or official source. Treat it as fabricated.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Skipping end chests “to save runs”: it locks you out of every dungeon.
  • Entering Krao Cave without a groggy skill per person.
  • Cleric: trusting Salvation against Ferocious Horn Den’s mechanic.
  • Two blockers on “the prey has been chosen”.
  • Spending Odyle at Uruguru when Vakron’s cube drops the wing tablets.
  • Selling or disenchanting Unique duplicates: they are Potential stones and soulbind fuel.
  • Reading Chalice of Muspel or Snowfield guides as Global homework.
