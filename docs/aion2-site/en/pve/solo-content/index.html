Solo content

Region: global and Korean/Taiwanese precedent · Version: Global Season 1 (LST 2026-09-17/19, Advanced Access from 2026-09-30) + KR/TW history · Reviewed: 2026-10-05 · Confidence: medium. The weekly counters and the single Daily Dungeon are seen in the Global client. Nightmare and Ascension Trial were closed in the September test, so their detailed workings (ladder, shop, difficulties) come from Korea and Taiwan or from creators explaining them for Global without having recorded it there.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Level-45 solo content is four things with their own counter: Nightmare (one-on-one bosses, 2 tickets a day per character, capped at 14), Ascension Trial (timed mob rooms, 3 a week per character), the Daily Dungeon (14 runs a week per account, 21 with membership), and, with no counter at all, the map’s sealed dungeons and strongholds, each cleared once per character.
  • None of them spend Odyle Energy. That makes them the perfect complement to group PvE’s dungeons: energy goes into Conquest and Transcendence, and this is played on the side.
  • Nightmare is season progression, not a weekly chore. Each boss has 10 levels; killing a high level pays out all the lower first-clears at once. What matters is reaching the season’s final boss (Zikel in Season 1), unlocking its statue for the Pantheon and buying it with Phantasmal Fragments.
  • Ascension Trial does not forgive failure: the attempt is spent on entry. Pick a difficulty you clear with margin.
  • Global’s Daily Dungeon is a single one: Daeva Bio Research Base, which pays enhancement stones based on how many mobs you kill before time runs out. The Korean pet variant (Odium Storage), the Kinah variant (Secret Depository) and the Unknown Gap Challenge Ticket that picks between them are not in the Global Season 1 [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19].
  • Spend your Daily Dungeon runs late in the week, when your character kills fastest: the payout scales with kills, not with the number of runs.
  • Sealed dungeons and strongholds are the best free power in the game and do not sync across alts: every character repeats them. On Global, sealed dungeons also give Wisdom Stones (skill points), which the Monolith no longer gives.
  • Instant-clear tickets (Daily Dungeon and Nightmare) exist in Korea and Taiwan; whether they exist on Global is not confirmed.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

Nightmare (악몽)

Section titled “Nightmare (악몽)”
  1. Access: level 45 [KR/TW, 2026-09]. It is strictly solo: no party [Global source, 2026-09-18]. Each season adds a “Dream Floor” with its own bosses, and old floors are not removed [TW, 2026-01-21]. Korea and Taiwan have 4 floors; Global starts with floor 1 [KR, 2026-08-20], with seasons of about 2 months according to a creator explaining it for Global [Global source, 2026-09-19].
  2. Tickets: 2 generate a day per character and bank up to 14; anything generated above that is lost [TW, 2026-09-04; Global source, 2026-09-18]. The ticket is only spent on killing the boss, not on entry, so you can practice a level without losing anything [Global source, 2026-09-18].
  3. Ladder: each boss has 10 difficulty levels [KR, 2026-09-15]. In the original design you had to kill each boss 10 times to open the next range of bosses (white → green → blue, the final boss) [TW, 2026-09-04]. Korea softened this in Chapter 1 (2026-07-01): all levels open at once, and a high-level kill retroactively pays all that week’s lower ones [TW, 2026-06-15]. Sources talking about Global repeat the softened version: you can go straight to level 10 if your gear is good enough, and you collect every intermediate first-clear with a single ticket [Global source, 2026-09-18/19].
  4. Currency: each kill pays Phantasmal Fragments. The first clear of a new level pays ~1,000-1,200 and a repeat 385 [Global source, 2026-09-18; numbers from the creator’s KR/TW playthrough]. That is why you never climb levels in order with tickets.
  5. Final boss and Pantheon: defeating the season’s final boss (Zikel in Season 1, also transcribed “Ziggurat Operation”) unlocks its statue in the shop; you then have to buy it separately. These are two steps, and a lot of people forget the second [Global source, 2026-09-19]. The statue goes to the Pantheon, the personal trophy room (not housing: real housing is announced with no date [TW, 2026-06-15]), and gives permanent god stats; how those stats break down is in stats.
  6. Ranking: rankings by class, Templar against Templar [Global source, 2026-09-20], and ranking rewards are purely cosmetic: the “heroic” title that once carried PvE power was removed [Global source, 2026-09-18].

Zikel mechanics (shown in a KR/TW run, explained for Global, 2026-09-18):

  1. Bleed on every hit: carry yellow cleanse potions and use them before it stacks. With gear that is just barely enough, the bleed alone kills you.
  2. Frontal grab: fight from the boss’s back.
  3. Ground slam: jump right on impact.
  4. Lane phase: stand in the safe lane before the explosion, then run to the boss’s feet afterward.

Ascension Trial (승천의 시련)

Section titled “Ascension Trial (승천의 시련)”
  1. Access: level 45 and 1,000 gear score for the first difficulty [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19, threshold only; the content was closed]. A Global source says “level 45 and ~1,000 CP for Easy” [Global source, 2026-09-19]. At first, only Easy will be available on Global [Global source, 2026-09-19].
  2. Entries: 3 a week per character, weekly reset; works on alts [Global source, 2026-09-19]. The attempt is consumed on entry even if you fail, and failing at a high difficulty does not drop you to a lower one for free [Global source, 2026-09-19].
  3. Structure: three rooms of mob waves with a boss at the end, against the clock [TW, 2026-09-04; KR, 2025-12-23]. Under 5 minutes per run [KR, 2026-09-19].
  4. Difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard, Extreme, Insane and, in Korea since Chapter 1, Hell and Doom: 7 in total [KR, 2026-07-20]. Mobs range from level 45 on Easy to 75 on the highest Korean tier, and difficulty rises every 500 gear score [KR, 2026-09-15/19].
  5. Scoring: completion plus time remaining, multiplied by difficulty [KR, 2026-09-15]. There is a ranking, with no reward [KR/TW, 2026-09-10]. It is the content where a Templar performs best: #1 on the Korean Ascension Trial ranking in December 2025 [KR, 2025-12-22].
  6. Rewards: Silentium (sold for Kinah) and a Superior Ascension chest with manastones, soul stones, enhancement stones and Stigma Shards [Global source, 2026-09-19]; Daevanion Ariel fragments, ~20 per clear on Normal [KR, 2025-12-23]; Unstable Stigma Shards on the higher difficulties [KR, 2026-07-20]. Whether Global adds pet-soul chests here, as two creators suspect, is unconfirmed.

Daily Dungeon: Daeva Bio Research Base

Section titled “Daily Dungeon: Daeva Bio Research Base”
  1. It is not daily. It is a weekly pool of 14 runs per account (server-wide), 21 with membership [Global, 2026-09]; it opens at level 30 [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19]. An alt cannot repeat what your main already spent. Details on caps and membership are in routine.
  2. A single dungeon on Global: Daeva Bio Research Base, enhancement stones [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19, four sources]. A Portuguese guide claims three tracks on Global; every source that actually played the test contradicts it.
  3. How it plays: you pick 1 of 3 random buffs at the start, clear mobs against the clock, and the reward scales with kills [TW, 2025-12-19; Global AA, 2026-09-30]. Korea cut the stages from 3 to 1 in December 2025 [KR/TW, 2025-12-24]. A run lasts 2-3 minutes [KR, 2026-09-27].
  4. Payout: in Taiwan, ~4,000-5,000 enhancement stones per run for someone freshly 45 and ~8,700-9,000 fully geared, with a cap of 10,000 nobody has reported hitting [TW, 2025-12-19]. One Advanced Access example: ~9,000 per clear before the clock plus a “fast clear” bonus of ~2,700 [Global AA, 2026-09-30; the footage doesn’t prove the image is from the Global client].
  5. Low-damage classes earn less: Templar and Cleric clear fewer mobs than Gladiator or Assassin in the same time [TW, 2025-12-19].

What Korea and Taiwan have on top (not yet on Global)

Section titled “What Korea and Taiwan have on top (not yet on Global)”
  • Odium Storage / Odylium Repository (오디움 저장소): second Daily Dungeon, added on 2025-12-24. Pays pet-growth points based on score [KR/TW, 2025-12-24]. Played like this: you destroy the unstable odylium, then have ~25 s to collect colored points, purple and gold first, green last [KR/TW, 2025-12-25]. In Korea it yields 30-40 Pet Soul Boxes a week, 10 souls per box [KR, 2026-01-14], and is considered the most valuable variant [KR, 2026-07-20].
  • Diver Research Base is the name some transcriptions use for Daeva Bio Research Base. The two share the same entry pool [KR/TW, 2025-12-25].
  • Unknown Gap Challenge Ticket: the ticket you use to pick between Odium Storage and Diver Research Base on entry [KR/TW, 2025-12-24]. On Global, with only one dungeon, there is nothing to choose between.
  • Secret Depository (Crobakhi): third variant, Kinah-bound, less efficient than other Kinah sources [KR, 2026-07-20].
  • Cap history: Odium Storage went from 7 per character to 14 per server in Korean Season 3 (2026-04-08) [KR, 2026-04-09]. That is the rule Global already ships with.
  • Several creators place pets and Kinah as “a later season” on Global [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19]; there is no date.

Sealed dungeons and strongholds

Section titled “Sealed dungeons and strongholds”
  • Sealed dungeons: the “?” marks on the map that turn into a ring icon as you approach [Global AA, 2026-10-04]. There are 62 on your faction’s map [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19] and, in Korea and Taiwan, a matching number mirrored on the enemy’s [KR/TW, 2025-12-18]; you reach that map through the Rift (opens every 3 h from level 45; see dungeons). Each one is completed once per character and does not sync across alts, unlike feathers and pets [KR/TW, 2026-05-12 and 2026-07-31].
  • Rewards on Global: Wisdom Stones (skill points) and Daevanion crystals, plus enhancement stones [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19; Global AA, 2026-10-04]. The ones on the enemy map also give Abyss Points [Global AA, 2026-10-04]. Exactly how many points each one gives is in leveling, with sources disagreeing (2+2 or 1+1). In Korea they also paid 15,000 Kinah per clear [TW, 2025-12-03].
  • Strongholds (hideouts): a bonfire-with-shield icon [Global AA, 2026-10-04]. Fixed structure: mobs → activate one or more “security modules” → boss → chest [KR, 2026-09-03]. They are the only source of the Noble Belt enhance scroll for the belt (full chain in gear). Korea has 15 per faction map, 30 in total [KR, 2026-09-03]; the Global figure is not published.
  • Each stronghold hides 2-3 extra one-time chests with Kinah past the cap; if you don’t open them during the run, they are gone forever [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19]. Save “pick a piece” chests until you only need one specific slot [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19].
  • The map’s exploration tab tracks sealed dungeons and strongholds cleared per zone [Global LST, 2026-09-17/19].

Weekly plan (3-4 h a day)

Section titled “Weekly plan (3-4 h a day)”

This plan covers only solo content; fit it into the full daily and weekly routine. The day and time of Global’s weekly reset are still unconfirmed, so “reset day” means whatever your client shows.

When What to do Time
Reset day 3 Ascension Trials at the difficulty you clear with margin (not the one that “almost” works) ~15 min
Every 2-3 days Nightmare: spend tickets on the highest level you actually kill; never let it reach 14 ~5 min per attempt at the ceiling
Any evening with a Rift 4-5 sealed dungeons or strongholds on the enemy map, without flagging for PvP if you only want to farm 30-45 min
Spare evenings Your own pending sealed dungeons and strongholds, until the exploration tab is complete 5 min each
1-2 days before reset All 14 Daily Dungeon runs (21 with membership) in one sitting, with that week’s gear upgrade already on ~30-45 min
Before reset Check that no Nightmare ticket is sitting above 12, and buy whatever’s due in the shop 5 min

At 3-4 h a day, the solo-content block fits comfortably into two evenings a week; the rest of the time goes to Conquest, Transcendence and the Abyss.

Priority order for the first week at 45: pending sealed dungeons and strongholds (permanent power, free, no counter) → Ascension Trial on Easy → Nightmare as far as your gear holds → Daily Dungeon at the end of the week.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Climbing Nightmare in order. Every low level you kill with a separate ticket is a wasted ticket: the high level pays out all the lower first-clears.
  • Letting Nightmare tickets sit at 14. Anything generated above that is lost.
  • Killing Zikel and not buying the statue. Unlocking it doesn’t give it to you; you have to buy it.
  • Spending fragments on lesser statues before you have the 14,000 for Zikel’s.
  • Entering Ascension Trial at a difficulty that’s just barely enough. The attempt is lost even if you fail.
  • Burning the Daily Dungeon on the first day of the week with your weakest gear: it pays by kills.
  • Spending Daily Dungeon runs on an alt. The pool is account-wide: what the alt spends, your main doesn’t get.
  • Walking past a stronghold’s extra chests: they don’t come back.
  • Opening “pick a piece” chests right away instead of saving them for the slot you’re missing.
  • Taking Korean Daily Dungeon guides at face value: almost all of them talk about Odium Storage and the Unknown Gap Challenge Ticket, which don’t exist on Global for now.
  • Counting on instant-clear tickets on Global. They are not confirmed.
  • Leaving scroll auto-use set to “always” instead of “in combat”: in Nightmare you burn expensive consumables for nothing. Use white scrolls first and save the blue ones [Global source, 2026-09-18].
