Party, matchmaking and loot

Region: Global and Korean/Taiwanese precedent · Version: Global LST of 09-17/19/2026 and Advanced Access of 09-30 to 10-04/2026, plus KR/TW patches · Reviewed: 2026-10-05 · Confidence: medium. What shows up in the Global client is clear (5-player parties, 10-player raid, cross-faction and cross-server matchmaking, a private room for one, the ~5% damage gate to open the chest). The fine-grained split rules (dice rolls, leader settings), the kick vote and the anti-multibox lock are only documented in Korea and Taiwan.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • 5-player party, 10-player raid (2 parties of 5) on Global, from day one [GLOBAL, NC dev stream 2026-09-06; LST 2026-09-20]. Korea played with 4 and 8 until Chapter 1 on 2026-07-01.
  • Dungeon matchmaking is cross-faction and cross-server within your region [GLOBAL, 2026-09-06]. An Asmodian friend can run dungeons with you even if you’re Elyos; in the open world, on the other hand, you can’t party up [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20].
  • For solo play, a private room. Expedition → create room → Private → Start: monsters and bosses scale to a single player [GLOBAL, Advanced Access 2026-10-04]. It’s the way to clear story Exploration without relying on Quick Join.
  • Two loot sources per run: ground drops (tradeable) and the final boss’s cube, which you only open by spending Odyle Energy (40 per open, 80 with membership for a double reward) [GLOBAL, 2026-10-04]. The cube is personal: each player opens their own.
  • No ~5% of the final boss’s damage, no chest [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20]. You can’t be carried through it, not you and not an alt.
  • Not claiming the cube counts too: the “unclaimed cube” counter caps at 10 a week, after which you can’t enter any dungeon [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-19; KR 2026-09-14].
  • Weekly boss reservations per character: 35 final bosses in Expedition (Exploration + Conquest) and 28 in Transcendence, separate pools but the same Odyle Energy [KR/TW, 2026-04-14 and 2026-06-07]. A Korean creator who played the LST says Global’s entry limits are the same [GLOBAL, 2026-09-18].
  • Korea and Taiwan also have a dungeon kick vote (2026-05-27), a loot lock for restricted accounts (anti-multibox, 2025-12-24) and per-player Kinah caps on specific bosses (2025-12-17). None of the three is confirmed or ruled out on Global.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

Finding a group

Section titled “Finding a group”

The Expedition menu (and the Transcendence one) has two paths: join something that already exists or create a room.

  1. Quick Join / drop-in. Puts you into a party at random and instantly, often into a run already in progress. Fast, no control over who you’re with [KR/TW, 2026-06-29]. In the Global Portuguese client it’s called “Participação rápida” [GLOBAL, 2026-10-04]; the exact Spanish-client label is not recorded.
  2. Request to join. You pick a public room from the list and the leader accepts or rejects you based on combat power, gear and class [KR/TW, 2026-06-29].
  3. Create a room. You’re the leader and decide two things [KR/TW, 2026-06-29; GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20 for public/private and roles]:
    • Public (shows up on the list) or Private (invite-only).
    • By request (you vet each applicant) or Instant join (anyone enters, no filter). Instant join only makes sense where the comp doesn’t matter; use By request for Conquest.
    • The Global finder lets you flag which roles are missing (tank, healer, DPS) [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20].
  4. A private room with nobody invited = an instance for one. The dungeon scales to a single player [GLOBAL, Advanced Access 2026-10-04]. For story Exploration it’s faster than waiting on strangers, and don’t spend Odyle on that cube (see leveling).

Outside the finder:

  • Open world: left-click a player → Invite to party (the same menu offers add friend, follow, duel and inspect) [KR/TW, 2026-06-29].
  • Legion: right-click the member in the legion panel → Invite to party [KR/TW, 2026-06-29].

Factions and servers

Section titled “Factions and servers”
  • Global: dungeon matchmaking is cross-faction and cross-server within the region [GLOBAL, NC dev stream 2026-09-06]. During Advanced Access the pool was Advanced-Access servers only; since launch they’re merged with launch servers (see launch).
  • The “same race” filter means same faction. Leave it off if you want the other faction included; turn it on for same-faction-only parties [KR/TW, 2026-03-17 and 2026-06-29].
  • Open world: your faction only. Elyos and Asmodians don’t party up outside dungeons [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20]. PvP (Rift, Abyss, sieges) remains faction versus faction.
  • Talking to the other faction. In Korea, since 2026-03-11, a mixed party shares party chat and voice chat, without the special tool that used to be needed to understand the other faction’s language [KR/TW, 2026-03-11]. The Global client has a chat auto-translate toggle in settings [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-18].
  • Korean exception: Subjugation (the former “Raid”) stayed single-faction when Expedition, Transcendence and Sanctuary opened up to mixed parties [KR/TW, 2026-03-11]. Whether it exists on Global is unclear.
  • Arena and battleground use their own matchmaking button, not rooms, and don’t go by server pairs: see arena.

Inspecting others

Section titled “Inspecting others”

Clicking a player → inspect shows their full gear; it’s what a leader checks before accepting you [KR/TW, 2026-06-29]. Since the Korean patch of 2026-03-25 you can also inspect characters from the other faction, and the panel shows their legion name [KR/TW, 2026-03-17]. Global likely carries this (it comes from a build after that patch) but it hasn’t been seen in any source.

Composition

Section titled “Composition”
  • Standard: 1 tank, 3 DPS, 1 support [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20]. Doubling up on DPS is fine; two classes that give the same party buff don’t stack it (two Clerics, for example) [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20].
  • A tank or support is often accepted below the requested combat power, because they’re scarce; but they still need their ~5% of damage to open the chest [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20].
  • Comps per dungeon and Fire Temple Hard’s second healer: see dungeons.

Kicking someone

Section titled “Kicking someone”
  • Korea has a dungeon kick vote since the patch of 2026-05-27, announced alongside an account- and character-blocking system in the April livestream [KR, 2026-04-21 and 2026-05-26]. The rules (how many votes, whether the leader can kick alone, any penalty) appear in no source: the summaries only name it.
  • Before that, NC shipped a personal DPS meter, visible only to yourself, precisely to curb damage-based kicks (see regions and patches).
  • Kick culture exists: in Taiwan the Templar “gets kicked from a lot of groups” in high-level PvE [TW, 2026-01-09], and a Global LST creator says he’d instantly kick a Cleric without the resurrection stigma [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-17, opinion].
  • If your connection drops inside a dungeon, the run’s progress resets to the start [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-18].

How loot is split

Section titled “How loot is split”

Final boss cube (personal).

  • Each player opens their own cube by spending Odyle Energy: 40 per open; with membership it costs 80 and the reward is doubled [GLOBAL, Advanced Access 2026-10-04]. Global membership also gives “more choices” in Expedition and Transcendence Odyle cubes [GLOBAL, client 2026-10-01]. Regen rate and energy cap: see dungeons.
  • Contribution gate: ~5% of total damage to the boss for the loot queue to open for you, even when running solo [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20, two sources]. In Korean Sanctuary that floor dropped from 5% to 3% on 2025-12-17 [KR/TW]; the Global raid’s figure is not verified. A Reddit thread mentions a healing minimum for a Cleric to get paid [GLOBAL, Reddit 2026-09-22, no figure].
  • What drops from the final cube is character-bound: it can’t be traded or sold [KR, official pre-launch Q&A, 2025-11-13]. Global exception: Pantheon wings and paintings drop as a tradeable sealed item (detail in dungeons).
  • Reward pools: on Global loot no longer comes from one single table; there are several pools, each with its own odds of triggering, and only after that does it roll among the items inside (for example, a Kromede pool at 5.4%) [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-17].
  • The pity counter runs on cubes opened: see dungeons.

Ground drops and dice rolls.

  • Besides the cube, the run drops direct loot from bosses. These are tradeable [KR, 2025-11-13] and every extra boss you clear is an extra roll [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-19].
  • In a party those drops are rolled for with dice among members [KR, official Q&A, 2025-11-13]. A Korean raid video has “인정” (pass or need) rolls audible on weapons after the boss [KR, 2026-09-09]. The split settings the leader can choose (free-for-all, round-robin, leader distributes…) are described by no source.
  • In the open world, ground items are per player up to a certain quest level; past that, if one person picks it up, it disappears for everyone else [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-20].
  • Looting is manual [KR, 2025-11-13], but Global ships a pickup filter by rarity (epic, unique, rare, common) [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-18] and pets that pick items up on their own [GLOBAL, 2026-08-11 and 2026-08-16].
  • Field bosses: loot goes to the party with the highest damage contribution; see dungeons. Abyss bosses: reward by contribution rank, even if you died less than 100 m away; see Abyss.

Loot lock for restricted accounts

Section titled “Loot lock for restricted accounts”

Korea and Taiwan have a drop restriction against multiboxing and bots: a flagged account can still party up and items still drop normally, but that player can’t pick them up [KR/TW, 2025-12-24]. On 2025-12-24 it was extended to sealed dungeons, strongholds, Transcendence and Expedition. It’s not explained what flags an account. Global hasn’t mentioned it, either way.

Practical consequence if it lands: running several clients at once into a dungeon gives no extra loot, and a flagged party member doesn’t take anything away from you, because their share simply never gets picked up.

Weekly and Kinah caps

Section titled “Weekly and Kinah caps”
  • Boss reservations per character per week: 35 final bosses in Expedition, 28 in Transcendence; once a character hits the cap, it can’t enter that dungeon type again until reset [KR, 2026-04-14]. Energy is shared between the two pools [KR/TW, 2026-06-07]. In April NC said “cap-reset tickets” already exist but aren’t implemented [KR, 2026-04-14].
  • Per-player Kinah cap on specific bosses: since 2025-12-17 each player has a Kinah cap per boss: 1,000,000 on Guardian Diet/Inama and 200,000 on Spirit King Agro (names from auto-captions) [KR/TW, 2025-12-17]. The same patch added a weekly cumulative server-wide tracker for Odyle cube rewards.
  • Daily open-world Kinah cap: 1,000,000, confirmed on Global; the Abyss is exempt [GLOBAL, LST 2026-09-19]. Detail and per-account alt rules: see routine.
  • Star-bound Kinah (1-2★ bound, 3★ half-bound…): see dungeons.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Running story Exploration with Quick Join when a private room scales it to just you.
  • Opening the Exploration cube during the campaign: it burns Odyle that’s worth more in Conquest.
  • Skipping the cube to “save it”: it counts as an unclaimed cube and at 10 you’re locked out of dungeons for the whole week.
  • Riding along: an alt or a healer who doesn’t reach ~5% of the boss’s damage doesn’t open their chest.
  • Bringing two classes that give the same buff thinking it stacks: it doesn’t.
  • Instant join in Conquest: anyone can get in; use By request.
  • Leaving the faction filter on by accident: it halves the pool and lengthens the queue.
  • Trusting that cube loot can be sold: it’s bound; what’s tradeable is ground drops and the sealed item.
  • Burning through the 35 Expedition bosses on your main before you have the gear you’re after: the pool is weekly and doesn’t come back.
