Skills browser

Region: KR · Data: Inven Korea skill DB dump of 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: high on the numbers (they come straight off the API), medium on the English names (they are working translations).

Every class page carries its own full table, but comparing across classes means jumping between them. This is the same data in one place: the 313 skills of all eight classes, with a class selector, a kind filter (active, chain, passive, stigma) and a name search that takes English or Korean. The list shows rank 1 cooldown, cost and range; clicking a skill opens a detail panel with the attributes and description of rank 1 and rank 20, the specialties, the linked chains (which are clickable in turn) and links to that class’s skills guide and full table.

The class and the filter you pick are remembered in your browser.

      What this data is and is not

      The numbers come from the Korean Inven skill DB, dumped on 2026-09-22, and describe the Korean build. The English names are working translations, not the official localisation: they are filled in only where the hangul matches one of our class syntheses or the glossary exactly, so coverage is very uneven per class. Cleric 39/39 and Templar 37/39, but Sorcerer 19/39, Gladiator 18/41, Chanter 16/39, Spiritmaster 12/38, Ranger 10/38 and Assassin 4/40. Where there is no confident translation only the Korean name is shown. Global names may differ and will not be known until Advanced Access on 30 September. The descriptions are deliberately left in Korean rather than run through a translator.

      Keep in mind too that Global Season 1 equips 4 stigma slots (Korea and Taiwan get 6), so the 13-14 stigmas you see per class are the catalogue, not what you will actually have equipped.
