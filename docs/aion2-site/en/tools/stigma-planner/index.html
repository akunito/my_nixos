Stigma planner

Region: mixed · Rules: Global Launch Scale Test build (2026-09-17/19) · Skill data: Inven Korea dump of 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium.

On Global Season 1 you equip 4 stigmas out of your class’s 13, and the slots unlock at character levels 22 / 27 / 32 / 37 (Korea and Taiwan get 6). Each stigma levels to 20, with specialisation bonuses at 5, 10, 15 and 20. That turns the choice into a budget problem rather than a taste one: taking a single stigma from 1 to 20 costs about 75 shards, and taking all four to the cap is 300.

This tool puts numbers on that budget. Pick your stigmas, set a target level for each, and it shows the total in shards, the Abyss Point equivalent, and how many days of Duty Missions that would take if they were your only AP income. The selection is saved in your browser, per class.

The rules it encodes

Section titled “The rules it encodes”
Rule Global Season 1 value
Equipped stigma slots 4 of 13, at character levels 22 / 27 / 32 / 37 (KR/TW: 6)
Stigma level cap 20 (25 is deferred to a later season)
Shards per level 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 for levels 1-5 / 6-10 / 11-15 / 16-20 → ≈75 to reach 20
Specialisation bonuses levels 5 / 10 / 15 / 20
Shard price in the Abyss shop 25,000 AP
Shugo shop 35 shards per character, once (season cap)
Duty Missions 5 a day × 500 AP = 2,500 AP/day, no weekly cap

Every one of these is a Launch Scale Test figure (2026-09-17/19) or a Korean-build one, not a value read off the 5 October launch client. Source: research/notes/deltas/2026-09/_consensus.md §1 (slots, unlock ladder, the level-20 cap and the 1/2/4/8 scale with its ~75 shards), plus templar-pve.md and templar-pvp.md for the 25,000 AP Abyss price, cleric-pvp.md and gear.md for the one-off 35-shard Shugo batch, and economy.md for the 5 daily Duty Missions per server account.

      What is assumed and what is unverified

      Assumed, not measured: that the per-level cost is charged for the level you buy (1-5 at 1 shard, 6-10 at 2, and so on), which is the reading that lands exactly on the ~75 shards the sources quote for a 1→20. If the game instead charges the band of the level you are coming from, every total drops by the same proportion and the relative ordering does not change. It is also assumed that the 35 Shugo shards do not restock within the season.

      Unverified: the shard sources beyond the shops. Shards are known to come from the Abyss, Nightmare, Arena, the Shugo Festival, Battlefield and Ascension, but nobody has measured a rate per hour for any of them, so the “days of Duty Missions” figure is a theoretical floor, the worst case with only the fixed uncapped source, not a prediction of how long it will actually take you. The Global stigma names are unverified too: the names on the cards are working translations of the Korean Inven dump, not the official localisation, and they may change on 30 September with Advanced Access. The descriptions are deliberately left in Korean. And the cost of resetting a stigma was never seen during the test.
