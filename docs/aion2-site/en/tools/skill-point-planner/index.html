Skill-point planner

Region: mixed · Rules: Global Launch Scale Test (2026-09-17/19) plus Korea/Taiwan · Costs: Inven Korea dump of 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium.

Skill points are the abundant, free-to-respec resource, but they are not unlimited. The level-37 test gave you 150, and taking a single skill from 1 to 10 costs 21. Seven maxed skills and the budget is gone. That is why the correct shape for a build is narrow and deep rather than wide and flat.

Pick your class, set the budget, and give each skill a target level. The tool sums what you have spent and what is left, and tells you which skills reach the level-8 specialisation step, the only one points alone can reach, because points stop at 10 and the next steps sit at 12, 16 and 20. Everything is saved in your browser, per class.

Spend your skill points

Skill points only reach level 10. Levels 11-20 come from the Daevanion board, Arcana cards and soul-bind rolls on gear, never from points.

What is assumed and what is unverified

The default 150-point budget is a Launch Scale Test observation at level 37, not a figure read off the launch client: several testers quote the same number, and one adds that extra feather and dungeon farming can push it to 250. No settled figure exists for level 45, so that field is deliberately left free rather than shipping a made-up second preset.

The per-level costs come from the Korean client (Inven dump of 2026-09-22) and are identical for every skill of all eight classes: 1 point for levels 2-4, 2 for 5-7 and 4 for 8-10, so 21 to reach 10. Passives cost points exactly like actives and are listed here. Chain skills appear in the data as independent skills with their own cost table and are treated that way; whether the Global client charges them separately from their parent skill is unverified.

The English names are working translations of the Korean dump, not the official localisation, and may change with Advanced Access on 30 September. Stigmas are not here: they level with shards, not points; that is what the stigma planner is for.
