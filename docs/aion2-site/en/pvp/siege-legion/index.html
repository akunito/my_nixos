Sieges, artifacts and the legion

Region: Korea and Taiwan · Version: up to 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Siege mechanics are cross-checked; the legion is barely documented (one low-quality source).

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • There are no fortress sieges in Aion 2. Aion 1’s Divine, Miren, Kysis and Croton wars do not exist and are on no roadmap. The TW launch stream already showed an Abyss map with artifacts and no fortresses.
  • What “siege” means here is the artifact war (아티팩트 점령전, “아티전”): 3 artifacts in the Abyss, faction vs faction, ~3 times a week at your server’s prime time, a few hundred players a side at most. Winning opens the Abyss Corridor, the best AP source in the game.
  • Show up even if you lose. Losses pay AP; wins pay medals by contribution, and medals are the hard gate on Centurion and Tribunus gear. Holding artifacts also raises Abyss mob loot rates for your whole faction.
  • Join a legion before 45, not after. Every source says solo Abyss play is untenable. If you play with a fixed group, get everyone into the same one.
  • Templar + Cleric is the siege-meta duo. Two independent KR commentators single out the “수호치” synergy as the strongest, arguably overtuned, comp in Abyss PvP. Whichever of the two you main, the other is the ideal partner. That reading is KR, early 2026: between 2026-08-26 and 2026-09-22 KR nerfed Templar repeatedly (cancel delays on Judgment and Continuous Flurry, Noble Armor cut from 5 minutes to 2, Provoke made damage-proportional) and KR players now call it a net loss. That tuning is Chapter-1-era and is not what Global Season 1 ships.
  • Cleric caveat worth knowing: the author of the most detailed KR PvP-gear guide, a Cleric main, says gold medals are scarce specifically for clerics because they score badly on siege contribution; and KR players complained healing granted no AP.
  • Legion is not legion gear. “Legion Commander” (군단장) and “Legion Loyalty” are names of PvP gear tiers, not guild content.
  • There is no guild war. No legion-vs-legion territory, no Boonstone equivalent, no Dredgion, no Kamar. The only announced legion-exclusive content is a co-built ship for PvE sea monsters.
  • The 25-minute territory mode is KR-only. The rift contest (시공의 균열 쟁탈전) requires level 50, which Global does not have. Do not confuse it with Battlefield, a separate 10v10 gear-equalized mode with 3 wins a week per character.
  • A realistic siege week at launch: 3 artifact wars and 2 Abyss world bosses, all at prime time (a European evening), inside 7 h a week of Abyss. That fits a casual evening schedule comfortably.

The artifact war

Section titled “The artifact war”
  • Three artifacts at fixed points, contested simultaneously. A 2025 KR session named them Ereshkigal’s Root (centre), Sulfur Tree Island and the City of Wings archipelago; Global names them core A (north), B (middle) and C (south).
  • Meter-driven, not just timer-driven: each artifact fills a 0-100% “resonance” from faction members farming mobs nearby; on a TW server in September the window ran ~2 h 45 and filling it took 2-3 h of farming; then a 10-minute countdown and the boss spawns.
  • Capture is decided by the last hit on the artifact, with rewards split by contribution. One TW player claims to have taken an artifact solo with the last hit. An enemy-held artifact can be flipped in the same session.
  • The prize is the Abyss Corridor: a portal at the captured site into a timed instance of dense trash, the fastest AP (and uncapped Kinah) farm in the game. Entries scale with the artifacts your faction holds.
  • Medals are the real siege currency: silver, gold and platinum open Centurion, Tribunus and High Commander; awarded by contribution only to the winning faction, rank-gated and weekly-capped.
  • The siege chains into field bosses: on KR, Kaira, Agro and Tamasa spawn 30 min after the war, and a “Spirit King’s Agro” right as one ends.
  • Official scale “up to 400 vs 400”, relayed from memory and never shown; observed, “a few hundred”.
  • On KR it is scored server against server, out of six: results are reported as 6-0, 5-1, 4-2, and one alliance’s account has a single siege fought across the lower and mid Abyss tiers at once. Which server you pick decides who you face and how lopsided it is: KR players report 6-0 routs as routine where populations are mismatched.

Access budget

Section titled “Access budget”

Abyss: level 45 and item level 1,000 (Chaotic Lower Rashanta); 7 h a week F2P, 14 with membership on KR. The Global Launch Scale Test (2026-09-17/19) gave 7 h to everyone, membership included; the 14 h membership allowance is announced for Global but was not visible in the test; per-area timers on KR; Abyss time stones dropped by mobs or traded, each worth +1 h and stacking to +30 h over the base allowance (KR); Upper Rashanta capped at item level 4,300 and 400,000 power (KR only).

Aion 1 bleed-through to reject

An English mechanics pass flagged, and could not verify, a scheduled “Vulnerable” window, a fortress “Guardian General” raid boss and “Glory Points”: almost certainly Aion 1 wiki content leaking into search summaries. In Aion 2 the Guardian General is a high-rank player transformation.

The legion

Section titled “The legion”
  • Created at a registration NPC in either capital for a Kinah fee; no gate beyond being a Daeva.
  • It can rent a hall (아지트) for 1,000,000 Kinah a month, which condenses crafting benches, merchants and training dummies; on the TW stream it is an airship with stations and a circular plaza.
  • Its practical purpose is social and logistical: forming Abyss and siege parties, dungeon groups and Sanctuary raids.
  • No legion-vs-legion content. Legion “chores” are being reduced on KR, not expanded. Sizes cited: a TW Templar guide recruits into a ~45-member “veteran” legion.
  • Sieges are party content, and the party is Templar + Cleric. Party synergy buffs in Abyss and siege are described as “broken”; forming a party is a near-hard requirement for serious Abyss play, which pushes solo players out. Global marketing matches: Templar “leading the push”, Cleric “decides whether the team survives”.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Expecting fortress sieges.
  • Reading “legion gear” guides as siege guides.
  • Skipping a siege you will lose: losses pay.
  • Reaching 45 without a legion.
  • Planning around the 25-minute territory mode: level 50, outside Global.
  • Repeating the 48-hour figure: a superseded KR state. Wednesday and Saturday at 22:00 is not superseded, it is the live KR schedule as of September 2026, but it is a KR schedule, and the cadence table above carries NCsoft’s only Global figure.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Exact cadence and schedule on Global. Advanced Access (early access) and free-launch servers are separate groups and NCsoft says it will manage Abyss balance between them for an unstated period, with one-way migration from free servers onto Advanced Access ones, so who you siege against on day one is still undecided. Corridor access on KR is one entry per captured portal between sieges (2026-09); whether Global keeps that, or NCsoft’s “weekly” means something stricter, is open. Whether the “3-core siege with a 10-minute timer” in one Global guide is the same thing as the artifact war (almost certainly yes). Nothing documents Templar’s or Cleric’s specific role inside a siege beyond “tank” and “healer”.
