The Abyss and Abyss Points

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Global ships only two of the four PvP gear tiers, so everything above Centurion is KR/TW content for now. Nearly every number here is still Korean, nobody reached the Abyss shop in the test, and Global ships a much smaller PvP surface.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • The Abyss is still the best shard tap, but Global loosened the chokehold. Abyss Points (AP) buy Stigma Shards, and stigmas are the biggest single power lever in the kit: on Korea that made the Abyss compulsory (PvE-only shard income ~2.6 a week against ~20). Global adds Nightmare, the Shugo Festival, Battlefield and the Ascension Trial as shard sources, and the Shugo shop alone sells 35 per character with no time limit, about half of what one stigma needs to reach 20.
  • It is also not a time sink. Global gives 7 h of Abyss time a week to F2P (14 with membership). On a casual schedule most of your play goes to the PvE routine (~21 h a week of baseline chores), not to PvP.
  • Global removed the thing that made the Abyss scary. You do not lose AP on death, only Ranking Points, and almost nothing at low rank. At the KR launch enemy players literally stole your banked AP.
  • Global ships ONE Abyss map (Chaotic Lower Rashanta, level 45 and item level 1,000).
  • Templar and Cleric are both good here, differently. After the -30% (Korean figures, July 2026): Templar Abyss KDA 11.5 (A), 1v1 70.8% (S), co-op 41% (A). Cleric 11.36 (A), 66% (B), 47.5% (S). Templar is the better solo and duel class and farms corridors faster; Cleric decides team fights. Treat the Templar numbers as stale: Korea then ran weekly Templar hotfixes from late August through September that its own community reads as a net nerf, and one September leaderboard ranks Templar last of eight for solo PvP.
  • Cleric has one real PvP-economy tax, and Season 1 defers most of it: healing your group grants no AP, and both Cleric and Templar climb the PvP ladder more slowly than the kill-credit classes. But the gold and platinum medal shop that punishes that does not exist at Global launch, and the climb is not closed: a Taiwanese Cleric main ground all the way to Supreme Commander.
  • The legion matters more than skill. Corridor access (the single best AP source, 50-55k per run on TW) only exists if your faction wins the artifact siege. A coordinated 4-man with stacked synergy beats 16 unorganised solos.
  • Buy accessories first, never mid-tier armour. Tanks and healers take defensive pieces first; save AP for level-50 gear.
  • You do not need to whale. A KR Templar main on ~$30 a month sat top 10% for his class; whales cannot 1vN a coordinated group and the gap only decides in the top ~5%.
  • The September Launch Scale Test taught nothing about PvP. Cap 37, PvP unlocks at 45, Rift and battlefield disabled; the Abyss is first reachable from Advanced Access (early access) on 09-30.
  • The one Abyss rule to keep: hoarding AP was dangerous on KR and is not on Global; farm, spend on accessories, and show up to every artifact siege even when your faction is losing: losses still pay.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • The Abyss (어비스, Reshanta) is the open-world, always-PvP faction zone; it is the only route to PvP gear, which cannot be bought with money or Kinah, only AP and medals.
  • PvP unlocks at level 45 across the board (Abyss, Rifts, arena, battlefield): a deliberate “learn your rotation first” gate.
  • Two currencies: spendable AP (the shop) and Ranking Points (the ladder). Since February 2026 on KR the death penalty moved from AP to Ranking Points.
  • AP splits into capped (open-world kills and mob farming, weekly and seasonal ceilings) and uncapped (corridors, Shugo, invasions, battlefield, arenas, supply requests, quests). New players live on the uncapped sources.
  • Layers: Lower (flight enabled), Middle (item-level gated, no flight), Upper (a capped low-gear “newbie” Abyss). Global ships Lower only.
  • Artifact siege → Abyss Corridor is the core loop: three artifacts contest at once; the winning faction opens a short farm instance at that point.
  • Rifts (시공) are opt-in PvP. You flag manually; most never do, because they are there to clear the enemy side’s sealed dungeons and strongholds. Once flagged you cannot unflag for ~1 h. Kisks (placed respawn points) exist on the enemy side and are destructible.
  • PvP gear carries a large PvE damage penalty (-15 to -20%) and a large Abyss-only bonus (+40% vs its monsters): a separate wardrobe. Presets are the answer: a PvE and a PvP loadout on a hotkey (blocked while a stigma is on cooldown). On Global the wardrobes overlap more: crafted chest and helm roll weapon damage tolerance, a PvP mitigation stat, so armour crafting matters even for a PvP-only player.
  • Global fights last longer. Defence and HP on comparable gear are roughly doubled against Korea (551 against 260 on one level-86 helm) while weapon attack rose, and the percentage stats came down: Daevanion nodes now pay 1.5% where KR/TW paid 2.5 to 5%, Smite (the client calls it Double Chance) is capped at 3% where TW reaches ~24%, and one Global report has the divinity conversion cut as well, single source. Four playtesters read this as a deliberate lengthening of PvP time-to-kill.
  • The pay-to-win PvP board was defanged. The Asphel PvP Daevanion board survives but its PvP damage node drops from 2.5% to 1.5%, and its crystals can no longer be bought: you farm them from PvP and they bind to your account. The PvE Daevanion board, whose points cost around a million Kinah each on Korea, is absent from the Global LST build; its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch.
  • You cannot see the people killing you. Players do not render until close range while flying, which one max-level playtester calls the reason he would not recommend the game to a PvP player. Set “Player Particle Effect Display” to self-and-party, turn on “Identical Player Appearance”, and drop “Mass Combat Quality” first when the Abyss stutters (setting names read off a TW client, so they may move at launch).
  • Aion 2 has no Aion 1-style fortress siege, no Dredgion, no guild war. Artifact wars, garrisons and the 3-zone “Battlefield: Siege” are the substitutes.
  • Every English creator reaches the same framing: the game is PvX; PvE players fall behind without AP and PvP players without dungeon gear.

What is contested

Settled, listed here because older guides still disagree. AP loss on death: none on Global (ranking only). Maps: one at launch; KR has four. Weekly time: the apparent 7 h vs 42 h contradiction resolves because KR’s timer is per area and there are three; Global has one.

Still open. Team arena size: 1v1 and 5v5; the 5v5 is not a Global-only bump from NC’s showcase but what now looks like the live KR size, with 4v4 the pre-September name, and it is still unconfirmed in writing for Global. Weekly AP cap on Global: unknown, and no figure is worth printing yet: one playtester states a week-one ceiling, another only guesses at one, neither could show it, and the Abyss was locked all test. 12-piece Abyss set stacking: re-tiered three times in a single month (a September 3 test, the September 9 patch, and again on September 16, where the top tier’s 12-set reads 55%); Korea only, do not plan around it. Templar’s PvP standing: the July tier data in the Summary is contested by the weekly Templar hotfixes Korea ran from late August into September, which its own community calculates as a net nerf. Ranking fairness: NCsoft admitted on September 8 that ranking rewards favour classes with stronger Abyss buffs, with a fix “being tested” and no date; less urgent now that only the silver medal tier ships at launch.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Skipping the Abyss because you prefer PvE: it throttles your stigmas.
  • Buying mid-tier armour with AP instead of accessories.
  • Dissolving gear for the 80%.
  • Going without a legion.
  • Flagging in a Rift “just to look”.
  • Copying KR AP figures as if they were Global’s: nobody is overgeared at launch and AP scales with player kills.
  • Skipping sieges when your faction is losing.
  • Farming AP in a zerg: kill credit is proportional to your own contribution, so a solo kill pays several times what a kill shared with thirty people pays.
  • Flipping your PvP status in your own territory on a whim: the switch locks for 70 minutes, and attacking an enemy player or kiosk (or just grouping with someone flagged) forces about 10 minutes on you anyway.
  • Saving Korean gearing plans above Centurion: nothing above the silver medal tier is buyable at Global launch.
