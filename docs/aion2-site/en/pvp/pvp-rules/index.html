PvP Combat Rules

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW patch notes through 2026-09-16 + what was seen on the Global client between 09-17 and 10-04-2026 · Reviewed: 2026-10-05 · Confidence: medium. Almost every PvP multiplier comes from Korean and Taiwanese patch notes. NCsoft keeps class balance synced across regions, so the default assumption is that Global inherits them, but no tester has measured a PvP multiplier on the Global client. What IS confirmed on Global is the flag (War Mode) and a set of stats that lengthen fights.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • This page is the shared rulebook. What is specific to each mode lives on its own page: the Abyss and Abyss Points, arenas and Battlefield and sieges, artifacts and legion.
  • Critical and Smite hit less hard in PvP than in PvE. Since 2026-01-14 the PvP critical multiplier is ×1.25 (was ×1.5) and PvP Smite is ×1.5 (was ×2) (KR/TW, 2026-01-14). Smite is the double-damage hit; the Global client calls it Double Chance.
  • Item-level difference counts, but it is capped. The same patch dropped the item-level-difference scaling ceiling from 100% to 80% and raised the floor from 40% to 45% (KR/TW, 2026-01-14). Stated goal: longer fights.
  • All of 2026 was about lengthening time-to-kill. PvE and PvP damage split apart and healing received in the Abyss and arena -50% (KR, 2026-03-25); lifesteal halved, from 1.5% to 0.7% (KR/TW, 2026-07-01); -30% PvP damage for every class (KR, 2026-07-08).
  • Global lengthens fights on its own, and that one IS confirmed on the client: gear defence more than doubled versus Korea (551 against 260), Double Chance capped at 3%, the Asphel PvP board at 1.5% where Korea gives 2.5%, and titles with less PvP damage (4% against 5.5%) (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19/21).
  • War Mode on Global (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17): you switch your PvE/PvP status by hand, only inside your own territory, and switching it locks you out for 70 minutes. Attacking an enemy player or a kisk, or partying with someone flagged, forces a flag on you for about 10 minutes.
  • The flag is contagious. If someone in your group or force turns on PvP mode near you, it flags you into PvP too for 10 minutes (KR/TW, 2026-01-14). Who you travel with matters as much as what you do.
  • The faction that loses the artifact war gets help: in the worst case (losing all three artifacts and capturing none), +35% PvP damage resistance and +20% PvP damage in the next war (KR/TW, 2026-01-21). Whether Global carries this, and with what numbers, is unknown.
  • The Status Abnormality Resistance Scroll (formerly Shock Resistance Scroll) blocks shock effects and, since 2026-01-28, also freeze, fear, sleep and seal (KR/TW, 2026-01-28). It is used before the burst lands, not after.
  • Godstones decide little in Aion 2: 1-5% procs (gear) whose durations were cut across the board on 2026-02-04, for example from 12s to 7s and from 15s to 7s (KR/TW, 2026-02-04).
  • Dying is cheap on Global: you lose no AP, only ranking points. The Korean history of the death penalty and the rank-based AP bonus is on the Abyss page.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

Damage multipliers

Section titled “Damage multipliers”

A PvP hit rolls the same dice as a PvE one (critical, Smite/Double Chance), but with its own multipliers. Since 2026-01-14 a critical against a player does ×1.25 instead of ×1.5, and a Smite ×1.5 instead of ×2 (KR/TW, 2026-01-14). PvE was untouched: that is why Smite is still the best offensive stat in PvE and pays off noticeably less against players (see stats).

Since 2026-03-25 PvE and PvP damage are calculated separately (KR, 2026-03-25). From then on, each skill can carry a PvP coefficient different from its PvE one, and patch notes adjust the “PvP damage” of individual skills without touching their PvE side. A tier list written before that date mixes the two together.

Item-level difference

Section titled “Item-level difference”

When you attack someone of a different item level, damage scales by the difference. On 2026-01-14 that scaling landed between a 45% floor (was 40%) and an 80% ceiling (was 100%) (KR/TW, 2026-01-14). The creator who translated it reads it as: the better-geared side still wins, just less and more slowly. The exact formula (which difference gives which percentage) has not been published by anyone.

Blanket damage and healing cuts

Section titled “Blanket damage and healing cuts”
  1. 2026-03-25: healing received in the Abyss and arena -50%; immunity and “Immortal” cannot be chained within 30s; PvE and PvP damage split apart (KR, 2026-03-25).
  2. 2026-04-21 (developer livestream): fast lifesteal shifts to slower healing over time, with potions buffed to compensate (KR, 2026-04-21, approximate translation).
  3. 2026-07-01: all lifesteal and life absorption halved, typically from 1.5% to 0.7% per hit (KR/TW, 2026-07-01).
  4. 2026-07-08: -30% PvP damage for every class (KR, 2026-07-08). Longer fights; tanks and healers come out ahead, pure burst loses out. The PvP tier lists from July onward (arena, the Abyss) already have this baked in.

Which of these four reach Global: the 2026-03-25 one is inside global Season 1 per the cutoff in versions. The July ones shipped with Korea’s Chapter 1, which Global does not have, but they are balance, and NCsoft says balance is shared. No tester has confirmed the -30% or the 0.7% on the Global client.

War Mode and the PvP flag

Section titled “War Mode and the PvP flag”

In your own faction’s open world, PvP is optional. Here is how it works on Global (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17), with the Korean detail noted wherever Global has not yet been seen:

  1. You choose your own base status, PvE or PvP, and you can only switch it inside your faction’s territory. On the enemy map (Rift) you cannot switch it at all, so decide before you cross over (KR/TW, 2025-12-03).
  2. Every manual switch locks you out for 70 minutes before you can switch again (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17). On Korea this unlocked at level 45 (KR/TW, 2025-12-03); the unlock level has not been seen on Global.
  3. A forced flag of ~10 minutes if you attack an enemy player or a kisk, or if you party with someone flagged (GLOBAL, 2026-09-17). On Korea, attacking an enemy NPC or a kisk flags you for 10 minutes without triggering the 70-minute lock (KR/TW, 2025-12-24).
  4. Group contagion: if a member of your group or force turns on PvP mode within a certain range of you, everyone nearby flips to PvP for 10 minutes (KR/TW, 2026-01-14). The exact radius has not been published.
  5. Guards: in a Rift, getting close to the enemy guards makes them aggressive and can force your flag even if you were in PvE (KR, 2026-09-12).
  6. Being flagged pays: flag mode gives more AP (KR/TW, 2025-12-24), and PvP deaths in a Rift pay double while you are flagged (figures on the Abyss page).

The Abyss is always PvP, flag or no flag: War Mode only governs the open world and the Rifts.

The losing faction’s buff

Section titled “The losing faction’s buff”

After every Abyss artifact war, the faction that lost artifacts gets PvP buffs for the next one, scaled by how many it lost and how many it captured. The worst case (losing all three, capturing zero) used to give +15% PvP damage resistance and +10% PvP damage, and season 2 raised it to +35% and +20% (KR/TW, 2026-01-21). The full table was shown on screen but nobody has transcribed it. The same patch went from 3 to 6 artifacts (3 on the lower layer, 3 on the middle) and cut the AP per capture from 5% to 2.5%.

Second valve: if a faction is down 3-0, a portal to the artifacts opens in its home city (KR/TW, 2026-01-07 and 2026-02-03). The siege schedule and rewards are on sieges and legion.

Status control and the resistance scroll

Section titled “Status control and the resistance scroll”

The resistance scroll is the insurance against the control that opens a burst. Until January 2026 it was called the Shock Resistance Scroll and only covered shock effects; on 2026-01-28 it was renamed the Status Abnormality Resistance Scroll and now also blocks freeze, fear, sleep and seal (KR/TW, 2026-01-28). That was an indirect nerf to Sorcerer (freeze, sleep) and Spiritmaster (fear), both of whom open their fights with those controls.

Recommended use from a Korean PvP player (KR, 2025-12-17):

  1. Put the resistance scroll on F5 and the shock-mitigation scroll (a shield) on F6.
  2. F5 before a Gladiator, Assassin or Templar’s burst lands, when you see it coming.
  3. F6 reactively, once you are already eating heavy damage.

The universal Shock Release skill (break free from control) was also buffed: its damage resistance went from 20% to 30% (KR/TW, 2025-12-24) and then to 50% (KR/TW, 2026-01-28).

Godstones in PvP

Section titled “Godstones in PvP”

Godstones (Theostone in the client) add a proc to a weapon or guard; base values are on gear. In PvP they matter for their control procs (stun, root, seal, paralysis), not for damage. On 2026-02-04 durations were cut across the board, for example 12s → 7s, 15s → 7s, 9s → 5s, 6s → 4s, 5s → 4s, 4s → 3s, with one rare type that went up from 4s to 5s (KR/TW, 2026-02-04). Which duration maps to which stone is not stated by the source.

A Taiwanese arena player claims the paralysis one is the strongest (~2-3% proc chance, ~5s internal cooldown) and that it procs far more often on the faster-hitting classes, Gladiator and Assassin, deciding 1v1 duels (TW, 2026-09-21; figures he himself calls approximate). Another creator with over 1,000 hours says the opposite: the effect exists but is smaller than in Aion 1 (KR, 2026-08-19). Slot one in and do not plan around it.

Death, AP and rank

Section titled “Death, AP and rank”

On Global dying costs no AP, only ranking points, and almost nothing at low rank (announced for Global, 2026-09-10). Korea got there in three steps (AP loss -50% on 2025-12-24, no AP loss at all since 2026-02-11, ranking-point loss -87% on 2026-02-25), and it also gives a rank-based AP bonus (a flat +30% since 2026-02-11). All of that, with dates and sources, is on the Abyss page. On Global, dying in a Rift sends you back to your own map with no re-entry until the next opening (GLOBAL, 2026-10-04).

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Flipping War Mode “just to see what happens.” That is a 70-minute lock. And even if you switch back to PvE, attacking an enemy or a kisk, or partying with someone flagged, flags you for another 10 minutes.
  • Joining a group without checking who is flagged. The flag is contagious to nearby group or force members (KR/TW): one person flagging on their own drags you into PvP too.
  • Reading damage figures off a 2025 or early-2026 tier list. Before 01-14 PvP critical and Smite multiplied harder, before 03-25 PvP and PvE shared the same calculation, and before 07-08 everything hit 30% harder.
  • Betting on Smite for PvP as if it were PvE. In PvP it multiplies ×1.5, not ×2, and on Global Double Chance is capped at 3%.
  • Using the resistance scroll once you are already stunned. It is preventive: it goes on before the burst. The reactive one is the mitigation scroll.
  • Planning around a godstone. 1-5% procs with trimmed durations; slot one in and forget about it.
  • Copying lifesteal values from before July 2026. They were cut in half, and by 04-21 they had already shifted to healing over time.
  • Giving up after losing the artifacts. On Korea, the next war is played with up to +35% resistance and +20% PvP damage on your side.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”
  • Whether Global applies the -30% PvP damage (KR, 2026-07-08) and the 0.7% lifesteal (KR/TW, 2026-07-01). Both shipped with Korea’s Chapter 1, which Global does not have, but they are balance, which NCsoft says is shared. Nobody has measured it on the Global client.
  • Whether the PvP critical (×1.25) and Smite (×1.5) multipliers are the same on Global; nobody has measured them on the client.
  • Exactly how item-level-difference scaling works: which difference gives which percentage between the 45% floor and the 80% ceiling, and whether the 80% is the maximum in favor of the better-geared side or a cut to damage against them. The source only gives the two extremes.
  • Whether Global has the losing faction’s buff and with what values; the full Korean table is not transcribed either.
  • The radius of flag contagion from group or force, and whether Global applies it the same way (the Global source only says “partying with someone flagged”).
  • The level at which War Mode unlocks on Global (45 on Korea).
  • What exactly the original “shock” scroll covered, and whether Global’s scroll is already the expanded version.
  • Godstone durations by type after the cut, and whether Global uses the pre- or post-2026-02-04 values.
  • Whether “no AP loss on death” on Global also removes the AP theft from the Korean launch.
