Crafting, gathering and Supply Requests

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Global does ship the Season 3 crafting rework and crafted gear lost its built-in PvE damage line; there is still no Kinah figure from Global, because the playtest ran with the Market and all trading switched off.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Crafting is not a side hobby: crafted gear is still PvE best-in-slot, but for a different reason than on KR/TW. The Global build stripped the ~+5% PvE damage line that used to be baked into a crafted piece; that percentage now comes from the Potential system, the same one dungeon gear uses. What is left is a real gap anyway: crafted gear carries 5 soulbind slots against 4 on a dungeon drop, and the crafted chain tops out at item level 102 where the best dungeon weapon stops at 86, roughly a 20% damage difference at the ceiling. The crafted weapon also beats the PvP-shop weapon on soulbind lines and item-level scaling.
  • On Global you cannot buy your way out of crafting: the endgame piece is bound on craft. The Market sells materials and lower-tier crafted gear, never the finished transfer-crafted weapon or armour, so the chain has to be run on your own character. The casual path is still to level the cheap professions and sell into the Market for income, but plan to craft your own top piece rather than to buy it.
  • Global S1 has no Heroic, from dungeons or crafting, now verified in the playtest client rather than inferred. Every KR guide about Dragon Lord, Rudra, Horn Dragon and Magical Breath describes content you will not see at launch. Unique is the ceiling, and the crafted item level 102 piece is what replaces Heroic at the top.
  • The single most valuable thing crafting does for a PvP player is Supply Requests, and the playtest confirmed they ship on Global with the AP still out of cap. You hand in crafted items for Abyss Points, the board is the same as KR/TW and the AP values are unchanged: a white delivery is 1,500 AP, two blue swords were 9,000. One reward was cut, the bound Soul Crystals KR/TW paid alongside the AP. The board unlocks at level 45. No weekly Abyss Point cap figure is published here: the cap is confirmed to be cumulative rather than resetting, the numbers in circulation are one creator’s stated figure against another’s self-labelled guess, and the Abyss was locked for the whole test.
  • Gathering is a one-hour investment, not a grind. Maxing the extraction line took ~1 h on KR. Do it while questing at low level; the same nodes cost ten times as much at 45 with everyone on them.
  • Two professions to start: Alchemy and Cooking. They make what every player burns daily (potions, scrolls, manastones, 5-minute food). For a Templar or Cleric, Blacksmithing is the weapon profession (longsword + shield and mace both come from it) and Handicrafting makes your accessories: the ring is the one accessory whose crafted version can roll active skills.
  • The core mechanic is a 25% proc that chains, and on Global only the first step can go wrong. Craft the base item, 25% it procs a tier up, and each roll is independent, so 25% does not mean one in four. Above the first Unique/Gold result the chain runs on Transfer Crafting at 100% success, which carries your enhancement, breakthrough and soulbinds across: the RNG only decides how many steps you skip. Note that the 25% shown in the UI is the combo chance, not the base craft’s success rate.
  • The September test did settle the mechanics, even though nothing about the economy could be tested. Level cap 37, a full wipe, and the shop, Quna, Marketplace and all trading switched off for the whole twelve hours: every Kinah figure on this page is still KR/TW, and prices are the thing to re-check in Advanced Access (early access) from 09-30.
  • Two things print Kinah with almost no effort: Alchemy manastone conversion (buy raw stones on the Market, craft in 999 batches, resell at 30-50% margin after tax) and crafting at the town NPC, which is free, instead of the crafting menu, which charges Kinah per item.
  • Membership gates the Market, not crafting, and direct player trading is gone entirely. NC disabled player-to-player trades as a stated anti-bot measure and left the Market as the only channel, and a new Trust score gates marketplace access for accounts that look automated. What free accounts can do on the Market is genuinely unsettled: TW let them buy but not list, most Global sources say locked out entirely, one says they can list but not buy. Assume locked out, and test it in the first hour of Advanced Access. Without membership, plan for self-supply.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Two life skills are live: Crafting and Essence Extraction (gathering). Fishing, naval content and Housing are announced and unreleased as of 2026-07.
  • Five professions with fixed outputs:
Profession Makes For you
Blacksmithing Greatsword, Longsword, Shield, Dagger, Mace, Fist, Guard Templar longsword + shield, Cleric mace, and the Guard every class wears
Armorsmithing Helm, pauldrons, chest, leggings, gloves, shoes, cloak class-agnostic
Handicrafting Longbow, Chanter staff, necklace, earring, ring, bracelet, brooch accessories: the hardest slots to drop and the highest Market value
Alchemy Spellbook, Orb, manastones, spirit stones, potions, scrolls the Kinah engine and the consumable supply
Cooking food and drink, 5-minute buffs stamina-regen food is called mandatory on bosses
  • Levels: Novice 1→50, promotion quest, Professional 1→50 (70 in KR S3). Do the promotion quest the moment it appears: XP past the cap is wasted. Cooking’s needs 10 fresh raw meat + 6 fresh fruit with very low drop rates; people get stuck at Novice 50.
  • Crafting and gathering open early on Global. KR/TW gated gathering nodes behind level 45 as an anti-bot measure; the Global build has no such gate (one creator puts the unlock at level 10 for both lines). Which means the “gather while you quest” advice below is actually possible from the start, unlike on Korea.
  • Gathering has three allocatable stats (Proficiency Handling raises the success gauge, Delicate Touch slows the failure gauge, Lady Luck gives large jumps) and per-resource branches: name on the minimap → 2× yield → a chance to upgrade material quality. Points respec. XP comes per successful gather; failure gives nothing.
  • Odal (Ode, Odel) is the universal material, gathered on the ground and in mid-air while flying (costs flight stamina; carry Alchemy Wind Serums). After a dungeon boss kill you gather a fixed 6 Ode.
  • Crafting and gathering share the success/fail gauge: two meters race; a “luminous” bonus exists. Attempting a recipe above your level carries heavy hidden penalties or is blocked.
  • The 25% chain: white → green → blue → yellow/gold, and no heroic on Global. The non-proc is the material for the next attempt. On consumables the same roll doubles output. On Global the gear chain continues past the first gold result through Transfer Crafting, tier by tier and never skippable: item level 62 → 70 → 78/86 → 94/102, with a 25% proc deciding how far each step jumps. Stage one costs 1,000,000 Kinah and no materials (Taiwan charged materials instead); the top “Shining” tier wants extra materials on top of the Kinah, and the whole path costs roughly 25% more resources than the Taiwanese equivalent.
  • Substance Morph (Material Conversion) is a separate system with no failure roll: it converts a grade of Odal or bound and duplicate drops into other materials at 100% and no Kinah. It makes Ode Energy (extra cube entries), medals from fragments, and belt and amulet upgrades. Instant, but with no quality-upgrade chance; manual gathering has one. Its menu is shorter on Global: wings no longer come from feathers, stigma shards can no longer be morphed (buy them in the Shugo shop, 35 per character), and Daevanion and Asphel crystals are absent. What it gained: it now outputs a finished succession stone where KR only gives a fragment.
  • Bound items are why crafting is near-mandatory: converting them via Alchemy or Morph produces a tradeable output even when the input was not.
  • Where you craft changes the cost: the menu or a field station charges Kinah per item (300 per Orichalcum Ingot); the town NPC is free. You need not stand at a station to queue.
  • It runs semi-AFK: 5-10 s per item; 999 units per action for consumables (10 for bulk gear); Escape stops after the current item with no loss.
  • Supply Requests: four tiers (Urgent daily, Weekly with Wednesday 05:00 reset, Season Crafting over ~2 months, Season Boss). The list is identical for everyone, so crafting into it is a reliable sale, and the AP is out of cap.
  • Crafted gear replacement order: weapon → guard → accessories → armor. The usual endgame mixes crafted offence (weapon, guard, accessories) with enhanced dungeon armour.

Contested or changed by patch

Answered: Global ships the Season 3 rework, and goes further. In KR S1-S2 a high-tier failure deleted everything (one creator lost ~300M Kinah in one click). The Global playtest client shows the fixed version and then some: only the base craft can fail and eat its materials, and every tier above it is reached through Transfer Crafting (계승 제작) at 100% success, which carries enhancement, breakthrough and soulbinds across while stripping Potential, the soulbind extra effect, mana stones, spirit stones and the Theo stone. The piece binds to the crafter and cannot be sold. The trade-off is that the chain is now mandatory: where KR/TW let you craft the top grade directly if you could pay for it, Global makes you climb, and Potential has to be re-invested at every step because it does not transfer. Heroic and its whole chain (Magical Breath 5 per server per season, cores 1/2/3/4, 50-100M buy-outs) do not apply on Global. KR moved Ode and special-shop limits from per character to per server. Crafting one at a time gives more XP; batching is more time-efficient.

Plan

Section titled “Plan”

Global S1, week 1 (levels 1-45).

  1. Gather opportunistically while questing, never in a dedicated session. Push the gathering line to at least rank 5 early.
  2. Gathering points: general skills first (map overlay, 2× yield, the appraiser reroll), then Ore before Herb.
  3. Start Alchemy and Cooking: stamina food, accuracy food, flight potions, Wind Serums for rifts.
  4. Promotion quest the moment it appears, in each line.
  5. Hoard, do not vendor: one storage tab for shards and odd materials.
  6. Craft only white for Supply Requests: 1,500 out-of-cap AP per delivery is worth more than any week-one gear craft.

Level 45+, PvE gearing (no Heroic). Order weapon → guard → accessories → armor. Buy the base item off the Market if short on time, since that part is still tradeable, then run the transfer chain yourself, because the finished piece binds. Handicrafting to 40 and the blue brooch (3M, guaranteed, two can be made), enhance to +11 and socket; to level Handicrafting cheaply, spam the level-1 craft overnight with Market spirit stones. Prefer a crafted ring (active skills); on Global the crafted accessories also roll Might, Precision, Combat Speed (necklace) and Move Speed (earrings), which are called best-in-slot on every class. If you PvP, crafted chest and helm roll weapon damage tolerance, so armour crafting stops being optional. Keep duplicate dungeon pieces for Morph and the Potential track, and budget Potential per tier, since it is wiped on every transfer.

Kinah loop (needs membership). Manastone conversion in 999 batches; price-check before every bulk craft; PvP manastones and soul stones craft from AP and often sell above PvE ones; Artisan gear is cheap and is an input to Balic crafting; craft at the town NPC; check the delivery board before selling.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Selling or dismantling an intermediate item: the green you just made is the material for the blue attempt.
  • Attempting a recipe above your level.
  • Equipping a craft you meant to sell: permanent soulbind. Check the broker icon first.
  • Crafting in volume from the menu instead of the NPC.
  • Vendoring unidentified shards in week 1.
  • Dismantling an item a Supply Request wanted.
  • Morphing with the belt or amulet equipped: the equipped one gets consumed. Take them off.
  • Letting Ode Energy overflow the weekly cap: the excess is destroyed.
  • Skipping the promotion quest.
  • Treating 25% as “one in four”. Budget for the tail, not the average.
  • Buying all materials in one click: buying across the day moved raw stones from ~374 to ~300-350.
  • Dedicated farming sessions.
  • Expecting alts to multiply crafting: KR limited it per server.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Where Potential stones come from: nobody found them in the playtest’s morph menu, and they now carry all of a crafted piece’s PvE damage. Whether the removed PvE damage line returns at launch (one datamine rumour says yes, relayed second-hand and explicitly unverified). Whether the Professional cap is 50 or 70 on Global. The exact sealed-wing drop rate: creators read 1.25%, 1.5% and 2.5% off the same build. Whether gathering opens at level 1 or level 10. Whether free accounts can use the Market at all. No Kinah figure is from Global: the playtest ran with the Market off, so every price here is still KR/TW. Market fee and listing limits are still undocumented, and the new Trust score’s thresholds are unpublished. No consumable is known to raise base success. Promotion quest names and places are mistranscribed across three sources.
