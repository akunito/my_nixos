Monetization, Kinah and spending

Region: mixed · Version: Global prices announced; Kinah figures from KR/TW · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium. The business model is confirmed by NCsoft; almost no Kinah figure is from Global.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • One subscription, ~$15 a month, and it gates the Market, not power. Global collapsed KR’s stacked memberships into a single tier. It unlocks the player Market, the Kinah↔Quna exchange, remote storage, personal trade, the Wind Breeze merchant, more Odyle Energy and better reward picks. Everything else (classes, dungeons, Abyss, crafting, drops) is free.
  • For a legion-PvP player it is effectively mandatory. Not for the shop, but because the Market is where enhancement materials, crafted gear and manastones come from, and because membership roughly doubles dungeon reward output (two cube opens per run). Budget it as a sub, not an option.
  • PvP gear cannot be bought. At all. Abyss gear costs Abyss Points, and AP is not purchasable with money, Quna or Kinah, and is not transferable between characters. The competitive ladder is time-gated, not wallet-gated. Money buys PvE gear, enhancement and stat rerolls.
  • Global S1 ships without Heroic (orange) gear, KR’s biggest whale lever (a crafted Heroic chest cost ~2.05 billion Kinah). Unique +15 is the launch ceiling and everyone is on the same ladder.
  • Days 1 to 4 nobody can pay to win. Quna converts to Kinah only through the Market, and until F2P players list Kinah there is no liquidity. Your early-access head start is worth more than someone else’s credit card.
  • How much to spend: Founder Standard $24.99 once (5-day early access + 30-day membership + consumables), then the $15 membership; anything beyond that is optional. Do not put extra money into Quna→Kinah in month 1; decide in month 2 whether a Deva Pass on your main is worth it. Deluxe and Ultimate add only cosmetics.
  • Kinah on a casual schedule is dungeon-driven, and alts are the multiplier. Exploration dungeons drop bound Kinah; conquest dungeons drop unbound Kinah you can funnel to your main (~400k per Crow Cave clear, ~1.2M a day from 3 alts in ~30 min). Run 2-3 alts, not 7.
  • The real Kinah sink is enhancement and rerolling, not buying items. Plan the spend order and do not over-enhance gear you will replace at the next breakpoint.
  • The honest cost is time: ~21 h a week (~3 h a day) on one character for the basic recurring content alone. On a casual evening schedule that means keeping pace on one character, not with an alt army.
  • Trust the model only as far as this month. KR sold progression items in launch packages and pulled them within ~15 h after backlash; the Season 2 pass price hike was reversed with a public apology. NC responds to pressure, in both directions.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Two headline currencies: Kinah (earned in game; Market, enhancement, socketing) and Quna (real money; cosmetics, convenience and the premium Deva Pass track). Plus Abyss Points (PvP gear and Stigma Shards), Centurion Tokens (Shugo mini-games), Phantasmal Fragments (Nightmare bosses), Oath Coins (~3-month season), Pass Coins and Mileage (cosmetics).
  • Bound and unbound Kinah. Bound cannot leave the character; unbound trades and moves through the server warehouse. The game spends bound first. On Global the split is by dungeon type: exploration → bound, conquest → unbound.
  • The shop does not sell power. Cosmetics, mounts, pets, wings (no stats on Global), storage vouchers and consumables. No stat items, no gacha.
  • The pay-to-win vector is the Kinah↔Quna exchange, not the shop. You buy Quna, another player trades it for Kinah on the Market, and that Kinah goes to crafted PvE gear and above all to enhancement, stat rerolls and manastone perfection. Players with the most hours say there is no automated conversion: it is player-supplied, membership-gated, with a multi-day ramp.
  • What money cannot buy: Abyss gear, AP itself (except a capped trickle via Supply Requests), Arcana cards (weekly Transcendence only), the Genus Insight pet system (2-3 months of daily play), and the skin Collection Effect, where cash-shop skins do not count.
  • The Deva Pass is per character. Free track plus a Quna premium track; it contains materials and currencies, not only cosmetics. With membership you can buy the premium track with farmed Kinah.
  • F2P is viable for PvE and not for top-bracket PvP. A KR F2P with 300 h says progression slows around 2.4-2.5k GS but the game sustains itself without the Market; a TW player with 1,000 h cleared all PvE in 3 months without a sub. A moderate spender (~$30 a month) on KR sits in the top 10% of his class in PvP.
  • Alt farming is nerfed, not dead. Dungeon payout decays account-wide past ~200 runs (80% → 60% → 40% → 20%); another source puts the weekly decay at clear 84-85. Optimum: 1 main + 2-3 alts.

Contested or changed by patch

Whether F2P can buy on the Market without listing (as on TW) or is locked out entirely (every Global source): assume locked out and verify in week 1. Whether the Kinah↔Quna exchange is automated or player-to-player: the most experienced players say player-to-player. Alt funnelling died for exploration and survives for conquest. The three caps (200 account runs, 84 weekly cubes, 35 final-boss touches) come from different KR/TW patches and none is confirmed on Global. Power figures (“2,400 GS”, “350-600k combat power”, “item level 4,500”) are metrics from different patches: check the date before believing a threshold.

How much to spend

Section titled “How much to spend”

Month 0 (until 09-30). Founder Standard, $24.99, on the platform you will play on. The only tier with non-cosmetic value. Play the 09-17/18 playtest (nothing carries over).

Month 1 (10-05 → 11-05). $0 extra. Membership is already paid, the Market has no liquidity for the first days, there is no Heroic to chase, the Deva Pass is rated low value except maybe week 1, and PvP gear cannot be bought at any price.

Month 2 onward. $15 membership; anything beyond it is optional. Ranked by value for a legion-PvP player: nothing (the ladder is AP and AP is time); Deva Pass premium on the main only, never on alts; Quna→Kinah for enhancement materials only once you have a Unique set worth enhancing and a specific target; cosmetics for pure preference, remembering shop skins do not feed the Collection Effect.

Alts: 1 main + 2-3. Push each alt to level 22 to unlock Odyle Energy, then run conquest Crow Cave for unbound Kinah. Make them the same class as your main: drops favour the played class, so they generate more extractions of your weapon type for the Collection Effect. Each alt has its own Black Cloud Merchant rotation and its own AP to buy cheap Abyss gear purely for guaranteed skin extraction.

Kinah routine on a casual schedule. Conquest dungeons via Odyle Energy; Ascension Trials (3 a week per character, Selentium sells to the NPC); Alchemy and Cooking for consumables the whole server burns; hidden cubes and Shugo Festa (spread the 6 keys across cards); Supply Requests for AP outside the weekly cap. Market flipping returns 30-50% but needs capital and patience.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Buying Deluxe or Ultimate expecting an advantage.
  • Buying Quna on day 1: no Kinah is listed.
  • Buying a Deva Pass on every alt.
  • Over-enhancing gear you will replace: stop at +10-12 at the 1,000 / 1,200 / 1,600 breakpoints.
  • Soulbinding or socketing before an item is confirmed BiS.
  • Spending Odyle Energy on low content: bank it in exploration, spend it in conquest.
  • Running past the ~200-run account decay; more alts does not fix it.
  • Gambling enhancement stones into amplification stones: you need them for >+15.
  • Bulk crafting without a margin check after the Market tax.
  • Vendoring unidentified shards and materials in week 1: they feed Substance Morph and Supply Requests.
  • Equipping a crafted item you meant to sell: permanent soulbind.
  • Forgetting the per-item lock against accidental extraction.
  • Activating the Deva Pass late in the week: its weekly XP is capped.
  • Spending your main’s AP on skin extraction: use the alts’ AP.
  • Building an alt army “to keep up”: the direct route to burnout.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Exact Global membership price. F2P Market access. Automated vs player exchange. Global Kinah values and caps (only four figures documented). Whether the premium Deva Pass track contains power. Whether face-to-face trade exists. When the Cross-Server Market opens (NC says after launch) and when the Heroic patch lands. Bot and RMT pressure (KR sanctioned 72,000+ accounts in 65 rounds). The playtest answers none of this: shop, Market and Founder items are disabled.
