Cleric: what to level at each level (PvE and leveling)

Region: global · Version: global client in Spanish from early access (2026-10-02), with a Cleric leveled to 4, plus Inven’s skill database (Korea, 2026-09-22) and leveling guides from the global test, Korea and Taiwan · Reviewed: 2026-10-02 · Confidence: medium. The names, texts and unlock levels come from the client; the priority order is our recommendation for leveling, backed by those guides. We haven’t seen the Cleric’s Daevanion board yet, and past level 4 we haven’t played it.

This page is meant to stay open while you level. Every time you level up: open Skills, spend your points in the order below until the game won’t let you spend more, and check your level’s row in the table. What to press and in what order is on the sister page, Cleric: rotation and macros (PvE).

What the Cleric is while leveling, in three sentences

Section titled “What the Cleric is while leveling, in three sentences”
  1. A ranged (20m) damage-over-time caster who heals itself. While leveling you play a damage build: in the global test, level-37 dungeons didn’t call for healing, and the global guide spent all 150 points on damage.
  2. Its engine is a pair: Chain of Pain (Enlace de dolor) puts damage-over-time on up to 4 enemies, and Condemnation (Condena) can only hit the ones carrying it. The global client’s own Condemnation card says so: “a target at 20m affected by Chain of Pain.”
  3. It’s the slowest class at killing: mana and the left click set the pace.

The rules that explain everything else

Section titled “The rules that explain everything else”
  1. The game caps each skill by your level. On the skill card you’ll see “Adquirible en el nv. X” (“acquirable at level X”) when it can’t rank up any further. With the Templar we measured that rank 4 arrives about 6 levels after the skill unlocks (one rank every 2-3 levels is a deduction). Early on you’ll have points left over; the order below says where the leftover goes.
  2. Skill points reset for free. Try things without worrying.
  3. Cost per rank: 1 point from 2 to 4, 2 from 5 to 7, and 4 from 8 to 10. Going from 1 to 8 is 13 points; from 1 to 10, 21. Above 10 you only rank up with the Daevanion board’s “+1” nodes, Arcana cards, and soulbind: the rank-12 and rank-16 specializations are endgame business.
  4. Points: every sealed dungeon gives +2 skill points and +2 Daevanion; every green side quest gives +1 and +1.
  5. Don’t level up heals while you’re leveling. Every guide that corrected itself landed on the same conclusion: heals get points at the level cap. One exception, Light of Healing, further below.

Spending order, as a list

Section titled “Spending order, as a list”
  1. Earth’s Retribution (Retribución terrestre) — left click. Your basic attack at 20m and your mana engine: it gives back 110, and its chain Discharge (Descarga) gives back 150. The global guide calls it non-negotiable.
  2. Condemnation (Condena), from level 8. Your strong hit, in an area on 4, but only against enemies carrying Chain of Pain. You press it nonstop while it lasts.
  3. Chain of Pain. Damage-over-time on up to 4 enemies for 10s, lowers their damage tolerance by 5%, and it’s the key to Condemnation. 20s cooldown.
  4. Mark of Weakening (Estigma debilitante). A 4m area around a target at 20m, with damage-over-time and −5% to the enemy’s damage. Its rank-8 specialization removes the mana cost, but reaching rank 8 takes a while: don’t expect that saving early on.
  5. Judgment Lightning (Rayo del juicio) — right click. Area on 4, costs 120 mana: filler when you have mana to spare.
  6. Thunderclap (Centella), from level 14. A charged area hit, 45s cooldown and 300 mana: only charge it when your mana allows it.
  7. Light of Healing (Luz de curación), from level 10: just enough until the mid-leveling band; it’s the only heal the guides level up early.
  8. Passives, as they unlock: Sovereign’s Grace (Gracia empírea) (9, crit and extra damage on every hit) → Grace of the Earth (Gracia terrestre) (21, +10.5% crit damage on the global client, much more than in Korea). If you’re dying a lot, take Warm Blessing (Favor cálido) (HP and mana) first. The global guide’s level-37 character put nothing into passives, because a leveling Cleric has almost 0% crit.
  9. Don’t spend points on Healing Power Boost (Mejora curativa): a Taiwanese guide measured it at around +0.4% healing per point, and while leveling it loses out to any point spent on damage.

What we haven't seen yet

Read from the client: all the unlocks from 5 to 25. The stigmas (22/27/32/37) and the boards (12/20/30/40/45) come from the client with the Templar and Chanter and from the global test. We haven’t played the Cleric past level 4.

Specializations: which to pick

Section titled “Specializations: which to pick”

At rank 8 you pick one of three and slot it into the star socket. The rank-12 and rank-16 ones are fixed and endgame business.

Skill For leveling Endgame / group Why
Earth’s Retribution +20% mana recovery +50% multi-hit Mana is what holds the Cleric back while leveling. The third option (+20% chance to trigger Discharge) also gives mana, because Discharge gives back 150
Condemnation +100 mana on hit You press it often: 100 mana every time is huge. At 12 (endgame) it resets on crit (in Korea, 75% with any hit): with the almost-0% crit of a leveling Cleric, that reset barely triggers unless Earth’s Punishment works the way it does in Korea
Chain of Pain +3s of damage-over-time slow or knockdown More time with the damage up is more time Condemnation is available
Mark of Weakening removes the mana cost -15% target defense Turns a cost into free
Judgment Lightning -20% mana cost same It’s your main mana expense
Thunderclap skill speed (charges faster) The global client also showed a “guaranteed crit” option that the Korean database doesn’t have at rank 8: it might be the fixed rank-12 or rank-16 effect. Check all three when you get there
Light of Healing +2 chained casts same Several heals in a row, once it reaches rank 8 (in the 27-32 band)
Shock Release recovers 10% HP adds Blessing of Regeneration The Korean database says Blessing of Regeneration gives back 40% of your mana; we haven’t read that on the global client
Lightning Barrage +120 mana same For a Cleric short on mana

The non-main skills (Mark of Weakening, Judgment Lightning, Thunderclap, Shock Release) probably won’t reach rank 8 while leveling; only pick for the ones that do.

Stigmas: which ones, when, and how far

Section titled “Stigmas: which ones, when, and how far”

Four slots: 22, 27, 32 and 37. They’re leveled with Stigma Shards (1 per level from 1 to 5, then 2, 4 and 8). Damage goes up every level and extra effects kick in at 5, 10, 15 and 20. Finish one to 5 before moving to the next (don’t spread shards at once, as the reference page also says), and take all four to 5 before leveling any further; resetting may cost something: pick and don’t switch.

Slot Stigma (ES client) What it does Why this one
22 Earth’s Punishment A hit at 20m and damage every second for 10s; 30s cooldown and 200 mana (Korean database). At level 5, “guaranteed crit”: the global client’s text is cut off there, and the Korean database says it’s for Condemnation on that target The leveling-guide reading: if it works like in Korea, every Condemnation on that target crits, and Condemnation’s rank-12 specialization (endgame) resets on crit. 32 Summon Revive in a group; Prayer of Amplification solo Revive: resurrects the group within 40m (2 min). Prayer: +20% attack for 10s In a group, a Cleric without a mass revive gets kicked on the spot; at level 10 it gives invulnerability on revive. Solo, more damage

This order is the solo-leveling reading, not a consensus: no source publishes a slot order, and two of the three loadouts in Cleric: what to level and when put Light of Protection first. If you’re grouping from the start, lead with it.

Burst of Authority (Explosión de poder) requires level 22 and the quest «Quien escucha la voz», and it depends on Stagger: don’t take it with PvP in mind. Voice of Ruin (Voz de perdición) (−60% healing received by the enemy) is for PvP.

Daevanion board

Section titled “Daevanion board”

Five boards: Nezekan at 12, Zikel at 20, Vaizel at 30, Triniel at 40 and Azphel at 45 (PvP). Each node is bought with crystals (2 to 15); refunding one costs 5,000 Kinah on the global client.

  1. “+1 skill level” nodes: Condemnation → Chain of Pain → Earth’s Retribution → Mark of Weakening. On the Templar and the Chanter there was one for almost every skill.
  2. Attack: on the Cleric it also boosts healing.
  3. Combat speed: the Cleric starts around 42%, the slowest starting point of any class.
  4. Accuracy only up to the minimum for the content you’re doing.
  5. Later on, cooldown reduction: the guides’ target is ~34%, which brings Earth’s Punishment down to every 20s; with its level-20 effect (+10s duration) its damage stays up the whole time.

Where each “+1” node is

Section titled “Where each “+1” node is”

Every active skill has one “+1” node on each of the four boards (3 crystals each), so the boards take it to rank 14 at most (10 from points + 4); beyond that you need Arcana cards or soulbind. Each passive has four (2 crystals each), two of them on the same board. The table gives the position as the game shows it: r = row counted from the top and c = column from the left (Nezekan, Zikel and Vaizel are 11×11; Triniel is 13×13). Buying a whole board costs 134 crystals for Nezekan, 134 for Zikel, 134 for Vaizel and 168 for Triniel.

What not to do

Section titled “What not to do”
  • Leveling up heals while you’re leveling (except Light of Healing in the 27-32 band).
  • Putting Thunderclap in a macro: it’s a three-stage charge and the macro only fires the first one.
  • Pressing Condemnation without Chain of Pain up: it won’t fire.
  • Standing in the middle of the group: take the edge; your heals reach 40m.
