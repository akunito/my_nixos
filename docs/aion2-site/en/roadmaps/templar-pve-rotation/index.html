Templar: rotation and macros (PvE)

Region: global · Version: skill texts from the global client in Spanish (2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01) and macro rules from the global Launch Scale Test (2026-09-20) · Reviewed: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: medium. What each skill does and what windows it opens is read straight from the client; the rotation and setup are our recommendation for leveling and PvE. What to level at each level is on the sister page, Templar: what to level at each level.

How the Templar deals damage, in five sentences

Section titled “How the Templar deals damage, in five sentences”
  1. Judgment (Juicio) is your strong hit, but it only exists during a short window opened by your four shield skills: Shield Smite (Golpe de escudo) (2s), Protective Strike (Estacazo de amparo) (2s), Shield Charge (Asalto con escudo) (2s), and Shield of Ruin (Escudo de perdición) (3s). The game says so right on Judgment’s own skill card: it activates “when using shield skills.” And it has a 5s cooldown: one Judgment per window, and it’s best to open the next window once Judgment is already back.
  2. The windows open when you use the skill, not when you land a stun, so the combo also works against bosses, which are immune to stun.
  3. Annihilation (Aniquilación) finishes off whatever is stunned or knocked down. Against normal mobs, Shield Smite always stuns them and Shield of Ruin always knocks them down: after either one, Annihilation is ready.
  4. Punishment (Castigo) opens every fight: it’s your biggest area hit and leaves you with Executor (Ejecutante) for 20s (+20% PvE damage and +200 accuracy). It charges in three stages and you release it at max charge.
  5. Mana comes from the left click. Fierce Strike’s (Estacazo impetuoso) chain gives mana back on every hit; everything else spends it.

And a sixth one that isn’t about buttons: blocking is attacking. Every block triggers Fury (Arrebato) (damage for you and your group), Shield of Protection (Escudo de amparo) (healing), and Weakening Strike (Embestida decadente) (lowers the enemy’s defense). Don’t dodge normal hits.

How to set up your bar and macro

Section titled “How to set up your bar and macro”

The bar

Section titled “The bar”

Place the keys wherever’s comfortable for you; what matters is what’s stacked together and what’s on its own.

The priority macro

Section titled “The priority macro”

The Skills window has a Skill Macro grid at the bottom (with 3 presets, 1/2/3) where you can stack several skills onto a single key. When you press the key, the game fires the first available skill starting from the highest-priority one and skips any that can’t be used at that moment (on cooldown, no window open, no stunned target). One global-test guide states this is confirmed to go “top to bottom”; other sources contradict it, so check it yourself (below).

That’s why conditional skills go on top and filler goes at the bottom: if the filler were on top, it would always fire and the others never would.

Right click, from highest to lowest priority:

  1. Judgment: only fires if a window is open.
  2. Annihilation: only fires if the target is stunned or knocked down.
  3. Weakening Strike: only fires after a block.
  4. Continuous Flurry: the filler, fires when none of the above applies.

Left click: Flash Rampage on top (only fires during Stagger) and Fierce Strike at the bottom.

With this you play with two mouse buttons plus 1 and 2: the right click handles Judgment and Annihilation by itself at just the right moment.

Check it before you trust it

We haven’t verified three things ourselves: which direction priority counts in the grid (whether the top row goes first), whether mouse clicks accept a macro stack at all, and whether Fierce Strike and Continuous Flurry still run their chains (Decisive Strike, Desperate Strike, Punishing Strike) when the macro fires them. Test it on a mob: put Judgment in the top row and Continuous Flurry below it, press Shield Smite and then right click. If Judgment fires, it’s fine; if Continuous Flurry fires, flip the order. And check whether the left-click chain still plays out; if not, leave Fierce Strike alone on its own key.

What never goes in a macro

Section titled “What never goes in a macro”
  • Punishment. A macro only fires the first charge stage, the weakest of the three. Always do it by hand, holding the key.
  • Shield of Ruin and Shield Charge against bosses. They’re charges: in a macro they’ll launch you straight into a hit you were trying to dodge.
  • Shock Release. It’s a reaction; if the macro burns it, you won’t have it when you actually get stunned.
  • Protective Strike. It’s your heal: you decide when.

Game settings

Section titled “Game settings”
  • Macro delay: the game ships with 30ms. If your ping goes above 80-100ms, raise it to 40-50ms; with very high ping (≈200ms) the macro doesn’t perform well and it’s better to press Judgment by hand.
  • Skill queue: try it both on and off. On, it chains for you but sometimes fires something you no longer wanted; off is more precise.

The step-by-step rotation

Section titled “The step-by-step rotation”

Against a group of mobs (the normal case while leveling)

Section titled “Against a group of mobs (the normal case while leveling)”

From 1 to 13 (no Punishment yet):

  1. Shield Smite on the group: stuns everyone and opens Judgment.
  2. Right click: Judgment fires; from level 12, the next press fires Annihilation on the stunned target.
  3. Left click in a chain until Shield Smite comes back. Right click when you have mana to spare.
  4. From level 8, Protective Strike when your HP drops, or as a second window while Shield Smite is coming back (about 5s after the last Judgment, so it’s already back).

From 14 to 21 (with Punishment):

  1. Target a mob in the middle of the pack from a distance and charge Punishment to max. Release it: area damage and Executor for 20s.
  2. Close in and Shield Smite → right click (Judgment) → right click (Annihilation).
  3. Left click in a chain for a few seconds. Protective Strike as a second window once Judgment is back (5s): right click again after it for Judgment.
  4. When Punishment comes back (30s), fire it even if you’re already in melee: it’s your biggest hit.
  5. When you finish the group, Capture the next one.

From 22 onward (with Shield of Ruin):

  1. Punishment, charged, from a distance.
  2. Shield of Ruin on the same mob: you charge in, knock it down, get the barrier, and Judgment opens for 3s.
  3. Right click, right click: Judgment and Annihilation (on the knocked-down target).
  4. Left click for two or three hits, just long enough for Judgment to come back (5s).
  5. Shield Smite → right click (Judgment). Annihilation will still be on cooldown (20s): it fires on the next Shield Smite.
  6. Left click as filler; Protective Strike when you drop or as the next window, again letting Judgment come back first.
  7. From 27, on groups of 4 or more, open with Sovereign’s Punishment after Punishment: you stun everyone and Annihilation has a target when it comes back.

When Shield of Ruin reaches level 5, its level-5 effect resets a cooldown on hit (Annihilation’s, according to the Korean database). At that point change the order so you don’t waste it: Shield Smite → Judgment → Annihilation, a few left clicks, then Shield of Ruin → Judgment → Annihilation again. To get into the group, walk in after Punishment or use Capture to pull one in.

Against an elite or a boss

Section titled “Against an elite or a boss”

Bosses can’t be stunned or knocked down, so Annihilation almost never fires (only with its 5% chance; the anti-immune specialization doesn’t make it fire more, it doubles the damage when it does). The combo falls back on the windows:

  1. Punishment, charged, at the start and every time it comes back.
  2. Shield Smite → Judgment, left click ~3s, Protective Strike → Judgment, left click ~3s, Shield of Ruin → Judgment. Always leave about 3s between windows: Judgment takes 5s to come back and a window lasts 2-3s, so opening two in a row wastes the second one.
  3. Left click between windows.
  4. Flash Rampage as soon as the boss goes into Stagger (the stagger gauge fills): all your skills fill that gauge, especially Punishment, Shield Charge, and the stigmas.
  5. Weakening Strike every time you block.
  6. Face the enemy: Fury and Roar of Insult (Rugido agravante) give damage when attacking from the front, and Templar armor is built to take the hits.

Mana and HP

Section titled “Mana and HP”
  • Running out of mana → more left click, less right click. Punishment, Annihilation and Protective Strike cost 200 each; Fierce Strike’s chain gives back 100 + 100 + 120.
  • Low on HP → Protective Strike (heals and +20% tolerance), and let blocks do their job with Shield of Protection. From level 13, Seal of Guardianship (Sello de protección) triggers on its own below 50%.
  • Getting stunned in the middle of a group → Shock Release.

Common mistakes

Section titled “Common mistakes”
  • Pressing Judgment outside a window. It won’t fire. That’s why it goes in the macro, behind the shield skills.
  • Putting Punishment in the macro. You lose the two strong charge stages.
  • Dodging normal hits. You lose out on Fury, Shield of Protection’s healing, and Weakening Strike.
  • Opening with Annihilation. Without a prior stun or knockdown it won’t fire: Shield Smite or Shield of Ruin first.
  • Opening two windows in a row. Judgment has a 5s cooldown: the second window closes before you can use it.
  • Using the right click as your main filler when you’re low on mana. The filler that pays for itself is the left click.
