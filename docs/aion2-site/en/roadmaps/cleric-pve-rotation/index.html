Cleric: rotation and macros (PvE)

Region: global · Version: skill texts from the global client in Spanish (2026-10-02), Inven’s database (Korea, 2026-09-22) for cooldowns the client didn’t show, and macro rules from the Launch Scale Test (2026-09-20) · Reviewed: 2026-10-02 · Confidence: medium. What each skill does is read from the client; the rotation and the setup are our recommendation for leveling and PvE. What to level at each level is on Cleric: what to level at each level.

How the Cleric deals damage, in five sentences

Section titled “How the Cleric deals damage, in five sentences”
  1. Everything is at 20m. You play from range, at the edge of the group, and damage-over-time does half the work.
  2. Chain of Pain (Enlace de dolor) opens the door: it puts damage-over-time on up to 4 enemies for 10s, and Condemnation (Condena) can only hit the ones carrying it. Unlike the Templar and the Chanter, it isn’t a 2-3s window: Condemnation is available for as long as Chain of Pain’s damage lasts.
  3. Earth’s Punishment (Castigo terrestre) (stigma, 22): according to the Korean database, at level 5 it makes Condemnation a guaranteed crit on its target, and Condemnation’s rank-12 specialization resets on crit. If it works the same way on the global client (the client’s text is cut off there), together they’re the endgame damage engine.
  4. Mana comes from the left click (Earth’s Retribution (Retribución terrestre) and Discharge (Descarga)) and from the mana specializations of Condemnation and Mark of Weakening (Estigma debilitante).
  5. You heal on very little: Light of Regeneration (Luz de regeneración) before each group and Light of Healing (Luz de curación) when you’re low cover you through leveling.

How to set up your bar and macro

Section titled “How to set up your bar and macro”

The bar

Section titled “The bar”
Key Skill Why there
Left click Earth’s Retribution (+ Lightning Barrage stacked above it, in the macro) You press it constantly
Right click Priority macro (below)
1 Chain of Pain The start of every group
2 Mark of Weakening Every 10s
3 Thunderclap Held down to charge: its own key
4 Light of Healing Your heal, by hand
5-8 Light of Regeneration, Holy Aura, stigmas
Q / E Radiance of Recovery, Shock Release

The priority macro

Section titled “The priority macro”

The Skill Macro grid in the Skills window lets you stack several skills onto one key; pressing it fires the first one available, starting from the highest priority, and skips the ones that can’t be used. Conditional skills go on top; filler goes at the bottom.

Right click, from highest to lowest priority:

  1. Condemnation: only fires if the target is carrying Chain of Pain and it isn’t on cooldown.
  2. Mark of Weakening: fires every 10s.
  3. Judgment Lightning: the area filler.

Left click: Lightning Barrage on top (only during Stagger) and Earth’s Retribution at the bottom.

Check it before you trust it

We haven’t verified ourselves which direction priority counts in the grid, whether mouse clicks accept a macro stack, or whether Earth’s Retribution’s chain still fires from inside a macro. Test it on a mob: Condemnation on top and Judgment Lightning below it, press Chain of Pain and then right click. If Condemnation fires, it’s fine; if not, flip the order.

Never in a macro: Thunderclap (it’s a charge: the macro only fires the first stage), heals (you decide when), and Shock Release (it’s a reaction). The September Korean guides recommend few skills per macro: every extra one reduces how often the rest fire.

Settings

Section titled “Settings”
  • Macro delay: 30ms by default; with ping above 80-100ms, 40-50ms.
  • Skill queue: try it both on and off.
  • “Aion 1” control mode (tab-target): it suits the Cleric well, because its whole kit is at 20m with a target.

The step-by-step rotation

Section titled “The step-by-step rotation”

Against a group of mobs

Section titled “Against a group of mobs”

From 1 to 7 (no Condemnation): Chain of Pain and Mark of Weakening on the group, left click in a chain, right click (Judgment Lightning) with mana to spare.

From 8 onward:

  1. Before you get there: Light of Regeneration (and from 27, Light of Protection always on).
  2. Chain of Pain at the center of the group: damage-over-time for 10s on 4.
  3. Right click: fires Condemnation. Repeat every time it’s back, for as long as Chain of Pain lasts.
  4. Mark of Weakening when it’s back (every 10s).
  5. From 14, Thunderclap fully charged when it’s back.
  6. Left click between Condemnations, for the mana.
  7. Chain of Pain lasts 10s and comes back at 20s: Condemnation is only available half the time (a bit more with the +3s specialization). During the other 10s, left click and Mark of Weakening; re-apply Chain of Pain as soon as you can.
  8. Light of Healing when you drop; Holy Aura in large groups.

From 22, before the Condemnations, put Earth’s Punishment on the toughest mob.

Against an elite or a boss

Section titled “Against an elite or a boss”
  1. Light of Regeneration, Holy Aura and Noble Energy (from 37) at the start.
  2. Chain of Pain → Earth’s Punishment → Condemnation every time it’s back. Watch the timing: Chain of Pain comes back every 20s and lasts 10; Earth’s Punishment comes back every 30s and lasts 10. Line the two up when they overlap.
  3. Mark of Weakening every 10s (lowers the boss’s damage and, with Noble Energy at level 10, raises yours), Thunderclap every 45s if your mana allows it.
  4. Lightning Barrage as soon as the boss goes into Stagger.
  5. Left click in between, for the mana.
  6. Move out of the hits marked on the ground: from 20m it’s easier than for melee.

Mana and HP

Section titled “Mana and HP”
  • Out of mana → left click, and no right click or Thunderclap at all. With Shock Release’s rank-8 specialization, its chain Blessing of Regeneration gives back mana (40% per the Korean database), but you spend your antidote against crowd control.
  • Low on HP → Light of Healing (with its specialization, three in a row) and Light of Regeneration always up.
  • In a group, your job changes: Light of Regeneration and Radiance of Recovery for everyone, and Summon Revive if someone goes down.

Common mistakes

Section titled “Common mistakes”
  • Pressing Condemnation without Chain of Pain up. It won’t fire.
  • Putting Thunderclap in the macro.
  • Leveling up heals instead of damage while you’re leveling.
  • Standing in the middle of the group: take the edge and don’t stray out of your heals’ range (40m).
