Chanter: rotation and macros (PvE)

Region: global · Version: skill texts from the global client in Spanish (2026-10-01) and macro rules from the global Launch Scale Test (2026-09-20) · Reviewed: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: medium. What each skill does and what windows it opens is read from the client; the rotation and the setup are our recommendation for leveling and PvE. What to level at each level is on Chanter: what to level at each level.

How the Chanter deals damage, in five sentences

Section titled “How the Chanter deals damage, in five sentences”
  1. Dark Crush (Fractura sombría) is your strong hit, but it only exists during a short window opened by four skills: Impact Strike (Fractura impactante) (2s), Spin Strike (Golpe giratorio) (2s), and the stigmas Marcutan’s Wrath (Ira de Marchutan) (3s) and Binding Brand (Estigma de amarre) (3s). The client says so on each one: “when using the skill, you can use [Dark Crush] for 2s.”
  2. The windows open when you use the skill, so the combo also works against bosses, which are immune to stun.
  3. Crowd control chains together: Impact Strike stuns (always on mobs), Wave Blow (Golpe ondulatorio) knocks down the stunned target if it’s within 4m, and Spell of Fury (Hechizo de furia) hits harder against anything stunned, knocked down or in Stagger (Tambaleo).
  4. Mana comes from the left click. Incredible Blow’s (Fractura trituradora) chain gives back 100 + 100 + 120, and Charge Strike (Trituración de acometida) another 100. The right click and Spin Strike spend it.
  5. You heal yourself and the group with Recuperation (Hechizo de recobro): the Chanter doesn’t stop between groups to drink potions.

The priority macro

Section titled “The priority macro”

In the Skills window, the Skill Macro grid lets you stack several skills onto one key; pressing it fires the first one available, starting from the highest priority, skipping the ones that can’t be used. Conditional skills go on top and filler goes at the bottom.

Right click, from highest to lowest priority:

  1. Dark Crush: only fires with an open window.
  2. Wave Blow: only fires if the target is stunned.
  3. Scorch Strike: only fires after a block.
  4. Incandescent Blow: the filler.

Left click: Windrage Fury on top (only during Stagger) and Incredible Blow at the bottom.

Check it before you trust it

Same as with the Templar, we haven’t verified ourselves which direction priority counts in the grid, whether mouse clicks accept a macro stack, or whether the left-click chain still fires from inside a macro. Test it on a mob: Dark Crush on top and Incandescent Blow below it, press Impact Strike and then right click. If Dark Crush fires, it’s fine; if not, flip the order.

Outside the macro, always by hand: Recuperation (it’s your heal), Charge Strike and Vibration Strike (they’re charges), Shock Release (it’s a reaction) and Power of the Storm (you decide the moment). The September Korean guides also recommend putting few skills in each macro: every extra one reduces how often the rest fire.

Settings

Section titled “Settings”
  • Macro delay: 30ms by default; with ping above 80-100ms, 40-50ms.
  • Skill queue: try it both on and off.

The step-by-step rotation

Section titled “The step-by-step rotation”

Against a group of mobs

Section titled “Against a group of mobs”

From 1 to 13:

  1. Charge Strike on the group: you get in, hit 4, and recover mana.
  2. Impact Strike on the strongest mob, already in melee → right click (Dark Crush). From level 12, another press fires Wave Blow on the stunned target (4m range).
  3. Left click in a chain until Impact Strike comes back. Right click only with mana to spare.
  4. Recuperation when you drop below ~70%.

From 14 onward (with Spin Strike):

  1. From a distance, Spin Strike at the center of the group → right click (Dark Crush, at 20m).
  2. Charge Strike to get in (recovers mana) and left click two or three hits: that’s how long Dark Crush takes to come back (5s).
  3. Already in melee: Impact Strike → right click (Dark Crush) → right click (Wave Blow on the stunned target, who’s within 4m).
  4. Left click in a chain until the next window. Always leave a few seconds between windows: opening two in a row wastes the second one.
  5. Recuperation when needed.

From 22: turn on Undefeated Mantra and don’t turn it off. From 27: Power of the Storm at the start of big groups. From 32 (if you take Marcutan’s Wrath): it’s a third window, the longest one (3s); use it when Impact Strike and Spin Strike are on cooldown.

Against an elite or a boss

Section titled “Against an elite or a boss”

Bosses can’t be stunned, so Wave Blow almost never fires (7% chance) and Spell of Fury only kicks in when the boss is in Stagger. The combo falls back on the windows, and it costs a lot more mana than against mobs (Spin Strike 250, Impact Strike 100, plus the stigmas): lean on the left click between windows.

  1. Power of the Storm (from 27) and Spin Strike at the start.
  2. Impact Strike → Dark Crush, left click for a few seconds, Spin Strike → Dark Crush, Marcutan’s Wrath → Dark Crush: a window every time Dark Crush comes back.
  3. Left click between windows, for the mana.
  4. Windrage Fury as soon as the boss goes into Stagger.
  5. Scorch Strike every time you block.
  6. Recuperation for you and the group; in a group, that’s half your job.

Mana and HP

Section titled “Mana and HP”
  • Out of mana → left click and Charge Strike; no right click at all. Spin Strike costs 250, Impact Strike 100.
  • Low on HP → Recuperation (with its specialization, two in a row).
  • Getting stunned in the middle of the group → Shock Release.

Common mistakes

Section titled “Common mistakes”
  • Pressing Dark Crush outside a window. It won’t fire: it goes in the macro, behind Impact Strike and Spin Strike.
  • Spending mana on the right click and running out for Spin Strike.
  • Turning off Undefeated Mantra or not turning it back on after dying.
  • Putting your heal in the macro. You want it when you decide.
