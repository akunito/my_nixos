Templar: crafting and farming route

Region: global (anything that comes from Korea or Taiwan is flagged on its line) · Version: global client between the September test and the 2026-10-05 launch · Reviewed: 2026-10-06 · Confidence: medium. The mechanics (chain, Combo, Transfer) are seen on screen across several global sources; the prices are from one or two servers in the first days of early access and will come down. Our own calculations are marked as estimate. The crafting order is our recommendation, reasoned below.

This page is the practical plan for a Templar on global who already has level 45, is around 1,450 gear score, plays Elyos, has membership (and therefore the Market), has already taken Essence Extraction to Professional and is about to start Blacksmithing, with 3-4 hours a day. If your case is different, the order changes little; the Kinah numbers, a lot. The general system (professions, Substance Morph, Supply Requests) is in Crafting, gathering and Supply Requests and gear by slot in Gear.

Recipe and material names are in English, as they appear in the global client’s English-language videos; a client in another language may translate them. Before buying anything, compare the name against your own client’s recipe list.

The short answer

Section titled “The short answer”
  1. Order: longsword → the five accessories (necklace, 2 earrings, 2 rings) → guard → armor. Armor stays with whatever drops from Vakron’s Sky Island and Ferocious Horn Den.
  2. The global chain: Orichalcum (white) → Expert’s (green) → Artisan’s (blue) → True/Star Dragon Lord (gold, Unique, item level 62). Each step consumes the Splendent result of the previous step, and Splendent only comes from the 25% Combo. After that, Transfer Crafting at 100%: Splendent Dragon Lord (70) → White/Dark Dragon (78) → Splendent (86) → Ice/Wise/Ebony Dragon (94) → Splendent (102) (Global, 09-20 to 10-04).
  3. Real cost of a gold piece: about 4.3 attempts per Splendent, so if you do it all yourself that’s ~18-20 Expert’s crafts per gold piece, or ~80 white crafts if there’s a white step underneath (estimate based on Aragon, Global 10-04, and IruMinaTi, TW 09-27). One global crafter needed ~80 attempts for their Splendent True Dragon Lord Longsword (Global, 10-04).
  4. The money goes into refining stones and Wrathful materials, not the ore.
  5. It pays for itself if your server’s Market is moving: non-Splendent Expert’s guards sold for 2,750,000 and Artisan’s greatswords for 5,000,000 in early access (Global, 10-03 and 10-04). Those are day 3-4 prices and will come down.
  6. Projected income: about 13-15 million free Kinah per week with 3-4 h a day and membership (estimate). The longsword up to Splendent Dragon Lord: 15-25 million at early-access prices (estimate).

Why the longsword first

Section titled “Why the longsword first”

What the global guides say:

Source Order Why
Grogn (Global, 10-05) weapon → accessories → guard → armor the weapon is the biggest offensive jump; accessories are many slots; dungeon armor holds up
IruMinaTi (TW, 09-27) weapon > accessories > guard > armor the weapon has the highest soulbind value
Aragon, 12-step plan (Global, 09-29) 5 crafted accessories + weapon to True/Star Dragon in the 1,269 → 1,400 stretch; weapon to Splendent + accessories to 1,600; guard and dungeon armor 62 item level per accessory
Aragon, crafting and Kinah (Global, 10-03 and 10-04) accessories before the weapon 5 slots against 1; the weapon costs about twice the materials of one accessory
SywoGG (Global, 10-04) only craft in the 2,100-2,800 band: necklace → ring → weapon crafting exceeds Expedition’s (86) ceiling
Spid (Global, 10-01) craft the weapon instead of chasing the Fire Temple one the Fire Temple one asks for three times the voucher pity

The global consensus is weapon and accessories before the guard, with armor last. The only debate is weapon or accessories first, and it depends on what you’re after: accessories give more item level per Kinah (5 × 62), the weapon gives more damage per piece.

For this Templar we picked the weapon for four reasons:

  1. You’re going to level Blacksmithing anyway, and the longsword and the guard come from that same profession. Accessories are Handicrafting.
  2. It’s the biggest damage jump from a single piece: a +10 Spiritforged story greatsword (item level 45) deals 195-263 attack; a Splendent Star Dragon Lord (item level 70), 305-413 (Global, 10-03).
  3. The Templar’s PvE problem is its own damage against enrage timers (Global, 10-05).
  4. The dungeon alternative is slow: Fire Temple’s guaranteed class weapon asks for three 28-clear pity cycles (Global, 09-20 and 10-01).

The Korean order was different

A Korean guide put the guard second (weapon → guard → accessories → armor). Global guides put it behind the accessories, and Crafting and Gear already follow the global order.

Estimate, per gold piece and doing every step yourself: at ~93% success × 25% Combo you get a Splendent about 23% of the time, i.e. ~4.3 attempts per Splendent. One Dragon Lord attempt spends 1 Artisan’s Splendent (≈ 4.3 Artisan’s attempts), each of which spends 1 Expert’s Splendent (≈ 18.5 Expert’s attempts), and if there’s a white step underneath, ≈ 80 whites. Just in refining stones: ~4.3 × 5 ≈ 21-22 Artisan’s Refining Stones ≈ 8 million at 370,000 each. The gold step only needs one success, because the guaranteed Transfer gives you the Splendent: ~1.06 attempts.

Profession level needed: the weapon’s high-end chain asks for at least Professional 5 and “is really worth it” by around Professional 50 (Global, 10-05); Aragon says Professional 15-20 as a floor, and the more the better (Global, 10-04). Practical target (estimate): about 30 levels above the Dragon Lord recipe, i.e. Professional ~35 if the recipe is Professional 5 (with a 20-25 difference, Professional 25-30, around 90%). The client shows the percentage before you confirm: check it. That the gap between ranks counts as “Professional n = 50 + n” is a deduction from Aragon’s numbers, not something published.

Can the Combo fail on its own and eat the materials? One source says yes (KR/TW, 09-25), another says the base craft and the Combo can each fail independently (Global, 10-05), and no global video shows an item lost to a failed Combo. Open.

Step by step in the client

Section titled “Step by step in the client”

1. Blacksmithing from Novice 1 to 50

Section titled “1. Blacksmithing from Novice 1 to 50”
  1. Buy whatever’s cheapest that works as a base resource. In one Market screenshot, Orichalcum Ingot was at 496 and Strong Leather at 6,500 (Global, 10-04); on some servers the ingot sells below what it costs to make (Global, 10-02 and 10-04).
  2. If you make the ingots yourself: Orichalcum Ore + Oricalcum Solvent; from level 25 they come in batches of 10.
  3. Spam your level’s cheapest recipe in batches of 20-40 (Global, 10-05). That’s how Professional 20 is reached, with the cheapest item (Aragon, Global 10-03).
  4. Always craft in Safe Haven: getting hit during the animation loses the materials.
  5. Keep your failed crafts and the ones you don’t need: half of the daily Supply Requests and at least half of the weekly ones ask for crafted items, and they pay Abyss Points (Global, 10-02).

2. The promotion to Professional

Section titled “2. The promotion to Professional”
  1. On reaching Novice 50 a regional quest appears that opens Professional, same as gathering (Aragon, Global 10-04). The next rank caps again at 50 and the quest asks for a material from the tier above (TW, 10-05).
  2. No source gives the name, NPC or material for the Blacksmithing quest. Essence Extraction’s, which you already did, was a Splendent Ruby for Korius in Verteron.
  3. Do it the moment it appears: until you do, Blacksmithing stays stuck at Novice 50.

3. Expert’s: the money and leveling engine

Section titled “3. Expert’s: the money and leveling engine”
  1. From Novice 20, craft Expert’s Orichalcum Guards in batches (the filmed recipe). For the longsword, look up its Expert’s recipe in your own recipe list: level and materials are unfilmed.
  2. The ones that come out Splendent (Combo) are the next step’s material: don’t sell or dismantle them. Each line consumes its own Splendent (the Artisan’s Guard asks for an Expert’s Splendent Guard), so for the longsword you need Expert’s Splendent longswords: ~4-5, around 20 attempts (estimate). The guards are good for leveling, for selling, and for the guard chain later on.
  3. The non-Splendent ones sell: GAC got 2,750,000 per guard (Global, 10-04). Sell one at a time, because on the Market the buyer takes the whole batch.

4. Artisan’s

Section titled “4. Artisan’s”
  1. Per attempt: 1 Expert’s Splendent + 5 Artisan’s Refining Stones + 8 Pure Orichalcum Ore + 4 Pure Odyle (the guard recipe).
  2. Make the attempts with the biggest level difference you can: every point of success you give up is a whole attempt thrown away.
  3. Plan for ~4-5 attempts per Splendent (estimate). The Splendent goes to the gold step; the ~3 non-Combo successes sell (5,000,000 per greatsword in early access, Global 10-03).

5. True/Star Dragon Lord (gold)

Section titled “5. True/Star Dragon Lord (gold)”
  1. 1 Artisan’s Splendent + 4 Artisan’s Ultimate Refining Stones + 22 Fine Thick Balaur Horns + 10 Wrathful Mind + 9 Radiant Orichalcum Ore + 5 Radiant Odyle (the guard recipe).
  2. Here a plain success is enough: the next Transfer gives you the Splendent at 100%. If the Combo lands, you skip that Transfer.
  3. If you can wait, level the profession before this attempt: 77.5% against 94% (Aragon, Global 10-04).

6. Transfer Crafting

Section titled “6. Transfer Crafting”
  1. It has its own tab, with endgame materials (Global, 10-03). The first step accepts the plain Unique, no Splendent needed.
  2. First Transfer on the guard: 11 Fine Thick Balaur Horns + 4 Wrathful Mind (Global, 10-04). The Kinah fee doesn’t show in that video; the site read 1,000,000 in the September test and Taiwan charges ~1,000,000.
  3. 100% success, never destroys the base item. There’s also a 25% Combo inside the Transfer, which skips that step’s Splendent (up to ~3 rolls across the whole chain), but “don’t build your plan around it” (Grogn, Global 10-05).
  4. Before confirming, check what you lose (table below).
In the Transfer What happens
Enhancement (Growth), breakthrough/amplification kept
Soulbind kept, but the percentage is recalculated against the new cap: 91.5% → 67.5%, and GAC leveled it back up to 93.5% (Global, 10-04)
Socketed manastones, Potential, Theostone lost (Global, 10-04 and 10-05; TW, 09-27)
Can you sell it? no: whatever comes out of a Transfer is bound. Plain crafted items and their Splendents can be sold (Global, 10-03)
Can you buy the finished piece? no, only the materials (Global, 09-20)

Aragon says soulbind is lost in the Transfer (Global, 10-04); GAC films it being kept with the percentage recalculated, and three other global sources agree. We’re going with GAC.

Enhancement: +1 to +10 always succeeds and is fully recovered with Extract below +11; +10 to +15 can fail without breaking; +15 to +20 is a breakthrough with Amplify Stones, ~+1% stat per level (Global, 09-23 and 10-04). Since the Transfer keeps the level, enhancing the Dragon Lord to +10 early isn’t wasted. Soulbind, only once the piece is a keeper (GAC); Spid says wait until ~2,100 (Global, 10-01).

Potential: on crafted gear it’s added by hand with Potential stones, which come from Substance Morph on spare gear (TW, 09-27) and, per one hedged source, from the Abyss shop with a weekly cap (Global, 10-03). Since it doesn’t survive the Transfer, only put it on the tier you’re going to keep for a while.

Don’t confuse this with Equipment Transfer (dropped gear to dropped gear): it moves enhancement and soulbinds, destroys Potential, manastones and Theostones, and on global it costs 0 Kinah and one Transfer Stone made from Transfer Fragments off spare gear (Global, 09-23; TW, 09-27).

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Crafting where you can get hit: the craft fails and eats the materials (Global, 10-03).
  • Attempting the gold step with a small level difference: 77.5% against 94% (Aragon, Global 10-04).
  • Selling or dismantling intermediate Splendents: each one is the next recipe’s input.
  • Socketing expensive manastones or setting Potential before a Transfer: both are lost.
  • Counting on selling a Transfer piece: it comes out bound.
  • Soulbinding early: it blocks selling, and the percentage drops after the Transfer, so you have to level it back up (GAC, Global 10-04).
  • Buying a whole batch by accident: the Market sells the listing’s full quantity.
  • Treating 25% as one in four: plan for the long tail (~80 attempts, Global 10-04).
  • Spending Abyss time on Odyle with a siege coming up: the time is weekly.
  • Over-claiming pity chests: Exploration’s pity is 2-3 clears; more than that just throws Odyle away (Global, 10-01 and 10-02).
  • Leveling an equipped Clash Rune: it can break above +1 (Global, 10-01 and 10-02).
  • Taking Korean or Taiwanese prices as global ones: every absolute figure from IruMinaTi is from Taiwan.
  • Splitting Radiant Odyle expecting to downgrade its tier: no longer possible on global.
