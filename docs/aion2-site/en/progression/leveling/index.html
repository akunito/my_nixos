Leveling 1-45 and the first days

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Gates, stigma slots and energy rates were measured first-hand on the Global test build, and skill points moved off the Monolith onto sealed dungeons and side quests; no XP curve is published anywhere.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Your level cap is 45. KR/TW raised it to 50 on 2026-07-01 with Chapter 1, but Chapter 1 (Brawler, Eltnen/Morheim) is not in Global Season 1, so Global launches at 45. The 40, 45 and 50 figures floating around English guides are three different patch snapshots.
  • Levelling is short: hours, not weeks. Main story alone is roughly 4-5 h; a routed speedrun hit 3 h 21 m; a careful solo F2P run with no help took 13 h. On a casual evening schedule you are at cap in two or three evenings. The game starts after 45.
  • Do not story-rush to 45. A Gladiator taken to 45 on story plus the occasional side quest finished at gear score 962 / combat power 52,093, under the hard 1,000 gear score gate on Krao Cave (Crow Cave), Draupnir (Conquest) and the open-world Abyss, and could not even finish the story’s own Draupnir chapter.
  • The fix is cheap: main story + 10-15 sealed dungeons + a few strongholds + the feathers you walk past. That is the only measured minimum for crossing 1,000.
  • Level 22 is the most valuable day-one decision. At 22 Expeditions unlock and Odyle Energy starts accruing on that character, and Odyle is what opens dungeon reward cubes. The standard KR/TW plan: push your main to 22, then each alt to 22, and only then push the main to 45. How many alts depends on your hours, not on the meta: one character at 20-30 h a week, three to five only at 8+ h a day, because an alt only pays for itself if you actually spend the energy it banks.
  • Spend skill points as you level and chase the 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 breakpoints. “Three abilities at those thresholds outperform eight abilities at level 6.” Skill point respec is free, so there is no excuse.
  • Levelling gear is disposable and +5 on the weapon is the ceiling. You replace it four or five times before 45. Extraction refunds 100% of the enhancement stones but the Kinah does not come back, and Kinah is the real bottleneck of the first week. Never sell gear to a merchant: break it down.
  • Belt and Amulet are the exception. They are unique, one per character, non-farmable and cannot be warehoused. Their only upgrade materials are stronghold scrolls (Belt) and feather turn-ins at the Monolith (Amulet). Push them from the start.
  • Your class is locked. There is no class change at Global launch beyond an expensive, level-gated ticket (~2,000 Quna, unconfirmed). If you are choosing between Templar and Cleric, there is no free trial left to compare them on: the 2026-09-17/18 Launch Scale Test (cap 37, no shop, market or Closet, everything wiped) was it, and the choice now gets made for real in Advanced Access (early access) from 09-30.
  • Good news if you go Templar or Cleric: Global uses cross-faction and cross-server matchmaking for Expeditions and raids, and expedition parties are 5 players (KR/TW: 4). Tank and healer queues are described as near-instant. The 10-player first raid is no longer a Global-only concession, since Korea moved its sanctums from 8 to 10 players on 2026-09-09. Both classes also levelled fast in the test, and one creator’s sponsored dummy test at level 37 with gear equalised put Templar second of eight at 432,000 damage a minute and Cleric sixth at 326,000, with Templar, Cleric, Chanter and Assassin all rated easy to level while Ranger and Sorcerer dragged. Treat that as a single-source level-37 bracket reading, not an endgame ranking.
  • 4 character slots at launch. Gear, currencies and professions rebuild per character; only skins, pets, wings and stones travel.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

Aion 2 splits character power into a vertical track (gear score, enhancement, item level) and a much larger horizontal one (Daevanion board, skill levels, titles, feather resonance at the Monolith, pets, Arcana cards, Pantheon). One Korean creator puts the Daevanion board at roughly 70% of total power against 30% for gear score. It is an unverified estimate, but every other source agrees directionally. This is why “rush the story to 45” fails. The story hands you levels and a gold weapon, and almost none of the horizontal track, which is exactly what the 1,000 gear score gate actually measures.

The second structural fact is the Odyle Energy economy. Since Chapter 1 there are no dungeon entry tickets: clearing one is free, but opening its reward cube costs 40 energy, and energy regenerates at 15 per 3 h per character on the Global build, free and subscribed alike, up to a storage cap of 560 without membership and 840 with (about a week banked). It only starts accruing at level 22. That single rule generates the whole day-one meta: every character you will ever play should hit 22 as early as possible, because a character parked at 22 and logged off is still producing the resource that converts clears into loot. It also explains the recurring warning never to open a low-tier cube “because the item looks better than what I have”: you are spending a weekly-capped resource on gear the story will hand you anyway.

Third: everything in the world scales to you, so nothing is missed, but nothing gets easier either. Sealed dungeons, strongholds and regional quests re-tune upward when you return above their intended level. Skipping them to “come back at 45” trades a five-minute clear now for a level-45-difficulty clear later, with the same reward. That is the real argument for the measured slice: you do not lose the content, you pay more for it, and you pay it exactly when you want to be in Conquest instead.

Fourth: skill levels are stepped, not linear. A skill gains a perk slot and new perks at 8, 12, 16 and 20, and skill points alone only take a skill to 10; Daevanion nodes and gear carry it past that. Because the steps are large and points respec for free, the correct levelling shape is narrow and deep: two or three core abilities parked on breakpoints, everything else on whatever is left.

Finally, the Global build is deliberately softer than KR was at the same point: no Heroic tier, 5-player expeditions instead of 4, a 10-player first raid, and cross-faction plus cross-server matchmaking so queues actually pop. For a casual player with a group already inside, that is the difference between a Korean grind MMO and something you can keep up with.

Contested or changed by patch

The Stigma question is settled for Global: 4 equipped slots, unlocking at 22, 27, 32 and 37, one every five levels, out of 13 stigma skills in total, with a stigma level cap of 20. Korea and Taiwan instead allow 6 of 13 with no staggered unlock, which is where the older 21-22 / 23 / ~25-26 / level-~35 figures came from. The 2026-03-25 class overhaul and Chapter 1’s stigma level cap of 25 are Korean history and do not describe the Global build. The Daevanion board is called the Divination board by some English creators. On Global it is smaller than the Korean board by two divinities, and the tradeable PvE Daevanion board is absent from the Global test build, its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch; the PvP board stays. On Korea those PvE points sold for about a million Kinah each and a full board was worth up to 232 gear score. Board unlocks are quoted as 12, 20, 30, 40 and 45, and three of four boards were open at the test’s level-37 cap with the fourth at 40. Two Global sources disagree on which divinities survive, so do not plan a build off a Korean board simulator: a tester states flatly that the Korean board does not match the Global one. Sealed dungeon counts still disagree (62 on the Global map, or 50-60, or 60 per faction map), as do strongholds (~20, or 15 per faction). The feather count is now moot rather than resolved: on Global one pickup counts as 3, they show directly on the map, and the recommended target is simply enough to take the Amulet to blue. Time to cap ranges from 3 h 21 m to 13 h, but that spread is route knowledge and party versus solo, not patch drift: for Global S1 assume 6-10 h if you also clear 10-15 sealed dungeons.

Plan

Section titled “Plan”

1 to 22 (on the main and on every alt)

Section titled “1 to 22 (on the main and on every alt)”

Main story only. Spend skill points every level. Clear the story-required Expedition once and do not open its cube. When the MSQ blocks, fill the ascension bar with regional quests (J key). Target: 1 h 15 m to 1 h 30 m per character. Stop at 22 and roll the next alt.

22 to 45 (main only)

Section titled “22 to 45 (main only)”
  • Backbone: main story. Green and regional quests when you are blocked or when the reward is an accessory.
  • 10-15 sealed dungeons + side quests + a few strongholds + the feathers you pass. That is the minimum to cross 1,000 gear score. On Global the order changed: each sealed dungeon gives +2 skill points and +2 Daevanion points, each regional side quest +1 and +1, and the Monolith no longer gives skill points at all, so dungeons and side quests come first and feathers exist to feed the Amulet. Green regional quests also give +3 permanent passive stat points each. Strongholds are still the only source of Belt scrolls, and they hide 2-3 one-time chests of off-cap Kinah that are gone for good if you leave the run without them.
  • Skills: the left-click and right-click core abilities to 8, then to 12; then the stagger or control skill; then passives. On Templar the order is now published for the Global build: left-click (the mana generator) and right-click first, then the fourth-slot charge ability for pack clearing, then the passives Punishing Benedict (crit up), Insulting Roar (magic defence plus attack % on a frontal hit) and Fury (party-wide frontal, PvE and PvP damage, procs every second while you block). Once dungeons start, add Enhanced Health, the block-% passive, and Survivor Willpower for boss stuns and slows. A second Global source instead specs the absorb and heal-over-time options while levelling and respecs to full DPS at 45; respec is free, so either is cheap to test.
  • Daevanion board: orange (corner/core) nodes first, then blue (active skill levels), then green (passives). Bank leftover points for the next board rather than dumping them.
  • Gear: quest gear only to 20; keep the weapon current from 20 to 35 and take it to +5; from 35 to 45 equip upgrades but do not invest. Spread what little you do enhance across weapon, guard/gaiter and accessories rather than one slot.
  • Belt and Amulet: spend every stronghold scroll and Monolith scroll as it arrives; at item level +10 convert their grade with Material Conversion (Alt+H) toward Unique.
  • Gathering (Essence Extraction): Taiwan locked gathering behind level 45 as an anti-bot measure; Global opens it from level 1, confirmed in the test. Grab the nodes you walk past, though one Global tester says gathering is not worth prioritising while levelling and that only early-game materials sell for much. Novice rank caps at 50 pending a promotion quest in Verturon Village, still unconfirmed for Global.
  • Titles: equip the best Attack title, the best Defense title and one utility title. They give three simultaneous stat effects.

The published route (Elyos, 10 to 25)

Section titled “The published route (Elyos, 10 to 25)”

Dawn Legion Base → Cantas Valley (Coastal Camp, Collapsed Barrier Tower, Spirit’s Rest, Marsh Outpost) → Verteron Fortress Ruins → Astin Mercenaries Base (~25). That is 31 chained main quests between 11 and 25. Nothing is published above 25, and the Asmodian route is undocumented. The home regions are Verteron (Elyos) and Altgard (Asmodian); Eltnen and Morheim are Chapter 1 and therefore KR-only for now.

Immediately after 45 (the band that actually matters)

Section titled “Immediately after 45 (the band that actually matters)”

KR benchmark path from ~900 combat power: awakening quest weapon (≈1,000) → buy cheap Unique armour on the market and take armour to +5, weapon and guard to +10 → roll low manastones to two green lines (≈1,100-1,200) → clear every sealed dungeon, garrison and regional quest for Daevanion crystals, where 1 crystal = 1 combat power (≈1,400, about 10 h) → Abyss Points (5 Duty Missions × 500, Supply Requests, Corridor at 5-8k a run) → buy one Abyss ring and one earring and socket upper-grade manastones → Krao Cave / Draupnir cube #14 with its guaranteed necklace (≈1,600) → Transcendence for Arcana cards → conquest dungeons → enhance and Material-Convert Amulet and Belt (2,000+) → Rift of Time and Space into the enemy map for their sealed dungeons (2,200+).

Speed tricks if you want the 3-4 h run

Section titled “Speed tricks if you want the 3-4 h run”

Cap weapon and accessory enhancement at +5; keep the divination board topped up; skills to 8 then 12; from 22, slot one new stigma shard per level; do all your upgrade paperwork on loading screens and auto-path; after finishing anything inside an instance, open the map and teleport out rather than walking. In flight, hold Shift and fold and unfold your wings to glide on momentum: more ground than normal flight, and a Global tester measured it at roughly half the stamina of flying the same distance. Use wings under about 400-500 m and a teleport beyond 600-700 m, collecting waypoints as you pass them. Above all, play in a party: four people pulling packs and AoE-ing reached 45 in about 9 h where the same creator solo took 13. One warning about all KR times: Global has no quest auto-teleport between delivery points, which KR and Taiwan do have for the whole 1-45 stretch, so every Korean figure understates the walking.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Arriving at 45 under 1,000 gear score. The canonical trap: it locks Krao Cave, Draupnir, the Abyss and the end of the story itself.
  • Socketing junk manastones to fake your way past the 1,000 gate. It works on the number and fails in the dungeon: the character is still too squishy to function.
  • Auto-running the story without spending skill points, then walling on the first serious boss.
  • Burning Odyle Energy before 45 on low-tier cubes.
  • Leaving a stronghold without its 2-3 one-time chests. They pay off-cap Kinah and they do not come back.
  • Spending a key on a yellow cube. Yellow cubes open for free; only red cubes need a key.
  • Dodging on Korean muscle memory. One creator, counting frames off his own footage, puts the boss hit at about 24 frames after the telegraph fills on Global against about 8 on Korea, roughly three times the reaction window. It is a single reading, but if it holds, dodge to the attack landing rather than to the circle filling.
  • Only levelling one character on day one. You permanently forfeit the energy those alts would have been banking.
  • Selling gear to merchants instead of breaking it down. Enhancement stones matter far more than the gold.
  • Over-enhancing levelling gear. Stones come back on extraction; Kinah does not.
  • Leaving Belt and Amulet unenhanced. They cannot be warehoused, extracted or refarmed.
  • Enhancing the Clash Rune casually. Unlike normal gear it is destroyed on a failure from +2, and the warning text is easy to miss.
  • Equipping Belt or Amulet before a morph or conversion: you can feed the equipped item into the upgrade.
  • Spending general Daevanion essence expecting it to work on the specialised PvE and PvP Daevanion boards. Those need their own essence, earned only after 45, and on Global the PvE board is absent from the test build while the PvP one stays.
  • Buying on the Market in the first three or four days. Prices collapse once farmers list; it is the standard Korean-launch pattern.
  • Having treated the 09-17/18 test as a head start. Everything wiped, and the client was a separate 60-80 GB Steam app (4972320). If it is still installed, uninstall it and download the real one (3393110) in time for Advanced Access on 09-30.
  • Assuming a class change exists. It does not, cheaply.
  • Skipping Shugo Festa and Dimensional Invasion while levelling is correct, because their reward tiers scale down below max level and they consume limited keys. Save them for 45.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

No XP curve, per-level zone list or time-per-level table is published anywhere. The only published English routing covers Elyos levels 10 to 25; the Asmodian route and the 25-45 stretch are undocumented, and the September playtest (cap 37) covered most of that ground without producing a published route. The Daevanion board count and unlock levels are not settled. Nor is it confirmed whether Conquest Hard ships in Global S1, whether the KR Growth Support / Deva Express catch-up package exists (both modern KR levelling guides are built entirely on it), or whether the Essence Extraction Novice-50 rank wall applies. The 84-dungeon-entries-per-week-per-account claim comes from a single source and conflicts with the per-content caps documented elsewhere. Templar levelling priority is no longer an open question: it is published for the Global build and sits in the plan above. What replaced it: which divinities survive on the trimmed Daevanion board and whether the tradeable PvE board returns at launch, whether characters can migrate between the Advanced Access servers and the free-launch servers and in which direction, and whether Korea’s rolling Templar nerfs from 2026-08-26 onward are in the Global build at all. That last one matters if you main Templar: Global shipped with a recent Korean balance patch rather than an old one, so it cannot simply be assumed either way.
