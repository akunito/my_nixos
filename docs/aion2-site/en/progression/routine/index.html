Daily and weekly routine

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The entry counts and caps are now confirmed on the Global build, and the Daily Dungeon turns out to be one dungeon rather than three tracks; no time figure is verified against that build, and Global’s weekly reset day is still unknown.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Almost nothing in this game is truly daily. The only real daily reset is the 5 Duty Missions (~15-20 min). Everything called “Daily Dungeon”, Ascension Trial, Nightmare, Transcendence and Sanctuary is a weekly budget you can burn in one sitting. That fits a casual evening schedule far better than the checklist videos suggest.
  • The honest number is ~21 h a week (~3 h a day) per character on basic recurring content, excluding pets, Abyss, gathering, crafting and story. Top 100 is quoted at 8 h a day. On a couple of hours an evening you keep pace with the content, not the ladder.
  • The minimum viable day is ~20 minutes: 5 Duty Missions. Miss them and you lose only that day’s pet crystals; they do not bank. Everything else waits for the weekend.
  • Two resources are destroyed by neglect and they are the whole routine discipline: Odyle Energy (Global storage cap 560 without membership, 840 with; overflow lost) and the weekly Abyss timer (7 h F2P / 14 with membership, no rollover). Nightmare tickets still cap at 14, but Nightmare turns out to be seasonal rather than weekly: a cleared tier never needs repeating and the ladder does not reset, so a missed week costs only the tickets. Set an alarm on reset day, not a daily one.
  • Duty Missions and the 14 “Daily Dungeon” runs are per server, not per character. An alt cannot redo what your main already did. Settle on a main before spending a single daily on anyone else.
  • As a Templar or Cleric you earn AP more slowly than a DPS from the same content. In the Abyss Corridor a tank got ~6,500 AP per 5-minute window where a ranged DPS got ~10,000. Lean on the uncapped sources (Supply Requests, Duty Missions, Command Scrolls), which pay a flat rate regardless of your damage.
  • AP is no longer the only road to Stigma Shards. It still buys them, but Global adds Shugo Games and Nightmare as sources and sells shards directly in the shop, so a PvE player who dislikes the Abyss is no longer structurally starved.
  • Alts are a real system. The standard KR/TW plan is main + 3 alts pushed to level 22 on day one so all four bank Odyle Energy. But KR has nerfed alts repeatedly and one creator’s headline is “don’t build a large roster to keep up; that burnout is self-inflicted”. Recommendation: 1 main + 1 alt, reviewed after week 2.
  • The 09-17/18 playtest showed almost none of this: season content, Transcendence, Nightmare, Ascension Trial, Abyss, arenas and the Market were locked at the level-37 cap. Advanced Access (early access) from 09-30 is where this routine first becomes real.

The three clocks

Section titled “The three clocks”
  1. Daily reset at 05:00 server time: Duty Missions, the Urgent Supply Request tab, login and event claims.
  2. Weekly reset (Wednesday 05:00 on KR, Tuesday on TW; Global unconfirmed): the 14 “Daily Dungeon” runs, the 3 Ascension Trials, the Sanctuary cube, Command Scrolls and Abyss Contracts, Battlefield tiers and the Abyss hours.
  3. Wall-clock events: Shugo Festa on the hour, Dimensional Invasion every 30 min, Rift of Time and Space every 3 h (KR: 02:00, 05:00, 08:00, 11:00, a 10-minute window and then an hour inside), artifact siege every ~48 h. On KR the siege is Wednesday and Saturday at 22:00 with the Abyss Boss at 22:30, and the Abyss Rift is Tuesday and Thursday at 22:00; Global’s clock times are not published.

The game punishes weekly neglect hard and daily neglect barely at all.

Odyle Energy is not spent to enter a dungeon but to open the reward cube at the end. On the Global build it regenerates 15 per 3 h for everyone (KR/TW: 10, or 15 with a subscription, and two Global-labelled sources still report that split), a dungeon cube costs 40 (80 with membership, which is two opens, and 80 for the Sanctuary cube), and the storage cap is 560 without membership and 840 with. Topped up by Substance Morph and the weekly membership shop. Spend it on the highest-value content you can clear, not on familiar low runs.

Capped and uncapped Abyss Points. Mob and player kills inside the Abyss count against a weekly cap; quests, contracts, Supply Requests, Corridor and events do not. The uncapped sources pay a flat rate regardless of your damage: they are yours.

The horizontal layer, on no clock: sealed dungeons, strongholds, feathers to the Monolith, Daevanion boards, pets and Genus Insight, Pantheon, Wardrobe, titles. Every “what to do at cap” source leads here, because Daevanion points convert to item level 1:1 and the whole block is free and never expires. On Global it matters more than it did on KR/TW: skill points no longer come from the feather turn-in at the Monolith at all, they moved to the sealed dungeons (2 skill points and 2 Daevanion points each, 60+ of them on the map) and the regional side quests (1 and 1). The feather collection itself is account-wide, so do it once, on whichever character is convenient, and let an alt pick up the Abyss-side feathers so the main’s 7 hours go to something else.

Contested or changed by patch

Settled this round: “Daily Dungeon” is 14 base and 21 with membership, not 7 and 14, and Global ships one of them rather than three tracks; the launch raid is Abyssal Forge: Ludra, 10 players; the party is 5; Pet Insight caps at 3; there is a single Abyss (“Chaotic”); there is no Heroic gear. Still contested: the Shugo Festa storage cap (7, 14, 21, or “more than KR/TW”); whether the open-world 1M Kinah cap also covers unbound dungeon Kinah; the Abyss contract allotment (15, 20 or 5 a week depending on the source). Arena sizes differ by region and the page keeps both: TW ran 1v1 plus 4v4 with 30 participations and 10 wins, KR runs 1v1 plus 5v5 with 30 wins each, and 5v5 now looks like the live size with 4v4 the pre-September name, though nothing is confirmed in writing for Global. Still unknown: the weekly AP cap. No figure is published here, because the only numbers in circulation are one creator’s stated figure against another’s self-labelled guess and the Abyss was locked for the whole test; what is confirmed is that the cap accumulates rather than resetting. KR Season 3 (2026-04-08) consolidated Ode Energy, tickets and shop limits per server and cut Expedition to 2 a day and Transcendence to 1 a day; nobody knows whether Global ships with or without that nerf. Suppression War was removed in S3 (Awakening War replaces it). Battlefield on KR S3 only in 11:00-14:00 and 20:00-22:00; nothing this round touches it, so if it reaches Global it is still the one hard clock of the week.

Plans

Section titled “Plans”

A. The normal evening

Section titled “A. The normal evening”

Every login, in this order (~25 min):

  1. Mail and login; activate the Deva Pass before running anything (its weekly XP is capped).
  2. 5 Duty Missions (J key). Reroll bad slots; avoid named monsters that are not up.
  3. Urgent Supply Requests (ESC → Supply). Take anything at ≤100 Kinah per AP; buy materials on the Market if faster.
  4. Shugo Festa if the hour lands; Dimensional Invasion at the half hour.

Rest of the evening, rotating (~2-3 h):

  • After reset: the 14 Daily Dungeon runs (on Global that is the single dungeon, Daeva Bio Research Base, for enhancement stones); 3 Ascension Trials; buy all 12 Command Scrolls and the non-gold Abyss Contracts.
  • Mid-week: Expeditions and Conquest to spend Odyle down (never at 560); Transcendence for Arcana; Sanctuary night with the legion.
  • Any evening with a rift: cross, clear 4-5 sealed dungeons on the enemy map, leave before the zerg.
  • Abyss: ~1 h a day with a group, never AFK inside. Once the 1M daily Kinah cap is hit on normal maps, move into the Abyss (exempt, and it pays AP).
  • Artifact siege and Corridor: non-negotiable; the largest AP block of the week.
  • The night before reset: Nightmare tickets at the highest unlocked difficulty (rewards are retroactive); all 7 Material Conversions; the weekly membership-shop staples (resurrection stones, Odyle Energy).

B. The 1-hour minimum day

Section titled “B. The 1-hour minimum day”

5 Duty Missions → Urgent Supply Requests → Nightmare tickets near the cap → one Shugo Festa if it lands → log out. Enough not to fall behind on a weekday.

C. The weekend day (4-6 h)

Section titled “C. The weekend day (4-6 h)”

The full Daily Dungeon pool, 3 Ascension Trials, Sanctuary, Odyle drained on the highest conquest you can clear, the full Command Scroll and Contract allotment, and the Abyss hours. The weekly caps are designed for this shape.

D. If you take an alt (day one)

Section titled “D. If you take an alt (day one)”

Push main and alt to 22 before pushing either to 45: that is where Expeditions and Odyle Energy unlock, and energy banks ~7 days without logins. The main does Duty Missions and the season-shop purchases; the alt absorbs flat-rate Supply Requests and event and login rewards (warehouse-shared). Fuse Daevanion fragments on the alt before transferring; unfused ones cannot move.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Letting Odyle Energy sit at the storage cap (560, or 840 with membership): 40 energy is worth ~300k Kinah (600k with membership) at Krao Cave (Crow Cave) Conquest.
  • Letting Nightmare tickets sit at 14. Spend them at the highest unlocked tier; clearing tiers in order is pure waste.
  • Skipping the end-of-dungeon chest to “save” energy: at 10 unclaimed, entry is blocked.
  • AFK in the Abyss: idle burns the 7 h.
  • Claiming both Sanctuary final rewards early: the raid closes for the week even with attempts left. Claim gates 1 and 2, then push gate 3 with what is left.
  • Entering Ascension Trial the moment you meet the item level: a failure is lost until reset. Bring a buffer.
  • Activating the Deva Pass late.
  • Not buying the Command Scrolls you cannot complete: bought ones carry over, unbought do not.
  • Buying gold contracts (150k Kinah) or top-tier Directives: bad value.
  • Rerolling contracts past 5-6 attempts: the cost escalates.
  • Spreading dailies across characters: you take them from the main.
  • Farming pets near enemy guard NPCs: it flags you for PvP even in PvE mode.
  • Crafting everything on one day: check the delivery board first.
  • Treating combat power as readiness: it opens the door, it does not say whether your build fits the role.
  • Spending Odyle below 80: the Sanctuary cube costs 80 and it is the highest-value open of the week. Keep that much in the bank at all times.
  • Clearing Nightmare tiers in order on a fresh ticket: a first clear of a tier pays ~1,000-1,200 points, a repeat pays 385. Jump to the highest tier you can actually kill.
  • Spending a hidden-cube key on a yellow cube: yellow opens for free, keys are only for red.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Global’s weekly reset day and hour: still nothing, after a full round of playtest coverage. KR patches on a Wednesday maintenance and TW resets on a Tuesday; Global has said neither. The weekly AP cap value, which no one has seen and which this page therefore does not put a number on; what is confirmed is that it accumulates rather than resetting. Whether Odyle regen really is identical with and without membership on Global, since two sources still report the KR split. The Shugo Festa storage cap. Whether Battlefield keeps the fixed windows. Whether Global ships with the Season 3 alt nerfs. No per-activity time is verified against the Global build: the 21 h figure is still live theorycrafting. All of this must be rechecked in Advanced Access from 09-30.
