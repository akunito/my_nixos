Versions and regions

Region: all of them (KR, TW, Global; TW mirrors KR) · Version: KR patches up to 2026-09-16 and the Global Launch Scale Test build of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The content cutoff is still inferred, but the September playtest let players read the Global build directly: the systems below come from the client, not from dates.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Global Season 1 is a fork, not a snapshot. Roughly KR content from launch through February 2026, plus the 2026-03-25 systems and balance overhaul, minus Chapter 1 (July), minus the Heroic gear tier, plus a set of “friendlier” changes KR never had. No single KR guide describes the version you will play.
  • The biggest divergence: no Heroic gear at launch, from drops or crafting. Unique +15 is best-in-slot. Every KR gearing guide from February 2026 on is about Heroic tuning, Heroic Succession and 2-billion-Kinah crafts: dead content on Global.
  • Class balance is not forked: KR patches reach Global at the same time. NCsoft said so on a Japanese stream, and the playtest client already had Chanter’s Spinning Strike as a ranged skill, a change Korea made on September 1. So the August 26 Judgment rework and the September 16 Templar pass are in your game: recent KR class guides are usable, unlike KR content and gearing guides.
  • Cleric is on safer ground. Its changes that matter (Reincarnating Touch, healing scaling with attack) come from March 25, inside the fork. It got buffs on April 1 and September 9 that you will not have.
  • Level cap 45 (KR is on 50 since Chapter 1). The September playtest stopped at 37.
  • Parties are bigger on Global: Expeditions of 5 (KR 4) and the launch raid, Abyssal Forge: Ludra, of 10 (KR 8). One more flex slot: good for a Templar and Cleric pair inside a fixed group.
  • The KR patch cadence is weekly, on Wednesdays, and violent. Four classes rebalanced on September 1 and four more the week after. Global will inherit that rhythm after a few months. Do not build around a single patch note.
  • No Global Season 2 is announced. Brawler, level 50, Morheim and Eltnen and Heroic gear are the obvious candidates; NCsoft has said nothing.
  • Do not go play KR or TW first. The January 27 patch added overseas VPN blocking and hardware-based restrictions, applied to the same PC you will use on October 5.

The four regions

Section titled “The four regions”
  • KR and TW launched together on 2025-11-19, same content and patch cadence; TW tracks KR day for day at least through Chapter 1. A TW video is KR content.
  • Global launches 2026-10-05 on PC only (Steam and PURPLE), ~10.5 months behind KR, with Advanced Access (early access) from 09-30. Five regions: NA West, NA East, South America, Central Europe and Japan; no region lock.
  • KR and TW have a mobile client; Global does not. A console version is in development with no date.

What Global Season 1 contains (confirmed)

Section titled “What Global Season 1 contains (confirmed)”
  • 8 classes. Brawler out.
  • Level cap 45; the Abyss unlocks at 45.
  • Expeditions in Exploration (easy) and Conquest (normal); no hard Conquest at launch. Stars: 1★ Krao Cave (Crow Cave) and Draupnir, 2★ Uruguru Canyon and Vakron’s Sky Island, 3★ Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den.
  • 2 Transcendence dungeons (Deus Research Base, Shattered Arkanis) with 4 difficulties.
  • 1 Sanctuary raid: Abyssal Forge: Ludra, 10-player. Chalice of Muspel (KR May) and Sorrow’s Snowfield (KR September) are not in.
  • Daevanion: the PvE board is absent from the Global LST build; its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text; unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch. The PvP board (via the Abyss) is in, and its crystals no longer come from Substance Morph, so they are farmed and account-bound rather than bought. Creators disagree on the board names, so read it by function: the PvE board is gone, the PvP board stays and is untradeable.
  • PvP: 1v1 and co-op arena (4v4 or 5v5, unresolved), non-equalised; a 10v10 equalised Arena of Tactics expected; at least one Abyss map. No Aion 1-style sieges, no Dredgion, no guild-vs-guild.
  • One $15/month membership gating the Market, personal trading and the Quna↔Kinah exchange; a per-character Daeva Pass; Founder’s Packs at $24.99, $49.99 and $99.99, all with 30 days of membership since August 7.

Where the cutoff is

Section titled “Where the cutoff is”

NCsoft has never published a list. Best reconstruction:

  • Before Chapter 1 (July 1): no Brawler, level 45, no Morheim or Eltnen. Exception: Odyle energy, which reached KR with Chapter 1, is in Global.
  • Before May 27: no Chalice of Muspel, no brooch slot, no 250 stat caps.
  • Includes March 25: the PvE/PvP damage split, perfect block and parry, the 30 s rule between immunities, the new per-class stigmas and the Chaotic Abyss. But Global does not have the fifth stigma slot: the playtest capped equipped stigmas at 4, unlocked at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37, which is Korea’s original Season 1 pacing rather than the March 2026 rules. This was the page’s biggest open question and it is now closed.
  • Conclusion: Global S1 ≈ KR Season 1-2 content + the March overhaul + a few later quality-of-life items, minus Heroic. Medium confidence: an inference chain.

Level 45: disputed as late as August 16 (one creator warned that “45” was the community reasoning backwards from the Brawler patch); the September dev stream settled it and two September sources state it flatly.

Heroic: removed, or never really there? One source says it was practically unobtainable at the KR launch (minimal drop chance, ~205M Kinah for a chest); another, from February, says by Season 2 Heroic enhancement cost had fallen to near-Unique levels. For Global it does not matter: it is not there.

Four Templar states, and all of them affect you, because balance is shared: March 25 (Battlefield Banner, reliability fixes, Shield of Ruin knocks down and shields, a defensive passive reduced in PvP only); August 26, the break (a game-wide minimum cancel time, Judgment as a 3 s window behind shield skills, PvP coefficient from ~34% to ~20%); September 1 (damage reshuffle); September 16 (cancel delays raised again against macro-cancelling, +10% and +20% PvE damage on four skills, Noble Armor cut from 5 minutes to 2, and Provoke changed from flat aggro to damage-proportional). Korean players reading the cumulative effect since August 26 call it a net damage loss, and the class board has been running a quit-and-reroll argument for a month.

“Three factions” is marketing: two playable factions, with the Dragon race as an enemy NPC faction.

How to read a KR guide for Global

Section titled “How to read a KR guide for Global”

Three filters, in order

  1. Date. Before 2026-03-25: predates the overhaul and the damage split; the skill numbers are wrong even for KR. For skills and balance there is no upper cut-off any more, since NCsoft keeps class balance in sync across regions, so the newest KR class material is the right material. The cut-off applies to content and gearing: after July 1 a guide describes level 50, Chapter 1 zones and gear slots you do not have. And no KR guide’s stat maths carries over at all, because Global cut the percentage layer (see the table above).
  2. Tier. Delete everything about Heroic grade, Heroic Succession, Heroic Bonding, Heroic tuning, brooch, pendant, seal, wing enhancement and 250 caps. None of it exists in Season 1. Your target is a Unique set at +15.
  3. Scale. Where it says “4-man”, read 5. Where it says “8-man raid”, read 10. Where it gives a boss item level, expect a higher gate relative to your progress.

What survives all three filters is most of what matters: rotations, stigma choices, stat-weighting logic, dungeon mechanics, the Ariel and Asphel boards, Arcana, Soul Fuse and the general shape of the daily and weekly routine.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Reading a September 2026 KR guide as if it described your game: it describes level 50, seal slots, 6,200 Sanctuaries and a reworked Templar.
  • Planning around Heroic gear.
  • Taking the September playtest’s numbers as final. It was an infrastructure test and NCsoft published no patch notes for it: every figure on this page from that build was read off the client by players, and several creators warn it can still be retuned before October 5. The direction, a cut percentage layer, no Heroic, no PvE board, is corroborated across a dozen independent sources and is safe to plan around.
  • Trusting English videos about “$40-50 a month”: that was KR’s stacked-subscription era, killed in July.
  • Playing KR or TW “to get ahead”.
  • Treating TW as a different version.
  • Expecting fortress sieges at launch: Aion 1’s sieges, Dredgion and guild war do not exist; Chaotic Abyss and Battlefield: Siege reached KR after the fork.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Is the Market the only trade channel after launch, or was the playtest’s trading block temporary? What raises and lowers Trust, and what exactly does a low score block? Does Season 1 really end on December 16? Two creators read that date off an item’s duration and NCsoft has not announced it, so treat it as inferred. Global Season 2: date and content, with Heroic gear, the PvE Daevanion board and Brawler the obvious candidates. Abyss Point refund rate on Global. The complete KR weekly patch log (the timeline was built from press releases because the official board is a client-rendered app). Whether hard Conquest and the easy/normal/hard Sanctuary format arrive “a few weeks in” (one creator’s speculation). NCsoft’s own English press is inconsistent on class and dungeon names (Elementalist for Spiritmaster, Marksman for Ranger, Cradle of Nothingness and Cradle of Nihility are the same); the Korean mapping is the reliable one.
