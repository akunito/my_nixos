Glossary

Region: mixed (Korean originals from KR/TW material, English names from NCsoft Global) · Version: KR/TW up to 2026-09-15 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: medium. The English meanings are well attested; several Korean spellings come from auto-captions and are marked where uncertain. Empty Korean cells mean we did not find a source we trust.

How to use this page

Section titled “How to use this page”

The Korean column is here for one reason: almost every useful Aion 2 guide is Korean, and the English names NCsoft uses for Global are not consistent with the Korean ones (the press releases alone call Spiritmaster “Elementalist” and Ranger “Marksman”). Search Korean, read English. If you paste a Korean term into YouTube or Inven you will find the guide; if you search the English name you will find a launch trailer.

Community shorthand is included where it dominates: Koreans almost never write 어비스 포인트 in a comment, they write 어포. Those short forms are given after a slash.

A blank Korean cell is deliberate. We would rather leave a gap than invent a hangul spelling, and several terms in this game only ever appear in English on the Global side.

World and factions

Section titled “World and factions”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Elyos 천족 One of the two player factions, literally “heaven kin”. Getting started
Asmodian 마족 The other faction, literally “demon kin”. Faction is picked at character creation and gates which map your quests sit on. Getting started, Daevanion
Reshanta / Rashanta 어비스 (the zone is called just “Abyss” in Korean) The floating Abyss region inherited from Aion 1. NCsoft Global spells it both ways in its own material; Global Season 1 ships one map, Chaotic Lower Rashanta. The Abyss
Season / Chapter 시즌 / 챕터 KR’s content cadence: a Season is roughly 10 to 12 weeks, a Chapter is a larger expansion (Chapter 1 landed 2026-07-01 and is not in Global Season 1). Versions and regions

Currencies and resources

Section titled “Currencies and resources”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Kinah 키나 The main in-game currency. Sinks: enhancement, socketing, the Market, Daevanion respecs. Monetization, Gear
Bound / unbound Kinah Bound Kinah cannot leave the character; unbound moves through the warehouse and the Market. Global splits it by dungeon type: Exploration drops bound, Conquest drops unbound. Monetization
Quna 퀴나 / 큐나 The real-money currency. Cosmetics, convenience, the premium Deva Pass track, and the only pay-to-win vector when traded for Kinah through the Market. Monetization
Abyss Points (AP) 어비스 포인트 / 어포 The PvP currency. Buys PvP gear, accessories and, critically, Stigma Shards. Cannot be bought with money and does not transfer between characters. The Abyss, Stigma
Capped vs uncapped AP Kills inside the Abyss count against a weekly ceiling; quests, contracts, Supply Requests, Corridor and events do not. A tank or healer should live on the uncapped sources. Routine
Ranking Points The PvP ladder score, separate from spendable AP. Since February 2026 on KR, death costs Ranking Points and not AP. The Abyss
Stigma Shard 스티그마 샤드 The only thing that levels a stigma skill. The scarcest progression currency in the game and the reason a PvE player still needs the Abyss. Stigma
Odyle Energy 오드 에너지 (auto-captions also render it “Ode” or “Odd” Energy) Not an entry fee: it opens the reward cube at the end of a dungeon. 10 per 3 h, 15 with membership, 40 per cube, weekly cap 560. Overflow is destroyed. Routine
Daevanion Points and Crystals 데바니온 포인트 / 데바니온 결정 The fuel for the node boards. Points come from side quests, sealed dungeons and strongholds; the Ariel and Asphel boards use separate crystals. Daevanion
Medal 훈장 (공훈 훈장), silver 은공 / gold 금공 / platinum 백금 PvP rank tokens from artifact sieges, won only by the winning faction. Silver buys Centurion gear, gold Tribunus, platinum High Commander. The Abyss, Sieges and legions
Minor currencies Centurion Tokens (Shugo mini-games), Phantasmal Fragments (Nightmare bosses), Oath Coins (season, ~3 months), Pass Coins and Mileage (cosmetics). Monetization

Progression and gear

Section titled “Progression and gear”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Item level 아이템 레벨 / 템렙 The gate number for content. Daevanion points convert into it at 1:1, which is why the free horizontal layer outruns gear early. Gear, Daevanion
Combat power (CP) 전투력 / 투력 A score of how many systems you have filled, not how much damage you deal. Two KR videos with over 150,000 views exist purely to say so. Use it as a content gate. Stats
GS (gear score) GS / 전투력 English-language shorthand that KR creators use interchangeably with combat power. Before the March 2026 overhaul the same ~900 to 3,000 number was called both item level and combat power: date-check every threshold you read. Gear
Rarity tiers 희귀 (Rare) / 유일 (Unique) / 영웅 (Heroic) Common, Rare (blue), Epic, Unique (gold), Heroic (orange). English sites sometimes write Blue, Purple, Gold, Legendary instead. Global Season 1 has no Heroic at all. Gear
Enhancement 강화 (stones: 강화석) The +N upgrade. Guaranteed to +10, about 65% from +11, and a failure never destroys or de-levels the item: it burns stones and raises the next attempt’s odds. Gear
Breakthrough 돌파 (stones: 돌파석) Five extra stages past maximum enhancement, Unique and above only. Gear
Manastone 마석 Socketable stat gem for weapon and armour. Top-tier rolls are about 0.07%; high-grade stones become mandatory from item level 67. Gear
Spirit stone 영석 The same idea for accessories. Gear
Godstone 신석 Adds a proc to a weapon or guard. Duplicate effects do not stack, and replacing one destroys the old one. Gear
Soulbind 영혼각인 / 영각 Three actions on one system: engrave raises a rolled line toward 100%, sync destroys a spare Unique to reroll exactly one line on a keeper (once per item), reset clears. Never sell a Unique drop. Gear
Inheritance 계승 Carries enhancement level, breakthrough stage and soulbind lines from an old item to its same-grade replacement. Investment in an early piece is not lost. Gear
Transfer Crafting 계승 제작 A KR Season 3 addition that carries enhancement, breakthrough and soulbinds across a crafted replacement. The piece binds to the crafter. Not confirmed for Global. Crafting
Guard 가더 The offhand slot. Every gearing guide that gives an order puts weapon first and guard second, ahead of armour. Gear
Stigma 스티그마 The second skill track: about 13 class-specific skills levelled only with shards, of which four to six are equipped. Build-defining, mostly instant-cast CC and burst. Stigma
Daevanion 데바니온 (auto-captions: 다바니온, 대반이온) The node board. Every point spent raises item level and combat power by 1 regardless of the node. Opens at level 12; Global ships four base boards plus Ariel (PvE) and Asphel (PvP). Daevanion
Pantheon 만신전 The board of ten god stats named after the Empyrean Lords: Justice, Freedom, Illusion, Life, Time, Space, Destiny, Wisdom, Death, Destruction. Conversion is (value × 2) ÷ 10 = %. Stats
Arcana 아르카나 Cards from weekly Transcendence that push a skill past the board cap toward level 16 and 20. Cannot be bought. Gear
Revelation Amulet and Monolith The amulet is upgraded with Abyss feathers turned in at the Monolith. A Unique +10 amulet is +10% damage, +1,600 HP, +800 defense. Gear

Stats and combat

Section titled “Stats and combat”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Base stats Might (위력), Dexterity, Precision (정확), Willpower, Intelligence, Constitution. Conversion is value ÷ 10 = %. Stats
Smite 강타 A chance to deal double damage, and the single highest-value PvE stat. Fed by Wisdom in the Pantheon and by Genus Insight slots 3 and 9 only. Bosses carry about 30% resist, so push past 100%. Stats, Gear
Combat speed 전투속도 Shortens the whole cast and animation cycle, and since 2025-12-17 charge speed too. Rolls mainly on weapon, guard and gloves. The stat you actually feel. Stats
Accuracy 명중 A threshold, not a curve: every 10 points of enemy block above your accuracy is a 1% chance to be blocked, and a blocked hit deals half. Hit the raid number and stop. Stats
Block 막기 The shield check accuracy is measured against. Back attacks ignore it entirely. Stats
Penetration 관통 Ignores a defensive stat. Iron Wall Penetration (철벽 관통) is the notorious one: not obtainable from manastones, which is why stacked Iron Wall is effectively uncontested. Stats
Damage amplification 피해 증폭 / 피증 A multiplier layer above flat attack. Weapon (무피증), critical (치피증) and Abyss-specific amplification are separate lines. Stats
Tenacity 근성 The resistance stat against CC and status effects. Stats
Cooldown reduction 재사용 시간 감소 Capped at 60%, sweet spot 33 to 36%. For Cleric the number is ~34%: it drops Earth’s Punishment from 30 s to 20 s for permanent uptime. Cleric PvE
Animation cancel 평캔 (평타 캔슬), “weaving” in English Cancelling the auto-attack recovery with a skill. The core manual-skill mechanic of the game and the reason Korean DPS charts do not match yours. Combat and macros
Aggro 어그로 / 어글 Threat. Tanks generate it through skills, not through damage alone, and lost aggro is what wipes KR groups on the Uruguru boss. Templar PvE

PvE content

Section titled “PvE content”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Expedition 원정 / 원정대 The standard group dungeon. Global runs them with 5 players (KR 4). Dungeons
Exploration 탐험 The easy difficulty of an Expedition. Drops bound Kinah on Global. Dungeons
Conquest 정복 The normal difficulty. Drops unbound Kinah you can funnel to your main. Conquest Hard (정복 어려움) is not in Global Season 1. Dungeons
Sealed dungeon 봉인 던전 / 봉던 Small solo or duo instances scattered across both factions’ maps. The main Daevanion point faucet, and per character rather than per account. Daevanion
Stronghold 주둔지 The open-world equivalent of a sealed dungeon. Feeds Daevanion points and the belt. Daevanion
Daily Dungeon 1일 던전 Badly named: it is a weekly budget of 14 runs per account and server, which you can burn in one sitting. Routine
Ascension Trial 승천의 시련 A solo timed trial, 3 a week per character. Also the cleanest class-speed benchmark the community has. Routine
Nightmare 악몽 The hard boss tier, gated by tickets (cap 14) and paying Phantasmal Fragments. The one tier where class choice genuinely shows. Dungeons
Transcendence 초월 A weekly staged dungeon and the only source of Arcana cards. Global ships two of them: Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis. Dungeons
Sanctuary 성역 The raid tier. Global’s launch raid is Abyssal Forge: Ludra, for 10 players (KR 8). Source of bracelets, brooches and seals. Dungeons
Named boss 네임드 A dungeon boss. Korean guides count them as “1네임드”, “2네임드” rather than by name. Dungeons
Duty Mission 사명 (사명 퀘스트) The only true daily: 5 of them, about 15 to 20 minutes, 500 AP each. Per server, not per character. Missing a day costs only that day’s pet crystals. Routine
Supply Request 보급 의뢰 Material turn-ins that pay Abyss Points outside the weekly cap. Split into Urgent (daily), Weekly and Season tabs. The reason not to vendor unidentified shards in week 1. Crafting
Command Scroll 지령서 Weekly PvE ticket-quests, 12 a week per account, unused ones carry over. Flat AP regardless of your damage. Routine

PvP

Section titled “PvP”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Abyss 어비스 The open-world, always-flagged faction zone and the only route to PvP gear. Unlocks at level 45. Layers are Lower, Middle and Upper; Global ships Lower only. The Abyss
Abyss Corridor 어비스 회랑 / 나락회랑 A short farm instance that opens for whichever faction wins the artifact siege. The best AP source in the game and the reason legion choice matters. The Abyss
Abyss Contract 어비스 지령서 The PvP twin of the Command Scroll: 15 a week per character, in white, green and blue tiers. Routine
Artifact 아티팩트 Contested capture points in the Abyss. Three are fought over at once. Sieges and legions
Artifact siege 아티팩트 점령전 The recurring faction battle over artifacts, roughly every 48 h on KR and three times a week per NCsoft for Global. Medals only if your faction wins; losses still pay AP. Sieges and legions
Rift 시공 / 시공의 균열 Opt-in PvP invasion of the enemy map, every 3 to 4 h with a 10-minute window and an hour inside. Once flagged you cannot unflag for about an hour. The Abyss
Kisk 키스크 A placed, destructible respawn point used on the enemy side during a Rift. The Abyss
Arena 투기장 Ranked and non-equalised. Solo arena is 고독의 투기장, the co-op arena 협력의 투기장. Arenas
Legion 레기온 The guild. Legion Commander is 군단장. Aion 2 has no guild war; the legion matters because it decides whether you get Corridor access. Sieges and legions
PvP rank tiers 십부장 / 백부장 / 천부장 / 군단장 Decanus, Centurion, Tribunus, High Commander. They are gear tiers and medal tiers at once, and Korean guides also use them as gear shorthand (십부, 백부, 천부). The Abyss
Shugo Festa 슈고 페스타 A wall-clock mini-game event on the hour, paying about 1,000 uncapped AP a clear, gated by keys. Routine
Dimensional Invasion 차원 침공 A wall-clock event every 2 h at the half hour, not guaranteed, key-gated. Skip it before max level. Routine

Economy and character systems

Section titled “Economy and character systems”
Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Market 거래소 The player auction house. Membership-gated, which is what makes the ~$15 subscription effectively mandatory: enhancement materials and manastones live here. Monetization
Membership 멤버십 Global’s single ~$15 tier. Unlocks the Market, the Kinah to Quna exchange, remote storage, personal trade, the Wind Breeze merchant, more Odyle Energy and more reward picks. It gates convenience, not power. Monetization
Deva Pass 데바패스 A per-character battle pass with a free track and a Quna premium track. Contains materials and currencies, not only cosmetics. Monetization
Founder’s Pack 한정팩 Standard $24.99, Deluxe $49.99, Ultimate $99.99. All three carry 5 days of Advanced Access (early access) from 2026-09-30 and 30 days of membership; Deluxe and Ultimate add only cosmetics. Monetization
Substance Morph 물질 변환 Converts junk drops into other materials at 100% for no Kinah: Odyle Energy, wings from feathers, Daevanion crystals, medals from fragments, belt and amulet upgrades. Unequip the belt and amulet first or they get consumed. Crafting
Crafting 제작 Five professions. Crafted gear is PvE best-in-slot per tier: +5% PvE damage built in, +2.5% per crafted accessory, more soulbind lines. Crafting
Genus Insight 종족 이해도 The pet stat board, literally “race understanding”. Rerollable, capped at 3 on Global, and the only place Smite rolls outside slots 3 and 9. Crystals come from Duty Missions and Command Scrolls, per account, and cannot be bought. Character systems
Collection Effect 도감 / 내실 (compendium, “inner substance”) Permanent stat bonuses for registering skins and items. Cash-shop skins do not count toward it, which is the one honest anti-pay-to-win detail in the model. Character systems
Wardrobe (the Closet) 옷장 Skin storage and extraction. Locked during the 2026-09-17/18 playtest. Character systems

Classes

Section titled “Classes”

Korean class names all end in 성, which is why they look interchangeable at a glance. Learn these nine and every Korean guide becomes searchable.

Term Korean and other spellings Meaning Where it matters
Templar 수호성 Plate tank with a shield, the Warrior line with Gladiator. Manual block, real threat generation, and the only class whose survivability has its own party value. Templar PvE, Templar PvP
Cleric 치유성 The healer, and in practice a debuff maintainer rather than a healbot. Healing scales off attack power since the 2026-03-25 overhaul. Cleric PvE, Cleric PvP
Gladiator 검성 Greatsword melee DPS, the other half of the Warrior line. Gladiator
Assassin 살성 Dagger melee DPS, the Scout line with Ranger. No cooldown ceiling on cooldown reduction. Assassin
Ranger 궁성 Bow ranged DPS. NCsoft’s own press releases sometimes call it Marksman. A slow starter by Korean reputation (대기만성형). Ranger
Sorcerer 마도성 Burst caster, the Mage line with Spiritmaster. Sorcerer
Spiritmaster 정령성 Pet caster. NCsoft’s press releases sometimes call it Elementalist. Spiritmaster
Chanter 호법성 Mace support, the Priest line with Cleric. Widely called the hardest class to play well; the Chanter and Cleric pairing is the KR “호치 조합”. Chanter
Brawler 권성 Gauntlet melee class. Exists on KR and TW and is not in Global Season 1. Versions and regions

Terms we could not pin down

Korean for Odyle Energy beyond the auto-caption spelling 오드 에너지, the original of Reshanta or Rashanta (Korean simply says 어비스), the Korean name behind “Arena of Tactics”, and the exact hangul NCsoft will use for Command Scrolls versus Abyss Contracts on a Korean-language Global client. Several skill names in our class notes are flagged tentative because they come from auto-generated subtitles: 송곳화살, 통곡타살, 아이젤 and 다다니트 among them.
