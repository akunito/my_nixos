What to check on day one

Region: mixed (KR/TW baseline + what the Global Launch Scale Test of 2026-09-17/19 measured) · Version: open items as of 2026-09-22, to verify in Advanced Access from 09-30 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Everything here is something we publish unresolved: either it rests on a single source, or two sources contradict each other, or it was measured on a test build that has already moved.

If you check any of these in the client, you can report what you see via the site owner. Nothing personal is needed: the line from the list, what you saw, and the date.

How to read this page

The Launch Scale Test of 2026-09-17/19 ran for two 6 h blocks with a level cap of 37, and had the Abyss, the arenas, the battlefields, the Market, the shop, Quna, trading and the Closet all switched off. Any Global figure on this site that touches one of those systems is a shop price or a KR/TW extrapolation, never a measured rate. NCsoft also moved several values during the test itself, so “LST” does not mean “launch”.

Always note the date and the region of what you see. A Global datum from 09-30 beats one from 09-17; a TW one beats neither.

1. Account and Market

Section titled “1. Account and Market”

This whole block is a first-hour check: log in, open the Market, see whether it lets you.

  • Can a F2P account use the Market at all? We publish: assume locked out, flagged as an unresolved three-way conflict (TW lets free players buy but not list; most Global sources say locked out entirely; one says free accounts can list but need membership to buy). Page: Monetization, Crafting.
  • Can a F2P account list even if it cannot buy? That is the concrete half of the item above, and one attempted sale answers it. Page: Monetization.
  • Does any direct player-to-player trade exist? We publish: it does not, the Market is the only channel, a stated anti-bot decision (GLOBAL, 12 deltas). The closing notice implies permanence but never says so. Page: Monetization.
  • What raises and lowers the Trust score? We publish: built by normal play, gates Market access when low, thresholds unpublished by anyone. Page: Versions and regions.
  • What exactly does a low Trust score block, beyond the Market and the priority queue? No source. Page: Launch and servers.
  • Does Trust start at zero on a fresh Advanced Access account? No source, and it decides whether you can sell anything on day one. Page: Monetization.
  • Membership price and tier count. We publish: one tier, $15/month, stated on camera by NCsoft (GLOBAL, 2026-09). Page: Monetization.
  • The membership perks, one by one. We publish: Market, Kinah to Quna, remote storage, Wind Breeze, more rerolls and more Abyss, a members’ Kinah shop, the double cube open (80 energy), Odyle cap 840 and a Shugo key stack of 21. It does not include personal trade or faster Odyle regen. Page: Monetization.
  • Is Odyle regen really identical with and without membership? We publish: 15 per 3 h for everyone, with a hedge, because two sources still report the KR 10/15 split. Page: Gear, Daily and weekly routine.
  • The priority queue: when it kicks in, and what revokes it. We publish: members “in good standing” enter a priority queue when a server fills, and it is explicitly revocable (official source). Nobody says what takes it away. Page: Launch and servers.
  • Market tax: 10% and 20%. We publish both figures on a single source. Page: Monetization.
  • Market fees and the per-account listing limit. Undocumented in every source. Page: Crafting.
  • Any Kinah price on this site. We publish: no Kinah figure comes from Global, because the Market was off for the whole test. Everything you see is KR/TW. Page: Crafting, Monetization.
  • Is the Kinah to Quna exchange automated or player-supplied? Unanswered. Page: Monetization.
  • What the “Unclaimed Quna” currency is for. It showed up in the client and nobody explains it. Page: Monetization.
  • Does the premium Deva Pass track carry power or only cosmetics? Open since before the test. Page: Monetization.
  • When the Cross-Server Market opens. NCsoft says “after launch” and gives no date. Advanced Access has a per-server Market and no cross-server one. Page: Monetization.
  • Bind-on-craft on end-game gear. We publish: high-tier crafted gear is bound on craft and cannot be listed, so the Market sells materials and lower-tier gear only. Page: Crafting.
  • Reinstalling the client. We publish: on Steam you must uninstall the Playtest app and install AION 2 as a separate title for Advanced Access (official source). It is the one mandatory action before 09-30. Page: Launch and servers.
  • The crash with no error window. We publish: the game exits with no message during the loading screen, the opening cutscene or the first 30 seconds, on the Global build only, while the Taiwan client runs fine on the same PC (GLOBAL, 2026-09-19, r/Aion2). No confirmed fix. Check whether it is still there. Page: Launch and servers.
  • The real download size. We publish the official 60-80 GB order of magnitude and nothing more precise. Page: Anti-cheat and KR/TW.

2. Character and skills

Section titled “2. Character and skills”

This is checked by opening the skill panel and the stigma panel, without leaving the starting city.

  • The official English name of every skill. We publish working names across all eight classes: Inven translations, names read off a stream, and our own. NCsoft has published no Global localisation. One screenshot of a skill panel closes hundreds of lines at once. Page: every skills guide under Classes.
  • The stigma reset cost. We publish: nobody has seen that screen. Skill-point resets are free and are a different system. Page: Stigma.
  • The stigma slot unlock levels. We publish: 22 / 27 / 32 / 37, four slots, with the first tied to the level-22 Ascension quest. Five sources list only 27/32/37 and two outliers give 23/30/32. Page: Stigma, Stigma planner.
  • Is it really four slots and not six? We publish: 4 on Global against 6 on KR/TW, on 12 Global sources. No official note gives a count. Page: Stigma.
  • Is the four-slot cap a Season 1 squeeze that lifts to six later? Claimed by one pre-test source, unconfirmed. Page: Stigma.
  • The stigma level cap. We publish: 20, with 25 deferred to a later season, read off the client. Page: Stigma.
  • The shard cost of levelling a stigma. We publish: 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 per level across 1-5, 6-10, 11-15 and 16-20, about 75 shards for 1 to 20, and bonus effects at 5 / 10 / 15 / 20. Pool of 13 stigmas per class. Page: Stigma.
  • The size of the stigma pool. We publish 13 per class, but the Gladiator dump has 14. Count them in the panel. Page: Stigma, Gladiator: every skill.
  • Do the level 1/2/3 loadout presets also save stigma level? We publish: they save skill level; on stigma level nobody knows. Page: Stigma.
  • What each rung of the 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 specialty ladder actually unlocks. We publish the ladder without claiming what each step grants, because two readings are incompatible: one says 16 is the fifth and strongest effect and 20 is a third specialisation slot; the other says 16 unlocks the third slot and 20 lets three be equipped at once. Page: Daevanion.
  • Is there a fourth rung at 20, or does the ladder end at 16? The live client shows three tiers (8, 12, 16) and five picks in total; two Korean guides describe a further tier at 20. Page: Spiritmaster: every skill.
  • The skill level cap and where it comes from. We publish: cap 20, skill points alone reach 10, the Daevanion board adds +4 to 14, and 20 needs soul-bind lines or Arcana cards. Page: Daevanion.
  • The skill-enchant cap. We publish: +20 on Global against +25 on KR/TW, and it is a single source relayed by four deltas. Page: Stigma.
  • Are skill-point resets free? We publish yes, on two LST sources. Confirm it with a real reset. Page: Stigma, Leveling 1-45.
  • Does the Global build have a Global Cooldown? We publish: unresolved. KR shipped a GCD on 2026-08-26, a Taiwanese review of 09-20 states its own client has none, and no LST source mentions one at all. This decides whether an in-game-only macro is competitive, which is to say whether you need a programmable mouse. Page: Combat and macros.
  • The fire order of the official macro. We publish: it fires top down, seen on Templar during the LST. A Korean Gladiator guide describes the opposite, that the lower ability has higher priority. Page: Combat and macros, Gladiator: every skill.
  • How many entries the official macro takes. We publish 20 as the best-attested figure, unconfirmed on the Global client. Page: Combat and macros.
  • The macro’s tick interval. We publish the rule (a delay at or above your ping), not the figure: one source says ~10 ms and the same build shows a 30 ms tick. Page: Combat and macros.
  • Is an external hardware macro against Global’s ToS? No source says. The only official sentence about enforcement (2026-01-28) names VPN blocking, reporting and hardware restrictions, nothing about input automation. Read the ToS before building one. Page: Combat and macros.
  • What the 2026-09-16 cancel delays actually cost. We publish: the patch raised the cancel delay on the Templar’s Continuous Onslaught, Punishing Strike and Judgment, and on the Assassin’s Beast’s Roar and Heart Gore, with no coefficients in the notes and nobody re-measuring since. If animation cancelling was worth 10-12%, nobody knows how much is left. Page: Templar: every skill, Assassin: every skill.
  • Does animation cancelling work on casters? Korean sources say the left click cancels and the right click does not, which implies it is per-skill rather than per-class. Unverified. Page: Combat and macros.
  • Skill Reservation on Templar and Cleric. Nothing is written about the two classes this site covers in depth. Page: Combat and macros.
  • The ~24-frame dodge window. We publish: the boss hit lands about 24 frames after the telegraph fills, against KR’s ~8. It is one creator’s frame count off his own footage, relayed by 11 deltas. Page: Combat and macros, Dungeons and raids.
  • Is there an active block button? We publish no claim either way. Four Global sources contradict each other (an active block of about 1.5 s, “melee get a block button”, a parry stigma as the only route, and “there is no button, block is a passive stat”). Page: Gladiator: every skill.
  • The god-stat (divinity) conversion. We publish the TW formula with a flag: one Global report has 1 point of Destruction giving ~0.1 attack on Global against ~0.2 on TW, single source. Page: Stats.
  • Does Global rename Spiritmaster to Elementalist? NCsoft’s English press calls Spiritmaster “Elementalist” and Ranger “Marksman”, and its own dungeon names are inconsistent. The Korean mapping is the reliable one. Page: Classes, Glossary.

3. Progression

Section titled “3. Progression”
  • Dungeon gear-score gates. We publish: 1★ 700, 2★ 1,400, 3★ 2,100 as LST figures, with the clause that NC moved them mid-test and that one source read 1,500 / 2,200. KR/TW run 1,000 / 1,600 / 2,200. Page: Dungeons and raids, Gear.
  • Odyle regeneration. We publish: 15 per 3 h, identical free and member (KR/TW run 10, and 15 with a sub). Page: Gear.
  • The Odyle storage cap. We publish: 560 F2P and 840 with membership. A 540 variant exists on a single source, and a self-hedged “cap 2,000”. Page: Gear, Daily and weekly routine.
  • Odyle cube costs. We publish: 40 per dungeon cube, 80 for the member double-open and for the Sanctuary cube. We do not publish the “cost 20, regen ~10 per hour” reading, which is one source on year-old KR footage. Page: Gear.
  • How many Daily Dungeons there are. We publish: one, Daeva Bio Research Base (enhancement stones), with KR/TW’s pet-soul and Kinah tracks marked “absent from the test build, expected later”. Two guides still list three tracks. Page: Dungeons and raids, Daily and weekly routine.
  • Weekly Daily Dungeon entries. We publish: 14 per week base and 21 with membership, not 7/14. Two sources. Page: Dungeons and raids.
  • The 84-dungeon-entries-per-week-per-account claim. Single source, and it conflicts with the per-content caps documented elsewhere on the site. Page: Leveling 1-45.
  • Which weekday and hour the weekly reset lands on. We publish: nothing, after a full round of coverage. KR patches on a Wednesday maintenance and TW resets on a Tuesday; Global has said neither. It is the first thing visible on 09-30 if you look at the reset timers. Page: Daily and weekly routine.
  • Nightmare tickets: how many per week, and what each step buys. We publish: the Nightmare shop still prices PvE board points (step 4 = 1,400) even though the board is not in the build. Page: Dungeons and raids, Daevanion.
  • The level-22 Ascension quest. We publish: it is what grants the first stigma slot, with three Global sources tying the two together. Check it is still 22 and that the quest exists under that name. Page: Leveling 1-45, Stigma.
  • The level cap. We publish: 45, seen in the playtest client; the LST itself capped at 37. Page: Launch and servers.
  • The skill-point sources. We publish: regional side quests give +1 skill and +1 Daevanion; sealed dungeons give +2 and +2, and there are 60+ on the map. This replaces the feather and Monolith route. Page: Leveling 1-45.
  • The 25 to 45 route and the Asmodian route. Published nowhere, even after the playtest. Page: Leveling 1-45.
  • Does the Growth Support / Deva Express catch-up package exist? Both modern Korean levelling guides are built entirely on it. Page: Leveling 1-45.
  • Does the Essence Extraction Novice-50 rank wall apply? Unconfirmed for Global. Page: Leveling 1-45.
  • Does Conquest Hard ship in Season 1? Unconfirmed. Page: Leveling 1-45.
  • The Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid. We publish: it ships in Season 1, 10 players in two groups of five, entry around 2,700 gear score (KR reads ~2,800), and its weapon moved from item level 98 to 102. Check the real gate. Page: Dungeons and raids.
  • Enhancement caps while levelling. We publish: green (Common) +5, blue (Rare) +10, and +5 is the ceiling on any transitional piece. That kills the KR “blue gear from a high-tier dungeon” route. Page: Gear.
  • Heroic rarity. We publish: absent at launch, Unique is the Season 1 ceiling, on 23 deltas plus a dev statement relayed via TW. Its replacement is craft plus Sanctuary raid gear at item level 102. Page: Gear.
  • Can characters migrate between the Advanced Access servers and the free-launch servers? We publish: they are separate groups, NCsoft says it will manage Abyss balance between them for an unstated period, and migration runs one way, from the free servers onto the Advanced Access ones. Confirm the direction before you pick a server. Page: Sieges and the legion.
  • The server transfer mechanism and date. Unpublished. Page: Launch and servers.
  • Does Season 1 end on 2026-12-16? We publish it as inferred, not fact. Three creators read the date off an energy item’s duration; NCsoft has announced nothing. Page: Versions and regions.

4. Systems

Section titled “4. Systems”
  • Is the PvE Daevanion board (Ariel) in the game? We publish, verbatim: absent from the Global LST build, its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch. We do not say “removed from the game”. The PvP board (Asphel) stays. Page: Daevanion.
  • The English name of the boards. They appear as Ariel, Aerial, Oral and Area Board depending on the source. We write by function and canonise no name. Page: Daevanion.
  • The per-node values of the board. We publish the figures one by one and never the word “halved”, because the cut runs a third to a half and is not uniform: crit 10 to 5, flat attack +5 to +3, defence 50 to 30, combat speed 2.5 to 1.5, cooldown reduction 2.5% to 1.5%, damage boost 5% to 1.5%. Point cap 45 (TW 50), the centre +50/+50 tiles removed, 11 active skills unchanged. Page: Daevanion.
  • The PvP board node. We publish: PvP damage 2.5% to 1.5%, and its crystals are farm-only and account-bound. Page: Daevanion.
  • How many boards there are and what they look like. We publish: no Global board is photographed. If you get in, screenshot all five. Page: Daevanion.
  • The board’s cost model: flat 1 point or by colour. Unresolved. Page: Daevanion.
  • The board’s reset rules. One source says node refunds get more expensive with each reset, against KR’s flat 500 Kinah. Page: Daevanion.
  • Do feathers still feed the board at all? We publish the doubt, now that the Monolith has stopped paying skill points. Page: Daevanion.
  • How many feathers a pickup gives. We publish: 3 per pickup on Global against 4 on TW, about 100 fill a Monolith, they are shown on the map and they are still account-synced. Page: Character systems.
  • What a Monolith pays. We publish: the named-stat payout drops from +4 (up to +8) to +1, the ~620 accumulated stamina is gone, the 2 exclusive skins are gone, and it gives no skill points at all. Ten deltas, unanimous. Page: Character systems.
  • What 500 feathers are actually worth. Nobody quantifies the per-Monolith payoff. Page: Character systems.
  • Where Potential stones come from. We publish: their source was not found anywhere in the LST client, and they now carry all of the PvE damage that crafted gear used to have. It is the largest hole in the round. Page: Crafting, Gear.
  • What Potential stones roll. We publish: they roll in a range (damage boost 1.5-3%, for instance), they apply to dungeon gear too, and they do not transfer on tier-up. TW’s full crafted set was worth ~22.5% total PvE damage, and that is the size of the hole. Page: Crafting.
  • Does crafted gear’s PvE damage line return at launch? We publish: a datamine rumour, relayed second-hand and explicitly unverified. Never in a table. Page: Crafting, Stats.
  • The pet level cap. We publish: 3 (KR/TW 5), on a lower stat curve built from a 3-star base, while the Genus board keeps 0-10. Thirteen hands-on sources. Page: Character systems.
  • The pet unlock cost. We publish: 5 souls, the same on KR/TW and Global. The “10 souls” from a q3 showcase is superseded. Page: Character systems.
  • The cost of maxing a pet. We publish: 105 kills total (5 / 25 / 75), footnoting that two sources say 80 (5 / 25 / 50). Page: Character systems.
  • How many pets and mounts there are. We publish: 250-253 on KR/TW and ~200 at Global launch. Page: Character systems.
  • Where Smite sits (the client calls it Double Chance). We publish: on Global it is the Special board, slot 4 only, capped at 3%, removed from slots 3 and 9 of every normal board and from titles as well. TW reaches ~24% via pets and keeps 3 and 9. Every “lock nothing before Smite lands on 3 or 9” line is now KR/TW only. Page: Gear, Daevanion.
  • Does the Genus board have 9 slots or 10? We publish 9, with the dissent noted. Two sources describe 1-9 and one describes ten. Page: Character systems.
  • The Genus slot roles. We publish: 1 and 5 attack, 2 and 8 HP and defence, 3, 6 and 9 accuracy and crit, 4 damage boost, 7 damage tolerance, with the special stats on 4 and 7. Page: Character systems.
  • The wing drop. We publish: a sealed wing drops from dungeon bosses at ~1.5% per clear, with no pity (about 67 clears), with dissents at 1.25%, 2.5% and 0.5-1.5%. Page: Gear, Crafting.
  • The cost to unseal a wing. We publish: 10 Radiant Odyle. The finished wing is not tradeable, only the sealed one is. Page: Gear.
  • Wing stats. We publish: one wing drops from 60 (TW) to 45, its owning-effect damage boost is absent, and dismantling gives 10,000 (sealed, Global) against 90,000 (finished, TW). Page: Gear.
  • Does wing enhancement exist? We publish: it does not in the Global build (TW also launched without it). That kills the “+10 on 15 Unique wings” title chase for Season 1. Page: Gear.
  • Which PvP gear tiers ship. We publish: 2 of 4, Decanus/Archon (no medal) and Centurion (silver medal), with no Tribunus and no High Commander, ceiling around ilvl 77. Cross-tier set-bonus stacking needs a third tier, so it stays KR/TW advice. Page: Gear, The Abyss.
  • The Closet. We publish: it was unavailable in the test, so extraction rates, the Collection Effect and the 700-skin title stay unverified. With no Heroic rarity, it is unknown whether the 10% extraction bracket vanishes or whether Unique rates move. Page: Character systems.
  • Arcana: 4 slots or 5, and the “+4 per card”. We publish 5 with a softening note, and we do not know whether the +4 is uniform across classes nor how random its “+1 level” roll is. Page: Daevanion.
  • The farming zone names. We publish: Isle of Spirits, Immortal Island, Elun River Midstream and Kantas Valley East are KR/TW map names that may not survive Global localisation. The method transfers, the coordinates may not. Page: Character systems.
  • The Professional cap: 50 or 70. Unresolved for Global. Page: Crafting.
  • Does gathering open at level 1 or level 10? Unresolved. Note that Global opens gathering, crafting and faction chat at level 10 where KR gated them at 45 as an anti-bot measure. Page: Crafting.

5. PvP

Section titled “5. PvP”

Nothing in this block could be seen in the test: the Abyss, the arenas and the battlefields were closed all three days.

  • Arena team size. We publish: 5v5, with the clause that nothing confirms it in writing for Global. 4v4 now looks like the pre-September name rather than a Global variant. Page: Arenas and battlefield.
  • Which arena modes ship. We publish the regional split: TW runs 1v1 and 4v4 (30 participations, 10 wins), KR runs 1v1 and 5v5 (30 wins each). Page: Arenas and battlefield, Daily and weekly routine.
  • Is the in-game arena equalised-stat and reward-free? We publish: no, we reject it outright. The source is low quality and everything else contradicts it. Page: Arenas and battlefield.
  • Does resurrection work inside the arena? Asked on the official stream and left unanswered. It is the question that decides Cleric’s value in 5v5. Page: Cleric in PvP.
  • The weekly Abyss Point cap. We publish no figure. What is confirmed is that it accumulates rather than resetting. There is a stated 5,000,000 and a self-labelled guess of 200,000-500,000; we publish neither as a datum. Page: The Abyss, Stigma, Daily and weekly routine.
  • The Abyss Point refund rate on Global. No source. Page: Versions and regions.
  • Abyss hours per week: F2P against member. We publish: 7 h/week on the Global claim against 14 h/week on TW live. Verify it in the first week. Page: The Abyss, Cleric in PvP.
  • Does membership double Abyss time? Open. Page: Templar in PvP.
  • Which Abyss maps ship. No list. Page: The Abyss.
  • Do support actions earn Abyss Points, and does healing? A December 2025 patch retuned the criteria but nobody confirms healing was added. It decides whether a Cleric can farm AP at all. Page: Cleric in PvE, Cleric in PvP.
  • Do battlefields keep fixed time windows? Unconfirmed for Global. Page: Daily and weekly routine.
  • Siege cadence and schedule. Unpublished. Page: Sieges and the legion.
  • Corridor access. On KR it is one entry per captured portal between sieges (2026-09). Whether Global keeps that, or whether its “weekly” means something stricter, is open. Page: Sieges and the legion.
  • Is the “3-core siege with a 10-minute timer” in one Global guide the artifact war? Almost certainly yes, unconfirmed. Page: Sieges and the legion.
  • Did the -50% Abyss healing ship? Open since before the test. Page: Classes, Cleric in PvP.
  • Did the non-stacking buff rules survive? Open. Page: Classes.
  • Real ping from Europe. We publish the LST’s European numbers: 40-80 ms to Frankfurt from Croatia, ~21 ms from France, 6-7 ms in São Paulo, 120-240 ms from EU or NA into Asia. We do not publish the “150-180 ms” figure in circulation: it is a Canada-to-Europe measurement inside a VPN sponsor’s ad read. Page: Arenas and battlefield.
  • Is there a 100-player battle royale? We publish: we do not take it. One Global source against three earlier ones calling it unlikely. Page: The Abyss.
  • Templar’s and Cleric’s specific role inside a siege. Nothing exists in English beyond “tank” and “healer”. Page: Sieges and the legion.

6. Per class

Section titled “6. Per class”

One line per class with the most decision-relevant items from its “what is still unresolved” Aside. All eight share the same background problem: the English names are working translations, and the Inven dump of 09-22 does not include the Korean patch of 09-16.

Templar

Section titled “Templar”
  • The Judgment window: 3 s, 7 s or 2+2+3 s chainable. Three incompatible figures (KR 08-25; Inven database 09-22; TW measured 09-13). It is the class’s highest-priority day-one check, because the whole loop hangs off it. Page: Templar: every skill.
  • What the level-16 specialty on the loop skills does, inside the 8/12/16/20 ladder this site publishes without interpreting. Page: Templar: every skill, Daevanion.
  • Fury: a 10 s or a 20 s buff. The database says 10 s; a Global-client guide describes a proc every second while blocking with a 20 s party buff. The likeliest reading is that the 20 is the separate “enhanced Fury” gear effect rather than the base passive, unconfirmed. Page: Templar: every skill, Templar in PvE.
  • Taunt and Noble Armor are stale in the dump: the 09-16 patch turned Taunt from flat threat to damage-proportional and cut Noble Armor from 5 min to 2 min in both duration and recast. Check both tooltips. Page: Templar: every skill.
  • Is Punishment ranged on Global? One recap says yes; the database still shows 5 m. Page: Templar in PvP.

Cleric

Section titled “Cleric”
  • Condemnation’s reset: 50% or 75%. Every Korean guide from 2025 and 2026 says 50%; the database of 09-22 says 75%. It decides how many Condemnations fit in a window, which is 30% of your damage. Page: Cleric: every skill.
  • Earth’s Punishment: the +10 s sits at level 20 or level 25. The database puts it in the level-20 slot, reachable in Season 1; a Global-facing September guide says 25, which is not. The ~34% cooldown-reduction target and the whole build depend on it. Highest-priority check. Page: Cleric: every skill, Cleric in PvE.
  • Radiance of Recovery: “+20% skill speed” or “+1 consecutive cast”. The 09-22 dump still says the first; the Korean patch of 09-16 changed it to the second. Nobody knows which one Global ships. Page: Cleric: every skill.
  • Condemnation’s level-16 specialty: the database says guaranteed multi-hit, two PvP syntheses say “20% root”. That is not the same skill. Page: Cleric: every skill.
  • Light of Protection against a Chanter. The Korean client text says the whole buff fails to stack with the Chanter’s mantra; the community reading is that only the amplification half fails. It decides whether a party with a Chanter wants your stigma. Page: Cleric: every skill.

Spiritmaster

Section titled “Spiritmaster”
  • Corrosion, and this is the one to check first. The live client says +10% damage taken from the spirit specifically, for 20 s; nearly every guide says +20% damage taken from all Spiritmaster sources, for about 10 s. Different magnitude, different scope, different duration, on the class’s mandatory debuff. Page: Spiritmaster: every skill.
  • The Ancient Spirit’s re-trigger: specialty or enchant level. The database puts the 7-to-5 basic-attack step on the level-15 specialty; a Global creator demonstrated it on camera as a function of the stigma’s enchant level (7 at +0, 5 at +15, 3 at +25). Page: Spiritmaster: every skill.
  • Fear’s own bonus: defense or attack. The live tooltip says defense, up to +20% in proportion to targets hit; the synthesis says +20% attack power on hit. Page: Spiritmaster: every skill.
  • The three Korean bugs nobody has checked on Global: Assault’s reset mistiming, Elemental Fusion’s charge node firing uncontrolled, and the elemental cast windup NCsoft acknowledged on 09-08 and has not fixed. Page: Spiritmaster: every skill.

Gladiator

Section titled “Gladiator”
  • Rage Burst’s cooldown: 45 s or 1 min 30 s. Two Global sources with the client in hand say 45 s (09-20); the Korean database of 09-22 says 1:30. That is the difference between two windows per fight and one. Page: Gladiator: every skill.
  • Is Experienced Counter Strike a party buff at all? Every guide says yes and that it clashes with a Templar’s Fury; the Korean text of 09-22 says “자신의”, you only. It decides whether running a Templar and a Gladiator in the same party of five makes sense. Page: Gladiator: every skill.
  • Is Zekiel’s Blessing still a party buff? Same shape: the database says you and up to four party members within 40 m, one Global source says self buff. Page: Gladiator: every skill.
  • Prepare for Battle’s duration: 5 s, 10 s or 20 s depending on the source. Page: Gladiator: every skill.
  • Page caveat: the line putting Smite on slots 3 and 9 as a Global fact is wrong and comes from porting KR slot numbers. On Global it is slot 4 of the Special board. Page: Gladiator, Gear.

Sorcerer

Section titled “Sorcerer”
  • The Firestorm data gap. The Inven dump carries all 20 rank rows for all 39 skills except Firestorm (불꽃 작살), which lists levels 1 to 10 twice. Its level-12 and level-16 specialties are listed but no row backs them, and it is precisely the skill that holds up the class’s cooldown cycle. Page: Sorcerer: every skill.
  • Six database numbers that disagree with the Korean guides from 2025: Blaze’s lifesteal 3% against 30%, Grace of Enhancement’s threshold 25% mana against 67%, Fire Mark’s bonus damage 20% against 10%, Hellfire’s cooldown 45 s against 41 s, and Delayed Explosion as +15% damage taken against “-20% tolerance”. Page: Sorcerer: every skill.
  • The missing 40% in Arctic Armor. Its Mana Conversion sends 50% of incoming damage to HP and 10% to mana: that is 60%, not 100%. Either the remaining 40% disappears, and then it is by far the strongest mitigation in the class, or the text is incomplete. Page: Sorcerer: every skill.
  • The multi-Sorcerer Frost Burst bug. On KR live, when several Sorcerers hit the same boss only the highest Frost Burst level applies and everyone else’s is voided, which is a tenth of your total damage. Unchecked on Global. Page: Sorcerer: every skill.

Chanter

Section titled “Chanter”
  • Spin Strike’s range: 4 m or 20 m. The dump says 4 m around a point 2 m in front of you; every source since 09-01 says 20 m ranged, and two creators verified it on their own characters in the Global build. The dump’s 250 mana cost and 30 s cooldown are already post-rework, so the entry is half-updated. The class’s highest-priority check. Page: Chanter: every skill.
  • 질풍남무 against 질풍 난무. One skill that cuts Power of the Storm’s cooldown against another described as a finisher on groggy targets. They may be one skill under two transcriptions or two different skills. We have not merged them: if the Global client shows one button, the question dies. Page: Chanter: every skill.
  • Which 4 stigmas for Global Season 1. No source in the corpus gives a 4-stigma set built for Global, nor says which one goes in which unlock slot. The only unanimous point is Undefeated Mantra to max, first. Page: Chanter: every skill, Stigma planner.
  • Barrier Spell’s HP floor: 10% or 20%. The dump says 10%; the synthesis and the creators covering the 03-25 patch say 20%. Page: Chanter: every skill.
  • The attack-speed cap. Three incompatible data points (122%, a forum thread titled 116.7 with no units, and a patch “122.6”). This site publishes no cap figure at all. Page: Chanter: every skill.

Ranger

Section titled “Ranger”
  • Gust Arrow (질풍 화살) and the other two follow-ups with no entry. Gust Arrow, Shackle Arrow (족쇄 화살) and Retreat Slash (퇴보 베기) exist only inside the text of a specialty: no entry, no numbers, no prose source. If you buy those specialties you do not know what you are buying. Page: Ranger: every skill.
  • Ranger stealth, and it has to be said twice. The 은신 / Stealth stigma is real, on a 1 min 48 s cooldown and restricted to out of combat until its level-15 specialty. What we reject is a different claim: that Ranger has innate, always-available stealth, asserted by two class-overview videos with no footage. What stays open is whether the stigma deserves one of Global’s four slots, because no source evaluates it against the other twelve. Page: Ranger: every skill, Classes.
  • Supporting Fire’s numbers do not add up. Rank 1 says a 15 s orb, a 25% chance and 1.5% HP absorb; the Korean syntheses say 30 s, 50% of damage as healing and around 13,000 per proc. The gap is an order of magnitude and no source bridges it. Page: Ranger: every skill.
  • Blessing of the Bow: self or party. The database says target “시전자”, yourself; the KR syntheses describe a party buff of +200 critical. Page: Ranger: every skill.

Assassin

Section titled “Assassin”
  • Stealth’s level-20 specialty resets its cooldown on taking part in a player kill. We publish that as the solo Abyss picker loop (engage, kill, vanish, re-engage), and also that you should not sink 15 skill levels into Stealth, because a cheap detection item nullifies it. The Abyss was closed for the whole test, so the loop has never been seen working. Page: Assassin: every skill.
  • Sling Dagger. One Taiwanese source names a Global gap closer by that name which resets on kill (09-22). It matches no entry in the Korean dump. Either it is a renamed Korean skill we failed to match, or it is Global-only. It is the biggest hole on the page. Page: Assassin: every skill.
  • The names, worse here than on any other class. Inven gives an English name to 3 of the 40 skills, and the Global-client names on the page are what one single creator read off his screen during the level-37 test, not a skill-panel capture. Two Global sources disagree with each other. Page: Assassin: every skill.
  • Elusive Clone’s real uptime. Between “never reaches 100%, which is why cooldown reduction has no ceiling” (TW) and “near-permanent, with a dead window of about 15 s” (single source). The direction agrees, the magnitude cannot be published. Page: Assassin: every skill.
  • Assassin against Templar and against Cleric. There is not a single source in the whole corpus. It is the matchup you will meet most and it has to be tested by hand. Page: Assassin: every skill.

What to report, and how

Most useful first: a screenshot of any class’s skill panel (it closes dozens of naming lines at once), a screenshot of the stigma reset screen with its cost, the exact weekly reset time, and photos of the Daevanion boards. That alone closes more than half of this page.

Send what you see via the site owner, with the date and the server. Do not include personal data, account names, or screenshots with your email or client ID visible.
