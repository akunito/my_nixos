Sources and method

Region: mixed · Version: research corpus frozen 2026-09-15, pages written 2026-09-16 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: high. This page describes our own process, so it is the one page here we can verify completely.

The pipeline

Section titled “The pipeline”

1. Cataloguing. 600 YouTube videos were collected and indexed across four languages: 230 English, 200 Korean, 90 Spanish and 80 Chinese, spread over 32 topics from single classes to monetization. Of those, 444 had a usable transcript, 116 had no subtitles at all and 40 turned out to be off-topic once read. Weighting favoured recency, view count and creators with a track record, not raw popularity.

2. Per-video notes. Every video with a transcript was read and summarised into its own note with timestamps, extracted numbers, and a term table pairing the Korean word with an English gloss: 460 notes, plus 147 re-done after the first pass turned up transcription errors in class and skill names. Those term tables are what the Glossary is built from. Web pages were read the same way into 60 further notes.

3. Synthesis. The notes were merged into 28 topic syntheses, one per subject, which is the layer where contradictions get resolved and dated. Eleven are class syntheses: Templar PvE, Templar PvP, Cleric PvE, Cleric PvP, Templar vs Cleric, Gladiator, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Chanter. Seventeen are topics: classes overview, getting started, leveling, Stigma, Daevanion, character systems, gear, stats, dungeons, PvP and Abyss, sieges and legions, arenas, economy and monetization, crafting, daily and weekly routine, combat mechanics and macros, and versions and regions.

4. Pages. Each synthesis becomes a page here, written in Spanish and English in parallel rather than machine-translated, with game terms left in English on both sides because that is how they appear in the client.

The region and version rule

Section titled “The region and version rule”

Aion 2 exists in three builds at once and they are not the same game. Every fact here carries three things: its region (KR, TW or GLOBAL), a version label saying which patch window it describes, and the date it was checked. Every page repeats those in its header box, so you can see at a glance whether what you are reading applies to you.

The hard rule is this: nothing dated before 2026-09-17 is GLOBAL. Global Season 1 is a fork of Korean content, not a snapshot of it, and no Korean guide describes the version you will play. Before the September playtest, the only GLOBAL-labelled facts were NCsoft’s own announcements. Everything else is KR or TW, which means it is reference material about mechanics, not about your numbers.

Taiwan is treated as Korea. TW launched the same day with the same content and has tracked KR patch for patch; a Taiwanese video is Korean content with different subtitles.

Confidence labels

Section titled “Confidence labels”

Each page carries one of two labels. High means the claim is directly attested by a primary source or is about something we control, like this page. Medium means the shape of the system is well cross-checked across independent creators but the numbers are Korean and may not survive the fork. Almost every page here is medium, and that is honest rather than modest. Contested claims go in a caution box instead of us picking a winner, and every page ends with its open questions.

Web sources

Section titled “Web sources”

Named, not linked to raw material: the official NCsoft Global site and its press releases and Team Updates, Inven KR, Namu wiki and its per-system subpages, Reddit, and the Korean patch timeline reconstructed from press releases (the official Korean patch board is a client-rendered app and cannot be read as text).

Skill icons and skill data. The icon next to each skill is an NCsoft game asset, mirrored from Inven’s skill simulator only to identify the skill in this guide — small icons, not full game art. The skill data underneath them comes from the same Inven KR dump. Credit: NCsoft (game assets) / Inven (source).

Reading a video id

Section titled “Reading a video id”

Citations in our notes are bare YouTube ids in square brackets, like RWyhmvmoiQw. Append one to youtube.com/watch?v= to open it: youtube.com/watch?v=RWyhmvmoiQw. We cite ids rather than titles because Korean titles get re-edited and an id does not.

What none of this proves

Not one number on this site has been verified against the Global build. Everything quantitative comes from Korean and Taiwanese play, and the Global fork removes Heroic gear, changes party sizes, caps the level at 45 and ships a single Abyss map. Treat every figure as a shape to expect, not a value to plan around. These pages will be revised after Advanced Access (early access) on 09-30 and again after launch on 10-05.
