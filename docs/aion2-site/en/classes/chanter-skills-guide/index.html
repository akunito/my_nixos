Chanter: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. Chanter also has one large, specific mismatch: the dump still describes Spin Strike as the melee skill it was before the September rework, and two creators saw the reworked version live in the Global build.

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients. Chanter has the worst naming problem in the game: the same slot turns up as Impact Strike, Impactful Crush and Impactful Crash depending on who is writing, and they are not all talking about the same button.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. On the actives almost nothing visible changes: what grows is the damage coefficient, which the database does not publish. On the passives it changes, and a lot.
  3. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  4. Tell a chain apart from a condition. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears after the skill that triggers it. A condition (Dark Crush needs the target incapacitated, Windrage Fury needs groggy, Scorch Strike needs you to have blocked) is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met.
  5. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in. If you came to understand where your damage comes from, section (b).
  6. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  7. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  8. Point cost: every normal skill costs 1 point per level from 2 to 4, 2 from 5 to 7 and 4 from 8 to 10, which is 13 points to level 8 and 21 to level 10. Past 10 they are no longer paid for with points. The 13 stigmas cost no skill points at all: they cost Stigma Shards.
  9. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on Chanter it is long: the class took four reworks in two months and the database and the guides contradict each other on several points that affect the build. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. Only 16 of the 39 skills carry an English name in Inven (name_en); the rest are community names or our own translations, and each entry says which. The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean client text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 4 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 39 in total. All 39 require a staff (법봉), with no exceptions.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Incredible Blow (격파쇄)

Section titled “Incredible Blow (격파쇄)”

Also Bursting Blow; creators use both names for the same button. Inven gives no English name.

Type: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: no target needed, can move Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 100 mana. What it is for: it is your left click, your mana battery and the source of the autocancel (평캔) that the Korean playstyle is built on. Early on mana is the real wall, so the mana specialties are worth more than the damage ones until your gear improves (TW, 2026-09-14). The 2026-08-26 patch buffed this chain by 40% and the next one by another 50% (KR, 2026-09-01). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% mana recovery / 1.5% lifesteal / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Spin Strike’s cooldown. 16: adds the 4th chain link, 폭풍쇄. Chains into: Resonance Strike (link 1), Thunderbolt Strike (link 2) and, with the level-16 specialty, a fourth link (폭풍쇄) that has no entry of its own in the database.

Incandescent Blow (백열격)

Section titled “Incandescent Blow (백열격)”

Type: active, chain trigger · Cost: 120 mana · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: no target needed, can move Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the other half of your filler, the half that builds groggy. Its level-16 specialty is one of the cleanest nodes in the kit: ignoring block and evade and landing as a multi-hit turns your filler into damage that never misses. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% mana cost / +200 block while using the skill / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit. 12: on a Rupture Strike hit, -200 accuracy on the target for 3 s. 16: ignores block and evade, lands as a multi-hit. Chains into: Rupture Strike (link 1).

Charge Strike (돌진 격파)

Section titled “Charge Strike (돌진 격파)”

Inven calls it Charge Strike; nearly every video guide calls it Rushing Smash (TW/KR, 2026-09). Which one the live client uses is unresolved.

Type: active · Cost: none · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front Rank 20: entry identical. Text: you charge a target within 20 m, deal damage to up to 4 enemies and restore 100 mana. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: this is why Chanter is the better farmer of the two supports. The level-12 specialty resets the cooldown on kill, so you chain pack to pack without dropping tempo (KR/TW, 2026-09). In the Global playtest a timed 1-to-cap run came in at 4 h 16 min on Chanter against 5 h 29 min on Ranger, and the creator puts the gap down to cleave plus built-in healing removing the downtime between pulls (Global, 2026-09-20). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% impact-type resistance on the target for 5 s / becomes a charge skill and damage goes up by as much as 200% / 50% chance to activate Scorch Strike on hit. 12: resets the cooldown on kill. 16: ignores block and evade, lands as a multi-hit. Data note: two Korean guides describe that second specialty as +20% attack on a tap and up to +60% on a 3-stage charge (KR, 2025-12 and 2026-03). The database says something else, +200% damage. They are two readings of the same node and nobody has reconciled them. Chains into: nothing.

Impact Strike (타격쇄)

Section titled “Impact Strike (타격쇄)”

Inven gives no English name. The synthesis maps it as Impact Strike; a Global overview from 2026-09-03 calls the same slot Impactful Crush.

Type: active · Cost: 100 mana · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and stuns it for 3 s with a 60% chance. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your ranged Dark Crush opener, and in PvP the finisher of the airborne combo. Against NPCs the stun is guaranteed, so in PvE this is the button that starts the class’s engine. The level-12 specialty adds a chain (파산격) that, against an already stunned target, lands as a guaranteed multi-hit. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): becomes area damage / +1 s stun / can be cast while moving. 12: adds the 파산격 chain, and against a stunned target that 파산격 lands as a guaranteed multi-hit. 16: +20% stun chance. Chains into: nothing on its own; the 파산격 chain exists only with the level-12 specialty and has no entry of its own.

Dark Crush (암격쇄)

Section titled “Dark Crush (암격쇄)”

Inven gives no English name. The community uses Dark Crush, Dark Crash and Dark Rush interchangeably; half the Korean guides transcribe it as 암격세.

Type: active, conditional on incapacitation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: can move Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a stunned, knocked-down or air-bound target within 20 m. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 7% chance on hit. What it is for: this is the heart of the class. Everything else exists to light it up, and the organising principle of every Korean guide is literally “never let it go dark”. That 7% against control-immune targets is why on bosses you have to manufacture windows instead of waiting for them. Read it in full in Combos, section (b). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 1.5% lifesteal / becomes an area skill / guaranteed crit on hit. 12: adds the 2nd chain link, 관통쇄. 16: removes its own cooldown. Chains into: nothing by default; with the level-12 specialty it gains a second link (관통쇄) that has no entry of its own.

Windrage Fury (질풍 난무)

Section titled “Windrage Fury (질풍 난무)”

Inven gives no English name. Windrage Fury is a community name (KR, 2026-09-17). Careful: the baseline synthesis cites a skill called 질풍남무 / Gale’s Charge with a different function; see the Aside at the bottom, because whether they are the same thing is unresolved.

Type: active, conditional on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target in the groggy state within 4 m. What it is for: a groggy-window finisher with no cost and no cooldown. Its level-16 specialty, -1 s to all cooldowns every time you hit, is what keeps it inside the one-button macro in the newest Korean doctrine: it is left there on purpose so it can be cancelled mid-animation (난무 캔슬), blending that cooldown reduction into normal-attack damage (KR, 2026-09-17). That same source names the technique and never explains it. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% lifesteal / restores 120 mana / can be cast while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Chains into: nothing.

Scorch Strike (열파격)

Section titled “Scorch Strike (열파격)”

Inven calls it Scorch Strike; the synthesis and the Taiwanese guides call it Heat Wave Blow.

Type: active, conditional on a weapon block · Cost: none · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: no target needed Rank 20: entry identical. Text: on a successful block, deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around the target and seals them for 3 s with a 40% chance. Against NPCs the seal lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: this is your answer to the class’s hard PvP matchups. With the seal specialty taken to rank 20 the proc sits around 60% (KR, 2026-06), and that seal is what buys you casting time against Gladiator and Templar, the two worst matchups Chanter has. In PvE the comfortable way to light it up is not blocking at all: it is Charge Strike’s level-8 specialty, which has a 50% chance to activate it on hit. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 40% chance to stun for 3 s / +20% seal chance / up to +20% damage the more targets you hit. 12: ignores block and evade, lands as a multi-hit. 16: on a successful block, 25% chance to reset the cooldown. Chains into: nothing; it is lit by a block or by the Charge Strike proc.

Recuperation (쾌유의 주문)

Section titled “Recuperation (쾌유의 주문)”

Inven calls it Recuperation; English guides also use Regeneration.

Type: active, heal · Cost: none · Cooldown: 15 s · Cast: instant · Target: you and up to 4 party members · Area: 25 m · Properties: no target needed, can move, cleanse Rank 20: entry identical. Text: restores health to you and to party members within 25 m and clears 1 weakening magic. For 8 s, restores health every 2 s. What it is for: your main heal and the reason a Chanter can solo-sustain most of the easier content. The oldest Korean levelling guide sends it to level 12 first, ahead of anything offensive, because that is where the continuous-healing window becomes permanent (KR, 2025-11-20). In PvP it takes one specialty only: casting it mid-fight is a net damage loss (KR, 2026-06). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +1 consecutive cast / clears up to 2 weakening magics / +4 s duration. 12: +10% health restored. 16: -3 s cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Vibration Strike (진동쇄)

Section titled “Vibration Strike (진동쇄)”

Inven gives no English name. Vibration Strike is a community name via the synthesis.

Type: active, conditional on emergency evasion · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 10 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: after using emergency evasion, you move to a target within 10 m, deal damage and stun it for 3 s with a 50% chance. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: dodge punish and your second gap closer. It is the exact twin of the Templar’s Shield Charge: it exists only in the window after a dodge, so you do not press it, you collect it. The Korean PvE build leaves it at 12 with the range specialty; PvP takes it to 16 for the stun chance. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +300 stamina on hit / +10 m range / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: adds the 2nd chain link, 격동쇄. 16: +25% stun chance. Chains into: nothing by default; with the level-12 specialty it gains a second link (격동쇄) that has no entry of its own.

Wave Blow (파동격)

Section titled “Wave Blow (파동격)”

Inven calls it Wave Blow; the synthesis and the Korean guides call it Wave Strike.

Type: active, conditional on a stun · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a stunned target within 4 m and knocks it down for 3 s. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 7% chance and the animation changes. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: two things at once. It converts your stun into a knockdown, which is another Dark Crush window, and its level-12 specialty is probably the biggest party contribution in the kit: -20% PvE and -10% PvP damage amplification on the target for 10 s. With the level-16 node (-5 s cooldown) the cycle drops to 15 s for 10 s of effect, which the Korean guide describes as near-permanent uptime on a boss (KR, 2026-06). That guide treats the two nodes as one; the database splits them across 12 and 16. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): guaranteed multi-hit on hit / +6 m range / +1 s knockdown. 12: for 10 s, -20% PvE and -10% PvP damage amplification on the target. 16: -5 s cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Spin Strike (회전격)

Section titled “Spin Strike (회전격)”

Inven calls it Spin Strike; in the guides it is universally Spinning Strike.

Type: active · Cost: 250 mana · Cooldown: 30 s · Range: 4 m in the entry · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies around you Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a point 2 m in front of you. On hit, for 30 s your critical damage amplification goes up 5% and your healing amplification 5%. Stacks up to 3 times. Groggy gauge damage: 15. Version warning, and it is the most important one on this page. The rework of 1-2 September 2026 turned Spin Strike from a 4 m melee skill on roughly a 10-15 s cooldown into a 20 m ranged area skill: +50% damage, cooldown roughly doubled, 250 mana, and because it now counts as a ranged normal skill it became a fourth Dark Crush trigger (KR, 2026-09-01 and 09-02). The cost and the cooldown in the dump are already the post-rework ones; the range is not. Two creators checked on their own Chanters during the Launch Scale Test that the ranged version is the one live in the Global build (Global, 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-19). Treat the 4 m in the entry as stale and the 20 m as what you will actually find. What it is for: between 13% and 15% of your rotation damage (KR, 2026-09-02), plus a stacking self-buff you want to pre-stack on trash on the way to the boss (KR, 2026-08-20). The rework made it better in mass PvP and in AoE and clearly worse in 1v1 and on bosses: the old 10 s version, which ignored block and evade, landed roughly 3 times as often (region unknown, 2026-09-02). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -5 s cooldown / +30% skill speed / up to +20% damage the fewer targets you hit. 12: 40% chance to seal for 3 s. 16: ignores block and evade and lands as a crit. Chains into: nothing. Incredible Blow cuts 1 s off its cooldown per hit with its level-12 specialty.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Type: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: yourself · Cast: instant · Properties: no target needed · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, air-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. Text: clears your stun, knockdown, air-bind, grab, freeze and fear states and puts you in the Tenacious state (강인함) for 5 s. Tenacious: immunity to status ailments. What it is for: the Korean rule is blunt: blocking and just-dodging are the two biggest avoidable damage losses in the class after boss downtime, so save the block for attacks that would hard-CC you and eat the rest with Shock Release while you keep attacking (KR, 2026-08-20). In PvP it goes to 20 with specialties 1/4/5 for a roughly 30 s cycle of 7 s Tenacious (KR, 2026-06). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Tenacious, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage resistance / on cast, restore 1000 stamina / on cast, restore 20% of max HP. 12: +2 s Tenacious. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Forceful Strike (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

The four chains are buttons that exist only after the skill that triggers them, and all four are 100% activation. One database detail: chains have no specialties of their own, they display their parent skill’s. On three of the four that makes sense. On Forceful Strike it does not, and we say so in its entry.

There are also four more links that exist only if you buy the matching specialty and that have no entry of their own in the dump: 폭풍쇄 (4th link of Incredible Blow, level 16), 파산격 (chain off Impact Strike, level 12), 관통쇄 (2nd link of Dark Crush, level 12) and 격동쇄 (2nd link of Vibration Strike, level 12). For those four we have neither an English name nor any numbers.

Resonance Strike (공명쇄)

Section titled “Resonance Strike (공명쇄)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Type: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: no target needed, can move Rank 20: the rank-20 text carries an unfilled placeholder: it says “deals n damage” instead of a number. That is a dump glitch, not a data point. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 100 mana. What it is for: second hit of the left click. Same mana as the first. It is not a decision, it is the rhythm. Chained from: Incredible Blow, position 1.

Thunderbolt Strike (벽력쇄)

Section titled “Thunderbolt Strike (벽력쇄)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Type: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: no target needed, can move Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 120 mana. What it is for: third hit of the left click and the one that returns the most mana of the three. With mana tight early, finishing the whole chain instead of cutting it short is literally your economy: 100 + 100 + 120 per full sequence. Chained from: Incredible Blow, position 2.

Rupture Strike (파열격)

Section titled “Rupture Strike (파열격)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Type: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 mana · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: no target needed, can move Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the heavy filler’s link. It is also the one that applies the -200 accuracy for 3 s when you take Incandescent Blow’s level-12 specialty, which is the only accuracy shred in the kit. Chained from: Incandescent Blow, position 1.

Forceful Strike (강격쇄)

Section titled “Forceful Strike (강격쇄)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours. It is the closest candidate to the “Impactful Crash” two English guides talk about, but it is not a confirmed match: see the Aside.

Type: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies around you Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around you and stuns them for 3 s. What it is for: this is the most ignored thing in the kit, exactly like the Templar’s Seize Alive. It is not a loose 2-minute skill: it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a control effect, which means every time someone stuns you in PvP they are handing you a 3 s area stun, which is a full Dark Crush window to punish them with. None of our syntheses records it as a chain; the database does (Shock Release’s link_skill points here). Data note: in the dump this chain displays Shock Release’s specialties (resistance during Tenacious, stamina on cast, 20% HP on cast), which make no sense on an area attack. Most likely a database artefact rather than a real shared-specialty mechanic. Unverified. Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Attack Readiness (공격 준비)

Section titled “Attack Readiness (공격 준비)”

Text: your PvE damage amplification goes up 5.5%, your PvP 2.75%, your defence 2% and your accuracy by 100. At rank 20, +15% PvE, +7.5% PvP, +40% defence and +100 accuracy. What it is for: first passive in the queue in every levelling guide (KR, 2025-12 to 2026-03). Look at the defence scaling: 2% turns into 40%, by far the biggest jump of any number in the kit.

Blessing of Life (생명의 축복)

Section titled “Blessing of Life (생명의 축복)”

Text: your max HP goes up 6.5% and your healing amplification 5%. At rank 20, 35% and 24%. What it is for: health and healing in the same line, and that healing amplification applies to what you heal, so on a Chanter that actually heals this passive pays twice what it pays on a DPS.

Impact Hit (충격 적중)

Section titled “Impact Hit (충격 적중)”

Text: your impact-type accuracy goes up 11.2% and your Smite 0.3%. At rank 20, 34% and 6%. What it is for: impact-type accuracy is what makes your stuns land, and your stuns are what light up Dark Crush. Stigmas

Section titled “Stigmas”

Stigmas run on a different scale: their specialties come at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, not 8/12/16, and they rank up with Stigma Shards, not skill points. The cost per level is 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 shards across the 1-5 / 6-10 / 11-15 / 16-20 bands, roughly 75 per stigma to reach 20 (Global, LST 2026-09-17/19).

That hurts Chanter more than anyone because two of its slots are permanent toggles (Undefeated Mantra and Sprint Mantra), so the staggered unlock delays the flexible slots, not the fixed ones (Global, 2026-09-19). No source in our corpus gives a 4-stigma set built for Global: every build below is a 5 or 6 slot KR/TW build, and trimming one to 4 is inference, not data.

(a) The chains the database defines

Section titled “(a) The chains the database defines”

There are only three chain routes with entries of their own in the whole kit, and they cover seven skills. Everything else the guides call a “combo” is a condition or a macro priority, not a chain.

Trigger Link 1 Link 2 Activation
Incredible Blow (격파쇄) Resonance Strike (공명쇄) Thunderbolt Strike (벽력쇄), plus a 4th link (폭풍쇄) with the level-16 specialty 100%
Incandescent Blow (백열격) Rupture Strike (파열격) 100%
Shock Release (충격 해제) Forceful Strike (강격쇄) 100%, conditional on Shock Release having fired

Four readings that come out of this and out of no guide:

  • The left-click chain is your mana economy, not a damage trick: 100 + 100 + 120 mana per full sequence. Cutting it in half to press something else costs you 120 mana you will miss later, and mana is Chanter’s real wall before gear.
  • The Incredible Blow chain can have four links, not three. The fourth (폭풍쇄) exists only if you take the level-16 specialty, and the database gives it no entry of its own.
  • There are four more links hidden inside the level-12 and level-16 specialties (파산격 on Impact Strike, 관통쇄 on Dark Crush, 격동쇄 on Vibration Strike, 폭풍쇄 on Incredible Blow). No guide names any of them. If your build takes those specialties, your bar is going to sprout buttons no guide has ever explained to you.
  • Forceful Strike is the only chain that fires off a defensive. Do not put it in the macro expecting it to come out on its own: it exists only in the window after breaking a control effect.

One difference between the database and the dossier worth knowing: the dossier describes Resonance Strike and Thunderbolt Strike as two alternative branches off Incredible Blow, while the dump numbers them positions 1 and 2, that is, a sequence, exactly like the Templar’s three-link chain. We go with the database and leave the note here.

And a warning from the macro mechanic itself: only links with activation close to 100% are worth putting in a macro. A 15% link on a Taiwanese Cleric more than halved its dummy DPS (TW, 2026-03-18). All three Chanter chains are 100%, so there is no trap here.

(b) The Dark Crush engine

Section titled “(b) The Dark Crush engine”

This is the mechanic that defines the class, and it is a state, not a rotation. Every guide sums it up the same way: never let it go dark.

What the database says: Dark Crush only hits stunned, knocked-down or air-bound targets. Against a control-immune target, meaning any boss, it activates with a 7% chance per hit. Marcutan’s Wrath activates Dark Crush on the target for 7 s when it lands. Those are the only two numbers the database publishes about the window, and the second is the one the whole rotation rests on.

What opens the window, per the dump:

Skill How it opens it Reliability
Impact Strike (타격쇄) 3 s stun 60%, 100% against NPCs
Vibration Strike (진동쇄) 3 s stun 50%, 100% against NPCs
Forceful Strike (강격쇄) 3 s area stun 100%, but only after breaking a control effect
Wave Blow (파동격) 3 s knockdown 100% on an already stunned target
Extinguishing Flame (멸화) knockdown 50%, 100% against NPCs
Assault Shock (강습 충격) 3 s area stun 30%, 100% against NPCs
Marcutan’s Wrath (마르쿠탄의 분노) activates Dark Crush directly, 7 s 100% on hit
Spin Strike (회전격) counts as a ranged normal skill after the rework fourth trigger, KR 2026-09-02

The uptime figures do not agree and nobody has reconciled them:

Source Region and date Dark Crush uptime
Korean guide before the tuning pass KR, 2026-08-31 6 s without Marcutan, 7 s with it
The same guide, one week later KR, 2026-09-02 6 s baseline, 15 s chaining Marcutan
Inven database, Marcutan’s Wrath rank 1 KR, 2026-09-22 7 s of activation per hit

Those 7 s in the database match the August reading and not the September one. Between the two guides, from the same creator, seven days apart, the mechanic changed. It is the cleanest example of why in this class you never copy a build older than about three weeks.

The Marcutan’s Wrath usage rule, which is what almost everyone gets wrong: you press it when the state is down, not when it comes off cooldown and not from the top of a macro (KR, 2026-08-20). Firing it while the window is already open wastes both.

The free proc: that 7% per hit against control-immune targets lasts 3 s (KR, 2026-08-20). It is not a rotation, it is a floor: even with no manufactured windows, the macro running lights Dark Crush up on its own now and then.

His own live test shows Dark Crush’s cooldown opening a damage and mana gap. It is an acknowledged, unresolved flaw.

The newest Korean doctrine goes the other way (KR, 2026-09-17): put fewer skills in the macro. Every extra slot reduces how often the others fire and lowers total damage. Macro delays: 40-50 ms above 80-100 ms ping, 10 ms below 50 ms ping (TW, 2026-09-14). Tune it to your real ping.

Two keyboard tricks worth more than anything else on this list:

  • Hold the heal key down through the damage macro instead of tapping it, so it fires in the gaps between auto-attacks (KR, 2026-06).
  • Put conditional skills above their unconditional counterparts in the macro (Scorch Strike above the normal strike, Vibration Strike above the normal dash), so the right one fires on its own (KR, 2025-11-20).

Boss-fight discipline (KR, 2026-08-20): pre-stack Spin Strike’s stacks on trash on the way to the boss; block and just-dodge only against attacks that would hard-CC you, and eat everything else with Shock Release without stopping your attacks.

Levelling order, short version (KR, 2025-11-20; TW, 2026-09-14): basic chain, Spin Strike whenever it is up, and Recuperation to 12 before anything else for its continuous-healing window. Single target: stun, knockdown, Dark Crush. Skip the PvP-only skills while you level, take the mana specialties over the damage ones and dump the two points that usually end up spare into PvP damage tolerance.

(d) PvP combos and openers

Section titled “(d) PvP combos and openers”

The Korean PvP build (KR, 2026-06), the most detailed source that exists on the subject. Level 20: Incandescent Blow, Impact Strike, Dark Crush, Scorch Strike, Spin Strike. Level 16 in the accuracy variant: Rupture Strike, Recuperation with a single specialty, Vibration Strike for the stun rate. Shock Release all the way to 20 with specialties 1/4/5, which gives a roughly 30 s cycle of 7 s Tenacious.

The signature combo: you fly, dive at the target, fold your wings mid-fall and land Impact Strike on an opponent who is still airborne. You stun them before they can react, and that stun is your full Dark Crush window.

The hard matchups are Gladiator and Templar, and there is one answer: Scorch Strike’s seal specialty at rank 20, around a 60% proc, is what buys you casting time against them.

PvP stigmas: Undefeated Mantra, Blocking Power and Marcutan’s Wrath fixed; the fourth slot is Focused Defense on the low floors, Sprint Mantra on the mid ones and Barrier Spell for players comfortable with its timing window.

In Artifact sieges: swap Dark Crush’s specialty from guaranteed crit to area, so hits on the Artifact splash to 3 nearby enemies and score more. In high-population fights use the in-game macro at 10 ms rather than a mouse macro, to cut client lag.

Two things you do not do in PvP: you never take Gale’s Charge, per that same source; and you cleanse an enemy Binding Brand on sight, because since 2026-07-01 it connects with certainty.

Where Chanter sits in PvP, with the numbers in hand and disagreeing: 67.9% win rate in 1v1 arena, A-tier tied with Sorcerer (KR/TW, 2026-07-22), against a 9.74 KDA and B-tier in the Abyss, because you are melee in a mass fight. This September round pushes that reading down: C-tier for solo PvP on leaderboard representation (region unknown, 2026-09-19), A-tier arena behind a single S-tier Gladiator (TW, 2026-09-22), third in 1v1 behind Gladiator and Assassin (TW, 2026-09-21). The Spin Strike rework softens the melee-in-a-zerg problem, since you now keep ranged options.

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Block and just-dodge. They are the two biggest avoidable damage losses in the class and their whole value is in the instant you press them. Explicit rule from the Korean optimisation guide (KR, 2026-08-20).
  • Marcutan’s Wrath. Not because of its cost, but because its usage rule is the opposite of what a macro does: it fires when the Dark Crush state is down, and a macro cannot know when that is.
  • Power of the Storm. It goes out before the pull so its cooldown reduction front-loads onto the party’s openers, and it is refreshed at 16-17 s. Putting it in the macro wastes it twice.
  • Heals and party shields (Recuperation, Hand of Grace, Blocking Power, Barrier Spell). Barrier Spell in particular has a 3 s window: either you press it on the mechanic or it did nothing at all.
  • Movement skills and defensives, which the game’s own macro system excludes by design anyway.
  • Passives, which simply cannot go in.
  • Too many skills. This is the new lesson of this round and it runs against what everyone was doing until August: every extra slot reduces how often the others fire and lowers total damage (KR, 2026-09-17).

What is still unresolved

Two naming clashes we deliberately do not close here.

질풍남무 against 질풍 난무. The baseline synthesis cites a skill called 질풍남무 (Gale’s Charge, also Gust Rampage) whose function is to cut Power of the Storm’s cooldown, and rates it the most valuable level-16 investment in the class, worth taking even at a personal damage loss (KR, 2026-06). The only similarly spelled skill in the 2026-09-22 dump is 질풍 난무 (Windrage Fury), described as a finisher against groggy targets, not a cooldown-reduction skill. They may be one skill under two transcriptions or they may be two different skills. One detail complicates the decision rather than settling it: Windrage Fury’s level-16 specialty does cut all your cooldowns by 1 s per hit, which would include Power of the Storm’s. That is not the same claim as “shortens Power of the Storm’s cooldown”, and nobody has confirmed the equivalence. We have not merged them. If the Global client shows one button, the question dies; if it shows two, this page has already marked the gap.

강격쇄 against “Impactful Crash”. Two English sources describe a skill called Impactful Crash whose cooldown went from 10 s to 50 s in the 1-2 September 2026 patch and which was used as a cheap Dark Crush trigger. No skill in the current dump carries that cooldown line. 강격쇄 (Forceful Strike), the 2-minute Shock Release chain, is the closest candidate per the dossier, but it is not a confirmed match and its numbers look nothing alike. To round the confusion off, a Global guide uses a third near-identical name, Impactful Crush, for 타격쇄 (Impact Strike), which has a 15 s cooldown and does not fit the 10 to 50 either. That is three similar names and at most two buttons. We are not resolving it on our own authority.

Spin Strike’s range. The dump says 4 m around a point 2 m in front of you. Every source since 2026-09-01 says 20 m ranged, and two creators verified it on their own characters in the Global build (Global, 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-19). The 250 mana cost and the 30 s cooldown in the dump are already post-rework, so the entry is half-updated. It is the highest-priority day-one check.

The English names. Only 16 of the 39 skills carry a name_en in Inven. The rest are community names reconstructed from auto-captions or our own translations, and several are contested: Charge Strike against Rushing Smash, Scorch Strike against Heat Wave Blow, Wave Blow against Wave Strike, Spin Strike against Spinning Strike, Power of the Storm against Gale’s Power. NCsoft has not published the Global localisation.

Which 4 stigmas for Global Season 1. Global gives you 4 slots (22, 27, 32 and 37) against Korea and Taiwan’s 5 or 6, and no source in the corpus gives a 4-stigma set built for Global, nor says which one goes in which unlock slot. Any trim of the Korean 5 or 6 slot builds is inference. The only unanimous point is Undefeated Mantra to max, first.

Marcutan’s Wrath is no longer mandatory, but that finding is KR endgame. The 2026-09-17 source frees the slot for mechanic-skip utility in Sanctuary-tier content, which Global does not have at launch. For Season 1, Marcutan stays in the rotating slot.

Barrier Spell’s HP floor: 10% or 20%. The dump says 10%; the synthesis and the creators covering the 2026-03-25 patch say 20%. Nobody has reconciled the two.

The dump-against-guides mismatches, in short: Blessing of Guardian still shows 5 minutes when the 2026-09-16 patch cut it to 2; Charge Strike’s second specialty says +200% damage in the database and +20% attack, up to +60% charged, in the Korean guides; Forceful Strike displays Shock Release’s specialties, which make no sense on an area attack and are almost certainly a database artefact; Extinguishing Flame’s rank row is malformed; and Resonance Strike’s rank-20 text carries an unfilled placeholder where the damage number should be.

The attack-speed cap. Three incompatible data points: 122% (KR, 2026-09-02), a Korean forum thread titled 116.7 with no units stated (KR, 2026-09-22), and a patch “122.6” that broke an attack-speed-piercing effect (KR, 2026-09-17). This page publishes no cap figure at all, because it needs a second controlled source.

The 난무 캔슬 technique is explained by nobody. The one source that names it defers the explanation to another video. We know it exists and what it is for; we do not know how it is executed.

The weapon. The dump says 법봉 (staff) on all 39 skills. One playtest source describes Cleric and Chanter craftable weapons as “books”, which clashes both with the dump and with the spellbook being the Sorcerer’s weapon. Single source and very likely a conflation, but it is noted.
