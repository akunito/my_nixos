Templar in PvP

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. NCsoft has stated class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so the kit here should transfer, but Global Season 1 ships a smaller frame around it (4 stigmas, Stigma +20, no Heroic gear) and the tier data below predates the 2026-09-16 patch.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 39 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Templar led the PvP numbers in July 2026; by late September, Gladiator has taken the top of the 1v1. The July figures (1v1 arena 70.8%, highest of 8; Abyss KDA 11.5; co-op arena 41%) were measured before the August Judgment rework and two further nerf passes. September sources put Gladiator alone in S for 1v1 with Templar in A, and a leaderboard-representation ranking puts Templar last for solo PvP: strong in group fights, short of the finishing pressure to close a duel. Nobody has published a win rate since 2026-08-26.
  • But it is also the class NCsoft has churned hardest, and the churn is live. On 2026-08-26 Judgment (심판), roughly two thirds of the damage, went from always-available to a proc gated behind shield skills, and a PvP coefficient dropped about 21 points. Templar was patched again on 09-01, on 09-04 and again on 09-16, when NCsoft added a cancel delay to Continuous Flurry (also translated Continuous Onslaught), Punishing Strike (Retribution Strike) and Judgment specifically to stop players macro-cancelling into Judgment, cut Noble Armor’s duration and recast from 5 minutes to 2, and made Taunt’s threat scale with damage instead of a flat value. Every damage buff in that patch was PvE-only. Korean players are calling the cumulative result a net damage loss against the pre-August baseline, and the Inven boards spent the week arguing about it. Any video older than late September is stale.
  • Global gets Korea’s balance in a smaller frame. NCsoft stated that class balance and quality-of-life stay identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, and that future balance patches land on both at once, so the kit below transfers. What does not transfer is the scaffolding: Season 1 ships 4 stigma slots (the first with the level-22 Ascension quest, then 27, 32 and 37) instead of Korea’s 5, a +20 stigma enchant cap instead of +25, no Heroic gear, PvP gear capped at the silver-medal tier, the PvE stat board absent from the test build and the PvP Daevanion node paying 1.5% damage instead of 2.5%. Eight classes, no Brawler.
  • This is a legion game, not a solo game. “Join a legion before anything else or you’re going to get completely diddled in the Abyss” is the most repeated instruction from Templar mains. That fits a legion-PvP goal, but it means picking the legion before launch day.
  • Playtime is not the constraint; Abyss time is. F2P gets 7 hours of Abyss per week at Global launch on a single map; KR membership doubles it to 14 (unconfirmed for Global). The bottleneck is Abyss Point income, not playtime.
  • Two skills decide every fight: throwing Capture (포획) and Shield Smite (방패 강타) together (the longer stun overwrites the shorter one instead of being wasted), and popping your defensive stigma at the exact instant the enemy uses their CC break.
  • Low interface load, high reaction load: “flow state rather than piano class”. The macro matters (500-700k per rotation from animation cancelling alone) and ping matters more than which macro you choose.
  • The downside: Templar’s PvE value is contested in Korea (manual perfect block, “terrible” aggro, documented rerolls). Templar’s case is PvP-first. If you want a guaranteed PvE slot too, that argues for Cleric.
  • A KR Templar PvP main spending ~$30/month reports top 10% for his class and top 20% overall: competitive without whaling, but not top 5%.

Mechanics that decide fights

Section titled “Mechanics that decide fights”

Stun overwrite. Capture’s stun is 3 s; Shield Smite’s specced stun is 4 s. Landing Shield Smite while Capture’s stun is running resets the clock to Shield Smite’s 4 s rather than adding. So they are thrown together, never in sequence.

Judgment is conditional damage. It only hits stunned or knocked-down targets (7% vs immune), 5 s cooldown, 4 m. Your whole damage profile depends on landing control first.

Defensive stigma timing. Once the opener is spent, pop Double Armor (이중 갑옷) at the exact moment the enemy triggers their CC break: not earlier (a Silence into burst punishes an early defensive) and not later (you die reacting). Layer Warding Strike (비호의 일격) on top for a combined ~50% reduction through that ~5 s window.

Anti-heal is your structural job in group PvP. Shield Smite’s Debilitating Smash proc (50%) applies -16% incoming healing, on top of -25% defense. Every class in Aion 2 self-sustains; this is not optional.

Animation cancelling is worth 10-12% of your damage. Full rotation with cancelling ~5.5M, without 4.9M. In a ~4 s PvP window: 2.5M with the Judgment chain vs 1.6M without. Above ~200 ms consistent cancels get very hard; a KR macro comparison found the variants within measurement noise (6.1 vs 6.1M) and concluded region, PC and latency cause more variance than macro type. Those measurements are from 2026-09-13 and the 09-16 patch went after them directly: Korea increased the cancel delay on Continuous Flurry, Punishing Strike and Judgment, and the patch note says the change targeted macro-cancelling into Judgment. Korean players report a measurable PvP damage and lifesteal loss from the combo it removed. Nobody has re-measured the 10-12% since, and nobody could test it on Global (the playtest capped at level 37).

PvP economy. Abyss Points (AP) are the PvP currency and also the source of Stigma Shards, so PvE players need the Abyss and PvP players need PvE: “Aion 2 is a PvX game”. AP is earnable without fighting players: daily supply quest ~10,000, weekly ~30,000, daily missions ~2,500, Abyss mobs 10-20 per kill, arena 50-100. The Abyss dailies “should never be skipped”. Templar farms AP unusually well because its gap closer resets on kill and chains pack to pack through the Abyss Corridors: 50-55k AP per corridor run, ~1M AP in two hours on a geared TW character. Global will be far slower: AP also scales with killing players and nobody is overgeared at launch. Corridor access depends on your faction winning the artifact siege: another reason the legion is the highest-leverage decision.

This whole ladder is Korea’s, and Global Season 1 does not have all of it. PvP gear at Global launch is reported capped at the silver-medal tier (roughly item level 77, Global), which is the Centurion rung, so the Tribunus and High Commander sets, and with them the 5 Centurion + 8 Tribunus mixing trick, are out of reach in Season 1. Heroic gear is absent at launch by design, to keep the early whale gap small. On the other hand, crafted chest and helm now roll weapon damage tolerance, a PvP mitigation stat, which makes armour crafting close to mandatory for a Global PvP Templar.

Builds

Section titled “Builds”

KR/TW builds, Global constraints

Where two sources disagree on a name, both are given. Global ships 4 stigma slots, the first with the level-22 Ascension quest and then at levels 27, 32 and 37, with stigma enchant capped at +20 (Korea has 5 slots and +25), so any Korean five-stigma setup below needs one pick cut, and the level-20 numbers quoted are the ceiling, not the starting point.

Abyss and open world (the main mode), KR baseline January 2026

Section titled “Abyss and open world (the main mode), KR baseline January 2026”

Stigmas:

  1. Sovereign’s Punishment (주신의 징벌): universal; at level 20, 75% stun at 20 m (50% base + 25%), +50 groggy and a 10 s / +10% damage proc. Below 20, take Grapple (나포) instead: it already matches 50% at level 5 and roots.
  2. Executioner’s Sword (처형의 검) for the Abyss: level 5 drops it from 45 s to 30 s; the spec adds up to +30% then a further +50% vs groggy targets, plus a guaranteed crit. It exists because Templar has no fast native burst.
  3. Double Armor (이중 갑옷): 30% base + 20% at level 20 = 50% PvP tolerance.
  4. Flex: Noble Armor (+3,800 max HP, persists through death; a +15% max-HP scroll multiplies after the flat bonus, taking ~38k past 40k). Since 2026-09-16 its duration and recast are both 2 minutes, not 5, so it is a cooldown you spend before a fight rather than a standing HP pad. Or a Shield of Nezekan variant absorbing 9,000 with +20% tolerance until it breaks / Armor of Balance against CC-heavy classes (Templar, Gladiator, Assassin, Chanter).

Specializations: Capture to at least 16 for its 50% stun node (mandatory); Shield Charge to 20 for +10 m range, 75% stun and a cooldown toward ~10 s, your only real second gap closer; Shield Smite takes +1 s stun and the Debilitating-Smash-enabling node, and deliberately skips the cast-time reduction (it roots you on cast; a mobile opponent walks out of 4 m).

Opener: Capture + Shield Smite together (guarantees the 4 s) → Executioner’s Sword before charging Punishment (the three-stage charge is slow enough that the target CC-breaks first) → Punishment → survive their break with Double Armor timed on it plus Warding Strike → re-engage on Sovereign’s Punishment → Judgment ×3-4 → Annihilation (knockdown) → Judgment to finish.

Structural weakness per the same source: Templar has no guaranteed hard-lockdown combo and only one real gap closer beyond Capture’s pull. Capture’s 20 m cast is dodgeable by an alert dash; after that you have Shield Charge and nothing else.

PvX “Berserk Templar” (Taiwan, September 2026)

Section titled “PvX “Berserk Templar” (Taiwan, September 2026)”
  • One hybrid set, not two. PvE weapon, PvP offhand and a few PvP accessories; runes are the only difference between loadouts. Two full sets is a progression trap for a non-whale. A separate test found PvP gear is often competitive in PvE once stacked damage boosts exceed ~100%, because a 15-40% single-source difference nets only 5-10% real damage.
  • Stats: Precision and Might above everything. Perfect Chance “insanely good”, second only to Smite. Block Penetration mandatory against Templars and Clerics: “without it you’re kind of just getting cooked”. Front Attack for PvE, Back Attack for PvP positioning.
  • PvX stigmas: Imperion Lord (top: ranged stun, 200% damage vs incapacitated or immune targets), Shield of Nezekan (elemental defense vs casters), Protective Shield, Doom Shield (resets Annihilate on kill), Executing Blade (locks the opponent’s skills on cooldown and applies Exposed). Battlefield Banner deprioritised for its 30 s cooldown; Taunt, Shield of Protection and Grapple out.
  • PvP rotation (open world and Rift, not arena): Poach pull → Shield Smite → move to the target’s back → Judgment and Pummel macro weave → Doom Shield if up → Punishment for burst → Executing Blade on low-HP targets → Imperion Lord or Shield Smite to punish disengages.
  • Numbers (TW, heavily geared, pre-squish): Punishment ~100k full charge plus ~56k DoT on ~30 s; Annihilate ~24k base, 100% knockdown in PvE, only 50% in PvP; Pummel’s level-20 Punching Strike ~13k. Only the priority logic transfers.

Macros (Taiwan, 2026-09-13)

Section titled “Macros (Taiwan, 2026-09-13)”

These macros predate the 09-16 anti-macro cancel delay described above by three days. The priority logic still holds; the timings below do not, and the Judgment chain in particular needs re-measuring on a post-09-16 client.

  • PvP and arena macro: Executing Blade and Doom Shield manual for control; the Pummel / Annihilate / Debilitating Smash chain automated; tight manual left/right-click cancelling.
  • PvX farming macro (best for Abyss corridors): Doom Shield → Annihilate (knockdown only) → Debilitating Smash (proc only) → Pummel (filler). Dashing in and out of mobs resets Doom Shield and Poach.
  • Judgment chain: a mouse-side macro firing Warding Strike cancelled into Judgment, alternating with the in-game right-click macro. Shield Smite 2 s + Warding Strike 2 s + Doom Shield +3 s ≈ 4+ s of continuous Judgment per cycle.
  • In-game delay 30 ms default; mouse macro ~10 presses per second. Tune to real ping.
  • Do not pre-buff before engaging: the cast telegraphs the fight. Buff mid-combo.

PvP settings and consumables (all classes)

Section titled “PvP settings and consumables (all classes)”
  • Turn off “attack-time main target lock” so skills follow the reticle instead of a stale target in a crowd. Called the single most important PvP setting.
  • Target search camera-forward, and in the detail, hostile players above monsters, so Tab finds the enemy player and not one of a hundred mobs.
  • A dedicated “nearest hostile player” key separate from the cycle key (Tab hostiles, Caps Lock cycle).
  • “Track target on skill use” on so you follow a moving target; “auto-target on skill use” on for large-scale Abyss, off for solo PK.
  • No in-combat camera shake; filter monsters off the minimap so only enemy players show.
  • Consumables: cheap drinks (Mela Juice, Coriolus Tea, ~1,400) for action-power regen, which feeds the Shift dodge and its brief invulnerability; the expensive tea is not worth it. Shock Resistance Scroll on F5 (proactively against Gladiator, Assassin and Templar burst) and Shock Mitigation Scroll on F6 (reactive).
  • Wing-fold escape: while flying, folding your wings drops you into free fall and incoming CC only lasts the fall. Learn each zone’s fall-death altitude first: some Abyss zones kill at ~160 m, others are safe.
  • Keep the Barrel Amulet (+15% PvP damage) equipped in the Abyss.

How it plays

Section titled “How it plays”

Templar’s PvP kit is a conditional damage engine bolted to a very large health bar. Almost nothing in the damage half works on a free-standing target: Judgment and Annihilation only hit stunned or knocked-down enemies, and Executioner’s Sword pays its +20% only against a target already in an impact state. A Templar fight is not a rotation, it is a lock: land control, cash in, lock again.

The second half is the part that wins fights, and it is defensive. Once the opener is spent and the enemy has burned their CC break, the Templar has no offensive cooldowns for about five seconds and simply has to not die. The class has an unusual amount of stacked mitigation for that: Double Armor +15% PvP tolerance, Warding Strike +10%, Pain Block +5.5% passively on being hit, Will to Survive adding more the instant you are controlled, and the automatic seal below 50%.

In group PvP the real job is neither damage nor tanking: it is debuff application and positional theft. Debilitating Smash cuts incoming healing by 16% and defense by 25% in a game where everyone self-sustains; Fury procs on your block and hands the whole party +5.5% PvP damage; and Capture pulls an overextended enemy off their line into yours.

What has changed, and what makes Global uncertain, is the input side. Before 2026-08-26 Judgment was simply available and carried ~65% of the damage; you spammed it. After the rework it is a proc window bought by cycling Shield Smite, Warding Strike, Shield Charge and Shield of Ruin, and keeping it up means tracking four cooldowns instead of one button. NCsoft framed the accompanying minimum cancel time as a measure to close the gap between fast- and slow-input players.

The honest counterweight is that Templar’s excellent PvP standing sits on a contested PvE role. See Templar in PvE.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Throwing Shield Smite alone, or after Capture instead of with it.
  • Using Shield Smite as a standalone opener: it roots you and a mobile opponent walks out of 4 m.
  • Charging Punishment inside a stun window: the target CC-breaks before it lands; spend Executioner’s Sword first.
  • Popping the defensive stigma early: getting Silenced with Double Armor spent is the standard way a Templar dies.
  • Treating any pre-September-2026 Templar guide as current.
  • Spamming Judgment on a macro without cycling the four shield triggers.
  • Maxing macro input rate on high ping.
  • Building Block instead of Iron Wall, or either on the strength of the January 2026 analysis: weightings changed on 03-25 and Perfect Block arrived.
  • Skipping Block Penetration against Templars and Clerics.
  • Maintaining two full sets as a non-whale.
  • Buying a full single PvP set instead of mixing (5 Centurion + 8 Tribunus).
  • Dissolving Abyss gear for the 80% refund.
  • Wandering near enemy guard NPCs in the Rift while flagged PvE.
  • Entering Upper Rashanta over the cap (if it reaches Global).
  • Going into the Abyss without a legion.
  • Expecting Templar PvP to have been testable in the September test: cap 37, Abyss at 45, Rift and Battlefield disabled. The first real look is Advanced Access from 09-30.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

What Judgment actually looks like at Global launch: gated or not, and for how many seconds per trigger (three sources say 3 s, 7 s and 2/2/3 s). The real size of the PvP coefficient cut; no post-08-26 win-rate data exists. Whether Punishment is ranged at Global (one recap says yes; the database still shows 5 m). Templar’s siege role, undocumented in English. No PvP matchup notes exist in English (Templar vs Assassin, Sorcerer, Cleric, Gladiator); the best indirect evidence: Assassin wins 70.5% of 1v1s, Gladiator and Assassin are the burst threats you pre-pop the resistance scroll against, and Doom Shield bypasses evasion and block outright, including the Assassin’s evasion stance. Arena format at Global. Whether Global membership doubles Abyss time. Which Abyss maps ship. Official English names (Second Skin = 이중 갑옷 is inferred). Whether Perfect Block triggers on rear hits. Whether Taunt against players (50%, 3 s) survived the 09-16 rework untouched, and whether it enables perma-stunning an enemy healer as one reviewer claims: unverified and, if real, siege-relevant. New this round: whether the 09-16 cancel delay costs the 10-12% animation-cancel gain outright or only part of it, since nobody has re-measured. Whether Korea’s rolling Templar nerfs since 08-26 really leave the class below its pre-August damage, as the Inven boards argue, or whether that is sentiment. Whether the reworked, damage-proportional Taunt still holds threat for an undergeared tank. And whether the “Templar bug” that one creator blames for the Global-balance rumour is real: he never demonstrates it.
