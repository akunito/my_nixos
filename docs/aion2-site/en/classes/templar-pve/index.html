Templar in PvE

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. NC has stated that class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, with balance patches applied to all of them at once, so the Korean kit on this page is the kit you get; what differs is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, Stigma 20, no Heroic gear), flagged where it applies. English skill names are working translations, not the official localisation.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 39 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Templar (수호성) is the only true tank. There is no competition for the seat and no gatekeeping, from hour one to endgame.
  • It is the third-easiest class to press buttons on (rotation 3/10, survival 2/10) with the second-highest group responsibility (8/10): positioning, aggro and defensive timing, which only really bite in Sanctuary raids. Low execution stress, high decision weight.
  • Your party value is a passive buff you generate by getting hit: Fury (격앙) gives you and the whole party +11% PvE damage for 10 s every time you block. Dodging normal attacks is the classic beginner mistake: it switches off your own buff.
  • Personal damage: ~88% of the balance baseline (7th of 9) in Taiwanese endgame data measured before the rework below, and on the Global Launch Scale Test, with all eight classes at level 37 on equalised gear and cast by hand, Templar came second of eight at 432,000 damage over one minute, behind only Chanter. That is one creator’s level-37 bracket test, not an endgame ranking. Not a joke number at either end. The damage-specced “Berserk” build is real, played, and drops Taunt.
  • Low ping dependence and low build dependence: it works from day one without a finished set, unlike Gladiator.
  • The knock: the Korean community has argued for a year that the timed manual block (Shift) lets any class tank mechanics for zero damage, and that Templar aggro is “still terrible” after three patches aimed at it. It is the main reason a Templar main regrets the pick.
  • Against that: second-most represented class on KR and TW PvE leaderboards (105 appearances of 800, A-tier) and present in most 10-player Sanctuary comps.
  • The build is mid-rework, and Global inherits the rework. NC has confirmed that class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, with patches applied to all regions at once, so Korea’s August and September changes are your launch kit, not a preview. August turned Judgment (심판), about 65% of the damage, from always-available into a 3 s window behind four shield skills and cut a coefficient; the 2026-09-16 patch added +10% PvE damage to Warding Strike, Annihilate and Punishment and +20% to Shield Smite, but also increased the cancel delay on Continuous Flurry, Punishing Strike and Judgment, made Taunt’s threat proportional to your damage, and halved Noble Armor. Korean players have spent a month calling the net result a nerf.

How it actually works

Section titled “How it actually works”

The loop is a conditional gate you keep propped open. Judgment legally only exists against a stunned or knocked-down target; against a boss, which is control-immune, it fires on a 7% chance per hit. That alone is unplayable, so the class is built around manufacturing Judgment windows: shield skills open them and you chain them to keep it open. After the August patch the chain is Shield Smite (2 s) → Warding Strike (2 s) → Doom Shield (+3 s reset); the trigger set is Shield Smite, Warding Strike, Shield Charge and Shield of Ruin. A one-minute KR test counts over 230 Judgment casts.

Defense is offense. Three of your most valuable passives only fire on a successful block: Fury (+11% damage for the whole party), Roar of Insult (threat spike) and Shield of Protection (heal). Weakening Strike, the -25% enemy defense that is your single largest party contribution, also applies on block (Shield Smite gives it a proc route so you are not hostage to RNG). That is why you tank by taking hits, not avoiding them.

Punishment is the biggest single hit and opens every PvE rotation: a three-stage charge, 30 s, and the Executor buff (+200 accuracy, +10% damage). It cannot be macroed: the macro only fires stage one.

Aggro is the ugly seam, and the 2026-09-16 patch made it a gear check. Taunt used to grant a flat 20,000 threat; it now grants threat in proportion to your damage. That is a buff for a geared Templar and a real problem for an undergeared one, and it turns the old “damage carries aggro” workaround into the actual design. Roar of Insult still doubles your threat amplification, but the Korean community has called Templar threat “terrible” through three patches now. Threat is fine while you are hitting and collapses when you stop to block or heal: the difference is that recovering with Taunt now costs you the same gear you did not have. Taunt is not your aggro engine; it is used as a diagnostic to test whether a boss pattern is scripted or aggro-driven, and the TW damage build drops it.

Animation cancelling is mandatory, ping-gated, and being actively nerfed. Taiwanese dummy tests from 13 September: ~5.5M per rotation with cancelling vs 4.9M without, a 500-700k gap. Treat that as a historical figure: three days later the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 increased the cancel delay on Continuous Flurry, Punishing Strike and Judgment, and the notes say the change was aimed squarely at players macro-cancelling into Judgment. Korean players read it as the end of the Flurry-into-Judgment rotation and calculate current Templar damage as lower than before the August rework. No one has published a post-patch measurement. At ~200 ms the Judgment / Continuous Flurry cancel macro is “pretty much useless” and pressing Judgment alone is better. KR creators say bluntly that region, PC and ISP cause more DPS variance than macro choice. A correct cancel leaves a visible after-image of the character.

Accuracy is a hard gate. A blocked hit deals half damage, and every 10 points of enemy block over your accuracy is 1% block chance. A Templar needs it on top for taunts to land: KR groups wiped on the Uruguru boss from lost aggro. One KR raid tier quoted 2,350 accuracy.

Builds

Section titled “Builds”

Global Season 1

Four equipped stigmas and a Stigma soft cap of 20 (6 and 25 later). No Heroic rarity: Unique is best-in-slot. Every 5-stigma KR build has to be cut to 4. Coefficients and damage figures on this page come from boosted KR/TW characters; transfer the priority logic, not the numbers.

Leveling (1 → cap)

Section titled “Leveling (1 → cap)”
  • The rule that matters most: bring every active skill to level 8 first. The first specialization unlocks at 8, the second at 12, and per-level cost rises. Then push your core skills to 16 for the third specialty slot. Do not forget to slot the specialization after unlocking it: it is a separate UI step.
  • Mana is the real early bottleneck (~2,700-3,000 naked). Fierce Strike restores 100 MP per cast. On Shield Smash, do not take the MP-cost-increase specialization: it turns a 100 MP skill into 300 (the KR client tooltip as of 2026-09-22 says the opposite, that the level-8 specialty removes the MP cost and restores 200 MP on hit; see the skills guide).
  • Leveling stigmas skew offensive; defensive shields do not pay off yet. Skip Stagger and Defiance early; skip defensive passives, early PvE is easy.
  • Passives: Fury first, then Roar of Insult, then HP and incoming healing.
  • Specialize Shield Charge into a mobility tool and Capture into a movement conversion: your only gap closers.
  • Do not rush max level: complete Traces of the Empyrean Lords and side quests; account power outvalues speed.
  • Global: main story → sealed (“?”) dungeons → nearby green side quests to reach the combat-power threshold that opens the Abyss; Expeditions only when the main quest requires them. Do not skip 1-star Conquest (Krao Cave (Crow Cave), Draupnir), not because of the gates, which the Launch Scale Test measured lower than Korea’s (700 gear score, Global), but because Krao Cave is your first weapon stop and Draupnir fills the back-armour slots: bracelet, greaves, helm, pauldrons, gloves, boots and cloak.
  • Spec for absorb while levelling, respec at 45. A Global-build guide puts the HP-absorb node on Judgment, the heal-over-time on Warding Strike and absorb on Punishment, then swaps the lot for maximum damage once you hit the cap. Two settings worth fixing on day one: set the boss orientation indicator to “front” (Templar fights from the front), and rebind Defiance off the space bar so mashing out of a stun does not summon your wings.
  • Solo you level slower but safer and wider: 7-8 mobs per pull where a Ranger handles 1-3. A KR player who capped all eight classes ranked Templar second for leveling, behind Gladiator.

Tank build (dungeons, group first)

Section titled “Tank build (dungeons, group first)”

Stigmas (pick 4 on Global): Taunt + Protective Shield as the KR core pair; Shield of Nezekan, rated “the best skill” for party survival, target level 15; Double Armor as the emergency mitigation, called required in higher dungeons; Noble Armor for the HP and healing bump, but note it is now 2 minutes up on a 2-minute cooldown, not the near-permanent buff Korean guides describe, so it is a pre-pull button and a weaker use of one of your four slots; Sovereign’s Punishment if a damage slot is free.

Right-click priority chain (order matters): Weakening Strike → Shield Smash → Annihilation → Continuous Flurry (no cooldown, the filler). Left click: Fierce Strike.

Playbook:

  1. Open by building threat with attack skills before anything else.
  2. Face the boss away from the party.
  3. Hold right click in safe windows; do not dodge normal attacks: every block is a party buff.
  4. Use Protective Shield (20 s) on floor or AoE attacks to guarantee the block and refresh Fury.
  5. Mitigation before the big hit, not after.
  6. On groggy: Weakening Strike (defense shred) → Sovereign’s Punishment → burst.
  7. Dodge only CC and one-shot mechanics; regather scattered adds with Capture. On Global you have more time than Korean footage suggests: one creator’s frame count of side-by-side footage puts the hit about 24 frames after the telegraph circle fills, against about 8 on the Korean client, roughly three times the reaction window, so do not copy a Korean player’s dodge timing.
  8. If a phase transition will reset the boss to full HP, stop damaging and bank cooldowns.

Damage build, “Berserk Templar” (Taiwan, September 2026)

Section titled “Damage build, “Berserk Templar” (Taiwan, September 2026)”

Deliberately drops Taunt, Shield of Protection and Grapple: “stopping to block loses DPS and the right-click macro holds aggro anyway unless someone is insanely gear-gapping me”.

  • Stigmas in order: Imperion Lord’s Punishment (200% vs incapacitated or control-immune targets, plus up to 20% via stun chance) → Doom Shield (resets Annihilate on kill and opens the Judgment window) → Executing Blade (Exposed, and pushes the target’s skills onto cooldown) → Battlefield Banner (attack off defense; +15% combat-speed buff duration at 25) → Protective Shield if a slot is free.
  • Passives: Impact Hit is the single biggest damage passive, level it as high as it goes; then Fury, then Roar of Insult (~15.5% frontal attack at level 20-21).
  • Skill levels: Judgment 20 (extra-damage node over crit; absorb HP), Continuous Flurry 20, Punishment (DoT + ignore shield + skill speed; never changes between PvE and PvP), Pummel 16 (its level-22 spec was patched into uselessness; at 20 it adds the ~13,000 “Punching Strike” hit), Shield Smash low priority in PvE, Defiance skippable in pure PvE. The level-20 cooldown-removal node this build banks on is a single-source claim: the KR client tooltip as of 2026-09-22 says the opposite and puts cooldown removal in the level-16 slot (see the skills guide).
  • Skip the crit-chance spec node once your crit is around 90%: redundant.
  • Daevanion: path to Punishment → Judgment → Pummel, then Warding Strike, then Annihilate.
  • Arcana: Judgment on Parchment; Pummel and Punishment on Chalice; Pummel on Compass. Flash Rampage is an early-game card only. God stats for PvE: Chalice = Time, Parchment = Life, Bell = Destruction, Mirror = Illusion early (cooldown reduction) then Wisdom later (Smite).

Macros (what decides your damage)

Section titled “Macros (what decides your damage)”

In-game macro chain, PvX and open-world farming: Doom Shield → Annihilate (knockdown only) → Debilitating Smash (proc only) → Pummel (filler). Conditionals go above the filler because the macro fires the first available skill down the chain. Dashing in and out of packs resets Doom Shield and Poach, which is what makes pack-to-pack farming work.

Dungeon chain: Annihilate → Debilitating Smash → Flash Rampage → Pummel, plus a separate macro on the Judgment key running Judgment → Shield Smite → Warding Strike → Doom Shield, looping as Shield Smite returns. Opener: Punishment first, then the Judgment spam.

Delays: the in-game default is 30 ms; TW players use ~10 ms; a creator at 35 ms ping uses 10 and recommends 40-50 ms above 80-100 ms ping. Tune to your real ping: input above your connection’s limit is wasted.

Keep out of the macro: Punishment (three-stage charge), any forward dash (you will spam it into a telegraphed one-shot), the party shield, Block and Parry, and any 20 m skill that forces awkward repositioning mid-chain.

Skill-cap cost: three active skills at 20 cost no passives; four cost 2 passives; five cost 6 (a single KR source).

Raid (Sanctuary): the role as actually played

Section titled “Raid (Sanctuary): the role as actually played”

From the September 2026 KR Snowfield of Grief hard-mode guides, the current top KR tier: the Templar opens as first tank from the 12 o’clock marker; the Gladiator is second tank and takes over after the swap. A boss slam targets the Templar alone and freezes them solid: the party must rope-pull and thaw them. You are a scripted mechanic, not a sponge. You get your own position in the wave-phase template (“1414”: Templar at 1, party 1 at 10, party 2 at 5, Gladiator at 7 o’clock). Live comms repeat “watch out for the Templar, don’t come near the Templar”: the tank baits an attack that splashes onto anyone nearby. PvE Daevanion books ran 2-3M Kinah early on TW and KR (up to 5M) and are expected pricier at Global launch. One more thing the Korean raid scene makes clear: a raid wants exactly one Templar and one Gladiator, never two of either. A Korean player who has cleared Snowfield of Grief every week since it opened says he has never once seen a party with two Templars, and the move from 8-player to 10-player sanctums shrank the effective Templar slots rather than adding one. Your seat is guaranteed; a second one is not.

Nightmare (solo), where the tank is the point

Section titled “Nightmare (solo), where the tank is the point”

Nightmare is a solo boss ladder: 2 tickets a day capped at 14, seasonal rather than weekly, so a cleared level never has to be repeated and there is no reason to rush it. Its leaderboards are per class (you are ranked against other Templars, not against Rangers) and class differences in it are small, with Templar and Gladiator performing unusually well purely on durability. The first boss, Zikel, wants around 2,400 gear score; clearing it unlocks the Zikel Colossus statue at 14,000 Nightmare points, the single highest-impact thing in the shop (+45 to named stats, including attack percentage and around 9% attack speed). Clearing the fight and buying the statue are two separate steps and people forget the second one. Do not sit at 14/14 tickets either: two more generate every day and the overflow is lost. (Global; the Zikel gear score comes from a Korean compilation.)

Stats and gear

Section titled “Stats and gear”
  • Offense first, even for the tank. “Offense is king regardless of class; the only thing that matters for PvE survival is not getting one-shot.” The meta is enrage-timer bound, so defensive stats are deliberately undervalued, and tank stats arrive free from the passives, the shield and heavy armour.
  • Smite (강타, a 2× damage proc) is the top endgame damage stat, “S+, no discussion”; Wisdom in the Pantheon feeds it. 90% unbuffed Smite converts to a real 60-70% against boss resist: push past 100%.
  • Might and Precision above everything: “if you can’t hit you do no damage; if you don’t crit you do a lot less”. Weapon rolls: weapon damage > precision > might > damage > multi-hit > combat speed. Front Attack over Back Attack for PvE: you face the boss. Armour mains: gloves combat speed, boots move speed, shoulders crit damage, chest damage amp. Precision and Constitution cap at 200: dump overflow into attack and defense.
  • Flat block as a stat: no. The ~500-point gap you need against high-accuracy enemies is not closable at high level; use the active block stigma. Iron Wall (halves damage from any angle) was stacked in PvP in January; in PvE block, evasion, regen and endurance are low priority.
  • Measured mechanics: crit caps at 80% (800 points); evasion at 50%; multi-hit is 4 extra hits at 10-15%; damage and tolerance cancel 1:1; crit damage is additive to the 1.5× base; cooldown reduction caps at 60%, most classes want 33-36%.
  • Enhancement order (any class): everything to +11 → weapon and accessories to Diamond 2 → weapon to Diamond 5 → accessories → armour to at least Diamond 2.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Dodging normal attacks: kills your Fury, threat and heal-on-block economy.
  • Unlocking a specialization and never slotting it.
  • Maxing one skill to 10 before the others reach 8.
  • Taking Shield Smash’s MP-cost-increase spec early (disputed: the KR tooltip as of 2026-09-22 says that specialty removes the cost; see the skills guide).
  • Putting Punishment or a forward dash in the macro.
  • Running the cancel macro at high ping, or putting conditionals below the filler.
  • Bursting into a phase transition that resets boss HP.
  • Under-building accuracy: missed taunts wipe groups, blocked hits deal half, and one source says low accuracy degrades the Templar’s own parry.
  • Chasing flat block as a tank stat.
  • Bringing PvP stigmas into a dungeon: with four slots on Global that is a wasted slot.
  • Copying a 5-stigma KR build into Global.
  • Spending Stigma Shards without checking the curve: levels 16-20 cost 8 shards each, four times the 11-15 band.
  • Stacking Fury with a Gladiator’s Experience Counter Strike: same buff.
  • Skipping 1-star Conquest on Global: the gear-score gate is low (700, Global), but Krao Cave and Draupnir are where your first weapon and your back-armour slots come from.
  • Farming pet-collection mobs near enemy-faction guards: auto-aggro flags you for PvP even in PvE mode.
  • Believing any numeric build from before 2026-08-26.
