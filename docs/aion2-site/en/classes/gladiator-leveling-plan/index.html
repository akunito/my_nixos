Gladiator: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Inven Korean skill database of 2026-09-22 over the KR/TW syntheses to 2026-09-16, with the Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the Overhead Slam loop and the macros live on the Gladiator class page. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free skill resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  4. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas from level 22 and leave them alone.
  5. Gladiator has very few actives worth deep investment, so its passives carry unusually high weight (KR, 2026-09-05). Do not treat them as leftovers.
  6. Any Korean or Taiwanese build you find runs 5 or 6 stigmas: cut it to 4 before you copy it.
  7. No source anywhere publishes a full point-by-point 1-45 ladder for this class. Every source stops at level these three skills to 20. What follows below that line is priority order, not a route.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: three buttons and the knockdown that feeds them

Section titled “1 to 22: three buttons and the knockdown that feeds them”
  • Skills, in this order: Rending Blow (절단의 맹타), a 0.5 s recast frontal 4-hit that is your whole filler, then Keen Strike (예리한 일격) and its chain, which is the mana engine (100 / 100 / 120 mana per hit, TW, 2026-09-09), then Ruinous Blow (파멸의 맹타, called Luminous Blow on the Global client), whose whole job is hanging Prepare for Battle on you.
  • Understand the Overhead Slam gate before you spend a point on it. Overhead Slam (내려찍기) only fires on a knocked-down target, or on a 7% chance per hit against an incapacitation-immune boss (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; confirmed Global, 2026-09-21). While levelling, the knockdown you supply yourself is Devastating Sword (유린의 검): 3 s knockdown, 20 s recast, 200 mana. That pairing is what makes Overhead Slam usable before you own the Rage Burst stigma.
  • Prepare for Battle is the metronome. Ruinous Blow grants +20% PvE damage, +10% PvP damage, +100 critical and +15% status accuracy for a base 5 s, extended by 5 s at specialisation step 12 (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). If that buff is down you are playing the class wrong; that is the one execution rule every source repeats.
  • Passives: Experienced Counter Strike (노련한 반격) first. Four of five source groups put it at the top, then Murderous Burst (살기 파열), Attack Readiness (공격 준비) and Impact Hit (충격 적중) in an order the sources shuffle (KR 2026-08-17 and 09-05, TW 2026-08-12, Global 2026-09-21). One Global source of 2026-09-21 puts Impact Hit first instead; it stands alone.
  • Allocation rule inside a band: unconditional actives first, then conditional actives, groggy-only skills last, passives after that. Respec is free, so a wrong guess costs nothing (KR, baseline).
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens around level 12-13. Spend points the day you earn them. For Gladiator the node order is Orange, then Blue, then Green, then White, following the same skill order as your points (Global, 2026-09-21; the Korean baseline says the same, orange DPS nodes before blue, green and white).
  • Gear: all disposable. Nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20). One Korean source says not to enhance at all before level 30, where soul-bind rolls are worth more (KR, baseline).
  • Gate before you move on: level 22 has a red quest that blocks the main story until the world-progress bar is at 100%, filled with regional quests and sealed dungeons. Every sealed dungeon is +2 skill and +2 Daevanion points, so the bar and your build fill together (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).

Do not:

  • Spend a point past 20 on anything. Levels only unlock specialisation slots; 20 against 24 is thrown away (KR, 2026-08-17 and 09-08).
  • Skip green side quests: each one is +1 skill point and +1 Daevanion point (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).
  • Plan around the Monolith for skill points. On Global it gives none (Global LST, 10 deltas).
  • Fight from behind. The frontal damage passive was moved to front positioning (KR/TW, baseline).

22 to 27: first stigma slot, and the one that fixes Overhead Slam

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot, and the one that fixes Overhead Slam”
  • Stigma, slot 1: Rage Burst (격노 폭발). Near-unanimous across Korean, Taiwanese and Global sources: it activates Overhead Slam for 10 s on hit, which is what turns Overhead Slam from a 7% lottery into a skill. It also puts a 10 s, 10% attack-down bruise on the target and gives you +10% PvE and +5% PvP damage. 90 s recast, 100 mana; its specialisation step 5 cuts 15 s off its own recast (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Stop it at 5 (4 shards) until you know your four. Effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20.
  • Skills: Rending Blow, Keen Strike and Ruinous Blow towards 8 (13 points each, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), then place their specialisations. Unlocking a specialisation and placing it are two separate steps in the UI.
  • The specialisation picks that matter at 8: Rending Blow, take mobile cast or -20% mana; Ruinous Blow, take +20% skill speed or +12.5 m range; Keen Strike, take the +20% mana recovery if your mana still runs out (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Gear: enhancement cap is still +5 green / +10 blue.
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: push one skill to 10 before the others are at 8; spread shards across four stigmas at once; copy a 5-stigma or 6-stigma build.

27 to 32: second slot and the throughput stigma

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot and the throughput stigma”
  • Stigma, slot 2: Lunge Stance (돌격 자세). 10 s of +20% combat speed and -20% mana cost on a 60 s recast. Its step-20 effect, a 50% chance on critical hit to cut every skill recast by 1 s, is called the single biggest throughput item the class owns (KR 2026-08-17, Global 2026-09-21, and one more Global build of 2026-09-20). Take it knowing you will be feeding it shards for a long time.
  • Skills: the core three towards 10, which is as far as points go. Overhead Slam joins them: its step 16 removes its cooldown entirely (재시전 시간 삭제, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), and every source calls that pick mandatory. You cannot reach 16 with points, so from here the board, Arcana cards and ring soul bindings are what finish the skill.
  • Overhead Slam’s step 12 is a guaranteed critical on hit. Two Korean build philosophies fork here (KR, 2026-03): take the guaranteed crit and then ignore the crit stat on gear entirely, the higher ceiling; or take the 2% lifesteal at step 8 and gear crit normally, which is more stable if you actually hold aggro.
  • Daevanion: keep following the skill order, Overhead Slam then Rending Blow then Ruinous Blow, then the top four passives (Global, 2026-09-21).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most.
  • Gate before you move on: no hard gate in this band. The next one is 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take a PvP stigma into a dungeon; with four slots that is a slot thrown away.

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar filled with exploration and side quests, then the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Stigma, slot 3: Zekiel’s Blessing (지켈의 축복). Mandatory in every Korean and Taiwanese source and still core in one Global build. Read the next line before you commit shards: the Korean database of 2026-09-22 has it as a party buff, +20% attack for 10 s to everyone within 40 m; one hands-on Global source of 2026-09-20 reports it changed to self-only, +50% stagger damage. Those are different skills doing different jobs.
  • Skills: spend the band on the second rank of utility. Sources that go deeper than the core three agree on the shape and not the list: one Global build takes two defensive skills to 16 and five utility skills to 12; another levels most of the kit to 12 and only pushes Overhead Slam to 20 at endgame (both Global, 2026-09-21 and 09-20).
  • The utility specialisations worth the points: Ankle Slice (발목 베기) step 8 +16 m range, taking it from 4 m to 20 m and turning a block-triggered poke into your second gap-closer; Leaping Slam (도약 찍기) step 8 resets on kill, which is the farming pick; Crushing Wave (분쇄 파동) step 12 resets its recast on a critical, which turns a mob pack into a self-healing loop; Shock Release (충격 해제) step 8 +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance during its 5 s immunity, the pick one Global source levels to 16 (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; Global, 2026-09-21).
  • Gear: start watching gear score for real. 1,000 is the first Ascension Trial rank’s requirement, and that trial also drops shards.
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2-star; 1,000 for the Ascension Trial.

Do not: level Sword Aura Rampage (검기 난무) to 16 for its recast reduction if your content has unstaggerable bosses. The skill only fires on a staggered target, so the bonus never happens (KR/TW, baseline). And do not put Ankle Slice, Leaping Slam or Ruinous Blow on a panic button expecting them to save you while rooted: since the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 none of the three fires while you are rooted or bound.

37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at 37, and this is the one nobody has settled. Three candidates, no head-to-head test on the Global build: Wave Armor (파동의 갑주), personal damage, whose steps 10 and 15 multiply your Blood Absorption and Murderous Burst passives by 1.5; Life Stealing Blade (흡혈의 검), 7% lifesteal rising to 10% at step 20 and a 20 s party leech effect at step 10; Focused Block (집중 막기), a guaranteed weapon block that reaches 3 charges by step 15 and blocks the yellow floor telegraphs. Global guides split (Global, 2026-09-20/21; Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40. The Asphel (PvP) board opens at 45; the PvE board was not in the test build.
  • Skills: the core three towards 20. Past 10 they no longer depend on points: they depend on the four boards (+4), on Arcana cards and on soul bindings from rings and weapons. A ring carries two active-skill slots on Global, so that is where Overhead Slam, Rending Blow and Ruinous Blow levels come from.
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20).
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100; Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: sit next to a Templar expecting both party buffs to land. Experienced Counter Strike and the Templar’s Fury are the same frontal damage buff and do not stack; one of you is contributing nothing there (KR/TW, baseline). The optimised Global 5-player comp still seats a Gladiator alongside a Templar (Global, 2026-09-20), so agree who carries it.

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global LST, 2026-09-20 and 09-21), so the lifesteal and survival specialisations you levelled with convert into endgame damage the day you arrive.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are paid in Stigma Shards, taking all four to 20 is roughly 300 shards (~75 each), and nobody has seen the reset screen on Global. That is why the four get chosen from 22 and then left alone. What softens this for Gladiator specifically is the stigma preset system, which lets you swap a PvE slate for a PvP one without a manual reset (KR, 2026-09-21); it swaps which stigmas are equipped, it does not refund a shard.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes, which refund for 500 Kinah each on Korea and are removed from the branch tips inwards (KR, 2025-11).

Gladiator’s real third mode is solo, not a hybrid: the class is rated S-tier for solo Abyss farming, where regeneration and invulnerability let it chain-kill PvE-geared players, while it drops to B-tier in large-scale Abyss where ranged classes win (2026-09-19, region unknown, relayed in the Global round).

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source publishes a node map for Gladiator, so this is a priority order, not a route, and it is provisional: the PvE board was absent from the Global test build entirely, so this advice cannot be checked against a board nobody has seen (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).

  1. Orange nodes before Blue, Green and White (Global, 2026-09-21; the Korean baseline agrees, DPS nodes first).
  2. Follow your skill order: Overhead Slam, then Rending Blow, then Ruinous Blow.
  3. Then the top four passives: Experienced Counter Strike, Murderous Burst, Attack Readiness, Impact Hit.
  4. Flat Attack (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan).
  5. Combat speed and cooldown reduction (1.5% per node on Global against 2.5% on Korea and Taiwan).
  6. Leave defence and mana nodes until the damage lines are full (KR/TW, baseline).
  7. The +50/+50 board-centre pieces no longer exist on Global.

Still contested or unconfirmed

Zekiel’s Blessing does two different things depending on the source. The Korean database of 2026-09-22 has it as a party buff, +20% attack for 10 s within 40 m. One hands-on Global source of 2026-09-20 says it is now self-only and grants +50% stagger damage. It is a core stigma in both readings, so the slot is safe; what changes is whether your party gets anything. Check it in the client before you tell a group you are bringing it.

Rending Blow’s mana-on-crit specialisation sits at a different step in different sources. The Korean database of 2026-09-22 puts +50 mana on critical at step 16. The KR/TW synthesis reports it at level 20. That gap decides whether Keen Strike retires as your filler at 16 or at 20.

The fourth stigma slot is genuinely open. Wave Armor, Life Stealing Blade and Focused Block; no source tests all three head to head on the Global build. Take whichever your content punishes you for lacking and accept that you may be wrong.

The passive order has one dissenting Global source. Four source groups put Experienced Counter Strike first; one Global guide of 2026-09-21 puts Impact Hit first. This page publishes the majority order, not a consensus.

Aerial Snare’s 5% proc chance against incapacitation-immune bosses comes only from the raw skill data of 2026-09-22 and is corroborated by no creator. Note that it is a different number from Overhead Slam’s 7%, which is confirmed on Global. Worth checking on the live client before you build a CC plan on it.

The Daevanion node order cannot be verified. The PvE (Ariel) board was absent from the Global LST build; its point sinks still exist in the Nightmare shop and in reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch. Do not plan Season 1 counting on it.

Do not target Smite on Genus Insight slots 3 and 9 on Global. One Gladiator source ports that Korean and Taiwanese targeting straight across. On Global the stat is called Double Chance and it sits on the Special board, slot 4 only, capped at 3%, removed from slots 3 and 9 of every normal board and from titles (7 deltas, Global LST). The 3 and 9 advice is correct for Korea and Taiwan and wrong for Global.

Whether a global cooldown exists on Global is unresolved, and every macro timing figure for this class is inherited from Korea or Taiwan. The animation-cancel mechanic itself changed on 2026-08-12, reverted on 2026-08-26 and was still being argued on 2026-09-05, so any macro guide older than about three weeks is wrong. Korea’s 2026-09-16 patch added anti-macro cancel delays to Templar and Assassin and left Gladiator untouched, which its own community reads as groundwork rather than a reprieve.

Which Korean patch state Global ships is unknown. NC said class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 should be your launch kit, but nobody tested it: the test capped at level 37 with the Abyss locked.
