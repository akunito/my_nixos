Spiritmaster: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Inven skill data (Korean client, 2026-09-22) over the KR/TW syntheses to 2026-09-16 and the Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the buff-before-you-summon rule and the macros live on the Spiritmaster skills guide. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. No source publishes a point-by-point 1-45 sequence for Spiritmaster. Every guide gives target levels per content stage, not an order. What follows turns those targets into an order and labels it as that.
  4. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  5. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas at level 22 and leave them alone.
  6. Any Korean or Taiwanese build you find runs 5 or 6 stigmas: cut it to 4 before you copy it.
  7. The most-repeated mechanical rule in the whole corpus: buff before you summon. The spirit locks in your stats at the instant it is summoned (KR, 2026-07-09 and 2026-07-13).

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: mana, one spirit and two passives

Section titled “1 to 22: mana, one spirit and two passives”
  • Skills, in this order: Cold Shock (냉기 충격) first and without argument. It is your mana battery (100 mana per hit, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22) and it is mandatory in the Global opening (Global, 2026-09-18). It chains 100% into Vacuum Blast (진공 폭발) or Earth Tremor (대지 진동) depending on the specialisation you place: the chain costs no points of its own.
  • Second: one single spirit. In the Global level-37 build only the Water Spirit (소환: 물의 정령) is worth levelling among the summons; Fire and Wind are left unlevelled to save points (Global, 2026-09-18). Its level-8 specialisation is what pays: -50% mana cost, and mana is your bottleneck before gear (TW, 2026-09-05). One synthesis source says the opposite, level all four summons before Elemental Fusion: it predates the Global test, do not follow it.
  • The spirit that saves you solo is the Earth Spirit (소환: 땅의 정령): it taunts players at 50% for 2 s and builds threat against NPCs. A playtester summoned it to tank a boss he could not otherwise kill, then swapped back to a damage spirit to keep levelling (Global, 2026-09). A low level is enough: you do not level it, you summon it.
  • Passives, and this part is unanimous: Elemental Strike (정령 타격) and Mental Focus (정신 집중) first, named as the two main passives in every build note (KR, 2026-07 and 2026-09; Global, 2026-09-18). Then Elemental Cohesion (원소 결집). Spirit Regeneration (정령 회생) is only worth it high, around 20-22, so its proc lines up with the Gale and Ancient Spirit cycle (KR, 2026-07-13).
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens around level 12. Spend the points the day you earn them and prioritise active-skill nodes (the 8/12/16 unlocks) plus the four corner damage and defence nodes over filling the board in (Global, 2026-09-21). On the Global test the rule was to always take offensive nodes, combat speed and damage boost, over defensive ones (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Gear: all disposable. Nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20).
  • Gate before you move on: level 22 has a red quest that blocks the story until the world-progress bar is filled, with regional quests and sealed dungeons (Global, 2026-09-18/19).

Do not:

  • Summon and then buff. The spirit inherits your stats at the moment of summoning, so Blessing of Flame and Gale go first, always. It is the most repeated named mistake of the whole class (KR, 2026-07-09 and 2026-07-13).
  • Level Soul’s Scream (영혼의 절규) for PvE: it is skipped entirely in Global levelling builds (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Level Continuous Barrage (연속 난사): the Korean endgame build drops it (KR, 2026-09-07).
  • Skip green side quests: each one is +1 skill point and +1 Daevanion point (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).
  • Chain the Water Spirit straight after another summon: it silently drops its own skill. It goes last, with about 1 s of spacing (KR, 2026-01-15).

22 to 27: first stigma slot

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot”
  • Skills: push Cold Shock towards 8 (13 points, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22) and place its specialisation. Unlocking it and placing it are two separate steps in the UI. Then level Elemental Fusion (원소 융합), the class’s payoff skill: it charges off four spirit-skill uses, not off Domination casts (KR, 2025-11-25).
  • Stigma, slot 1. The four for Global Season 1 are Blessing of Flame (불길의 축복), Gale / Enhancement: Spirit’s Benediction (강화: 정령의 가호), Ancient Spirit (소환: 고대의 정령) and Corrosion (협공: 부식), agreed by three independent builds (KR, 2026-07-09 and 2026-07-13; KR, 2026-09-15). No source publishes the order they arrive in. General rule, no source: take Gale first, because it is the only button that gives you damage and survival at once (+20% PvE amp and +20% PvE resist on you and the spirit for 10 s, plus a heal over time, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22) and because this class has the lowest HP pool in the game.
  • Stop it at 5 (4 shards) until all four are decided. Bonus effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20.
  • Daevanion: active-skill nodes for Cold Shock and for whichever spirit you are levelling, then combat speed.
  • Gear: enhancement cap +5 green, +10 blue.
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: spread shards across four stigmas at once; copy a 5 or 6-stigma build without cutting it.

27 to 32: second slot

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot”
  • Skills: the rest of the core to 8, and Cold Shock and Elemental Fusion towards 12. On Elemental Fusion the level-12 step turns it into a charge skill worth up to +200% damage (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), and there is a warning attached: on Korea that option is bugged and fires by itself instead of charging (KR, 2026-09-07). Unconfirmed on Global.
  • Stigma, slot 2. Ancient Spirit or Blessing of Flame. The Ancient Spirit is the class’s burst window (a 30 s summon that grants a four-element stack every time it uses its skill), but its rhythm depends on the stigma’s enchant, not on its skill level: at +0/+5/+10 it takes 7 basic attacks (Global, 2026-09-20). If you are not reaching +15 yet, take Blessing of Flame first: 10 s of a 50% chance of bonus damage per hit, plus 100 accuracy at level 5 (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Daevanion: active-skill unlocks for Elemental Fusion, then damage boost.
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most.
  • Gate before you move on: no hard gate. The next one is 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take a PvP stigma into a dungeon; with four slots that is a slot thrown away.

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar before the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Skills: two or three core skills to 16. On Flame Cremation (화염 전소) that step adds the Reburn chain (재의 부름), which one Korean guide calls “practically the main skill of the kit” (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; KR, 2026-02). On Joint Strike: Curse (협공: 저주) the level-16 step makes it ignore block and evade and land as a crit.
  • Stigma, slot 3. Whichever of the buff pair is missing, or Corrosion if you already have both. Corrosion is the mandatory debuff in every build because roughly 60% of the class’s output comes from the spirit (KR, 2026-07).
  • Daevanion: the four corner damage and defence nodes (Global, 2026-09-21). Do not copy the Taiwanese board (Nakiel, Ezekiel, Visial): the Global board is a different one, with no Aerial line and apparently no Stamina, and the creator who demoed it flagged that he was showing Korea’s (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-19).
  • Gear: 1,000 gear score is the first Ascension Trial rank, which also drops shards (Global, 2026-09-18/19).

Do not: push a skill past the specialisation step you actually need. Overshooting is described literally as a total waste (KR, 2026-09-07).

37 to 45: fourth slot and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at level 37. This is where you close the set you will carry all of Season 1.
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40 (Global LST, 2026-09-17 and 09-19).
  • Skills: the core towards 20. Past 10 they no longer depend on points: they depend on the four boards (+4), on Arcana cards and on gear soul bindings (Global LST, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Ancient Spirit enchant: the real target of this band is +15, which cuts its cycle from 7 basic attacks to 5. Korea’s +25 does not exist on Global, which caps at +20 (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20).
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100; Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: plan Season 1 counting on the PvE Daevanion board. It was not in the test build (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21). Everything you levelled to survive alone (the Earth Spirit, the mana line) converts into the PvE loadout the day you arrive.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are paid in Stigma Shards, taking all four to 20 is roughly 300 shards (~75 each), and the cost of resetting them on Global nobody has seen. That is why the four get chosen at 22.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes, 500 Kinah each on Korea (KR, 2025-11), removed from the tips inwards.

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source publishes a node map of the Global board, so this is a priority order, not a route.

  1. Active-skill nodes (the 8/12/16 unlocks) for Cold Shock, for whichever spirit you are levelling and for Elemental Fusion (Global, 2026-09-21).
  2. The four corner nodes of damage and defence, before filling branches in (Global, 2026-09-21).
  3. Combat speed and damage boost before any defensive node: that was the rule on the Global test (Global, 2026-09-18).
  4. Flat attack (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan).
  5. Spend the points the day you earn them rather than banking them: refunding costs 500 Kinah a node (KR, 2025-11).
  6. Do not translate the Taiwanese board (Nakiel = attack and CDR, Ezekiel = damage boost and ignore-evasion, Visial = four crit-damage nodes): Global is not that board (TW, 2026-09-05; Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-19).

Still contested or unconfirmed

There is no published levelling sequence for this class. Every source gives target levels per content stage, never an order. The cheat sheet above is that list of targets put in a line, not a tested route.

Corrosion reads two ways that do not reconcile. The guides say “+20% damage taken from all Spiritmaster sources, ~10 s”; the Korean client of 2026-09-22 says “+10% damage taken from the spirit, 20 s”. Check the tooltip before you build anything on it.

Fear / Assault Terror raises defence, not attack, according to the client. The July synthesis says “+20% attack power on hit”; the live tooltip says up to +20% defence scaled by how many targets you hit. Unresolved.

The specialisation structure does not match two Korean guides. The client shows exactly three steps (8/12/16, five picks) on every active; a guide of 2026-09-07 describes a fourth step at 16 and three more at 20. Whether that is a patch, a guide error or a regional difference is not known.

The third and fourth stigma slot levels were never played. The Global test capped at 37, and a second Global source says “one from the start and the second at level 30”. The 22 and the 27 are independently confirmed.

Two live Korean bugs, unconfirmed on Global. Assault mistimes the Curse and Corrosion resets (the Korean workaround is leaving it at 19, not 20), and Elemental Fusion’s charge option fires by itself instead of charging (KR, 2026-09-07). The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 does not mention Spiritmaster at all, so neither has been touched.

The English name mapping is not reliable. The Korean client ships no English names for any of the 38 skills: the ones on this page are working translations and need verifying against the Global client.
