Assassin: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable. What is “medium” is everything else, and on this class more than on any other: Assassin has the weakest name coverage in the whole corpus, because most of the guides that exist transliterate from auto-captions and their own authors flag the names as tentative. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, enchant cap 20, no Heroic gear).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients. On this class it is also the only reliable handle, because the English names in circulation come from what one creator read off his screen, not from a skill-panel capture.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. On the actives almost nothing visible changes: what grows is the damage coefficient, which the database does not publish. On the passives almost everything changes.
  3. The point cost is identical across all 27 non-stigma skills: 1 point per level from 2 to 4, 2 from 5 to 7 and 4 from 8 to 10, so 21 points take any skill to rank 10. Ranks 11 to 20 cost no points at all, because those ranks do not come from points: they come from gear affixes, the Daevanion board and Arcana cards. Keep any piece that grants +levels to a skill you use.
  4. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12 and 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15 and 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  5. Tell a chain apart from a condition, and on this class it matters more than anywhere else: six of the twelve actives are conditional. Heart Gore needs a critical, Storm Rampage needs groggy, Whirlwind Slash needs an evade, Infiltration needs the emergency dodge, Shadow Fall needs a stun and Shock Release needs somebody to control you. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears behind the skill that triggers it; a condition is a skill that lives on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met.
  6. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in.
  7. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  8. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  9. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on this class the list is long. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. Inven gives an English name to only three of the forty skills (Sigil, Shock Release and Throw Shadow Blade); everything else is our translation or the name the Korean syntheses use. Where a Global-client creator read a different name off his screen, the entry says so. The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 5 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 40 in total. All forty require a dagger (단검), without a single exception.

Before the entries, the one concept you need to read them: a sigil (문양) is a mark stamped on the target for 10 s, stacking up to 5, and two skills spend it, Sigil Explosion (damage and stun chance scale with the stack) and Airborne Bind (100% aerial bind against a target at 5 stacks). Half a dozen skills stamp them. The full accounting is in Combos, section (b).

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Quick Slash (빠른 베기)

Section titled “Quick Slash (빠른 베기)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 100 MP. What it is for: this is your left click, your mana battery and the anchor of the technique that defines the class, auto-attack cancelling (평캔). Holding it alongside Beast’s Roar raises basic attacks per minute rather than diluting them (80 to 90 in a controlled one-minute test, KR, 2026-03). The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 gave it +10% PvE damage. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% MP recovery / 1.5% HP absorb / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Sigil Explosion’s cooldown. 16: ignores block and evasion, and lands as a critical. Chains into: Soul-Severing Slash (link 1) and Swift Slash (link 2).

Beast’s Roar (맹수의 포효)

Section titled “Beast’s Roar (맹수의 포효)”

Read as Savage Roar on the Global client (Global, 2026-09-17).

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and stamps 1 sigil for 10 s. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the right click, your sigil generator and, outside the burst window, your biggest single damage source: around 37% of the total with the comfort macro, against Heart Gore’s 21% and Quick Slash’s 16% (KR, measured, 2026-03). The 2026-09-16 patch raised its cancel delay, and the official notes say explicitly that it was an anti-macro-cancel measure (KR, 2026-09-16). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% MP cost / +20% skill speed / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Ambush’s cooldown. 16: Beast’s Swipe pulls surrounding targets in. Chains into: Beast’s Hind Kick (link 1) and Beast’s Swipe (link 2).

Ambush (암습)

Section titled “Ambush (암습)”

Kind: active · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: moves you behind a target within 20 m, deals damage and leaves it spinning and stunned for 3 s. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your long gap closer and the opener for everything this class does, because it drops you exactly where you live, behind them. It is also the first piece of the reliable PvP back-attack chain: Ambush, Surprise Attack, Triniel’s Dagger (KR, 2025-11). Version note: the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 raised its 2nd specialty’s duration from 5 s to 10 s, and the Inven dump of 2026-09-22 still shows 5 s. Its mana cost was cut back in March and that cut is already in the current entry. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +1 s stun / on hit, +20% crit damage amplification for 5 s (10 s since 2026-09-16) / on hit, 50% chance of a 5 s seal (봉인). 12: on hit, 50% chance of a 5 s bind. 16: becomes an AoE skill. Chains into: nothing.

Surprise Attack (기습)

Section titled “Surprise Attack (기습)”

Kind: active · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 4 m. Attacking from behind increases the damage by 50%. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: the second hit of the PvP back-attack chain and the skill that converts position into sigils: its level-8 specialty stamps 2 sigils at once on a rear hit, which is half the maximum stack on one button. Its level-12 specialty is the only way to fire Whirlwind Slash without having dodged. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): on a rear hit, stamp 2 sigils for 10 s / 3% HP absorb / on hit, destroy shields. 12: on hit, 50% chance to activate Whirlwind Slash. 16: +2 consecutive casts. Chains into: nothing.

Heart Gore (심장 찌르기)

Section titled “Heart Gore (심장 찌르기)”

Heard as Heart Grow on the Global client, which is almost certainly a caption error; other guides call it Heart Stab.

Kind: active, crit-conditioned · Cost: none · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: activates on a critical hit, deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 100 MP. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: the burst button of the class and the reason Elusive Clone exists. Outside the window it contributes around 21% of your damage; inside the window, played by hand, around 50% (KR, measured, 2026-03). Its level-16 specialty is the single highest-value node in the class according to both baseline Korean guides: a critical resets its own cooldown, which turns 5 s into zero for as long as you keep critting. The 2026-09-16 patch raised its cancel delay (KR, 2026-09-16). Data note: both syntheses say that level-16 node “resets the cooldown and restores mana”. The database describes only the reset; the mana is already in the skill’s base text and does not depend on the node. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 1.5% HP absorb / on hit, stamp 1 sigil for 10 s / up to +20% crit the fewer targets you hit. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on a critical hit, resets Heart Gore’s cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Storm Rampage (폭풍 난무)

Section titled “Storm Rampage (폭풍 난무)”

Kind: active, groggy-conditioned · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a groggy target within 4 m. What it is for: groggy-window filler with no cost and no cooldown, the same shape as Templar’s Flash Rampage, and with the same hidden level-16 node: -1 s to all your cooldowns every time it lands, which in a class whose only uncapped stat is cooldown reduction is worth far more than it reads. Its real job is not firing on every groggy, it is filling the gap between Elusive Clone windows (KR, 2026-07). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% HP absorb / on hit, restores 120 MP / becomes usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Chains into: nothing.

Whirlwind Slash (회오리 베기)

Section titled “Whirlwind Slash (회오리 베기)”

Kind: active, evasion-conditioned · Cost: none · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: on a successful evade, deals damage to a target within 4 m and with a 40% chance leaves it spinning and stunned for 3 s. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: it is the equivalent of Templar’s Weakening Strike, but built on evasion instead of block, and that sums the class up: your defensive stat is evasion and so is your punish. With the level-16 specialty each dodge has a 25% chance to reset it, and with the level-8 range node it reaches 20 m, which makes it a different skill entirely. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +50% multi-hit on hit / becomes AoE damage / +16 m range. 12: +20% stun chance. 16: on an evade, 25% chance to reset the cooldown. Chains into: nothing; it fires off an evade or off Surprise Attack’s proc.

Flash Slash (섬광 베기)

Section titled “Flash Slash (섬광 베기)”

Kind: active · Cost: none · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies around the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: moves you to the far side of a target within 4 m, deals damage to up to 4 enemies and with a 40% chance blinds them for 3 s. Against NPCs the blind lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: the short positional swap, the one that puts you back behind them without spending Ambush. In PvE it is the cheap way to reposition when the boss turns; in PvP it is chainable CC, because its level-16 specialty stuns blinded targets, meaning the ones you just blinded yourself. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +2 s blind / +6 m range / up to +20% damage the more targets you hit. 12: +2 consecutive casts. 16: on hitting a blinded target, 50% chance to leave it spinning and stunned for 3 s. Chains into: nothing.

Infiltration (침투)

Section titled “Infiltration (침투)”

Kind: active, conditioned on the emergency dodge · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 10 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: after using the emergency dodge, moves you behind a target within 10 m, deals damage and with a 50% chance blinds it for 3 s. Against NPCs the blind lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your second teleport-behind, and the only one that does not spend Ambush. It sits behind the dodge, so it does not exist unless you dodge first: it is reactive by design and never goes in a macro. Its level-12 specialty adds a second chain link, 암격, for which the database publishes no entry of its own. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): on hit, restores 300 stamina / +10 m range / on hit, destroys shields. 12: adds the 2nd chain link 암격. 16: +20% blind chance. Chains into: 암격, only with the level-12 specialty.

Shadow Fall (그림자 낙하)

Section titled “Shadow Fall (그림자 낙하)”

Kind: active, stun-conditioned · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a stunned target within 4 m and knocks it down for 1.5 s. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 5% chance on hit. What it is for: the natural finisher inside Ambush’s stun, and against bosses a 5% proc you cannot plan around. It is one of the skills levelling guides enchant for HP absorb, because its level-8 specialty returns 10% of the damage, the highest absorb percentage in the kit (KR, 2026-09). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 10% HP absorb / on hit, stamp 3 sigils for 10 s / -5 s cooldown. 12: +1 s knockdown. 16: usable on blinded targets. Chains into: nothing.

Sigil Explosion (문양 폭발)

Section titled “Sigil Explosion (문양 폭발)”

Inven calls it Sigil. Heard as Inside Explosion on the Global client, almost certainly a misheard “Insignia Explosion”.

Kind: active · Cost: 200 MP · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 20 m, 4 m area around the target, up to 4 · Cast: instant · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around a target 20 m away and stuns them for 3 s. Damage and stun chance increase with the number of stacked sigils. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: your only 20 m damage button and your area stun, and the most contested skill in the kit. The community treats it as mandatory; one meter-backed test says it is overvalued, because using it wipes the sigil stack that was lowering the target’s back-attack resistance for the whole party (KR, 2026-03). That same source’s practical reading is: use it, do not over-invest points in it. The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +10% PvE damage. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): adds a spin effect / +50% multi-hit on hit / after Sigil Explosion, keeps 2 sigils stamped. 12: +1 s stun. 16: -3 s cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Properties: non-targeted · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, air-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. Text: removes your stun, knockdown, air bind, grab, freeze and fear states and puts you in Steadfast (강인함) for 5 s. Steadfast: immunity to status ailments. What it is for: it is your only hard defensive in the base kit, and on a class rated 2 out of 5 for survivability (Global, 2026-09-20) that makes it the button that decides whether you live. Its level-8 specialty adds +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance during Steadfast, which is the only real block of mitigation you own. Rebind it off the space bar on day one: mashing space to escape a stun summons your wings instead (Global, 2026-09-18). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Steadfast, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance / on cast, restores 1000 stamina / on cast, restores 20% of max HP. 12: +2 s Steadfast. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Storm Slash (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

The five chains are buttons that only exist after the skill that triggers them, and all five activate at 100%, so they always land. Just as on Templar, chains have no specialties of their own: they display their parent skill’s. What you pick on Quick Slash applies to both of its links.

Soul-Severing Slash (절혼 베기)

Section titled “Soul-Severing Slash (절혼 베기)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 105 MP. What it is for: the second hit of the left click. It returns 5 MP more than the first. The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +10% PvE damage. Chained from: Quick Slash, position 1.

Swift Slash (쾌속 베기)

Section titled “Swift Slash (쾌속 베기)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and restores 120 MP. What it is for: the third hit of the left click and the one that gives the most mana back. Cutting the chain before this link is what leaves you dry, and a dry Assassin has no defensives to fall back on. The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +10% PvE damage. Chained from: Quick Slash, position 2.

Beast’s Hind Kick (맹수의 뒷발차기)

Section titled “Beast’s Hind Kick (맹수의 뒷발차기)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and stamps 1 sigil for 10 s. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the second sigil of the right-click sequence. Same cost and same effect as the first: the Beast’s Roar chain is a sigil machine that charges 120 MP per link. Chained from: Beast’s Roar, position 1.

Beast’s Swipe (맹수의 후려치기)

Section titled “Beast’s Swipe (맹수의 후려치기)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and stamps 1 sigil for 10 s. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the third sigil, and the only link in the class that changes function through a specialty: with Beast’s Roar’s level-16 node it pulls surrounding enemies in, which is the closest thing to a gather the Assassin owns. The 2026-09-16 patch raised its cancel delay alongside Beast’s Roar and Heart Gore (KR, 2026-09-16). Chained from: Beast’s Roar, position 2.

Storm Slash (폭풍 베기)

Section titled “Storm Slash (폭풍 베기)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies around you · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and leaves them spinning and stunned for 3 s. What it is for: exactly what Templar’s Seize Alive is, with the same visibility problem: it is not a loose 2-minute skill, it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a control effect, which means every time somebody stuns you they are handing you a free 3 s area stun. None of our syntheses records it as a chain; the database does (Shock Release’s link_type points at it). Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Heightened Sixth Sense (육감 극대화)

Section titled “Heightened Sixth Sense (육감 극대화)”

Text: your evasion increases by 200 and your max HP by 6%, and you recover HP on a successful evade. Internal cooldown: 5 s. At rank 20, +25% max HP. What it is for: it is your entire defense in one line, and the reason the Assassin’s defensive stat is evasion and not block (KR/TW, 2026-02 and 2026-05). That jump from 6% to 25% max HP is the largest scaling in the class. Levelling guides put it first among the passives, ahead of the offensive alternative. Data note: the figure in circulation, “+20% and roughly 2,000 HP plus 1,350 flat” (KR, 2026-09-10), predates this percentage curve. Read it as historical and keep the 6% to 25% from the dump.

Seize the Opening (빈틈 노리기)

Section titled “Seize the Opening (빈틈 노리기)”

Text: your crit increases by 100. On a critical hit, a 25% chance of +5.5% attack power for 10 s and a 7% chance to summon up to 5 clones that each deal damage and stamp a 10 s sigil (up to 5 stacks). Internal cooldowns: 1 s for the clones, 1 s for the attack buff. At rank 20, +290 crit and +15% attack. What it is for: this passive is where the class’s “clone” identity comes from without the Elusive Clone stigma being involved at all, and it is the sigil source nobody counts: a 7% chance per critical, on a class that crits constantly, stamps stacks on its own. The Elusive Clone stigma multiplies this passive by 1.5 while it lasts (level-15 specialty), so the two are read together.

Poison Coating (독 바르기)

Section titled “Poison Coating (독 바르기)”

Text: every time an attack lands, a 15% chance to poison the target for 10 s: -12% healing received and damage over time every 1 s. Internal cooldown: 1 s. At rank 20, -50% healing received. What it is for: both baseline Korean guides say the same thing with no hedging: zero points, its function is keeping a target in combat state, not dealing damage (KR, 2025-11 and 2025-12). Data note: that advice is from 2025 and it is about damage. What the dump shows is that the anti-heal effect scales from -12% to -50%, which in group PvP is not a negligible effect. Nobody has re-evaluated the skill with that curve in front of them. We do not take it on that basis, but we leave the number visible.

Rear Strike (배후 강타)

Section titled “Rear Strike (배후 강타)”

Read as Rear Smite on the Global client.

Text: your rear damage amplification increases by 3%, PvE by 3% and PvP by 1.5%. At rank 20, +12.5%, +12.5% and +6.25%. What it is for: the core damage passive, and the first one a Global-client guide tells you to take while levelling, alongside Resolve (Global, 2026-09-17). Three readings that come out of this and out of no guide:

  • The two main chains are one single economy. The full left click returns 100 + 105 + 120 = 325 MP; the right-click chain costs 120 x 3 = 360. (b) The sigil economy and the Elusive Clone window

Section titled “(b) The sigil economy and the Elusive Clone window”

This is the mechanic that defines the class, and it is really two mechanics crossing.

Sigils (문양). They are stamped on the target, last 10 s and stack up to 5. This is everything that produces them and everything that spends them, taken whole from the database:

Source Sigils Condition
Beast’s Roar and its two links 1 per hit, up to 3 none
Beast’s Fang (stigma) 5 at once none
Shadow Step (stigma) 5 at once level-10 specialty
Shadow Fall 3 level-8 specialty
Surprise Attack 2 level-8 specialty, rear hits only
Heart Gore 1 level-8 specialty
Seize the Opening (passive) 1 per clone 7% per critical, up to 5 clones
Sigil Explosion consumes the stack keeps 2 with the level-8 specialty
Airborne Bind reads the stack 100% aerial bind at 5 stacks

Two practical consequences: sigils are not only damage, they are control reliability, because the only way to stop Airborne Bind being a coin flip is to reach 5; and spending the stack has a hidden cost, because Sigil Explosion wipes the back-attack-resistance debuff that was helping the whole party (KR, measured, 2026-03).

The Elusive Clone window. What the database says: the stigma removes Heart Gore’s cooldown for 10 s (20 s at rank 20) and adds +20% damage to all your attacks. That settles a two-year argument: the +30% over 12 s from the 2025 Korean guides is no longer the number. Underneath it sits a second mechanism doing the same thing partially: Heart Gore’s level-16 specialty resets its own cooldown on every critical.

What changes inside the window, measured (KR, 2026-03):

Rotation style Beast’s Roar Quick Slash Heart Gore
Comfort macro, outside the window ~37% ~16% ~21%
Holding all three ~30% ~10% >30%
Burst window, manual falls sharply rises ~50%

That is why cooldown reduction is the class’s stat and has no ceiling. Every other class targets a 33-36% band; the Assassin is explicitly told to keep stacking, because each point is another window per fight (TW, 2026-08). The Arcana mirror card is Illusion, permanently: it is the one class that never pivots to Wisdom. And the real uptime remains unresolved, between “never reaches 100%” and “near-permanent, with about 15 s of downtime” (single source, 2026-09-19).

The Global warning. Percentage multipliers are cut relative to Korea and Taiwan, and cooldown reduction and combat speed are named among the cut ones: the Daevanion cooldown-reduction node drops from 2.5% to 1.5% and the damage node from 5% to 1.5% (Global, 2026-09-18/19). Flat gear stats rose to compensate. Translation: the window the Korean guides describe takes longer to reach its uptime on Global.

(c) PvE rotations and macro chains

Section titled “(c) PvE rotations and macro chains”

The macro system rule: the in-game macro fires the first available skill going down the list, in strict priority order, and skips the ones whose condition is not met. Confirmed on the Global client (Global, LST 2026-09-20). So conditional skills go above the filler or they never come out, and this class has six of them.

Opener (KR, 2026-06): both burst cooldowns first (Elusive Clone and Swift Contract), then Ambush onto the boss to land behind it, then the macro.

Sustained rotation (KR, measured, 2026-03), and the important detail is what is not bundled:

Do not stack several skills into one macro slot. It causes visible auto-attack-cancel skips and a net damage loss. It is the most class-specific rule in this corpus and it comes from a meter-backed test.

Burst window (Elusive Clone and Swift Contract up, KR, 2026-03): hold the basic attack, mash Heart Gore and Sigil Explosion, and the instant the instant-cast Heart Gore enters cooldown, switch to holding left and right click together. Heart Gore’s share rises from 21% to 50%.

PvE skill-point order (KR, 2026-03): Heart Gore first, then Beast’s Roar to 20, then Quick Slash to 20. Sigil Explosion is used but not over-invested. And from the levelling side, what has not changed: Quick Slash, Beast’s Roar and Sigil Explosion to rank 12 first, because 12 unlocks two specializations plus a bonus effect while ranks 13 and 14 add only 2-3% damage each (KR, 2025-11). Poison Coating, zero points.

The state of macros in Korea after the 2026-09-16 patch, and this is the newest thing there is (KR, 2026-09-18, after three days of testing by a Korean player):

  • The 맨켄 (main cancel) technique is dead: the patch raised the cancel delay on Beast’s Roar, Beast’s Swipe and Heart Gore precisely for that.
  • The configuration that survives with the best hit count is 문신맥.
  • The groggy technique is now 폭란 캔, that is Storm Rampage plus Heart Gore. Folding it into the macro costs about 10 hits per minute but shows no meaningful DPS difference.
  • Adding Whirlwind Slash, Surprise Attack or Shadow Fall to the macro line gives no gain at all and causes visible character stutter.
  • Quick Slash with Triniel’s Dagger, and both options of Heart Gore’s 2nd specialty, showed no DPS difference: they are preference picks.

It is not verified that that cancel-delay change is in the Global build. If it is, every Korean macro guide older than that date, this section included, has to be re-measured.

Macro step delays: the game ships 30 ms by default. People run ~10 ms below 50 ms ping and 40-50 ms above 80-100 ms (KR, 2026-06). This matters more here than on any other class: the Assassin has the highest attack speed in the game, it is the most ping-sensitive of the eight according to three independent sources this round (Global and TW, 2026-09-20/22), and the figure in circulation is up to -40% DPS on a bad connection (KR, 2026-05).

One Global advantage that rewrites your muscle memory: the boss hit lands roughly 24 frames after the telegraph fills, against Korea’s ~8, close to three times the reaction window (Global, 2026-09-18, single source relayed widely). For a class that has to stand behind a boss through its casts, that is the most useful mechanical change of the round, and it means the timings learned from Korean videos will fire early for you.

The positioning entry, in one line: on the Global build, missing the rear angle produces “the worst damage in the group on the same gear”, and hitting it “deletes” single targets (Global, 2026-09-20). Turn on the ground indicator that marks the boss’s true rear facing: it stays correct when the model visually faces elsewhere (KR, 2026-06).

Section titled “(d) PvP combos and openers”

The base combo (KR, 2025-12): stun, back-attack skill, basic attack, third skill, back-attack skill again, Sigil Explosion as the finisher.

The reliable back-attack chain (KR, 2025-11):

What the database adds to that combo and no guide says: inside Ambush’s 3 s stun you have Shadow Fall available, because it is conditioned on exactly that state, and it adds 1.5 s of knockdown on top. And the finisher is not a single choice: Sigil Explosion stuns with a chance proportional to the stack, while Airborne Bind at 5 sigils air-binds at 100%. The disengage loop, which is what actually keeps an Assassin alive (KR, 2025-12):

  1. ESC to drop the tab target.
  2. Run to a farther, undamaged target.
  3. Land one light hit on it: that forces a Heart Gore critical.
  4. Lifesteal heals you while you reposition.

Movement rule: Shift for the speed boost and immediately V to fold your wings. Caught mid-flight with wings open, Shift locks out and you die helpless (KR, 2025-12).

Targeting settings: disable “set main target on attack” and set target search to camera-forward with hostile players sorted top, so Tab never grabs a mob mid-fight (KR, 2025-12).

The two stealths, and what each is for. Stealth is 2 minutes with a 20 s duration, unusable in combat until you take its level-15 node, and its level-20 node resets its cooldown when you take part in a player kill: that is the solo Abyss picker loop, engage, kill, vanish, re-engage (Global, 2026-09-20; TW, 2026-09-22). Shadow Step is 45 s with a 3 s duration, works in combat from the start and is the button for breaking off an exchange you are losing. Do not sink 15 skill levels into Stealth: a cheap detection item nullifies it (KR, 2025-12).

Evasion, all or nothing. Soul-engrave Evasion first and Agility second, targeting Evasion+Agility, Evasion+Status-Resist or Agility+Status-Resist pairs. A half-committed evasion build loses to everybody: go all in or not at all (KR/TW, 2026-02 and 2026-07).

The anti-buff button. Spiral Slash removes up to 2 buffs at base and up to 5 with its level-15 specialty, and stuns for 3 s when it strips 3 or more. It is the direct answer to a class that survives by stacking defensives. None of our sources covers the Assassin against Templar or against Cleric matchup, so this is a reading of the entry, not verified practice.

Where the class actually stands, to calibrate expectations: a 70.5% win rate in 1v1 arena, the highest figure in that data (TW, 2026-07), but the September tables put Gladiator ahead in representation at the top of the solo ladder, ~33% against the Assassin’s ~20% (2026-09-19). And in the Abyss it still posts the worst KDA of all classes, because the moment a group turns on it, it dies. It is the best duelling class and one of the worst zerg units, and both facts have the same cause.

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • The 20 m gap closer (Ambush), the 10 m positional swap (Flash Slash), the CC break (Shock Release), the dodge, and the proc-only teleport-behind (Infiltration). That is the literal list from two Korean and French macro guides (KR, 2026-06), and the reason is the same for all five: they decide where your body is, and that is not automatable.
  • More than one skill in the same slot. It causes visible auto-attack-cancel skips. A class-specific, measured rule (KR, 2026-03).
  • Whirlwind Slash, Surprise Attack and Shadow Fall, after the Korean patch of 2026-09-16: zero DPS gain and visible character stutter (KR, 2026-09-18).
  • Elusive Clone and Swift Contract. The burst window is the whole rotation; firing it when a macro reaches it instead of when the boss is hittable throws the fight away.
  • Evasion Stance. Those are guaranteed invulnerability frames. Like Templar’s block, they are pressed by hand or they are worth nothing.
  • Stealth, which cannot even be used in combat until you take its level-15 node.
  • Passives, which simply cannot be added.
  • More than one row. Two or more rows reduce how many times each skill in the macro fires (KR, 2026-09-17/22).

What is still unresolved

The names, and here more than on any other class. Assassin has the worst name coverage in the whole corpus: Inven gives an English name to only three of the forty skills, the existing syntheses transliterate from auto-captions and their own authors flag them as tentative, and the Global-client names on this page are what one single creator read off his screen during the level-37 test, not a skill-panel capture. Two Global-facing sources disagree with each other on some of the same skills. Check it during Advanced Access from 30 September.

Sling Dagger. One Taiwanese source names a Global gap closer by that name, which resets on kill (TW, 2026-09-22). It matches no entry in this Korean dump. Either it is a renamed Korean skill we failed to match, or it is Global-only. Unresolved, and it is the biggest hole on this page.

The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 is not in the dump. Three things are missing: the +10% PvE damage on Quick Slash, Soul-Severing Slash, Swift Slash and Sigil Explosion; the rise of Ambush’s 2nd specialty from 5 s to 10 s; and the cancel-delay increase on Beast’s Roar, Beast’s Swipe and Heart Gore. The entries on this page show the pre-patch values on those three points. And nobody has published a DPS measurement taken after that patch.

Three mismatches between the database and the guides. Smoke Bomb: the syntheses give it 7 s of projectile immunity, the Korean entry mentions none. Evasion Contract: 50% projectile immunity in the syntheses, 25% in the database. Triniel’s Dagger: the Korean text says it generates 5% of the recast time on all your skills, and its level-20 node raises that generation, while the guides describe it as removing 10% of the remaining cooldown per hit. None of the three is reconciled.

One internal database contradiction: Airborne Bind’s entry row says 10 m range and its own text says 4 m.

Elusive Clone’s real uptime. Between “never reaches 100%, which is why cooldown reduction has no ceiling” (TW, 2026-08) and “near-permanent, with a dead window of about 15 s” (single source, region unknown, 2026-09-19). The direction is the same, the magnitude cannot be published.

The accuracy target. Between ~3,000 (KR, 2026-02) and ~5,700 (KR, 2026-07), with the same creator saying in the same video that the Assassin needs no accuracy. And neither figure transfers to Global, where the whole percentage scale is different.

And the one that matters to you, which nobody covers: there is not a single source in the corpus on Assassin against Templar or Assassin against Cleric. It is the matchup you will meet most, and it has to be tested by hand.
