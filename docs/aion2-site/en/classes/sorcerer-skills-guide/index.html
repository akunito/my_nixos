Sorcerer: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, enchant cap 20, no Heroic gear). The Spanish client names the class Hechicero (Global, 2026-09-18).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. Almost no skill changes its visible numbers as it ranks up: what grows is the damage coefficient, which the database does not publish. The exceptions are marked.
  3. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  4. Tell a chain apart from a condition. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears after the skill that triggers it. A condition is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met. Sorcerer has three conditions and they are the spine of the class: Blaze needs Fire Mark, Frost Burst needs freeze, Flame Scatter Shot needs groggy.
  5. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in.
  6. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  7. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  8. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on this class that list is long: six database numbers disagree with the Korean guides from 2025. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. The English names in the entries are the ones Inven serves (name_en) where it serves any, and mostly it serves none: of the 39 skills, Inven names only 12 in English. The rest are our translation or the name the guides use. The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean client text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 4 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 39 in total. All of them require a spellbook (법서), with no exceptions.

Point cost, identical across the 26 non-stigma skills: 0 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 4 / 4 / 4 for ranks 1 to 10, that is 21 points to take one skill to 10. Ranks 11 to 20 cost no points at all: those come from Daevanion, soul-bind and Arcana. Stigmas cost no skill points whatsoever; they cost Stigma Shards and need 8 stigma to equip.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Flame Arrow (불꽃 화살)

Section titled “Flame Arrow (불꽃 화살)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals fire damage to a target within 20 m and restores 100 MP. What it is for: this is your left click, your mana battery and the entrance to the only three-link chain the class has. No cost, no cooldown, so it fills any gap. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% mana recovery / 1.5% lifesteal / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on a Heat Surge hit, applies Fire Mark for 5 s. 16: on a Heat Surge hit, resets Blaze’s cooldown. The detail that changes how you play it: the level-12 and level-16 specialties do not fire off Flame Arrow, they fire off the third link of its chain. Cutting the chain short switches off your Fire Mark engine and your Blaze reset, not just your mana. Chains into: Detonation (link 1) and Heat Surge (link 2).

Ice Chain (얼음 사슬)

Section titled “Ice Chain (얼음 사슬)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Area: 4 m around the target, up to 4 enemies · Cast: instant · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals water damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target at 20 m and slows them for 5 s at -40% movement speed. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: your AoE filler and your cheap slow. The slow matters more than it looks, because the Cold Summon passive pays out on slowed targets from any skill, not just the ice ones. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% mana cost / +20% skill speed / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Winter Shackles’ cooldown. 16: on a Cold Wave hit, 5% chance to freeze for 2 s. Macro warning: this is the hidden-switch skill. With Ice Chain on your bar, Skill Reservation must be OFF or the macro fires Ice Chain only and nothing else ever comes out, silently (KR, 2026-09-21). The whole thing is in Combos, section (c). Chains into: Cold Wave (link 1).

Firestorm (불꽃 작살)

Section titled “Firestorm (불꽃 작살)”

Inven calls it Flame Harpoon; the English guides call it Firestorm. Same skill.

Kind: active · Cost: 250 MP · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Text: deals fire damage to a target within 20 m. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: it is the class’s cooldown engine and the reason Sorcerer is not a spam rotation but an economy of timers. Each harpoon cuts 2 s off Hellfire’s cooldown, and the Korean guides put the cast at 5 projectiles, which gives -10 s of Hellfire per cast (KR, 2025-11 and 2026-07). The database does not publish a projectile count. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -50% mana cost / +20% skill speed / on hit, -50% healing received for 5 s. 12: per harpoon, -2 s to Hellfire’s cooldown. 16: per harpoon, +15% damage stacking to +75%. Data gap: the database stops at rank 10 on this skill and only on this skill. The other 38 carry all 20 ranks; Firestorm carries 20 rows, but with levels 1 to 10 listed twice each. The level-12 and level-16 specialties are listed, but no rank 11 to 20 entry exists to check them against. Cause unknown; we flag it rather than invent it. Chains into: nothing.

Bitter Cold Wind (혹한의 바람)

Section titled “Bitter Cold Wind (혹한의 바람)”

Kind: active, ground zone · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Area: 4 m, up to 4 enemies · Cast: instant Rank 20: entry identical. Text: summons a bitter cold wind at the position of a target within 20 m, lasting 2 s. The wind deals water damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of itself and roots them for 3 s with a 60% chance. Against NPCs the root lands at 100%. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your pack-clumping tool and your AoE root. It is also the perfect example of the class’s structural problem: it anchors to the ground, so a boss that moves turns it into lost damage. The pull specialty is what makes it a real clumping tool. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +1 s wind duration / becomes a skill usable while moving / pull effect. 12: ignores block and evasion, lands as a crit. 16: on hit, -1 s to Frost’s cooldown.

Blaze (불꽃 폭발)

Section titled “Blaze (불꽃 폭발)”

Kind: active, conditional on Fire Mark · Cost: none · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals fire damage to a Fire-Marked target within 20 m and restores 100 MP. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: it is the Fire Mark consumer and, with its lifesteal specialty, what makes levelling tolerable. The Korean rule is to take that specialty the moment it exists (KR, 2025-11). Watch the classic mistake: Blaze consumes the mark, so firing it right after applying one leaves you with no mark for whatever was queued behind it (KR, 2025-11). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 3% lifesteal / +50% mana recovery / delayed damage after 3 s. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -3 s to Wish of Concentration’s cooldown. Data note: the database says 3% lifesteal. The most-cited Korean guide from 2025 says 30%, around 600 HP per cast at level 30 (KR, 2025-11). That is a factor of ten, and nobody has reconciled it. Chains into: nothing; it fires off a condition.

Flame Scatter Shot (화염 난사)

Section titled “Flame Scatter Shot (화염 난사)”

Kind: active, conditional on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals fire damage to a groggy target within 20 m. What it is for: free groggy-window filler with no cost and no cooldown, exactly like the Templar’s Flash Rampage. It is useless while levelling, because bosses rarely stagger before 40; it becomes a core skill in dungeons (KR, 2025-11). Its level-16 specialty is the most underrated node in the kit: -1 s to all your cooldowns every time it lands. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% lifesteal / restores 120 MP / usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Macro warning: it has to sit in the first slot of the macro or it never fires at all, because it is conditional and the macro walks down the list (KR, 2026-08).

Frost (빙결)

Section titled “Frost (빙결)”

Kind: active · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 30 s · Range: 20 m · Area: 4 m, up to 4 enemies · Cast: instant · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals water damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target at 20 m and freezes it for 3 s with a 40% chance. Against NPCs the freeze lands at 100%. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: half of “freeze first, then fire”. The 40% against players is weak until you take the level-12 node, which lifts it to 65%. Against NPCs there is no gamble. Its level-8 node that resets Frost Burst is what turns the Frost plus Frost Burst pair into your levelling burst before you have Hellfire. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): restores 200 MP / -5 s cooldown / on a freeze, resets Frost Burst’s cooldown. 12: +25% freeze chance against players. 16: +1 s freeze duration.

Winter Shackles (겨울의 속박)

Section titled “Winter Shackles (겨울의 속박)”

Kind: active · Cost: 200 MP · Cooldown: 45 s · Range: 20 m · Area: 4 m, up to 4 enemies · Cast: instant · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals water damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target at 20 m and slows them for 3 s. What it is for: on the base entry it is the worst skill in the kit, 200 MP and a 45 s cooldown for a 3 s slow. Everything that justifies it sits in the specialties: level 8 turns it into a damage amplifier, level 12 gives it a chain link, and level 16 removes a third of the cooldown. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): usable while moving / on hit, 30% chance to freeze for 3 s / on hit, 5 s of +20% PvE damage amplification and +10% PvP. 12: adds the 2nd chain link, Winter Phantom (겨울의 환영). 16: -15 s cooldown. Data note: the Korean text of that third level-8 option does not say whose amplification it is. Everywhere else in the database, 피해 증폭 is a caster stat (the Robe of Flame passive uses exactly the same term for your own amplification), so the literal reading is that the buff is yours. Our own synthesis read it as a debuff on the target. Unresolved. Chains into: Winter Phantom, only with the level-12 specialty, and the database gives it no entry of its own.

Frost Burst (빙결 폭발)

Section titled “Frost Burst (빙결 폭발)”

Kind: active, conditional on freeze · Cost: none · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 20 m · Area: 4 m, up to 4 enemies · Cast: instant Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals water damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a frozen target at 20 m. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 7% chance on hit. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: it is your deepest lifesteal and a large slice of your real damage: a Korean endgame measurement puts it at roughly 10% of the total (KR, 2026-09-17). That 7% against bosses is the same mechanism as the Templar’s Judgment: the skill is always there, but against control-immune targets it only lands on a roll. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 7% lifesteal / usable while moving / 75% chance of a 2 m knockback. 12: ignores block and evasion, lands as a crit. 16: 50% chance to reset its cooldown. Raid warning: on KR live there is a bug where, with several Sorcerers in the same raid, only the one with the highest level applies Frost Burst damage and everyone else’s is voided. Agree levels before the pull (KR, 2026-09-17).

Wish of Concentration (집중의 기원)

Section titled “Wish of Concentration (집중의 기원)”

Kind: active, self buff · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: here the entry does change: duration goes from 10 s to 19.5 s, half a second per rank. It is the only skill in the kit whose visible numbers scale continuously. Text: for 10 s, your attack increases by 10% and your accuracy by 100. What it is for: your only damage window, and the reason for the PvP rule “retreat when the buff ends”: outside it you are naked. With the level-16 node it stops being a buff and becomes the button that reorders your whole bar, because it shaves 10 s off everything. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -10% mana cost / +10% additional attack / +500 max MP. 12: +10% combat speed. 16: -10 s to all cooldowns. For the party: nothing. It is a strictly personal buff, and it is the reason Sorcerer brings nothing to a composition beyond its own damage.

Hellfire (지옥의 화염)

Section titled “Hellfire (지옥의 화염)”

Kind: active, charge skill · Cost: 200 MP · Cast: 0.5 s / 1 s / 1.5 s (3 stages) · Cooldown: 45 s · Range: 20 m · Target: up to 4 enemies around the target · Properties: charge skill Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals fire damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target at 20 m. Maximum 3 charge stages. Groggy gauge damage: 20 to 35. What it is for: the biggest hit in the kit and the one that defines the whole rotation. The Korean patch of 2026-03-25 doubled the per-stage charge bonus from 20% to 40%, which made the full charge compulsory (KR, 2026-03-24). It cannot go in a macro: a macro fires stage 1 only and leaves you the weakest of the three. It is the single most repeated rule in this class’s entire corpus. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): applies Fire Mark / on hit, fire damage over time for 10 s / usable while moving. 12: ignores block and evasion, lands as multi-hit. 16: -15 s cooldown. Known trap: do not take the damage-over-time specialty. It only pays on dummies and stationary targets (KR, 2026-07). And a Korean endgame measurement adds that the slot-5 specialty is worth about 28 hits with it and near-identical without it, so in mechanic-heavy fights it gets traded for mobility (KR, 2026-09-17). Minor mismatch: the text says 4 m and the entry lists no area field. We go with the text.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Properties: non-targeted · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, airborne-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. Text: clears your stun, knockdown, airborne bind, grab, freeze and fear states and gives you Tenacity (강인함) for 5 s. Tenacity: immunity to status effects. What it is for: it is your only self-cleanse and, in a class with no dash and no gap-closer, one of only two movement tools you own. Get it off the space bar on day one: mashing space to escape a stun summons your wings (Global, 2026-09-18). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Tenacity, +50% PvE damage tolerance and +25% PvP / on cast, restores 1000 stamina / on cast, restores 20% of max HP. 12: +2 s Tenacity. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Curse: Ancient Tree (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

All four chains are buttons that only exist after the skill that triggers them, and all four activate at 100%. One database detail: chains have no specialties of their own, they display their parent skill’s. What you pick on Flame Arrow applies to both of its links too.

Detonation (작렬)

Section titled “Detonation (작렬)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals fire damage to a target within 20 m and restores 100 MP. What it is for: second hit of the left click. Same mana as the first. It is not a decision, it is the rhythm. Chained from: Flame Arrow, position 1.

Heat Surge (열화)

Section titled “Heat Surge (열화)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals fire damage to a target within 20 m and restores 120 MP. What it is for: it is the important link in the whole class and almost nobody frames it that way. The two specialties that turn Flame Arrow into the fire engine (apply Fire Mark at 12, reset Blaze at 16) fire here, on the third hit, not the first. Cut the chain and you have no engine. Chained from: Flame Arrow, position 2.

Cold Wave (냉기 파동)

Section titled “Cold Wave (냉기 파동)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Area: 4 m, up to 4 enemies · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals water damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target at 20 m. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the second hit of the ice AoE. Note what it does not do: it does not slow. The slow is on Ice Chain only, and this link costs the same 120 MP as its trigger. It is the most expensive chain in the class relative to what it brings. Chained from: Ice Chain, position 1.

Curse: Ancient Tree (저주: 고목)

Section titled “Curse: Ancient Tree (저주: 고목)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 20 m · Target: single Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: turns a target within 20 m into a tree for 7 s with -20% fire resistance, and displaces you backwards. The tree loses the effect if it takes a hit. What it is for: this is the most ignored thing in the kit. It is not a loose 2-minute skill: it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a control effect, which means every time a melee stuns you in PvP he is handing you a backward displacement plus a -20% fire-resistance debuff on himself. In a class with no dash and no escape, that step back is literally half your mobility. The 2-minute cooldown is what stops you building anything on top of it. Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Robe of Flame (불꽃의 로브)

Section titled “Robe of Flame (불꽃의 로브)”

Text: your accuracy increases by 100, your PvE damage amplification by 1%, your PvP by 0.5% and your Smite by 0.2%. At rank 20, +20% PvE, +10% PvP and +4% Smite. What it is for: it is the only passive that matters for damage, and the number-one Korean build says so without hedging: the rest of the points go to resistances (KR, 2026-08-01). The scaling explains why: going from 1% to 20% PvE amplification is the biggest percentage jump in the whole kit. Note that accuracy does not scale: it stays at 100 across all 20 ranks.

Grace of Enhancement (강화의 은혜)

Section titled “Grace of Enhancement (강화의 은혜)”

Text: when your mana is at 25% or more, your PvE damage amplification increases by 20% and your PvP by 10%, and on an attack hit you have a 50% chance of dealing bonus damage. Internal cooldown: 1 s. What it is for: it is an early levelling priority because it is flat: it does not scale with rank, so the first point gives you the full effect. Anything you put in above rank 1 only buys the point requirement, not more effect. Data note: the database threshold is 25% mana. The Korean guide from 2025 says 67%. Two numbers for the same condition, and nobody has reconciled them.

Robe of Earth (대지의 로브)

Section titled “Robe of Earth (대지의 로브)”

Text: your max mana increases by 7%, your mana regeneration by 5, and when your mana is at 50% or more your crit increases by 105. At rank 20, +45%, +100 and +200. What it is for: your entire mana economy, and at rank 20 also 200 free crit. The Element Enhancement stigma multiplies its max mana and its regeneration by 1.5 while it lasts.

Fire Mark (불의 표식)

Section titled “Fire Mark (불의 표식)”

Text: every time a fire attack lands, 20% chance to leave the target in the Fire Mark state for 5 s. Fire Mark: if the target takes fire damage while marked, 20% chance of bonus damage. Internal cooldown: 1 s. What it is for: it is the passive that opens Blaze’s door, not a damage source. And it is worth lowering your expectations: a Korean endgame measurement puts it at roughly 2% of total damage and 0.01% final damage per reforge level, with the explicit conclusion not to chase that roll (KR, 2026-09-17). Version warning: the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 raised that chance from 20% to 100% and reworked the follow-up effect into a flat +5% fire damage amplification. The Inven dump of 2026-09-22 still shows the old numbers, and that patch is Chapter 1, so almost certainly not in Global Season 1 (KR, 2026-09-16). Data note: the database says 20% bonus damage on a marked target; the Korean guide from 2025 said 10%.

Robe of Frost (냉기의 로브)

Section titled “Robe of Frost (냉기의 로브)”

Text: your defense increases by 1%, your crit resistance by 55, and every time an enemy attacks you, you slow it for 2 s at -25% movement speed. Internal cooldown: 10 s. At rank 20, +20% defense, +150 crit resistance and a 55% slow for 6.5 s. What it is for: the only reactive passive you have, and the only passive defence against the thing that kills you, which is a melee on top of you. The Arctic Armor stigma multiplies its effect by 1.5 while it lasts.

Section titled “Combos”

(a) The chains the database defines

Section titled “(a) The chains the database defines”

There are only three chain routes in the whole Sorcerer kit, and they cover four skills. Everything else the guides call a “combo” is a condition or a macro priority, not a chain.

Trigger Link 1 Link 2 Activation
Flame Arrow (불꽃 화살) Detonation (작렬) Heat Surge (열화) 100%
Ice Chain (얼음 사슬) Cold Wave (냉기 파동) 100%
Shock Release (충격 해제) Curse: Ancient Tree (저주: 고목) 100%, gated on Shock Release having fired

There are also two further links that do not exist as skills in the database and only appear if you buy the matching specialty: Winter Phantom (겨울의 환영), Winter Shackles’ second link with the level-12 node, and Fall (나락), Lumiel’s Space’s second link with the level-10 node. Neither has an entry, a cost or any numbers.

Three readings that come out of this and not out of any guide:

  • The left-click chain is not a mana economy, it is the fire engine. Yes, it is 100 + 100 + 120 MP per full sequence, but the important part is elsewhere: Flame Arrow’s level-12 and level-16 specialties fire off Heat Surge, the third hit. Cutting the chain switches off Fire Mark application and the Blaze reset at the same time, and those are the two pieces the whole fire rotation hangs from.
  • The ice chain is the worst of the three. Cold Wave costs the same 120 MP as its trigger and does not carry the slow, which is the only thing that makes Ice Chain valuable. You pay full price for half the effect.
  • Curse: Ancient Tree is the only chain that triggers off a defensive skill, exactly like the Templar’s Seize Alive. Do not put it in a macro expecting it to fire on its own: it only exists in the window after you break a control effect. And on a 2-minute cooldown it is not a rotational tool, it is a card you play once per fight.

One warning from the macro mechanic itself: only links with near-100% activation deserve a macro slot. A 15% link more than halved a Taiwanese Cleric’s dummy DPS (TW, 2026-03-18). All three of Sorcerer’s are 100%, so there is no trap here.

(b) The cooldown economy, which is the entire class

Section titled “(b) The cooldown economy, which is the entire class”

The Templar has a Judgment loop. The Sorcerer has something stranger: a network of skills that cut each other’s cooldowns, and learning the class is learning to make four timers converge on the same 20-second window. This is everything the database publishes about it:

Skill Specialty What it cuts
Firestorm 12 -2 s off Hellfire per harpoon
Blaze 16 -3 s off Wish of Concentration per hit
Flame Arrow (via Heat Surge) 16 resets Blaze’s cooldown
Wish of Concentration 16 -10 s off every cooldown
Flame Scatter Shot 16 -1 s off all cooldowns per hit
Ice Chain 12 -1 s off Winter Shackles
Bitter Cold Wind 16 -1 s off Frost
Frost 8 resets Frost Burst on a freeze
Frost Burst 16 50% chance to reset itself

What that means in practice. Hellfire comes out of the database with a 45 s cooldown. Firestorm fires 5 harpoons per cast according to the Korean guides, and at -2 s each that is -10 s per Firestorm cast, which itself has a 5 s cooldown. The Korean figure from 2025 describes the drop as 41 s, then 28, then 24, then 13 after three Firestorm casts (KR, 2025-11). That sequence matches neither the database’s 45 s nor a flat 10 s cut, and nobody has reconciled it; the order of magnitude does agree.

The point where the class changes. Two independent Korean creators put the jump at Firestorm rank 12, which is when the Hellfire cut appears: before that the class plays with no cycle, after it it turns into a burst DPS (KR, 2025-11-20 and 2025-12-01). This explains the universal complaint that Sorcerer “levels badly”: it is not that it hits softly, it is that its central mechanic does not exist yet.

And here is this page’s data problem. The Inven dump stops at rank 10 on Firestorm and only on Firestorm. Its level-12 and level-16 specialties are listed on the entry, but there is no rank 11 to 20 row backing them, while the other 38 skills carry all 20 ranks. We do not know whether it is a fault of the extraction or of Inven itself. We leave it as it is and flag it: the heart of this class’s rotation is exactly the data that is missing.

What this research round added about macros, and it is the most actionable part of the page (KR, 2026-09-22):

  • One queued row only. Two or more rows cut the per-slot hit count, and the drop-off is worst for Sorcerer of any class. If you need two, split one into the game macro and one into the mouse macro.
  • Click delay of 50 to 100 ms. Bind the actives to mouse buttons and leave the keyboard for movement.
  • Best-tested damage-line order: Bitter Cold Wind → Blaze → Firestorm → Wish of Concentration.
  • Opener: Element Enhancement → the Delayed Explosion buff → full-charge Hellfire → lay Firewall and Cold Storm → standard loop.

Skill Reservation (스킬 예약) is the hidden switch, and it fails silently (KR, 2026-09-21). With Ice Chain on your bar it must be OFF: with it ON, the macro fires Ice Chain only and the rest of your skills never come out, with no warning at all. If you drop Ice Chain from the bar, set it to ON, which is what lets a hand-pressed control (the Kangbo shield, Hibernation) fire instantly while the held macro keeps running. Zero delay values work on both Razer and Logitech.

Point cost and the Season 1 cap: taking a skill from rank 1 to 10 is 21 points; 11 to 20 costs none. The Global Season 1 cap is 20: 10 from points, +4 from Daevanion to 14, and the rest from soul-bind and Arcana. The point sources are region quests and sealed dungeons; Global’s Monolith pays +1 against Korea and Taiwan’s +4. Skill point resets are free on Global (Global, LST 2026-09-17/19). The skills the Korean build marks as mandatory at 20 are Hellfire, Blaze and Wish of Concentration, then Firestorm, Flame Arrow and Bitter Cold Wind (KR, 2026-08-01).

The practical reading: Soul Freeze and Lumiel’s Space are your real control; Frost and the Curse line are bets. Betting the opener on the transformation without stacking status accuracy is this class’s most repeated PvP mistake (KR, 2025-11).

You have no gap-closer and no dash escape. Your only two movement tools are Curse: Tree’s backward displacement and Curse: Ancient Tree’s, the Shock Release chain. The entire defensive plan is preventing contact, not surviving it: once a Templar, a Gladiator or an Assassin is on top of you, the fight is decided (KR, 2025-11-22). Hibernation is the exception, and it only buys 5 s.

One piece of good news for a European reader. The reading that Sorcerer suffers on high ping was wrong, and this round flipped it: on Taiwan, where there is no global cooldown, latency costs you actions per second, and that punishes whoever cancels animations (Assassin, Gladiator), not whoever charges a server-resolved nuke. Korea shipped a global cooldown on 2026-08-26 and nobody has checked the Global client, so the premise is Taiwanese. A purpose-built ping tier list puts Sorcerer first, as the least ping-impacted class with the highest damage at maximum latency (TW, 2026-09-22), and two independent Global sources point the same way.

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Hellfire. It is a 3-stage charge; the macro fires stage 1 only, so you trade your biggest hit for the smallest of the three and throw away the 40% per-stage bonus the 2026-03-25 patch put there. It is the most repeated rule in this class’s entire corpus.
  • More than one row. Two or more rows cut the hits per slot, and Sorcerer is the class it hurts most. If you need two, one goes in the game macro and one in the mouse macro.
  • Ice Chain with Skill Reservation ON. It does not perform worse: the rest of the macro stops firing, silently.
  • Reactive defensives: Steel Barrier, Arctic Armor, Hibernation, Shock Release and the Kangbo shield. Their entire value is the instant you press them.
  • Ground zones in content with movement (Firewall, Cold Storm, Bitter Cold Wind). A macro lays them where the boss was, not where it is going.
  • Any conditional below the filler. Blaze, Frost Burst and Flame Scatter Shot in a low slot are skills that never fire. That is not a damage loss, it is a dead skill.
  • Passives, which simply cannot be put in one.

Its level-12 and level-16 specialties are listed, but no entry backs them. It is precisely the skill that holds up this class’s cooldown cycle. Cause unknown.

Lumiel’s Space or Linear Space. The database Korean is 루미엘의 공간, literally “Lumiel’s space”, and that is the name we use. Our earlier synthesis called it “Linear Space”, a name that shows up only through a coincidence of the described mechanic and never as a translation. The pairing is unconfirmed until a Global client exists.

Six database numbers that disagree with the Korean guides from 2025, none of them reconciled: Blaze’s lifesteal is 3% in the data and 30% in the guide; Grace of Enhancement’s threshold is 25% mana in the data and 67% in the guide; Fire Mark’s bonus damage is 20% in the data and 10% in the guide; Hellfire has a 45 s cooldown in the data and 41 s in the guide; Delayed Explosion applies +15% damage taken from the caster in the data and “-20% tolerance” in the guide; and Glacial Smite is spelled 빙하 강타 in the data and 빙아 강타 in our synthesis.

Who Winter Shackles’ specialty amplifies. The Korean text of its third level-8 option reads “+20% PvE damage amplification and +10% PvP for 5 s” without naming the beneficiary. Everywhere else in the database that term is a caster stat; our own synthesis read it as a debuff on the target. There is no way to settle it without the client.

The missing 40% in Arctic Armor. Its Mana Conversion sends 50% of incoming damage to HP and 10% to mana. That is 60%, not 100%. Either the remaining 40% disappears, and then it is by far the strongest mitigation in the class, or the text is incomplete. Nobody has measured it live.

The English names. Of the 39 skills, Inven gives an English name for only 12. The rest are our translations or names taken from guides, and NCsoft has not published the Global localisation. The most visible case is 불꽃 작살, which Inven calls Flame Harpoon and every guide calls Firestorm. Check it during Advanced Access from 30 September.

Which stigma goes in which slot. Global opens the four slots at character level 22, 27, 32 and 37, and no source says which to put in which. The recommended four are clear; the unlock order is not.

The Korean patch of 2026-09-16. It raised Fire Mark’s proc from 20% to 100% and turned its follow-up effect into a flat +5%. The 2026-09-22 dump still shows the old numbers, and the patch is Chapter 1, so it is almost certainly not in Global Season 1. NC has said balance will be identical across regions from now on, so it will arrive eventually; just not at launch.

The multi-Sorcerer Frost Burst bug. On KR live, when several Sorcerers hit the same boss, only the one with the highest Frost Burst level applies its damage and everyone else’s is voided. That is a tenth of your total damage. Whether the bug exists in the Global build is unknown (KR, 2026-09-17).
