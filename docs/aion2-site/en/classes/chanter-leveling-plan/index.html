Chanter: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Inven Korean skill data of 2026-09-22 over the KR/TW syntheses to 2026-09-16 and the Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the Dark Crush proc window and the macros live on the Chanter page. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  4. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen on Global. Chanter is the one class where the sources still say pour them into a single stigma first: Undefeated Mantra to max is unanimous across every Korean guide in the corpus (KR, 2025-12 to 2026-09).
  5. The four-stigma sets on this page are an inference, not a sourced fact. No source anywhere in this corpus gives a Global 4-slot Chanter loadout. What exists is Korean and Taiwanese 5-slot and 6-slot builds, and the derivation from those to four is done here, in the open, in the loadout table.
  6. Two of your slots are permanent toggles (Undefeated Mantra and Sprint Mantra). That is why the staggered unlock at 22/27/32/37 hurts Chanter more than most classes: it delays the flexible slots, not the fixed ones (Global, 2026-09-19).
  7. Korean priority lists target the 50-level curve, where skill level 12 and 16 sit inside the plan. On Global, points stop at 10, so any Korean instruction of the form “take this to 12 first” is not a thing you can do with points.
  8. Sixteen of the 39 skills have an English name in the Korean database; the rest are translations. Check the name in your own client before you spend.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: the basic chain, the heal, and the mana wall

Section titled “1 to 22: the basic chain, the heal, and the mana wall”
  • Skills, in this order: Incredible Blow (격파쇄), the basic chain that is the whole engine: instant, 4 m, up to 4 targets, and it returns 100 mental power per hit (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). Both of its 100% chains (공명쇄 and 벽력쇄) share its level and its specialisations, so the one investment buys three buttons.
  • Second: Recuperation (쾌유의 주문), the main heal. Self and party in 25 m, cleanses one debuff, ticks every 2 s for 8 s, 15 s recast. Korean sources from launch say to take the heal to 12 first for the continuous-healing window (KR, 2025-11), and on Global you cannot: 12 is past the point cap. Take it to 8 and get the rest of the way from boards and Arcana.
  • Third: Dark Crush (암격쇄), 20 m, 5 s recast. It only fires at an incapacitated target, plus a 7% chance to fire itself free against one even off cooldown (KR, 2026-08). Everything the class does later is built on keeping this state lit.
  • Fourth: Spin Strike (회전격), and read this before you build round it: since the Korean rework of 2026-09-01/02 it is a 20 m ranged AoE, 30 s recast, 250 mental power, and because it counts as a ranged normal skill it became a fourth Dark Crush trigger. Two creators verified on their own Chanters that this version is already in the Global build (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-19). Anything you read that calls it a 4 m melee skill on a 10-15 s cooldown is out of date.
  • Fifth: Charge Strike (돌진 격파, called Rushing Smash by nearly every video guide), 20 m dash, 15 s, hits 4, returns 100 mental power. Take it to 8 (TW, 2026-09-14).
  • Mana is the wall, not survival. While gear is weak, take the mental-power specialisations over the damage ones: Incredible Blow’s level-8 +20% mental power recovery, and Incandescent Blow’s (백열격) level-8 -20% cost. Swap both for damage once gear carries you (TW, 2026-09-14; KR, 2026-01).
  • Passives: Attack Readiness (공격 준비), Blessing of Life (생명의 축복), Impact Hit (충격 적중), then Spell of Inspiration (고취의 주문, crit and Perfect) (KR, 2025-12 to 2026-03). Passives have no specialisation on this class, so every point in them is a plain stat.
  • The passive you never touch: Cross Guard (십자 방어), zero points. Five independent Korean and Taiwanese guides name it as worthless, and one of them says to reset it whatever it cost you (KR/TW, 2026-08 to 2026-09-14). The block-shield passive Protective Ward (보호진) is the second skip.
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens around level 12-13 and is worth “roughly a third of your early power” (Global, 2026-09-19). Blue nodes first (active-skill level): on Global the orange cores lost between a third and a half of their value while a blue node still buys a whole skill level (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Gear: staff or rod (법봉), all disposable, nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20). Belt and Amulet are the permanent exception.
  • Gate before you move on: level 22 has a red quest that blocks the main story until the world-progress bar is at 100%, filled with regional quests (J key) and sealed dungeons. Do the sealed dungeons: since Wisdom Stones moved off the feather and Monolith route, they are where your skill levels come from, and they are +2 skill and +2 Daevanion each (Global, 2026-09-18).

Do not:

  • Believe this class starts slow. A timed playtest run took a Chanter from 1 to cap in 4 h 16 min against 5 h 29 min on a Ranger, a 73-minute gap the creator credits to constant cleave plus built-in healing removing the downtime between pulls (Global, 2026-09-20). A separate all-eight-classes pass lists Chanter among the four that levelled easily (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Assume the meter will be kind later because it is kind now. With all eight classes at level 37 and gear equalised, Chanter posted the highest one-minute dummy result of the eight, 564,000 against Templar’s 432,000 (Global, 2026-09-17, single source). The same creator calls Chanter a low-DPS class at the Korean endgame, and the Taiwanese P90 figure is 83.3% of baseline, eighth of nine (TW, 2026-08-25). Both are true, at different brackets.
  • Skip green side quests: each one is +1 skill point, +1 Daevanion point and +3 permanent passive stat points (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Spend a key on a yellow cube: yellow cubes open for free (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Put block, just-dodge or the dash in a macro. They stay manual all the way to 45, and the in-game macro excludes defensives, bursts, blocks and movement skills by design anyway (TW, 2026-09-20).

22 to 27: first stigma slot, and the one stigma everybody agrees on

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot, and the one stigma everybody agrees on”
  • Stigma, slot 1: Undefeated Mantra (불패의 진언). This is the least contested call in the entire class: every Korean guide in the corpus puts it first and says take it to max before anything else (KR, 2025-12 to 2026-09-14). It is a toggle with no upkeep cost, it buffs the whole party, and its steps are +100 crit at 5, +100 accuracy at 10, +5% crit damage amp at 15 and +5% Heavy Strike at 20 (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). A Chanter alt is described as easy to get invited once this buff sits at 15-20 (KR, 2026).
  • The exception to the usual shard advice: every other class’s plan says park a stigma at 5 until you know your four. Chanter’s sources say the opposite for this one skill. At 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 shards a level, reaching 15 costs 35 shards and 20 costs 75 (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), and the whole levelling run yields on the order of 20 from quests (KR). Expect 10 to 15 by 45, not 20.
  • Skills: push Incredible Blow, Recuperation, Dark Crush, Spin Strike and Charge Strike towards 8 (13 points each, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), then place the specialisation: unlocking it and placing it are two separate steps in the UI.
  • Which level-8 specialisation to take while levelling (one per skill, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22):
    • Incredible Blow: +20% mental power recovery while gear is weak, 1.5% lifesteal if you are dying, +50% multi-hit once neither is a problem.
    • Dark Crush: convert to AoE while levelling, since you are clearing packs, not fighting bosses. Guaranteed crit is the boss answer and the 1.5% lifesteal is the panic answer.
    • Spin Strike: -5 s recast. On a 30 s skill that is the one that changes how the rotation feels.
    • Charge Strike: 50% chance to activate Scorch Strike on hit, which is how you get a free block-gated skill without blocking.
    • Recuperation: +1 consecutive cast.
  • Daevanion: blue nodes on Dark Crush and Spin Strike first, then attack, then attack speed, not cooldown reduction (TW, 2026-09-14).
  • Gear: Crow Cave at level 22 asks for around 700 on Global, against 800-1,000 on Korea and Taiwan (Global, 2026-09-18). Enhancement cap is still +5 green / +10 blue.
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: push one skill to 10 before the others are at 8; spread shards across several stigmas at once; copy a 5-stigma or 6-stigma build without cutting it.

27 to 32: second slot, and the buff that is not yours

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot, and the buff that is not yours”
  • Stigma, slot 2: Power of the Storm (질풍의 권능, also Gale’s Power). 2 min recast, and its level-5 step cuts that by 30 s, which is the single best shard spend in the class after Undefeated (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). It gives the party +5% weapon and magic damage amp, +200 accuracy and 10-20% cooldown reduction for 20 s, verified in a live party test (KR, 2026-07).
  • Learn its timing now, not at 45: cast it before you pull, so its cooldown reduction front-loads onto your teammates’ openers, and refresh it at the 16-17 s mark of its 20 s window (KR, 2026-06). Your best button is the one whose value shows up on somebody else’s bar.
  • Skills: the rest of the core actives to 8, then the main three towards 10, which is where the points run out. Dark Crush and Spin Strike are the two the Korean post-rework builds take highest (KR, 2026-08-31 and 09-02).
  • Shock Release (충격 해제) to 8 if you are dying: 1 min, self-only, clears stun, knockdown, airbind, grab, freeze and fear, and grants 5 s of status immunity. Its level-8 options are +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage resistance during that window, or 20% of max HP restored on cast (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). Korean practice is to eat the CC chain with this and keep attacking, and to reserve blocking and just-dodging for the attacks that would hard-CC you (KR, 2026-08-20).
  • Daevanion: keep the Naizerkan lane on attack then attack speed; start the Ezekiel lane on the Incredible Blow and Charge Strike damage line, skipping the Impactful Heat node; work all boards in parallel rather than finishing one (TW, 2026-09-14).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most.
  • Gate before you move on: 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score on Global against 1,600 on Korea and Taiwan (one source read 1,500) (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take Binding Brand (결박의 낙인) for PvE. It has “no PvE use case” in one Korean guide, and the creator who floated it as a high-ceiling pick walked it back three days later at a practical use rate under twice a minute (KR, 2026-08-31 to 09-02).

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar filled with exploration and side quests, then the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18). Side quests come back around 32-33 after being sparse since 21 (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Stigma, slot 3: the role fork. For dungeons and raids, Blocking Power (차단의 권능): a party save, +15% defence at 5, +10% incoming healing at 10, +10% PvE and +5% PvP damage tolerance at 15 (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). For PvP, Marcutan’s Wrath (마르쿠탄의 분노): the Dark Crush extender, 20 m, 30 s, with a 50% 3 s stun at its step 10.
  • Skills: what the points cannot buy. From here the levels come from the four boards, Arcana cards and ring and weapon soul bindings (Global LST, 2026-09-18 and 09-20). What you are chasing with them, in order: Dark Crush at 16 (재시전 시간 삭제, its recast is deleted), Charge Strike at 12 (recast resets on kill, which is the mob-to-mob chaining the whole farming reputation rests on) and Wave Blow at 12 (파동격: a 10 s party-wide window where the target takes +20% PvE and +10% PvP damage, described as near-permanent uptime in progression content) (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; KR, 2026-06).
  • Daevanion: the Weisel lane, critical damage, trading the HP-only Blessing node for the HP, crit and mental-power hybrid, because you are not the party’s primary healer (TW, 2026-09-14).
  • Gear: 1,000 is the first Ascension Trial rank’s requirement and the reason not to rush the story. It also drops shards, which is your Undefeated budget (Global, 2026-09-18/19).
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2-star; 1,000 for the Ascension Trial.

Do not: route a Daevanion path through a node you do not want. Going around a bad one, Cross Guard being the named example, can cost about 15 extra points on the shortest path (TW, 2026-09-14).

37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at level 37. This is the flexible one, and the mode decides it: Barrier Spell (결계의 주문) for group content you are under-geared for, a party-wide 20% HP floor for 3-5 s in 40 m and then a heal plus resist bonus on expiry, called near-mandatory by the creators covering the patch that added it (KR, 2026-03-25). Hand of Grace (쾌유의 손길) if the party needs the cleanse and the stamina. Sprint Mantra (질주의 진언) if you are mostly solo. Crushing Strike (분쇄격) if you are mostly hitting things.
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40 (Global, 2026-09-17). The Asphel (PvP) board opens at 45; the PvE board was not in the test build.
  • Skills: the core ones towards 20 through boards, Arcana and soul bindings. Keep any gear piece that grants +levels to a skill you actually press instead of disenchanting it (KR, 2025-11).
  • The levelling specialisation you will undo later: the mental-power recovery options on Incredible Blow and Incandescent Blow, and any lifesteal you took to survive. All of it becomes damage and multi-hit at 45. The point respec is free, so testing it is cheap (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21).
  • The macro rule inverted in September, so build the macro last. Korean doctrine is now to put fewer skills in one macro, because every extra slot reduces how often the others fire: basic attack, then the skill macro, then Windrage Fury, with a small input delay for stability (KR, 2026-09-17). The game ships its own macro system, one hotkey with a priority order that auto-cycles while held, and it excludes defensives, bursts, blocks and movement by design (TW, 2026-09-20; Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-21).
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20).
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100 (one source read 2,200); Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: expect gear to feel rewarding the way it does on a DPS. Two independent sources say the same thing about this class: your main job is buffing, and buffing does not visibly scale with your gear, so the numbers on your screen move less than a damage dealer’s for the same investment (KR, 2026-09-22; TW, 2026-09-21).

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21). On Chanter the trade is specific: out go the mental-power recovery and lifesteal options you levelled on, in come multi-hit, guaranteed crits and the AoE-to-single-target swaps.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are paid in Stigma Shards, one stigma from 1 to 20 is 75 shards counted straight out of the rank table (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), and levelling to 45 while doing every side quest yields on the order of 20 (KR). Nobody has seen the reset cost on Global. The only number that exists anywhere is Korean and from launch, 480,000 Kinah against 26,500 for a skill reset (KR, 2025-11), and it does not transfer.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes. On Korea they refund at 500 Kinah each and a whole branch for ~13,500 (KR, 2025-11), removed from the branch tips inwards.

The three level-45 loadouts

Section titled “The three level-45 loadouts”

You get three presets (1/2/3), which on Global save skill placement, skill level and macro together (Global, 2026-09-20). What is not known is whether they save stigma level. Until that is confirmed, the three setups below share as many equipped stigmas as they can, or you run out of shards.

How these four-stigma sets were derived, because the derivation is the honest part of this table. No Global-scoped 4-slot Chanter loadout exists in any source in this corpus. What exists is:

  • a Korean PvE core of Undefeated + Sprint Mantra + Power of the Storm always slotted, with a fourth and fifth rotating among Blocking Power, Marcutan’s Wrath, Barrier Spell and Focused Defense (KR, 2026-06 and 2026-09);
  • a second Korean guide running a four-name Transcendence set, Undefeated at 20 + Power of the Storm + Hand of Grace + Blocking Power, which is the closest thing to a Global-shaped list anybody published (KR, 2026);
  • a Korean PvP core of Undefeated + Blocking Power + Marcutan’s Wrath fixed, with the last slot Focused Defense on low floors, Sprint on mid floors and Barrier Spell for players comfortable with its timing (KR, 2026-06). That list is already four, so the PvP column below needed no cutting;
  • a Korean levelling and farming set of Undefeated + Sprint as permanent toggles plus two flexible damage slots when solo (KR, 2025-11), which is also already four.

So the only column genuinely cut from six to four is the PvE one, and what it drops is Sprint Mantra: the mantra whose payoff is movement speed and an on-hit heal chance, the cheapest of the three permanent toggles to lose when a party already has a Cleric. That call is an inference made on this page, not something a source says. If you disagree with it, the swap to make is Sprint in and Blocking Power out, and no source in this corpus settles the argument.

What is still disputed or unconfirmed

The four-stigma sets on this page are an inference. No source in this corpus gives a Global-scoped 4-slot Chanter loadout, and none says which stigma belongs in which unlock slot at 22, 27, 32 or 37. What the sources give is Korean and Taiwanese 5-slot and 6-slot builds; the cut to four, and the slot order in the level bands, are reasoning done here. The derivation is written out above the loadout table so you can disagree with it in the right place.

Gale’s Charge and Windrage Fury may or may not be the same skill. The Korean synthesis names 질풍남무 (Gale’s Charge) as the single most valuable level-16 investment in the class, because it shortens Power of the Storm’s cooldown and therefore everyone else’s cooldowns. The only similarly spelled skill in the 2026-09-22 Korean database, 질풍 난무 (Windrage Fury), is a groggy-target execute with no cooldown-reduction line at all. These may be a transcription variant of one skill or two different skills. This page does not merge them and does not build a plan on either.

Impactful Crash does not resolve either. Two English sources describe a skill whose cooldown went from 10 s to 50 s in the 2026-09-01/02 patch and which was used as a cheap Dark Crush trigger. Nothing in the current Korean data matches that description; 강격쇄 (Forceful Strike), a 2-minute chain off Shock Release, is the nearest candidate and is not a confirmed match. Relatedly, 강격쇄 carries Shock Release’s specialisation block verbatim in the data, which is almost certainly a data-entry artefact rather than a shared-specialisation mechanic.

No full 1-45 skill-point table for Global exists. Every detailed priority list in the corpus targets the Korean and Taiwanese 50-level curve, where 12 and 16 are inside the point budget, and several of them contradict each other because the class was reworked at least four times between 2026-07 and 2026-09. The cheat sheet above is reconstructed, not published by anyone.

The Marcutan-is-no-longer-mandatory finding is Korean endgame only. Its stated payoff is freeing a slot for mechanic-skip utility in Sanctuary-tier content, which Global Season 1 does not have at launch (KR, 2026-09-17). That is why Marcutan stays in the rotating slot here.

The attack-speed cap is unresolved, so this page quotes no breakpoint. The figures in circulation are 122% (KR, 2026-09-02), a Korean board thread titled 116.7 with units unstated (KR, 2026-09-22) and a Korean patch numbered 122.6 that broke an attack-speed-piercing effect (KR, 2026-09-17). They may not even describe the same cap.

The Korean 2026-09-16 patch cut Blessing of Guardian’s duration and recast from 5 minutes to 2, the only Chanter line in it. Whether that, or the two bug fixes confirmed on Korea a day later, are in the Global build is checked by nobody in this corpus.

The cost of resetting stigmas on Global has not been seen by anybody. Resetting skill points is free, but it is a different system.

The PvE Daevanion board was not in the test build. Its point sinks still exist in the Nightmare shop and in reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch. Do not plan Season 1 counting on it.

The Charge Strike name is unresolved. The Korean database calls it Charge Strike; nearly every video guide calls the same skill Rushing Smash. Sixteen of the 39 skills have an English name in that data at all, so most of the names on this page are translations, not attested client strings.
