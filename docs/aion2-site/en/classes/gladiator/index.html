Gladiator

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Global equips only 4 Stigmas against Taiwan’s 6, so the Taiwanese build below does not fit, and the core cancel mechanic is still moving: Korea added cancel delays to Templar and Assassin on September 16 and left Gladiator alone. Short guide; to be expanded later.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 41 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Gladiator (검성, greatsword, Warrior line with Templar) is the damage tank: melee bruiser and off-tank, sustained AoE with lifesteal, not a burst-delete class.
  • The safest strong pick in the game right now: S-tier in PvE and PvP at once, “no major weaknesses”, 128 PvE leaderboard appearances, second only to Ranger. Read that as consistency, not ceiling: Gladiator tops one-minute dummy parses and falls to 6th by median in endgame Expedition and Conquest, because its one real weakness is melee uptime against bosses that keep leaving weapon range.
  • The easiest class to execute: 4.25/10, the easiest of eight. Competitive PvP without a mechanical tax.
  • 1v1 king (70.1% win rate, statistically tied with Templar; KR/TW, 2026-07) and the most represented class at the top of the solo PvP leaderboard, roughly a third of the highest-ranked players (2026-09). It stays strong for solo Abyss farming and drops to B-tier only in large-scale Abyss (KDA 9.89) and co-op arena (37.8%), where ranged classes win. Since Abyss points scale with your share of each kill (Global, 2026-09), small fights are the efficient ones anyway.
  • Versus Templar, the other half of the Warrior line. Gladiator wins single-target damage by 15-20%, has 6 gap closers vs 2, +20% permanent move speed and clears Ascension Trial in 49-57 s vs 57 s-1:07; Templar wins survival, threat and defensive support. They also cancel each other out: its party buff Experienced Counter Strike (노련한 반격) and Templar’s Fury are the same 20 s frontal buff and do not stack, so one of the two is wasted in the same party.
  • You cannot opt out of tanking: aggro generation is built into its damage skills and cannot be disabled.
  • This class is played through a macro. Global ships an official in-game one (Global LST 2026-09-17/19): priority-ordered rows on a single held key, and a full Global build guide was made on it alone, no external device. Korea still spams Overhead Slam from a hardware mouse macro (Logitech G HUB, Razer) because it produces the highest hit count, ~95-100 taps per rotation. The developers have said they want to move away from the macro meta; nothing has changed yet.
  • Build volatility is the real risk: every macro guide older than three weeks is wrong.
  • Verdict: the better solo, 1v1 and leveling choice and the worse group-utility choice. The worst alt in the game (severe late gatekeeping) and the best first main.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Greatsword (대검) only; no polearm and no weapon swap.
  • Sustained damage, never burst; survival by lifesteal, not mitigation: it must keep attacking to live and struggles in mechanics where it cannot deal damage.
  • The class is built on one skill: Overhead Slam (내려찍기), with a 7% proc chance per hit against bosses, and a specialization that removes its cooldown entirely once triggered, so it can be spammed. Rage Burst (경로 폭발) is the stigma that bypasses that 7% gate and opens 10 s of uncapped Overhead Slam.
  • Ruinous Blow (파멸의 맹타) applies the headline self-buff, Prepare for Battle (전투 준비): +20% PvE damage, +10% PvP, +100 crit, +15% status accuracy, 10 s. Keeping it at ~100% uptime is the class’s execution skill.
  • Mana is solved by Rending Blow (절단의 맹타) at level 20 (~50 mana per crit).
  • Skill level only unlocks specialization slots; past 20 is wasted points. On Global, getting a skill to 20 takes three systems, not one: skill points, the Daevanion board, and Arcana cards farmed from Transcendence dungeons. A skill stalling at 15 is not capped, it is out of one of the three. Arcana upgrades that read “+1 skill level” land on a random skill in that card’s pool, not the one you picked.
  • Party value: only 3-5% more group DPS than a Templar if you stay offensive. Groups are 5 players (1 tank, 3 DPS, 1 support) and the most optimised composition is Templar + Gladiator + Chanter + Cleric + one ranged DPS (Global): the Gladiator seat sits next to the Templar, not instead of it. But it is exactly one seat: Korea’s sanctuary raids take one main tank and one sub-tank and no more (KR, 2026-09), and both fill instantly. Gladiator + Chanter is the sustain ceiling (stacked lifesteal).

PvE build (KR/TW, August-September 2026)

Section titled “PvE build (KR/TW, August-September 2026)”
  • Skills to 20 in order: Overhead Slam → Rending Blow → Ruinous Blow. The September KR “optimization project” trims this to two mandatory (Rending Blow and Overhead Slam).
  • Specializations: Overhead Slam with cooldown removal (mandatory; without it the skill “activates” then goes on full cooldown) and guaranteed crit at 20; Rending Blow with mana on crit, mobile cast and max damage; Ruinous Blow with +5 s buff duration, range and block-ignoring multi-hit; Crushing Wave with HP absorb for bosses and pool for packs; Leaping Slam AoE with pull for trash and reset-on-kill for farming; Ankle Slice to 20 m as a second gap closer. Sword Aura Rampage at 16 grants cooldown reduction on stagger, useless against unstaggerable bosses.
  • Passives (KR): Experienced Counter Strike > Murderous Burst > Attack Preparation, then Impact Hit and Weak Point Detection. With so few actives worth investing in, passives carry unusual weight.
  • Stigmas: Global equips only 4, the first arriving with the level-22 Ascension quest and the rest at 27, 32 and 37, so Taiwan’s six-slot builds do not fit. The four that fit: Rage Burst (it is what makes Overhead Slam a skill instead of a 7% lottery), Lunge Stance (max it: the level-20 50%-chance 1 s global cooldown reduction is the biggest throughput item), Zekiel’s Blessing (now a self buff, +50% stagger damage), and one of Wave Armor (personal damage), Life Stealing Blade (party lifesteal) or Focused Block (2 charges; blocks yellow floor telegraphs): the Global guides split on that fourth slot. Take the -20% MP consumption option.
  • Rotation: Zekiel’s Blessing → Lunge Stance → Wave Armor → gap-close with Leaping Slam or Ruinous Blow (triggers Prepare for Battle) → hold the macro (the Overhead Slam and Rending Blow weave, which is the hardware macro’s job: only Rending Blow belongs in the in-game one) → Rage Burst on cooldown to open the window → refresh Ruinous Blow the instant the buff drops.
  • Stats: Might > Precision > Intelligence > Constitution. God stats: Wisdom > Destruction > Time > Illusion. Multi-hit is S-tier specifically for Gladiator (fast-attacking class); Precision to 200 then dump into Attack. Soulbind: never skip damage amp, crit damage amp and Smite.
  • Leveling: at level 10 activate both event coins and the Growth Support premium activation; clear only one dungeon and one garrison with the express tickets (one auto-completes the rest); do not enhance gear before level 30 (soulbind rolls are lower value).

Mistakes

Section titled “Mistakes”
  • Leveling a skill past 20. Taking Overhead Slam without its cooldown spec. Letting Prepare for Battle drop. Playing next to a Templar expecting both buffs. Copying a TW or KR macro verbatim (tuned to 170-200 ms). Putting Overhead Slam in the in-game macro (it reduces its own taps; only Rending Blow belongs there). Adding a stigma line to the macro. Fighting from behind (the frontal damage passive moved to the front). Picking Gladiator as an alt. Bringing your PvE macro into PvP (only Rending Blow and Overhead Slam are safe to automate; everything else has to be fired by hand on the opponent’s state; KR, 2026-09). Firing Rage Burst into an opponent’s invulnerability: it does not detonate. Dodging on Korean timing: on Global the boss’s hit lands about 24 frames after the telegraph fills, against Korea’s 8, on one creator’s frame count from the Global playtest.
