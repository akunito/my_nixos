The eight classes

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline to 2026-09-16 plus the Global Launch Scale Test of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Roster, level cap and party sizes are now verified on the Global client and class balance is confirmed to track Korea’s; tier placement is still volatile and Korea is re-tuning Templar almost weekly.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • 8 classes at Global launch, not 9. Brawler (권성, gauntlets, rage gauge) reached KR on 2026-07-01 and is confirmed absent from Season 1; one estimate puts it 5-6 months out.
  • The Launch Scale Test settled the systems, not the roles (Global LST 2026-09-17/19). Twelve hours at cap 37, wiped afterwards, with the Abyss, arenas, the Battlefield, trading and the market all locked, so it showed feel, and it confirmed the roster, the level 45 cap, the 5- and 10-player group sizes and the four Stigma slots, but it could not show what a class does in the content that differentiates roles. That starts in Advanced Access (early access) on 09-30.
  • Korean class data applies better than expected; Korean economy data does not. NCsoft stated that class balance and quality of life will stay in sync across Korea, Taiwan and Global, and the Chanter’s reworked Spin Strike was already ranged in the September playtest build, so Global is not an older balance patch. What Global does change is progression and monetisation: the PvE Daevanion board was absent from the test build (its point sinks still show up in shop and reward text, so it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch), the board values are cut (damage boost 1.5% per point against Korea’s 2.5%), and there is no Heroic gear tier in Season 1.
  • Templar and Cleric, the two classes this site covers in depth, are the two nobody disputes. Templar is the only main tank; Cleric the only class with a resurrection. Every tier list, whatever its method, keeps both in guaranteed-invite territory, and both at the bottom of every raw-damage chart.
  • Tier lists contradict because they measure different things. Leaderboard presence, P90 DPS, KDA and win rate, and difficulty give four different orderings of the same patch. Read the method, not the letter.
  • For competitive legion PvP, Templar read stronger than Cleric, as of August. After July’s -30%, Templar was S-tier in PvP on several independent lists; Cleric is S in group PvP but B in 1v1 and the weakest solo-PvP class, which a Global-build source confirms. Treat the Templar half as dated: Korea has hotfixed Templar almost weekly since 2026-08-26, the 09-16 patch added cast-cancel delay to three of its core skills and halved the uptime of Noble Armor, and Korean players calculate the net as a damage loss against the pre-08-26 baseline.
  • For “what do I do”, Cleric is the lower-friction social path. Chanter and Cleric get into groups at ~300 combat power below a DPS. Cleric solo farming is the slowest in the game.
  • Neither asks much of the interface; Chanter does. Chanter is the hardest class in the game (7.75/10) precisely because it juggles melee damage, buffs, mantras and emergency heals at once.
  • Ping matters more than class for some picks, and the two readings disagree about Sorcerer. The older reading (KR creators, up to 40% DPS loss cited) names Assassin and Sorcerer as the two ping-sensitive classes, with Cleric, Chanter and Spiritmaster as the high-ping picks. The newer reading (TW, 2026-09-22) puts Sorcerer at the opposite end: with no global cooldown, latency costs actions per second, so Assassin is the worst hit and Sorcerer the least ping-impacted class in the game, with the highest damage at maximum latency. Both readings agree on Assassin. See Sorcerer. From Europe to an EU server this hardly matters either way.

The roster

Section titled “The roster”

Aion 2 keeps Aion 1’s 8 classes in 4 lines, each with a defensive or support half and an aggressive half:

Line Defensive or support Aggressive
Warrior (전사) Templar (수호성) Gladiator (검성)
Priest (사제) Cleric (치유성) Chanter (호법성)
Scout (정찰) Ranger (궁성), ranged Assassin (살성), melee burst
Elemental (정령) Spiritmaster (정령성), pets and DoTs Sorcerer (마도성), burst caster

Weapons are class-locked and there is no weapon swap: Gladiator is greatsword only, no Aion 1 polearm. Alts are expensive because gear does not transfer. Armour: plate on Templar and Gladiator, leather on Scouts, robe on Elementals, the Priest line in between. Chanter is not the Aion 1 Chanter: “same name, completely different class”. Watch the naming drift: the ranged scout was “Marksman” at the 2025 reveal and “Ranger” in 2026; Spiritmaster shows up as “Elementalist” in some NCsoft press releases.

Uncontested roles

Section titled “Uncontested roles”
Class Weapon Role
Templar long sword and shield the main tank; highest survivability; pull and taunt; party damage buff on block (Fury, 20 s)
Cleric mace and shield the only resurrection; the only active cleanse; lowest personal DPS of the 8
Chanter staff mantra buffer with off-healing and real melee damage; “gets invited everywhere”
Gladiator greatsword bruiser and off-tank; lifesteal sustain; the safest melee for a beginner
Assassin dual daggers back-attack burst, stealth, the best duellist, the squishiest melee
Ranger longbow ranged sustained DPS, kiting, no positional requirement, the easiest class to pilot
Sorcerer spellbook fire and frost glass cannon; the widest CC variety among casters; punished when caught
Spiritmaster orbs 4 elemental spirits and an Ancient Spirit; DoTs and sustained damage; shares incoming damage with its pet
Brawler (KR only) gauntlets rage gauge → “Rampage” state; squishy front-liner

Cross-cutting agreements: difficulty is far more stable than power (Templar, Ranger and Gladiator the easiest to execute; Assassin, Sorcerer and Chanter the hardest); tanks and supports are designed to do ~80% of a pure DPS’s damage, a design target not a bug; combat is manual (no autoplay, the dodge has real invulnerability and long attacks lock animation); duplicate classes in a group synergise poorly; and every creator says the same about picking: the class whose weakness you can live with, because tier placement will move. One thing the Global playtest added: the official macro chains a column of skills onto one held key, which flattens rotation difficulty between classes. Most classes end up cycling two or three skills through it, and macro play beats careful manual play because the macro makes no mistakes; the rule for setting its delay is to keep it at or above your ping. What stays manual is what matters: charge skills, gap-closers, defensives and crowd control all break when auto-fired. Global also widened the dodge window, with one creator’s frame count putting the boss hit about 24 frames after the telegraph fills against Korea’s 8.

The data

Section titled “The data”

Why the lists contradict

Four measurement families: leaderboard presence (rewards easy, high-uptime classes like Ranger, punishes niche ones like Spiritmaster), P90 parse (rewards ceiling, punishes supports by design), KDA and win rate (mode-dependent) and difficulty. Spiritmaster is third in P90 DPS and last in leaderboard presence, four days apart, same servers. Presence is not power.

One tester levelled all eight classes to 37 during the Global playtest, equalised their gear, and ran a manual one-minute dummy parse on each. What changed

Section titled “What changed”
  • Gladiator: from “avoid it” to “broken” in three months. Spanish testers in June called it the least polished class; by August and September it is S on leaderboards and meta lists, and one creator claims it has been S “from day one to day 300”.
  • Spiritmaster spans S and D in the same month: S in the Abyss by KDA (13.66, the best) but C in 1v1; third in P90 DPS but D in presence (34 of 800) and C on meta lists for pets dying.
  • Chanter: top of KR PvE lists, bottom of Western ones. KR buffed it monthly and it sits top-tier in dungeons; the recruitment data and the measured party buff corroborate it.
  • The 2026-03-25 overhaul is the most important balance event: separating PvE and PvP coefficients lets every class be tuned per mode, which is why later lists split into separate PvE and PvP orderings.
  • The 2026-08-26 rebalance is in Global. It reworked Templar (Judgment off shield skills, Punishment ranged) and Chanter (Dark Crush off ranged skills, Spin Strike more mobile at the cost of part of its buffing role). A Korean/Taiwanese player with over a thousand hours verified in the September playtest that his own Chanter’s Spin Strike was already ranged, and NCsoft has stated that class balance will stay in sync across Korea, Taiwan and Global from here on.
  • Korea has not stopped rebalancing Templar since. Continuous Flurry (also translated Continuous Onslaught) and Judgment were buffed, nerfed and re-nerfed across 08-26, 08-27, 09-02 and 09-04, and on 09-16 the rotation built around them was patched out. The 09-16 patch adds cast-cancel delay to Continuous Flurry, Punishing Strike and Judgment, raises Protective Strike, Annihilate and Punishment by 10% and Shield Bash by 20% in PvE, cuts Noble Armor from five minutes to two on both duration and recast, and turns Provoke’s threat from a flat value into a damage-proportional one, better for a geared tank and worse for an undergeared one. Chanter’s Blessing of Protection took the same five-to-two-minute cut, and Cleric’s Radiance of Recovery had its second specialisation swapped from +20% skill speed to +1 consecutive cast. Korean sentiment is openly negative, and because balance now tracks across regions, Global inherits the direction.
  • Party structure changed since the earliest guides: November 2025 sources describe content balanced around 4 players; Global ships 5 and 10. Discard the 4-man framing.

Composition and buffs that do not stack

Section titled “Composition and buffs that do not stack”
Buff A Buff B Effect
Templar Fury (party damage on block, 20 s) Gladiator Experienced Counterstrike (identical) do not stack: a party with both gets one
Cleric Light of Protection Chanter Undefeated Mantra do not stack (the resistance halves do)
Gladiator Rage Burst the strength effect of Templar’s Taunt do not stack
Chanter Barrier Spell, Templar and Cleric immunities each other do not stack; and since March 25 no two immunities chain within 30 s

What does complement: Cleric and Chanter are designed to pair (heals, cleanses and rez plus shields and mantras), and the 10-player Sanctuary requires both; Chanter and Gladiator lifesteal stack in raids; Cleric buffs stack with Chanter and Gladiator for a large group damage combo.

The Global build’s own framing of the same rule: the eight classes sit in three sub-groups, Tank (Templar, Gladiator), DPS (Assassin, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Ranger) and Support (Chanter, Cleric), and party buffs mostly do not stack inside a sub-group, with Chanter’s the exception that does stack with Cleric’s (Global, 2026-09-20, r/Aion2). The chart that claim travels with is a training-dummy parse: in real content the DPS classes pull well ahead of tanks and supports.

Healer coverage by content tier (KR/TW, Season 1 content)

Section titled “Healer coverage by content tier (KR/TW, Season 1 content)”
Tier Can a Chanter solo-heal?
Conquest and Expeditions (Krao Cave (Crow Cave), Draupnir, Uruguru, Vakron, Fire Temple normal) yes, ~90% of the tier
Fire Temple hard, Citadel hard no: 1 Chanter + 1 Cleric + 3 DPS
Transcendence (4 stages) 50/50, depending on a carry or a premade
Sanctuary no: Chanter and Cleric mandatory; only 2 resurrection stones per run

Recruitment bar (KR/TW, August): Fire Temple hard asks ~3,100-3,200 combat power from a DPS and accepts a Chanter at ~2,800-2,900. Ranger feels weak below ~2,800: its weakness is gear-dependent, not innate. The Global playtest confirmed the same preference, tanks and supports get taken below the posted requirement because the role is worth more than the number, with one hard floor: you must still do about 5% of the boss’s damage to open the loot queue, so no one gets fully carried. The most optimised five is given as Templar, Gladiator, Chanter, Cleric and one ranged DPS, but that is a min-max comp; one tank, three DPS and one support is the normal group.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Trusting an undated English tier list.
  • Reading leaderboard presence as power.
  • Picking Spiritmaster “because it is the best solo class”: a myth; all solo content is trivial for every class and others beat it in Nightmare, the one hard tier.
  • Picking Chanter expecting an easy support: it is the hardest class.
  • Stacking Templar and Gladiator for double buffs.
  • Building a full-DPS Cleric.
  • Assuming Gladiator refuses to tank: its aggro generation cannot be turned off.
  • Planning a Brawler main for day one.
  • Judging a class on twelve hours at cap 37, which is all the September test offered, and, worse, reading that test’s level-37 damage table as an endgame ranking. It inverts almost exactly.
  • Picking Assassin on bad ping. (For Sorcerer the KR and TW readings conflict: the older KR one calls it ping-sensitive, the newer TW one, 2026-09-22, ranks it the least affected class. Assassin is the one both agree on.)
  • Calling a class nerfed on Global before checking the enchant level. The Spiritmaster “Global nerf” that spread during the playtest was the Ancient Spirit stigma failing to re-trigger Elemental Fusion below +15 enchant; at +15 it worked exactly as written.

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Three of these closed in September. Global’s Stigma slots are four, unlocked at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37, against Korea’s flat six-of-thirteen. The level cap is 45, seen in the playtest client. Global ships the 2026-08-26 balance patch and NCsoft says balance stays in sync from here, so the “which Korean patch” question is now a question about systems, not classes, and there Global is clearly its own build: no Heroic tier, no PvE Daevanion board in the test build, trimmed board values, lower stat multipliers. Still open: no English or Spanish source publishes stigma or Daevanion specifics for six of the eight classes; the Namu per-class subpages are still empty stubs, untouched since 09-10, so Korean depth comes from Inven; whether Global renames Spiritmaster to Elementalist. Check at launch: whether the non-stacking rules survived, whether the -50% Abyss healing shipped, and whether Korea’s rolling Templar hotfixes land on Global before or after 10-05.
