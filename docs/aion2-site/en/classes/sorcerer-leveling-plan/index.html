Sorcerer: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Inven skill data (Korea, 2026-09-22) over the KR/TW syntheses to 2026-09-16 and the Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the cooldown loop and the macros live on Sorcerer and Sorcerer skills. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Sorcerer comes online late. Its skills only reach their real form at the level 8, 12 and 16 steps, so levelling is slow until Blaze has its lifesteal (KR, 2025-11-27). That is not a bad build, that is the class.
  4. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  5. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas at level 22 and leave them alone.
  6. Any Korean or Taiwanese build you find runs 5 or 6 stigmas: cut it to 4 before you copy it.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: two fire buttons and the Ascension bar

Section titled “1 to 22: two fire buttons and the Ascension bar”
  • Skills, in this order: Flame Arrow (불꽃 화살, left click) as filler and mana battery (it returns 100 MP a hit and chains at 100% into Detonation and Heat Surge), Firestorm (불꽃 작살, which the Inven database calls Flame Harpoon) on cooldown, and Blaze (불꽃 폭발) on marked targets (KR, 2025-11).
  • The specialisation that makes levelling bearable: Blaze’s lifesteal, on its level-8 step. It is around 600 HP a cast by level 30 and it nearly removes potions from your bar (KR, 2025-11-27). It is the first specialisation you place, ahead of everything else.
  • Spending trick: take core actives to 7 with points (9 points) and let a blue Daevanion node supply the eighth, which is the level that opens the specialisation choice. Going 7 to 8 with points costs 4 more (KR, 2025-11; costs from the Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • AoE: Ice Chain (얼음 사슬) as the hit-count tool and Bitter Cold Wind (혹한의 바람) to clump the pack. Both cap at 4 targets.
  • Early burst: Frost (빙결) then Frost Burst (빙결 폭발). That is your damage spike until well past level 30, not Hellfire.
  • Passives: Robe of Earth (대지의 로브, max MP and regen) and Robe of Flame (불꽃의 로브) first, then Grace of Enhancement (강화의 은혜), which gives +20% PvE and +10% PvP damage while you sit above 25% MP (KR, 2025-11; values from the Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: blue nodes on Flame Arrow, Firestorm and Blaze. On Global the orange nodes lost between a third and a half of their value while a blue node still buys a whole skill level (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).
  • Gear: all disposable, nothing above +5. Do the pre-level-20 regional quests before pushing the main story: their accessory rewards dominate your early attack power (KR, 2025-11, untested against Global’s quest structure).

Do not:

  • Level Flame Scatter Shot (화염 난사) while levelling. It only hits groggy targets, and bosses barely get staggered before the 40s; it is a dungeon tool (KR, 2025-11).
  • Put Hellfire (지옥의 화염) on the bar before your 30s. Its cooldown does not pay for itself until Firestorm starts cutting it (KR, 2025-11).
  • Skip regional side quests: each one is +1 skill point and +1 Daevanion point (Global LST, 2026-09).
  • Expect skill points from the Monolith: on Global it gives none at all (Global LST, 2026-09, 10 deltas).

22 to 27: first stigma slot

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot”
  • Skills: the fire core (Flame Arrow, Firestorm, Blaze) towards 8, then place their specialisations. Unlocking one and placing it are two separate steps in the UI.
  • Stigma, slot 1: Delayed Explosion (지연 폭발) or Element Enhancement (원소 강화). These are the two every source picks, without exception (KR, 2025-11 to 2026-09). Delayed Explosion leaves the target taking +15% more damage from you while it ticks down; Element Enhancement gives +10% fire and water attack and is your rotation opener.
  • Defensive flex, if you need one: Steel Barrier (강철 보호막) is the cheap panic shield for this band, but it is flat and does not scale with your max HP (2,424 at level 1, 8,188 at level 20, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). It is a now piece, not a season piece. Once Blaze’s lifesteal is in, you do not need it.
  • Stop it at 5. Stigma effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20, and 1 → 5 is 4 shards.
  • Daevanion: keep taking fire-core blues, and start combat speed, the stat the whole cooldown kit is built on (TW, 2026-05-24).
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: split shards across four stigmas at once, or copy a Korean 6-stigma build without cutting it.

27 to 32: second slot

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot”
  • Skills: finish whichever half of the level-8 pair you are missing and start pushing Firestorm towards 12. Its 12 step cuts 2 seconds off Hellfire’s cooldown per harpoon, and a Korean guide describes that rank as the point “where the character changes” into a burst DPS (KR, 2025-11-20; Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • Stigma, slot 2: the other half of the universal pair. If you took Delayed Explosion at 22, Element Enhancement goes here, and the other way round.
  • Daevanion: cooldown reduction after combat speed. It is more expensive on Global: the node dropped from 2.5% to 1.5% (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most. Sorcerer is a gear-hungry class: a free-to-play one does “significantly less damage” than other classes at equal time invested (KR, 2025-11-27).
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 gear score for 2-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: bring Soul Freeze or Curse: Tree into a dungeon. With four slots, a PvP stigma in PvE is a wasted slot.

32 to 37: third slot, and Hellfire for real

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot, and Hellfire for real”
  • Hellfire goes in here. With Firestorm at 12 you finally have the chain that recycles it: each harpoon cuts its cooldown, and its own 16 step takes 15 seconds off (Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • Hellfire’s specialisation: do not take the fire DoT. It only pays on stationary targets, which is exactly what a Sorcerer does not get in a dungeon (KR, 2025-11 to 2026-07). Take Fire Mark or the mobile cast.
  • Stigma, slot 3: this is where the content decides. Divine Burst (신성 폭발) if you run dungeons: its 50 groggy-gauge damage is the highest in the kit, against Bitter Cold Wind’s 10 and Ice Chain’s 2 (KR, 2025-11). Firewall (불의 장벽) or Cold Storm (냉기 폭풍) if the boss stands still: both are ground zones and both are worth nothing against a boss that moves.
  • Skills: the core towards 12. The 12 steps that matter: Flame Arrow applies Fire Mark through Heat Surge, Blaze gains a guaranteed multi-hit, and Wish of Concentration gives +10% combat speed (Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • Daevanion: critical. This is where Sorcerer parts company with Templar and Cleric, which both skip that line: the Korean endgame baseline build is max crit, precisely because crit does not care where you are standing relative to the boss (KR, 2026-09-17). Remember the Global crit node is worth 5, not 10.
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2 stars, and start building towards 2,100 for 3 stars.

Do not: count on the boss holding still. Sorcerer sits at C on PvE leaderboards precisely because movement and interrupt mechanics eat its realised uptime (KR, 2026-08-29; reproduced independently 2026-09-18).

37 to 45: fourth slot and the level-16 steps

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot and the level-16 steps”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at level 37. This closes the set of four you carry all season 1. The deep defensive is Arctic Armor (빙설의 갑주): +20% PvE resistance, a 5% reactive freeze and, above 50% MP, half your incoming damage redirected to HP and 10% to MP (Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • Skills, the level-16 steps that change the kit: Firestorm stacks +15% damage per harpoon up to +75%, Hellfire loses 15 seconds of cooldown, Flame Arrow resets Blaze through Heat Surge, and Wish of Concentration takes 10 seconds off every cooldown (Inven database, KR, 2026-09-22).
  • Past 10, points stop mattering: it is the four boards (+4, to 14), Arcana cards and gear soul binding (Global LST, 2026-09-17/21).
  • Gear: the season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09).
  • Gates: 3 stars 2,100; the Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid asks ~2,700 gear score and it does ship with season 1 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: keep Steel Barrier in the final set. Its shield is flat and your HP pool is not (KR, 2025-11).

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: your skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global LST, 2026-09-20/21). Sorcerer has a specific reason too: nearly everything you placed to level is lifesteal (Blaze, Frost Burst, Flame Scatter Shot), and at endgame that slot is worth more as damage. The skills you want at level 20 are Hellfire, Blaze and Wish of Concentration, then Firestorm, Flame Arrow and Bitter Cold Wind (KR, 2026-08).

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are not paid in points but in Stigma Shards, taking all four to 20 is around 300 shards (~75 each), and the Global reset cost nobody has seen. On Korea it ran around 300,000 Kinah (KR, 2025-11). That is why the four get picked at 22 and left alone.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes, refundable for Kinah on Korea. No source publishes the Global cost.

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source publishes a Sorcerer node map, so this is a priority order, not a route. Korean and Taiwanese stat priority (2025-11 to 2026-09) with the Global node-value cuts (2026-09-17/21).

  1. Blue nodes on the fire core: Firestorm, Blaze, Flame Arrow. Each one is a whole skill level, and those are the three 8/12/16 ladders that build the cooldown loop.
  2. Combat speed. It is the stat the class is built on, and the Korean endgame macro build assumes ~114% with buffs up (KR, 2026-08-01). On Global the node gives 1.5%, not 2.5%.
  3. Cooldown reduction, for the same reason: the whole kit is a chain of cooldown cuts.
  4. Critical, which Sorcerer does take, unlike Templar or Cleric: the Korean endgame baseline is a max-crit build because crit is facing-independent (KR, 2026-09-17). Global node is 5 instead of 10.
  5. Flat attack (+3 a node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan).
  6. Blue nodes on Hellfire and Bitter Cold Wind once they are on the bar.
  7. Skip multi-hit: it is a stat for fast-attacking classes (Assassin, Gladiator, Ranger) and explicitly lower value for a slow caster (TW, 2026-05-24).
  8. The +50/+50 board-centre pieces no longer exist on Global, and the PvE board was not in the test build.

What is still disputed or unconfirmed

No 1-45 Sorcerer skill-point list with per-level citations exists anywhere. This page’s cheat sheet is a Korean 2025-11 order priced with the Inven database. Treat it as priorities, not as a script.

No source states which stigma goes in which slot. The set of four is well supported; the split across 22, 27, 32 and 37 on this page is a reading of what each one does for you in that band, not a datum.

Firestorm (불꽃 작살) only has data up to level 10 in the Inven database, while the other 38 skills run to 20. It is the exact skill the cooldown loop turns on. Read its 12 and 16 tooltips in your own client before you spend.

The Global stigma reset cost has not been seen by anyone. Skill points are free to reset, but that is a different system.

Fire Mark at 100% is Korean, not Global. The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 raised its proc from 20% to 100% and reworked its follow-up into a flat +5% fire-damage amp. That is later than the Global test build. Even when it arrives, a Korean endgame analysis measures Fire Mark at ~2% of total damage: do not chase that roll (KR, 2026-09-17).

Whether Global ships the 2026-08-26 rebalance at all is unconfirmed. NC says class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global with patches applied simultaneously, but nobody tested it: the test capped at level 37 with the Abyss closed.

The ping reasoning is Taiwanese, not Global. A Taiwanese latency tier list puts Sorcerer as the least ping-impacted class, and its argument is that Aion 2 has no global cooldown (TW, 2026-09-22). That argument holds for Taiwan. Korea shipped a global cooldown on 2026-08-26, and nobody has checked the Global client: there it is unknown. Test it yourself in Advanced Access.

The PvE Daevanion board was not in the test build, and its point sinks still exist in the Nightmare shop and in reward text. Unconfirmed for 10-05: do not plan season 1 around it.

There are no confirmed damage figures for Hibernation or Arctic Armor, only a tooltip read by a creator who did not own a Sorcerer. And the name pairing between “Lumiel’s Space” and English guides’ “Linear Space” is unconfirmed.

Live Korean bug: when several Sorcerers hit the same boss, only the one with the highest Frost Burst level applies its damage and everyone else’s is voided entirely. Coordinate levels before a raid (KR, 2026-09-17).
