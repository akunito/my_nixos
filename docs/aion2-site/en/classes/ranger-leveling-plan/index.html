Ranger: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Inven skill database (Korean client, 2026-09-22) over the KR/TW syntheses to 2026-09-16, with the Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit skill by skill, the chains and the macros live in the Ranger skills guide. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  4. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas at level 22 and leave them alone.
  5. Daevanion nodes refund for 500 Kinah each on Korea, so spend points the day you earn them.
  6. Any Korean or Taiwanese Ranger build you find runs 5 stigmas (6 in Korea’s Chapter 1): cut it to 4 before you copy it.
  7. A Ranger cannot afford everything. The 25 point-funded skills cost 21 points each to reach 10, so 525 in total, and the budget measured at level 37 was about 150 (arithmetic over the Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; budget Global, 2026-09-17). What you chase is the specialisation steps, not the level.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: the cancel loop and the mana problem

Section titled “1 to 22: the cancel loop and the mana problem”
  • Skills, in this order: Snipe (저격) on left click. It anchors everything: 20m, instant, 1 s recast, and it restores 120 mana per hit (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). Its chain field offers two 100% follow-ups, Continuous Fire (연사, +120 mana) or Spiral Arrow (나선 화살, +150 mana): that is the mechanical shape of the animation-cancel loop (평캔) every Korean guide since 2025-12 calls mandatory, not optional.
  • Second: the spam AoE, with its −20% mana cost specialisation (Global, 2026-09-17 and 09-19). In the Korean database the only active carrying exactly that level-8 step is Rapid Fire (속사, up to 4 targets, 96 mana), which makes it the likeliest identification; the Global-client guides call it “Temple Shot” or “Tempest Shot” and none of them maps it to a Korean name (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; Global, 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-03).
  • Third: Aimed Shot / Dead Shot (조준 화살), the 3-stage charge skill and the class’s biggest single hit. Those three are the only ones that go to 10; everything else stops at 8 (KR, 2026-08-13 and 08-20).
  • Mana is the bottleneck, and it is Global-verified. Both Snipe and the AoE take their mana specialisation “since most players will already be having mana problems by this point” (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Target Arrow / Marking Shot (표적 화살) stays at level 1 for now. It grants 7 s of Precision (+300 critical) and is essential later, but it is not worth points before Precision feeds Aimed Shot (KR, 2026-08-20).
  • Drilling Dart (송곳 화살) levelled early is your solo-farm sustain before Supporting Fire exists (KR, 2026-08-13 and 08-20). Mind the condition the database makes explicit and the guides do not: it only fires on a critical hit, has a 5 s recast, returns 100 mana and leaves a bleed that cuts the target’s incoming healing by 50% (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Passives: Hunter’s Resolve (사냥꾼의 결의, crit damage amplification, 6% at rank 1 and 25% at rank 20) first, with Eye of Focus / Focused Eye (집중의 눈, a flat +100 accuracy plus PvE amplification from 1% at rank 1 to 20% at rank 20) right behind it (Global, 2026-09-17 and 09-19; TW, 2026-09-17; Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). Ten months of Korean guides name the same two in the opposite order, with Eye of Focus on top; the LST reading flips the pair. Take either first, but take both: Ranger’s passives have no specialisations, so every point is a direct effect.
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens at character level 12 (Global, 2026-09-19). Blue nodes for actives, green for passives (region unknown, 2026-09-19). Spend the points the day you earn them.
  • Gear: all disposable, nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20).
  • Gate before you move on: the level-22 red quest blocks the main story until the world-progress bar is at 100%, filled with regional quests (J key) and sealed dungeons. One measured route reached 97.1% using only stops on the natural path and took 1 h 10 min from 1 to 22 (Global, 2026-09-19).

Do not:

  • Skip regional side quests: each one is +1 skill point, +1 Daevanion point and +3 permanent passive stat points (Global, 2026-09-18). On Ranger it hurts twice over, because it is the class that needs the most points.
  • Put Target Arrow in a macro. Its cast animation is itself a DPS loss when spammed: cast it standing still and refresh at ~1-2 s remaining, not “just in case” (KR, 2026-08-07 and 09-06).
  • Expect to kill fast early. Ranger is a 대기만성형, a slow starter acknowledged from 2025-11 through the Global LST, and its timed 1-to-cap run was the second slowest of the eight classes (KR, 2025-12; Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Detour for feathers, spend a key on a yellow cube, or leave a stronghold without its 2-3 one-time chests (Global, 2026-09-19/20).

22 to 27: first stigma slot

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot”
  • Skills: push Snipe and the AoE towards 8 (13 points each) and then place the specialisation: unlocking it and placing it are two separate steps in the UI, and at 8 you pick one of three. On Snipe the three are +20% mana restoration, 1.5% life absorb, or +50% multi-hit on hit; while levelling, take mana (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Stigma, slot 1: Baizel’s Authority (바이젤의 권능). This is the reading of both sources that published a four-slot Global build, and both agree on the order: buffs first, damage second (Global, 2026-09-17 and 09-19). +20% attack power for 10 s at rank 1, rising to 20 s at rank 20, on a 1-minute cooldown; its level-5 step adds +10% Perfect for the duration (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Stop it at 5 (4 shards) until you know your four. Effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20.
  • Daevanion: attack and combat speed first, then damage boost (region unknown, 2026-09-19). No source puts accuracy early on Ranger the way it does on Templar: Eye of Focus already carries a flat +100 accuracy.
  • Gear: Crow Cave at level 22 asks for gear score 200 and the normal route clears it easily (Global, 2026-09-19/20).
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: push one skill to 10 before the others are at 8; spread shards across four stigmas at once; copy a Korean 5- or 6-stigma build. And do not count on Arrow Barrage (화살 난사) while levelling: the Korean database gates it on the target being Groggy, so outside bosses with a groggy bar it does nothing (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).

27 to 32: second slot

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot”
  • Skills: the rest of the core actives to 8, and Snipe, the AoE and Aimed Shot towards 12. Snipe’s step 12 takes 1 s off Aimed Shot’s recast every time you hit (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), which is the hinge between the cancel loop and the big hit.
  • Stigma, slot 2: Blessing of the Bow (축복의 활). It completes the buff pair in both Global builds (Global, 2026-09-17 and 09-19). +200 critical for 10 s at rank 1 and 20 s at rank 20, on a 2-minute cooldown; step 5, +50% multi-hit landing; step 10, +100 accuracy; step 15, +20% crit damage amplification; step 20, +7% Heavy Strike (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Daevanion: this is where all four critical damage sections come in. Ranger takes that line specifically, explicitly unlike classes that do not crit reliably (Global, 2026-09-17; region unknown, 2026-09-19).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most; nothing further until the gold tier (Global, 2026-09-18/20).
  • Gate before you move on: no hard gate in this band. The next one is 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score (one source read 1,500) (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take a PvP stigma into a dungeon; with four slots that is a slot thrown away.

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar filled with exploration and side quests, then the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18). Side quests come back around 32-33 after being sparse since 21 (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Skills: two or three core skills to 16. On Snipe that step adds a fourth chain hit, Gust Arrow (질풍 화살); on Aimed Shot it is a 25% chance to reset its own recast on hit (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Stigma, slot 3: Explosive Arrow (폭발 화살). This is where damage goes (Global, 2026-09-17 and 09-19). It does +20% against slowed or bound targets, and its level-5 step cuts 15 s off the cooldown, 45 s down to 30 s (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). That is the reason to keep Snare Arrow (올가미 화살, a 40% AoE slow) on the bar: the stigma cashes in on what the arrow leaves behind.
  • Daevanion: all four Multi Hit Chance branches (region unknown, 2026-09-19). This is not a detail: a Multi Hit of 154 at level 37 was named as the direct cause of Ranger’s last place on the LST dummy (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Gear: 1,000 is the first Ascension Trial rank’s requirement and the reason not to rush the story (Global, 2026-09-18/19).
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2-star; 1,000 for the Ascension Trial, which also drops shards.

Do not: copy Korean dodge timing. On Global the hit lands about 24 frames after the circle fills, against about 8 on Korea, roughly three times the window (one creator’s frame count on his own footage, Global, 2026-09-18). It matters more on Ranger than anywhere, because this is the class that never has to leave the target and whose entire survival plan is positional.

37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at level 37. Griffon’s Arrow (그리폰 화살) in both Global builds: AoE plus a 10 s fire DoT that grows while the target moves, 45 s cooldown, and a step 20 that halves the tick interval (Global, 2026-09-17 and 09-19; Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). The two swaps those same sources name: Supporting Fire (지원 사격) when survival is the problem, and Nature’s Breath / Mother Nature (대자연의 숨결) in content the healer cannot reach.
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40, the fifth at 45 (Global, 2026-09-19). The Ariel (PvE) board was not in the test build.
  • Skills: the core ones towards 20. Past 10 they no longer depend on points: they depend on the four boards (+4), on Arcana cards and on soul bindings from rings and weapons (Global LST, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Defence and HP, last. The Global-facing board order leaves defence and HP for the end (region unknown, 2026-09-19).
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20). The Korean gear plan of 2026-08 assumes 5-line Heroic pieces, so it cannot be copied whole.
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100 (one source read 2,200); Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: level a skill past its last specialisation step. It was tested directly at Rapid Fire 24 and Bombard Shot 26 with no measurable gain: only the specialisations bought at each step count, and the spare points go to passives (KR, 2026-08-09). In the mature Korean build Rapid Fire stops at 16, because its second specialisation is +10% skill critical and that is a dead stat once you are crit-capped.

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: the skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21). On Ranger the reset has a specific content: everything you levelled with is a mana and personal-sustain build (the mana-restoration specialisation on Snipe and on the AoE, the life absorb on Drilling Dart). At 45, with Season 1 gear and Supporting Fire equipped, mana stops being the problem and those steps trade for multi-hit and damage.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are not paid in points but in Stigma Shards, and the Korean database confirms the curve: exactly 75 shards to take one from 1 to 20, so about 300 for the four (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). Nobody has seen the Global reset cost; at Korean launch, resetting a level-10 stigma cost ~300,000 (KR, 2025-11). That is why you pick all four at 22 and leave them alone.

Middle ground: the Daevanion nodes. On Korea they refund at 500 Kinah each and a whole branch for ~13,500 (KR, 2025-11), removed from the branch tips inwards.

The three level-45 loadouts

Section titled “The three level-45 loadouts”

You have three presets (1/2/3), which on Global save skill placement, skill level and macro together (Global, 2026-09-20). What is not known is whether they save stigma level. Until that is confirmed, the three setups below share the same first two equipped stigmas, which are also the two every source puts first: you cannot fund four sets.

One honest warning. No Global endgame Ranger data exists. The LST capped at level 37 with the Abyss, arenas, battlegrounds and Market closed, and the PvP column below is Korean Season 2, predating both the four-slot cap and the ~30% PvP damage cut. Nobody has re-run PvP stigma priorities against either change.

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source publishes a node map, so this is a priority order, not a route. Global-facing order of 2026-09-19 with the node-value cuts of 2026-09-17/21.

  1. Flat attack (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan) and combat speed, together and first.
  2. Damage boost (1.5% per node on Global against Korea’s 5%).
  3. The four critical damage sections. Ranger takes this line specifically, unlike classes that do not crit reliably (Global, 2026-09-17).
  4. The four Multi Hit Chance branches. This is the stat the test itself named as the class’s hole at level 37.
  5. Blue nodes for Snipe and Aimed Shot, which are what push a skill past 10 without spending points.
  6. Green nodes for Hunter’s Resolve and Eye of Focus.
  7. Defence and HP last (region unknown, 2026-09-19).
  8. No source, general rule: do not take accuracy early the way a tank would. Eye of Focus already carries a flat +100 accuracy and no Ranger guide asks for more while levelling.

Still contested or unconfirmed

No Global endgame Ranger data exists. The Launch Scale Test capped at level 37 with the Abyss, arenas, battlegrounds and Market closed. The last place on the 1-minute dummy is a single source repeated by thirteen round summaries, and it is a test-bracket datum, not an endgame ranking.

Which Korean skill the spam AoE is remains unresolved. The Global-client guides call it “Temple Shot” or “Tempest Shot” and none of them maps it. This page bets on Rapid Fire (속사) because it is the only active whose level-8 step is exactly the −20% mana cost those guides describe, but that is an inference, not a confirmation. The database carries an English name for only 10 of the 38 skills.

Gale Arrow’s duration reads two ways. The Global build guide describes a 50 s buff on a 20 s cooldown, near-permanent; the Inven database of 2026-09-22 gives a 10 s buff (+7% combat speed), a 20 s cooldown and a three-stage charge skill whose step 8 adds 5 s more. If the database is right it is not a permanent buff and its place in the rotation has to be rethought. Read the tooltip in your own client.

Suppression Arrow also reads backwards depending on the source. Two Korean guides call it immobile, long-cast and lethal to you in PvE (one of them reversed its own earlier advice after players reported deaths); the database of 2026-09-22 lists it as an instant cast gated on the Precision state.

The Stealth stigma (은신) has been evaluated by nobody. It exists in the database as one of the 13 pool options and this page lists it as such. Two things that get confused are worth separating: this stigma is real and selectable, while the rumour that Ranger has an innate, always-available stealth was reviewed and rejected in this round, having come from two general overview videos that confused it with Assassin. No source compares the stigma against the other twelve.

Supporting Fire’s numbers do not reconcile. The database’s rank-1 tooltip gives a 25% proc chance and 1.5% life absorb; the Korean synthesis of 2026-03 cites ~13,000 per proc and 50% absorb. The likeliest reading is rank 20 with gear against the base tooltip, but no source bridges the two.

Nobody has seen the stigma reset cost on Global. Resetting skill points is free, but it is a different system. And the Ranger synthesis’s own slot ladder said 27/32/37: the 22 comes from the round’s consensus, with three Global sources tying the first slot to the level-22 Ascension quest.

Whether third-party macro software is permitted on Global is addressed by no source. The Korean Ranger endgame is built on it. On the Global build the in-game macro system alone already drives the class, so whether anything more is needed is unanswered.
