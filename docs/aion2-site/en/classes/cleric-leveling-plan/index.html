Cleric: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 over the KR/TW kit to 2026-09-16 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the Condemnation loop, the debuff job and the macro live on Cleric in PvE. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Skill points are free to reset on Global. That matters more on Cleric than on any other class: you level as a damage dealer and you arrive at 45 as a healer.
  4. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas at level 22 and leave them alone.
  5. Daevanion nodes refund for 500 Kinah each on Korea, so spend points the day you earn them.
  6. Any Korean or Taiwanese Cleric build you find runs 5 or 6 stigmas (Taiwan is on 6), so cut it to 4, and cut two, not one.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: the mana engine, the damage pair, and the Ascension bar

Section titled “1 to 22: the mana engine, the damage pair, and the Ascension bar”
  • Skills, in this order: Earth’s Retribution (대지의 응보) first: the left-click mana engine, called non-negotiable by the Global test guide (“without this skill, don’t even think about it”, Global, 2026-09-17). Then the damage pair, Chains of Torment / Chain of Pain (고통의 연쇄) and Condemnation (단죄): Condemnation only lands on a target already carrying Chain of Pain, and that pair is roughly a third of your damage (KR, 2026-09).
  • Third: Debilitating Mark / Mark of Weakening (약화의 낙인). Its level-8 specialisation removes the mana cost outright and returns 150 MP on hit (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), which is why a party-support debuff goes in this early.
  • Passives: Imperion Lord’s Grace / Sovereign’s Grace (주신의 은총) first, the class’s only pure damage passive, then Earth’s Grace / Grace of the Earth (대지의 은총) for crit damage (KR, 2025-11 to 2026-02; TW, 2026-09-21). Two dissents: the Global level-37 guide skips every passive at that bracket because a levelling Cleric has effectively 0% crit (Global, 2026-09-17); a solo-leaning Korean guide puts Warm Blessing (따뜻한 가호, HP) first for personal survival (KR, 2025-12).
  • Do not level heals yet. Every source that revised itself converged here: heals get points at max level (KR, 2025-11; Global, 2026-08 and 2026-09-17). One exception, in the next band.
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens around level 12-13. Blue nodes first (active-skill level): on Global the orange cores lost between a third and a half of their value while a blue node still buys a whole skill level (Global, 2026-09-18). Some Global creators argue orange first; the disagreement is live.
  • Gear: all disposable, nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20). Belt and Amulet are the permanent exception.
  • Gate before you move on: the level-22 red quest blocks the main story until the world-progress bar is at 100%, filled with regional quests (J key) and sealed dungeons, about six activities. One measured route reached 97.1% using only stops on the natural path: Treasure Hunter Residence (~11), Trickster Dent, a “?” sealed dungeon, skip the next one, Poisonous Plant Habitat (~13, the fastest dungeon in the game) and Quarry of the Noble (~15). Total 1 to 22: 1 h 10 min (Global, 2026-09-19).

Do not:

  • Skip green side quests: each is +1 skill point, +1 Daevanion point and +3 permanent passive stat points (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Detour for feathers. One pickup counts as 3 and they show on the map; their only job is taking the Amulet to blue (Global, 2026-09-19/20).
  • Spend a key on a yellow cube: yellow cubes open free (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Leave a stronghold without its 2-3 one-time chests: off-cap Kinah, and they do not come back (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Put Bolt / Thunderclap (벽력) in a macro: it is a three-stage charge skill and a macro fires one uncharged hit (TW, 2026-07 and 2026-09-21).
  • Ignore tab-target (“Aion 1”) mode. The base attack carries its own 8/12/16 specialisations, and Earth’s Retribution’s level-12 step cuts 7 s off Bolt’s recast, so weaving it is real damage (Global, 2026-09-21; Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).

22 to 27: first stigma slot, first dungeon

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot, first dungeon”
  • Skills: push Earth’s Retribution, Condemnation and Chain of Pain towards 8 (13 points each, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), then place the specialisation: unlocking it and placing it are two separate steps in the UI. Condemnation’s level 8 offers +100 MP on hit, up to +12% against fewer targets, or cast-while-moving; take the mana one while levelling (no source ranks the three).
  • Stigma, slot 1. No source publishes a slot order for the four, and the two readings pull apart:
    • Levelling (majority for solo play): Earth’s Punishment (대지의 징벌). Its level-5 specialisation makes Condemnation crit automatically against anything carrying its DoT, and Condemnation’s own level-12 specialisation resets its recast on hit. That pairing is the entire Cleric damage engine, re-derived independently by three Taiwanese sources (TW, 2026-09-21/22; Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
    • Group: Light of Protection (보호의 빛), the toggle giving you and the party +10.5% PvE damage amplification and PvE damage tolerance on a 5 s recast; the Global level-37 guide runs it as an always-on mantra (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; Global, 2026-09-17). It does not stack with a Chanter’s equivalent: only the higher skill level applies.
    • Whichever you take, stop it at 5 (4 shards) until you know your four. Effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20.
  • Daevanion: blue nodes for Condemnation first, then Chain of Pain, then Earth’s Retribution. Accuracy only to the minimum the content tier asks for; beyond that it is wasted (TW, 2026-09-21).
  • Gear: Crow Cave at 22 asks gear score 200 and the normal route clears it easily (Global, 2026-09-19/20).
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Krao Cave, Draupnir) (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: push one skill to 10 before the others are at 8; spread shards across four stigmas at once; copy a 6-stigma Taiwanese build; or take Burst of Authority (권능 폭발) and Lightning Barrage (벼락 난사) expecting them to work in PvP later: both are groggy-gated and players have no groggy gauge.

27 to 32: second slot

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot”
  • Skills: the rest of the core actives to 8, and Condemnation and Chain of Pain towards 12 through the board’s blue nodes. Condemnation’s level-12 step is the self-reset, the most valuable single specialisation the class owns. No source gives a fine order inside this band: general rule, take first whatever you press in every pull.
  • The one heal that goes in early: Healing Light / Light of Healing (치유의 빛) to 16. Its level-16 step cuts 2 s of recast, bringing it to roughly a 1 s spam heal; 97% of the Korean top 100 reach 16, and a Taiwanese rank-1 Cleric calls it the first heal to level (KR, 2026-01; TW, 2026-09-21).
  • Stigma, slot 2. Complete the pair: whichever of Earth’s Punishment and Light of Protection you did not take at 22. Both appear in both published Global Season 1 loadouts (TW, 2026-09-21; Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Daevanion: flat attack next (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan): on this class it is also a healing stat, because since the Korean patch of 2026-03-25 healing amplification scales off attack power, demonstrated live on Taiwan (TW, 2026-09-22). Then combat speed: Cleric’s base is about 42%, the slowest start of any class, and the practical target is 60-70% (KR/TW, 2025-12).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most; nothing further until the gold tier (Global, 2026-09-18/20).
  • Gate before you move on: no hard gate here. The next is 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score (one source read 1,500) (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take a PvP stigma into a dungeon; with four slots that is a slot thrown away. And do not spend points on Healing Enhancement (치유력 강화) yet: measured at roughly +0.4% per point, it loses to raw attack at every levelling bracket (TW, 2026-09-22).

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar filled with exploration and side quests, then the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18). Side quests come back around 32-33 after being sparse since 21 (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Skills: Condemnation to 16 (guaranteed multi-hit) and Chain of Pain to 16 (ignores evade and block, halves its DoT interval) (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). Radiant Recovery / Radiance of Recovery (쾌유의 광휘) enters here with the cleanse specialisation, and two Taiwanese guides pick cleanse over the skill-speed option (TW, 2026-09-21/22).
  • Stigma, slot 3. Resurrection / Summon Revive (소환 부활), the slot nobody argues about: the only mass resurrection in the game, in both published Global loadouts, and the Global guide says a Cleric without it is an instant kick (Global, 2026-09-17; TW, 2026-09-21). Do not leave it at 1: the Inven database puts a −1 s cast at 5 and a 3 s post-revive invulnerability at 10, and a Taiwanese guide says 10 is where it becomes usable at all.
  • Daevanion: the damage-boost line (1.5% per node on Global against 5% on Korea), then cooldown reduction, the class’s one real build threshold: ~34% turns Earth’s Punishment’s 30 s recast into 20 s and makes its crit window permanent (TW, 2026-08; corroborated 2026-09-18). Global halved the node from 2.5% to 1.5%, so treat 34% as a target, not a given.
  • Gear: 1,000 is the first Ascension Trial rank’s requirement and the reason not to rush the story (Global, 2026-09-18/19).
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2-star; 1,000 for the Ascension Trial, which also drops shards.

Do not: copy Korean dodge timing: on Global the hit lands about 24 frames after the telegraph fills against about 8 on Korea, roughly three times the window (one creator’s frame count on his own footage, Global, 2026-09-18). And do not stack in the middle of your party: hold the edge of the formation, because allies’ dodge-rolls body-block and displace you, while staying inside Light of Regeneration’s range: an ally’s name greys out when they leave it (TW, 2026-09-22).

37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at 37. Noble Aura / Noble Energy (고결한 기운) in both published Global Season 1 loadouts: a 5-minute companion that attacks with you every 2 s, taken for constant damage, and a developer livestream confirmed Cleric keeps it at release (TW, 2026-09-21; Global, 2026-09-17). The named swaps: Prayer of Amplification (증폭의 기도) if your own testing gives more damage, or Absolution (면죄) / Authority of Life (생명의 권능) when the content demands healing.
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40 (Global, 2026-09-17). The Asphel (PvP) board opens at 45; the PvE board was not in the test build.
  • Skills: Condemnation towards 20. Past 10 it depends on the four boards (+4), Arcana cards and soul bindings from rings and weapons, not on points (Global LST, 2026-09-18 and 09-20). A Taiwanese Cleric pushes 20 specifically because 16 unlocks the third specialisation slot and 20 lets three be equipped at once (TW, 2026-09-22).
  • The levelling build you will undo at 45: everything above is a damage build. In the Launch Scale Test the two available level-37 dungeons required literally zero healing, so the guide spent all 150 skill points on damage and skipped every healing, crit, shield and defence passive (Global, 2026-09-17). Respec is free, so this costs nothing, but it is not the endgame build.
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20). Do not socket manastones before 45 (KR, 2025-11).
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100 (one source read 2,200); Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: sit at 14/14 Nightmare entries without spending: two generate a day and anything over the cap is lost (Global, 2026-09-18/19). And do not skip Abyss Points because you dislike PvP: they buy stigma shards at 25,000 apiece, and stigmas are where a Cleric’s power lives. Global softens this with two shop sources, but the Abyss is still the biggest.

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: the skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21). On Cleric this is a role change, not a tidy-up: the damage-only bar you levelled with becomes a healer’s bar the day group content asks for one. Healing Light to 16, Radiant Recovery towards 20, Light of Regeneration to 16, and the passives you skipped all come back in.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are paid in Stigma Shards, not points; taking all four to 20 is roughly 300 shards (~75 each), and levelling to 45 while doing every side quest yields on the order of 20 (KR). The cost of resetting them on Global nobody has seen. That is why the four get chosen at 22 and then left alone.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes. On Korea they refund at 500 Kinah each and a whole branch for ~13,500 (KR, 2025-11), removed from the tips inwards. One low-quality Global source says the cost rises with each reset; with no second source, do not plan on it.

The three level-45 loadouts

Section titled “The three level-45 loadouts”

You have three presets (1/2/3), which on Global save skill placement, skill level and macro setup together (Global, 2026-09-20). Whether they save stigma level is not known; on Korea that shipped on 2026-09-09. Until it is confirmed, the three builds below share the same four equipped stigmas, or you run out of shards.

One honest caveat. Cleric has no damage specialisation in this game. The solo-leaning column is not a DPS build: it is the same class with the party-only pieces swapped for personal ones. Cleric is last of nine on measured endgame data, 71.7 against a 100 baseline (TW, 2026-08). Solo-leaning buys you fewer wasted slots when nobody is there to buff, not competitive clear speed.

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source publishes a node map, so this is a priority order, not a route. Built from the Global level-37 board plan (2026-09-17), the Taiwanese stat priority (2026-09-21) and the Global node-value cuts (2026-09-17/21).

  1. Blue nodes for Condemnation, roughly a third of your damage, and its self-reset specialisation sits at level 12, which skill points cannot reach.
  2. Blue nodes for Chain of Pain (the debuff Condemnation needs), then Earth’s Retribution (mana, and the −7 s on Bolt).
  3. Flat Attack (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan). On Cleric it is also a healing stat.
  4. Combat speed, on the Nezekan board per the Global guide. Base ~42% is the slowest in the game; target 60-70%.
  5. Damage boost, on the Zikel board (1.5% per node on Global against Korea’s 5%).
  6. Cooldown reduction, chasing ~34% so Earth’s Punishment’s 30 s recast becomes 20 s and its crit window never closes. Harder on Global: the node went from 2.5% to 1.5%.
  7. Accuracy, but only to the minimum the content tier asks for (TW, 2026-09-21).
  8. Skip the crit-damage line at levelling brackets, named for Cleric specifically, against Ranger which does take it (Global, 2026-09-17). The +50/+50 centre pieces no longer exist on Global.

Still contested or unconfirmed

The four-stigma Global loadout rests on exactly two sources, and one of them calls its own slot count “high rumours” (Global, 2026-09-17; TW, 2026-09-21). The slot count of four is far better supported (twelve Global sources across the round), but no NC note states it. Plan around four and re-check on 09-30.

Nobody has seen the stigma reset cost on Global. Skill-point resets are free, but that is a different system. Plan the four from level 22.

Two numbers on the damage engine read differently by source. Every guide from 2025 and 2026 puts Condemnation’s self-reset at 50%; the Inven database captured 2026-09-22 says the level-12 specialisation resets it at 75%. And a Global-facing guide from 2026-09-02 dates Earth’s Punishment’s +10 s duration at stigma level 25, out of reach on Global, while the same database puts it at level 20, which Global does reach. The second one matters: if the database is right, the permanent crit window is reachable in Season 1. Read both tooltips in your own client before you spend 60 shards.

Light of Protection’s level-15 step is disputed. Korean ranker coverage from 2026-02 describes it as taking party damage amplification from 10% to 20%, which is why adoption jumped 52% → 97%; the Inven database of 2026-09-22 lists level 15 as +10% max HP over a 10.5% base effect.

Radiant Recovery’s specialisation moved under you. The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 reworked its second specialisation from “+20% skill speed” to “+1 consecutive cast”. Whether Global ships the old one or the new one is unknown.

Whether 34% cooldown reduction is reachable on Global is the open question that would change this page most. The threshold comes from Taiwanese Season 3 gearing with brooches and Arcana that do not exist at launch, and Global cut the Daevanion cooldown node from 2.5% to 1.5%. If it is out of reach, Earth’s Punishment stops being a permanent crit window.

The PvE Daevanion board was not in the test build, and whether to weave Judgment Lightning at all is unresolved: Korean ranker data caps it at 20 and calls it core, while two independent Korean and Taiwanese measurements since 2026-08 say plain auto-attack beats it single-target. Both sides say to re-test on Global with the Ctrl+X meter.
