Ranger: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming (Inven gives an English name for only 10 of the 38 skills) and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, enchant cap 20, no Heroic gear).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients. On Ranger this matters more than on any other class, because only 10 of its 38 skills carry an English name in the database.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. On Ranger almost nothing changes its visible numbers: only four skills do, plus the ten passives, which is where the real scaling lives.
  3. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  4. Tell a chain apart from a condition, and here it is critical: five of Ranger’s twelve actives are condition-gated (Drilling Dart needs a crit, Arrow Barrage a groggy target, Rupture Arrow a slowed or bound target, Suppression Arrow needs you to be in Precision, and Shock Release needs you to be under a control effect). A condition is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met. This is what decides what can and cannot go in a macro.
  5. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in.
  6. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  7. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  8. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on Ranger that list is long. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. Inven’s database gives name_en for only 10 of the 38 skills. The rest are our translations or names taken from the KR/TW syntheses, and in three cases we already know the Global client name from the Launch Scale Test: Target Arrow appears as Marking Shot, Aimed Shot as Dead Shot and Nature’s Breath as Mother Nature (Global, LST 2026-09-17). The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle.

Split: 12 actives, 3 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 38 in total. All of them, passives included, require a bow (활).

Point cost, confirmed from the data: every active, chain and passive costs the same ladder to level 10 (0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 4, 4, 4), that is 21 points, and zero further points from 11 to 20: those levels arrive through the Daevanion board, soulbind lines or Arcana cards. Stigmas spend no skill points at all, they spend Stigma Shards: 1 per level in the 1-5 band, 2 in 6-10, 4 in 11-15 and 8 in 16-20, 75 shards to take one from 1 to 20.

Three skills with no entry of their own. The database names three follow-ups that do not exist as entries: Gust Arrow (질풍 화살), which appears with Snipe’s level-16 specialty; Shackle Arrow (족쇄 화살), with Snare Arrow’s level-12 specialty; and Retreat Slash (퇴보 베기), with Binding Trap’s level-5 specialty. There is no entry, no text and no description anywhere in our sources: only the name inside the specialty line. We document them under the skill whose specialty unlocks them and invent nothing further about them.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Snipe (저격)

Section titled “Snipe (저격)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and restores 120 MP. What it is for: this is your left click, your mana battery and the anchor of the cancel loop (평캔) that holds the whole class together. A large share of a Ranger’s total damage is basic attacks, not skills (KR, 2025-12-29). Its mana-restoration specialty is mandatory while levelling, because mana is the real bottleneck of the first 30 levels (Global, LST 2026-09-17). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% MP recovery / 1.5% HP absorb / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Aimed Shot’s cooldown. 16: adds the 4th chain link, Gust Arrow (질풍 화살), about which the database says absolutely nothing else: no entry, no text, no numbers. We know it exists and that it is the fourth hit of the left-click chain, and that is all. Chains into: Continuous Fire (연사) or Spiral Arrow (나선 화살), both at 100%. It is the only skill in the kit that offers two alternative chains in the same slot.

Rapid Fire (속사)

Section titled “Rapid Fire (속사)”

Kind: active · Cost: 96 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target up to 20 m away. Groggy gauge damage: 1. What it is for: the other half of the loop. It is the right click, your pack clear and the filler you alternate with Snipe to cut the recovery frames. Watch out for the classic trap: ranking it past 16 adds nothing, and its second level-8 specialty (+10% skill critical) is a dead stat once you are crit-capped, something a Korean creator tested and published after being mocked for saying it (KR, 2026-08-09). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% MP cost / +10% skill critical / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit. 12: 10% knockback on hit. 16: +20% skill speed. Chains into: nothing.

Snare Arrow (올가미 화살)

Section titled “Snare Arrow (올가미 화살)”

Kind: active · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target up to 20 m away and slows them for 5 s with -40% movement speed. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: this is what switches half the class on. The slow is what enables Rupture Arrow, what multiplies Explosive Arrow and what makes the Binding Eye passive start proccing. In PvP it is how you keep your distance without spending a hard control. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% to the slow / +20% skill speed / usable while moving. 12: adds the 2nd chain link, Shackle Arrow (족쇄 화살), of which we know only the name: no source describes it and the database gives it no entry. From the name (족쇄 means shackle) and from the pattern of the rest of the kit, a follow-up hit that binds is the likely shape, but that is our inference, not data. 16: +2 s to slow and bind duration. Chains into: Shackle Arrow, only with the level-12 specialty.

Target Arrow (표적 화살)

Section titled “Target Arrow (표적 화살)”

The Global client calls it Marking Shot (Global, LST 2026-09-17).

Kind: active · Cost: 50 MP · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m. You move in the direction you are holding and enter Precision (정밀) for 7 s. Precision: +300 critical. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: this is the upkeep that defines the rotation. Precision feeds Aimed Shot’s +35% directly and is the condition for Suppression Arrow. You recast it standing still and with 1 to 2 seconds left, never while moving and never just in case: overcasting it is itself a DPS loss, because the animation costs you (KR, 2026-08-07 and 2026-09-06). Data note: the synthesis says “+300 critical and +5% Perfect” with a 10 s duration, and the Global LST read 10 s duration on a 10 s cooldown. The database says 7 s and +300 critical only. They do not contradict each other: the +5% Perfect is the first level-8 specialty and the +3 s is the third. The guide’s 10 s is the specced skill, not the base. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +5% Perfect while it lasts / 75% knockback on hit / +3 s duration. 12: +1 consecutive cast. 16: ignores block and evasion, and lands as a critical. Chains into: nothing. That it “combos” into Suppression Arrow is a condition, not a chain.

Drilling Dart (송곳 화살)

Section titled “Drilling Dart (송곳 화살)”

Kind: active, conditional on a critical · Cost: none · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: on a critical hit, deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target up to 20 m away and restores 100 MP. The target bleeds (출혈) for 6 s: damage every second and -50% healing received. Only one of the game’s three healing-reduction effects applies at a time (this one, the Templar’s Weakening Strike, and Flame Harpoon). Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: two jobs at once. It is your solo sustain before Supporting Fire exists, through its lifesteal specialty, and it is the Ranger’s anti-healing tool in group PvP. It also explains why critical is worth more on Ranger than its damage suggests: critical gates skills, it does not only hit harder (region unknown, 2026-09-19). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 3% HP absorb / +2 s bleed / +20% skill speed. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: activates one extra time. Chains into: nothing.

Arrow Barrage (화살 난사)

Section titled “Arrow Barrage (화살 난사)”

Kind: active, conditional on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a groggy target within 20 m. What it is for: the most argued-over skill in the kit. Three Korean guides from August say flatly to take it off the bar because casting it already costs you DPS; a fourth, from September, places it under Rapid Fire in the cancel bar and watches it go from 12 to 30 hits while also feeding the Blessing of the Bow gauge (KR, 2026-09-06). Nobody has reconciled the two positions. Its level-16 specialty is one of the best in the kit in the abstract (-1 s to all your cooldowns on hit), which makes the argument more annoying still. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% HP absorb / restores 120 MP on hit / usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Chains into: nothing.

Gale Arrow (광풍 화살)

Section titled “Gale Arrow (광풍 화살)”

Kind: active, charge skill · Cost: 200 MP · Cast: 0.5 s / 1 s / 1.5 s · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 20 m · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target up to 20 m away and puts you in Gale (광풍) for 10 s. Gale: +7% combat speed. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: this is your combat-speed buff, and with the duration specialty it becomes effectively permanent. The Global LST measured it at 50 s duration on a 20 s cooldown, meaning full coverage (Global, LST 2026-09-17), which only adds up with the +5 s specialty at 8 plus whatever gear and ranks contribute. At the base 20 s cooldown and 10 s duration, the unspecced version leaves half the time uncovered. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +5 s duration / knockback on hit / usable while moving. 12: the fewer targets you hit, up to +8% more combat speed from Gale. 16: -10 s cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Explosive Trap (폭발의 덫)

Section titled “Explosive Trap (폭발의 덫)”

Kind: active, trap · Cost: 200 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Cast: instant · Target: an area at your feet Rank 20: entry identical. Text: places an area trap near you. The trap lasts 10 s and, when an enemy approaches, deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m and stuns them for 3 s with a 40% chance. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: hard control for solo pulls and for peeling someone off you in PvP. One warning that is not in the database and is in the Korean PvP guide: the damage only registers if the target is in your own line of sight when it detonates (KR, 2026-01-23), so placing it and turning around does not count. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): pulls enemies in on detonation / +1 s stun / delayed damage on detonation. 12: +25% stun chance. 16: becomes a charge skill. Chains into: nothing.

Rupture Arrow (파열 화살)

Section titled “Rupture Arrow (파열 화살)”

Kind: active, conditional on slow or bind · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single in the entry, up to 4 in the text Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a slowed or bound target up to 20 m away. Groggy gauge damage: 15. What it is for: it is Snare Arrow’s payoff. Without a slow or a bind it does not fire, so its place in the macro always sits below the skill that applies the state. The 2026-03-25 patch rewrote it from unused to core and raised its base from 13,900 to 15,900, around 19,000 with its new specialty (KR, 2026-03). Data note: the rank-1 entry says “single target” while its own text already talks about 4 enemies. It contradicts itself and we leave it as it is. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 10% HP absorb / +50% multi-hit on hit / usable while moving. 12: up to +20% damage the fewer targets you hit. 16: on hit, 25% chance to reset its own cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Suppression Arrow (제압 화살)

Section titled “Suppression Arrow (제압 화살)”

Kind: active, conditional on you being in Precision · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m while you are in Precision and stuns it for 3 s with a 40% chance. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: it is the stun that hangs off Target Arrow, and in PvE it is a trap. A Korean creator recommended it, players reported dying to it, and he publicly reversed himself: it roots you in place, the animation is long and it gets you killed (KR, 2026-01-16 and 2026-08-09). In PvP, on the other hand, it is still a real 20 m control that you already had loaded by keeping Precision up. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +50% multi-hit on hit / +1 s stun / +20% skill speed. 12: +20% stun chance. 16: on hit, 25% chance to reset its own cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Aimed Shot (조준 화살)

Section titled “Aimed Shot (조준 화살)”

The Global client calls it Dead Shot (Global, LST 2026-09-17).

Kind: active, charge skill · Cost: 200 MP · Cast: 0.5 s / 1 s / 1.5 s (3 stages) · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: charge skill Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m. If you are in Precision, the damage increases by 35%. Maximum 3 charge stages. Groggy gauge damage: 10 to 16. What it is for: it is “the flower of the Ranger”, the biggest hit in the kit and the only skill whose groggy-gauge contribution depends on how long you charge. Always level 20, always fully specced (KR, 2026-08-07 and 2026-08-09). Full charge, not tap: the 2026-03-25 patch raised charge-stage damage by 40% and that is what flipped the argument; the creator’s own parse gives around 430,000 at full charge against around 270,000 tapping it (KR, 2026-03 and 2026-08). For the same reason it never goes in the macro, exactly like the Templar’s Punishment: the macro fires stage one and leaves you with the weakest of the three. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): removes the MP cost / +30% skill speed (the one every levelling guide recommends) / usable while moving. 12: ignores block and evasion, and lands as multi-hit. 16: on hit, 25% chance to reset its own cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, air-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. Text: removes your stun, knockdown, air-bind, grab, freeze and fear states and puts you in Resolute (강인함) for 5 s. Resolute: immunity to status effects. What it is for: it is your only real defensive, and on a Ranger “defensive” means “stay alive long enough to get the distance back”. Against Assassin you always hold it: his opener is described as impossible to react to without a cleanse ready (KR, 2025-12-29). And it is not only a cleanse: it is the trigger for Lightning Arrow, which none of our prose syntheses records. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Resolute, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance / on cast, restores 1000 stamina / on cast, restores 20% of your max HP (the one the levelling guides recommend). 12: +2 s Resolute. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Lightning Arrow (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

The three chains are buttons that only exist after the skill that triggers them, and all three activate at 100%. One database detail to be clear about: chains have no specialties of their own, they display their parent skill’s. What you pick on Snipe applies equally to Continuous Fire and Spiral Arrow, and what you pick on Shock Release applies to Lightning Arrow. No chain relationship on Ranger is partial: there is not a single chance-based link in the whole kit.

Continuous Fire (연사)

Section titled “Continuous Fire (연사)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and restores 120 MP. What it is for: second hit of the left click, option 1 of 2. Same mana as Snipe. Chained from: Snipe, slot 1.

Spiral Arrow (나선 화살)

Section titled “Spiral Arrow (나선 화살)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and restores 150 MP. What it is for: the other option for the second hit, and the biggest mana return in the whole kit. If you are short on mana while levelling, and you will be, this is the branch you want. Chained from: Snipe, slot 2.

Lightning Arrow (번개 화살)

Section titled “Lightning Arrow (번개 화살)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 20 m · Target: single Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and stuns it for 3 s. What it is for: this is the most ignored thing in the Ranger kit and it needs saying plainly: it is not a loose 2-minute skill, it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a control, which means that every time someone stuns you in PvP they are handing you a guaranteed 3 s stun at 20 m. Cleanse and counter-attack on the same button. None of our prose syntheses records it as a chain; the database does (Shock Release’s link_skill points here). Chained from: Shock Release, slot 1.

Passives

Section titled “Passives”

The ten passives have no specialties. What they do have, and this is where the class’s real scaling lives, are numbers that do change with rank, sometimes brutally. Four of the ten change nothing visible: they are fixed-chance procs whose damage the database does not publish.

Eye of Focus (집중의 눈)

Section titled “Eye of Focus (집중의 눈)”

The Global client calls it Focused Eye (Global, LST 2026-09-17).

Text: your accuracy increases by 100, your PvE damage amplification by 1%, your PvP by 0.5% and your Smite (강타) by 0.2%. At rank 20, +20%, +10% and +4%. What it is for: it is the passive every source across ten months puts first, and the curve explains why: the 1% at rank 1 looks like nothing and the 20% at rank 20 is not. You chase it on every armour piece, on the necklace and earrings through soul engraving, and on both Arcana cards. The 1.16 patch (KR, 2026-01) rewrote it: it used to give flat attack power, now it gives Smite and damage amplification. Smite is the double-damage proc, and the Global client calls it Double Chance.

Hunter’s Resolve (사냥꾼의 결의)

Section titled “Hunter’s Resolve (사냥꾼의 결의)”

Text: your critical damage amplification increases by 6%. At rank 20, by 25%. What it is for: the second passive in the PvE queue, and on the Global LST the very first (Global, LST 2026-09-17). In PvP it is deliberately deprioritised: Ranger crit is not explosive and opponents stack critical resistance (KR, 2026-01-23).

Vigilant Eye (경계의 눈)

Section titled “Vigilant Eye (경계의 눈)”

Text: your evasion increases by 200 and your max HP by 6%, and a successful evade restores HP. Internal cooldown: 3 s. At rank 20, +25% max HP. What it is for: this is the Ranger’s PvP passive. The Korean Solo Arena Ranger’s build is built around evasion, with Agility capped at 200 and these +200 evasion on top (KR, 2026-01-23). The Nature’s Breath stigma multiplies its effect by 1.5 while it lasts.

Contract of Recovery (회생의 계약)

Section titled “Contract of Recovery (회생의 계약)”

Text: your status resistance increases by 17% and, while you are stunned, knocked down, air-bound, frozen or feared, every hit you take grants +10% status resistance for 5 s, stacking up to 10 times. When your HP drops below 10%, you instantly restore 35% of your max HP. Internal cooldowns: 1 min for the heal, 1 s for the resistance. At rank 20, +55% resistance and a 54% heal. What it is for: the automatic safety net that none of the Korean damage builds mentions. It shows up only in passing, in the Abyss gear guide, which recommends locking Contract of Recovery and Resolve of Resistance on your accessories when reforging (KR, 2026-09-19). If chain-control is what kills you, this is the answer.

Resolve of Resistance (저항의 결의)

Section titled “Resolve of Resistance (저항의 결의)”

Text: your physical and shock resistance increase by 11.5%. At rank 20, by 40%. What it is for: pure anti-chain-control, and the other half of that Abyss accessory recommendation. A stunned Ranger is not a Ranger doing less damage: it is a dead Ranger.

Hunter’s Soul (사냥꾼의 혼)

Section titled “Hunter’s Soul (사냥꾼의 혼)”

Text: on a critical, 50% chance of dealing extra damage. Internal cooldown: 1 s. What it is for: a new passive this round. Section titled “Stigmas”

Stigmas run on a different scale: their specialties land at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, not 8/12/16, and they rank up with Stigma Shards, not skill points. Combos

Section titled “Combos”

(a) The chains the database defines

Section titled “(a) The chains the database defines”

There are only five chain relationships in the whole Ranger kit, and three of them exist only if you buy the corresponding specialty. Everything else the guides call a “combo” on this class is a condition or a macro priority.

Trigger Link Activation Requirement
Snipe (저격) Continuous Fire (연사) or Spiral Arrow (나선 화살), slots 1 and 2 100% none
Snipe (저격) Gust Arrow (질풍 화살), 4th link 100% level-16 specialty
Snare Arrow (올가미 화살) Shackle Arrow (족쇄 화살), 2nd link 100% level-12 specialty
Binding Trap (결박의 덫) Retreat Slash (퇴보 베기), 2nd link 100% level-5 specialty
Shock Release (충격 해제) Lightning Arrow (번개 화살) 100% having eaten a control effect

Four readings that come out of this and out of no guide:

  • There is not a single partial chain on Ranger. All of them are 100%. This matters because a low-chance link is poison in a macro: on a Taiwanese Cleric a 15% link more than halved his dummy DPS (TW, 2026-03-18). Here that trap does not exist.
  • The left-click chain is your mana economy. Snipe returns 120 and its two follow-ups 120 and 150. It is literally where the mana you spend on Rapid Fire comes from.
  • Snipe is the only skill in the kit with two alternative chains. The database lists them in slots 1 and 2 and does not say how you choose between them: whether it is a player choice, an alternation or a random branch, no source clarifies it.
  • Lightning Arrow is the only chain that fires off a defensive. Do not put it in the macro expecting it to come out on its own: it only exists in the window after you break a control.

And the three follow-ups born of a specialty (Gust Arrow, Shackle Arrow, Retreat Slash) have no entry of their own anywhere. If you buy those specialties, you are buying a hit whose damage, cost and effect nobody has published.

(b) The cancel loop, which is the entire class

Section titled “(b) The cancel loop, which is the entire class”

This is the mechanic that defines Ranger, and it is on no skill entry.

What it is: the basic attack has recovery frames, and alternating Snipe (left click) with Rapid Fire (right click) cuts them. Korea calls it 평캔 and every Korean guide from December 2025 onward describes it as mandatory, not as an optional trick (KR, 2025-12-08, 2026-01-02 and 2026-08-07). A large share of a Ranger’s total damage comes from there and not from skills.

Why the cancel-bar order matters: placing Rapid Fire ahead of the classic “Sniper Cancel” spot is worth roughly 3% DPS on its own, and 6% cumulatively with the rest of the repositioning (KR, 2026-09-06). It is the only measured figure that exists about bar placement.

What has not changed, and it is good news: the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 added anti-macro cancel delays, but only to Templar and Assassin. Ranger’s loop was left untouched (KR, 2026-09-16). It is worth stating because the class’s entire endgame is that loop and Korea has just shown it is willing to nerf cancel play class by class.

Three settings that appear in every guide:

  • Skill reservation (스킬 예약) off. With it on, Snipe fails to fire under every setup described (KR, 2026-01 through 2026-09).
  • Swap left and right click in the key settings: you hold the cancel-safe button down and tap the other to inject skills.
  • Do not mash left click while holding right click: the inputs get eaten. Briefly release left click at the moment you fire a skill (KR, 2026-01-23).

(c) PvE rotations and macro chains

Section titled “(c) PvE rotations and macro chains”

The actual rotation, on the mature build (KR, 2026-08-07, 2026-08-09 and 2026-09-06): you open with the buff and with Target Arrow, and from there you hold left and right click down. The only two things that need manual timing are reapplying Target Arrow (standing still, with 1 to 2 s left) and the buff stigmas as they come off cooldown. That is all of it. There is no priority to memorise because the macro executes it for you.

The macro system’s rule: the macro fires the first available skill going down the list, in strict priority order, and skips the ones whose condition is not met. On Ranger this shows more than on any other class, because five of its twelve actives are conditional: a macro-chained skill does not fire just from sitting in the sequence, it needs its condition met (region unknown, 2026-09-19). Drilling Dart will not come out without a prior critical, Rupture Arrow will not come out without a slow, Arrow Barrage will not come out without groggy.

A Korean Ranger’s macro chain, after the LST (KR, 2026-09-18): mouse wheel for the held basic attack; right click (duplicated on key 0) held down for a skill macro of Drilling Dart (송곳) → Gale Arrow (광풍) → Rapid Fire (속사); key 9 for bundled buffs. What is honest about that source is what it admits: he found no rule for the internal order, leading with Drilling Dart sometimes beats leading with Gale Arrow and sometimes does not, and the delay he uses may be placebo. He rebuilt the whole setup after a dungeon DPS check came back lower than expected.

Macro delays: around 10 ms under 50 ping, 40 to 50 ms at 80 to 100 or more, and tune from there (KR and region unknown, 2026-09-19). A Taiwanese guide warns that the value has to be recalibrated for Global’s ping, which is not Korea’s (TW, 2026-09-17). Copying another player’s number verbatim does not work: the same macro performs differently on different hardware.

A Global-specific difference by combat mode: in tap-targeting mode Snipe keeps firing after another skill; in action-combat mode the character stops, so the mana-restoring basic attack has to be re-injected into the sequence (region unknown, 2026-09-19). Same setup, two behaviours, and it decides whether your macro runs dry on mana or not.

Point cost, to order your priorities: every active costs 21 points to level 10 and nothing from 11 to 20. Since there are 25 skills that rank up with points (12 actives, 3 chains and 10 passives) at 21 points each, a fresh character cannot fund all of it: what a build actually chases is the specialties at 8, 12 and 16, not the level number. Ranking a skill past its specialisation threshold adds nothing measurable, tested by hand at Rapid Fire 24 and another skill at 26 (KR, 2026-08-09). At level 37 on the Global LST you had roughly 150 skill points, 15 stigma points and 130 Daevanion points, with the Tridion board still locked (Global, LST 2026-09-17).

(d) PvP: openers, control and escapes

Section titled “(d) PvP: openers, control and escapes”

The one dense source on Ranger PvP is the build of a Korean player who claimed to be the #1 Solo Arena Ranger in Season 2 (KR, 2026-01-23). It predates the 30% PvP damage cut, the 2026-08-26 rebalance and Global’s 4-stigma cap, so read it as method, not as numbers.

Play from the air. You rebind “ascend while looking down” to a mouse thumb button so you can climb without releasing either click. It is the difference between a Ranger who keeps firing while gaining height and one who stops hitting every time he moves.

Your knockbacks are dashes that ground classes do not have. Three of them come from specialties and one from a passive: Target Arrow at 75% (level-8 specialty), Gale Arrow (level-8 specialty), Rapid Fire at 10% (level-12 specialty) and Close-Range Shot at 5% against anyone within 5 m. Every knockback you land is distance recovered, and distance is the whole class.

The counter-control chain almost nobody uses: Shock Release → Lightning Arrow. It cleanses stun, knockdown, air-bind, grab, freeze and fear, gives you 5 s of status immunity and hands you a guaranteed 3 s stun at 20 m. It is the only sequence in the kit that turns being controlled into controlling, and no guide describes it as a chain: it comes from the database.

Your three stigma controls, ordered by what they solve: Seal Arrow silences for 5 s at 75% and is what you want against a healer or a caster; Binding Trap air-binds for 3 s and also strips up to 3 buffs with its level-10 specialty, the class’s only dispel; Phantom Arrow blinds for 3 s on only a 30 s cooldown. Explosive Trap and Suppression Arrow round out the controls outside the stigmas.

Matchups, from the same source: against Gladiator, assume he closes and use the dash pre-emptively at 20 m, not reactively; if his gap-closer lands while you are both airborne, stop trading and disengage. Against Assassin, always hold Shock Release: his opener is described as unreactable (KR, 2025-12-29).

PvP stigmas: out of the thirteen, that build considers only Baizel’s Authority at 20 and Blessing of the Bow at 20 worth taking, for the synergy between their 60 s cooldown and 20 s duration, which with about 8 kills gives permanent Baizel uptime. That was written with 5 slots and before the PvP damage cut; nobody has re-ordered Ranger’s PvP stigmas against Global’s cap of 4.

And one settings rule that is not about combat but costs you fights: set automatic item use to “on movement”, not “on combat start”. Ranger only enters combat state by casting a skill, so with the default setting your buffs fire early and are wasted (KR, 2026-01-23).

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Aimed Shot (Dead Shot). It is a 3-stage charge; the macro fires stage one, so you trade around 430,000 for around 270,000 (KR, 2026-03 and 2026-08). The Global build guides keep it off the macro for exactly this.
  • Explosive Arrow. Off the macro on the Global build, so you control when it lands and make it coincide with the slow (region unknown, 2026-09-19).
  • Target Arrow (Marking Shot). Its animation is itself a DPS loss when overcast. It goes manual, standing still, with 1 to 2 s left (KR, 2026-08-07 and 2026-09-06).
  • Suppression Arrow, in PvE. Immobile, long cast, it gets you killed. The very creator who had recommended it reversed himself after players reported dying (KR, 2026-01-16 and 2026-08-09).
  • Rupture Arrow’s level-16 specialty, if you full-charge Aimed Shot. That 25% reset is exactly what desyncs a full charge; if you full-charge, do not buy it (KR, 2026-08-09).
  • Reactive defensives, Shock Release and Nature’s Breath included. Shock Release has a state condition, so technically the macro would skip it, but what you lose is worse: the moment you press it is its entire value.
  • Passives, which simply cannot be put in one.
  • More than one row. Two or more rows reduce how often each skill in the macro fires.

What is still unresolved

The English names. The database gives an English name for only 10 of the 38 skills. The rest are our translations or names taken from guides, and three we know from the Global client (Marking Shot, Dead Shot, Mother Nature). There is also one name we could not map: Global creators talk about “Temple Shot” or “Tempest Shot” for the spammable area skill and it is not clear which Korean skill it corresponds to (연사, 나선 화살 or 화살 난사). Check it during Advanced Access from 30 September.

The three follow-ups with no entry. Gust Arrow (질풍 화살), Shackle Arrow (족쇄 화살) and Retreat Slash (퇴보 베기) exist only inside the text of a specialty. None has an entry, text or numbers in the database, and no prose source describes them. If you buy those specialties, you do not know exactly what you are buying.

Ranger stealth, and why it has to be said twice. 은신 / Stealth is a real stigma, one of the thirteen, on a 1 min 48 s cooldown and restricted to out of combat until the level-15 specialty. That is in the Korean database of 2026-09-22. What we reject is a different claim: that Ranger has innate, always-available stealth, which came from two Global class-overview videos during the LST week, with no footage, and which the detailed Global LST Ranger guide contradicts by listing no such skill. We treat it as confusion with the Assassin. The two claims are not the same: the stigma exists, the class-wide stealth does not. And on the stigma, what is genuinely open remains: no source in the corpus evaluates it against the other twelve, so we do not know whether it deserves one of Global’s four slots.

Supporting Fire’s numbers do not add up. The rank-1 entry says a 15 s orb, a 25% chance and 1.5% HP absorb; the Korean syntheses talk about 30 s, 50% of damage as healing and around 13,000 per proc (KR, 2026-03). Most likely the same skill at rank 20 with gear against the base tooltip, but no source bridges the two and the gap is an order of magnitude.

Blessing of the Bow, self or party. The database says target “시전자”, that is yourself. The KR syntheses describe it as a party buff of +200 critical and also credit it with a rank-20 effect (resetting all cooldowns on a kill) that appears in none of its four specialties. Unreconciled.

Griffon’s Arrow, 0.5 s over 5 s or 1 s over 10 s. The database says a 1 s interval and a 10 s duration; the March patch synthesis says 0.5 s and 5 s. The level-20 specialty halves the interval, which explains one of the two figures but not the other.

Aimed Shot’s charge stages. The database says 0.5 s / 1 s / 1.5 s. One synthesis transcribes “4 s / 7 s / 1.1 s”, which is not internally coherent and is almost certainly an auto-caption transcription error. We keep the database.

Arrow Barrage: off the bar, or under Rapid Fire. Three Korean guides from August say the first, one from September says the second and measures 12 to 30 hits (KR, 2026-09-06). It is a single recent source against three earlier ones, and it depends on cancel-bar mechanics that may behave differently on Global.

Arrow Storm and the groggy bonus. The syntheses give it +50% damage against groggy targets; the database shows no such thing in any specialty. It may be a global groggy-state mechanic rather than a skill effect, but nobody has checked.

No Ranger measurement on Global past level 37. The LST capped at 37, with Abyss, arenas and battlegrounds closed. Last place on the one-minute dummy parse (around 225,000 against Chanter’s 564,000) is a figure from that bracket and from a single source, not an endgame ranking, and nobody has published a PvP win rate after the 2026-08-26 rebalance.
