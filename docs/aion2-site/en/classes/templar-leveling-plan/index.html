Templar: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 over the KR/TW kit to 2026-09-16 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the Judgment loop and the macros live on Templar in PvE. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  4. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas at level 22 and leave them alone.
  5. Daevanion nodes refund for 500 Kinah each on Korea, so spend points the day you earn them.
  6. Any Korean or Taiwanese build you find runs 5 or 6 stigmas: cut it to 4 before you copy it.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: two buttons and the Ascension bar

Section titled “1 to 22: two buttons and the Ascension bar”
  • Skills, in this order: left-click Fierce Strike (맹렬한 일격), your mana battery, and right-click Continuous Flurry (연속 난타, alias Vicious Strike). Both as early as possible: their level is capped by your character level (Global, 2026-09-21).
  • Third: the charge skill on the fourth slot, the only charge ability early Templar has, for clearing packs while questing and in dungeons. The source names it only by its slot, not by its name (Global, 2026-09-21).
  • Passives: Fury (격앙) first, then Roar of Insult (모욕의 포효), then HP and incoming healing (KR/TW, 2026-09). Two September sources disagree: one on the Global build puts Blessing of Condemnation (단죄의 가호, crit) first because a tank’s sustain is already high, and Fury third (Global, 2026-09-21); another gives Fury → Impact Hit → Roar (region unknown, 2026-09-20). The majority keeps Fury at the top: it is a buff for the whole party and it procs by itself while you block.
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens around level 12-13, after “Urgent Pursuit” (Global, 2026-09-19). Spend the points the day you earn them: the board is “roughly a third of your early power” (Global, 2026-09-19). Blue nodes first (active-skill level): on Global the orange cores lost between a third and a half of their value while a blue node still buys a whole skill level (Global, 2026-09-18). Some Global creators argue the opposite, orange first; the disagreement is live.
  • Gear: all disposable. Nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20). Belt and Amulet are the permanent exception.
  • Gate before you move on: level 22 has a red quest that blocks the main story until the world-progress bar is at 100%, filled with regional quests (J key) and sealed dungeons, about six activities. One measured route reached 97.1% using only stops that sit on the natural path: Treasure Hunter Residence (~11), Trickster Dent, a “?” sealed dungeon, skip the next one, Poisonous Plant Habitat (~13, the fastest dungeon in the game) and Quarry of the Noble (~15). Total 1 to 22: 1 h 10 min (Global, 2026-09-19).

Do not:

  • Detour for feathers. On Global one pickup counts as 3 and they show on the map; their only job is taking the Amulet to blue (Global, 2026-09-19/20).
  • Skip green side quests: each one is +1 skill point, +1 Daevanion point and +3 permanent passive stat points (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Spend a key on a yellow cube: yellow cubes open for free (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Leave a stronghold without its 2-3 one-time chests: they pay off-cap Kinah and they do not come back (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Leave Defiance on the space bar: mashing it out of a stun summons your wings (region unknown, 2026-09-18).
  • Leave the boss orientation indicator on “back”: Templar hits from the front (region unknown, 2026-09-18).

22 to 27: first stigma slot, first dungeon

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot, first dungeon”
  • Skills: keep pushing Fierce Strike and Continuous Flurry towards 8 (13 points each, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22) and then place the specialisation: unlocking it and placing it are two separate steps in the UI.
  • Stigma, slot 1. There is no consensus and the sources pull two ways:
    • The levelling reading (majority for solo play): a damage or clear stigma. Shield of Ruin / Doom Shield (파멸의 방패) does the most for you: its level-5 specialisation resets Annihilation’s recast on hit, and the skill itself activates the Judgment window on the target for 7 s (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22; TW levelling order, 2026-09-01). Alternative: Sovereign’s Punishment (주신의 징벌), which at level 5 gains up to +20% damage the fewer targets it hits.
    • The tank reading (Korean guides): Taunt (도발) plus Protective Shield (보호의 방패) as the core pair (KR, 2026-04). Against that reading sits the Korean patch of 2026-09-16: Taunt’s threat went from a flat +20,000 to damage-proportional, so it is weakest exactly when you are undergeared, which is this whole band.
    • Whichever you take, stop it at 5 (4 shards) until you know your four. Effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20.
  • Daevanion: Accuracy early and unconditionally, so your Taunts do not miss; Korean groups wiped on the Uruguru boss from lost aggro (TW, 2026-09-05). Then blue nodes for Punishment and Judgment.
  • Gear: Crow Cave at level 22 asks for gear score 200 and the normal route clears it easily (Global, 2026-09-19/20). Enhancement cap is still +5 green / +10 blue.
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Krao Cave, Draupnir) (Global LST, 2026-09). Do not skip it: that is where your first weapon and your back-armour pieces come from.

Do not: push one skill to 10 before the others are at 8; spread shards across four stigmas at once; copy a 5-stigma build.

27 to 32: second slot

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot”
  • Skills: the rest of the core actives to 8, and the two main ones towards 12 (the second specialisation slot). No source gives a fine order inside this band: general rule, take first whatever you press in every pull.
  • Stigma, slot 2. Complete the pair: if you took damage at 22, group utility goes here; if you took Taunt, damage goes here. Protective Shield (보호의 방패) is the piece that repeats most in Korean tank guides: guaranteed block, +100% block damage reduction and 110 MP restored on block (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Daevanion: attack, then attack and cooldown speed, favouring speed because Judgment is a static cast (TW, 2026-09-05). Skip the crit-damage line: in Global level-37 builds a Templar’s crit rate is too low to pay for it (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most; nothing further until the gold tier (Global, 2026-09-18/20).
  • Gate before you move on: no hard gate in this band. The next one is 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score (one source read 1,500) (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take a PvP stigma into a dungeon; with four slots that is a slot thrown away.

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar filled with exploration and side quests, then the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18). Side quests come back around 32-33 after being sparse since 21 (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Skills: two or three core skills to 16, which is the fifth specialisation effect. On Judgment (심판) that step 16 is 재시전 시간 삭제, removing its cooldown (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). One source of unknown region puts it at 20; the Korean database puts it at 16.
  • Stigma, slot 3. The group-survival piece: Shield of Nezekan (네자칸의 방패), rated “the best skill” for keeping a party alive, target level 15 (KR, 2025-11). If you play alone, Double Armor (이중 갑옷) pays more.
  • Daevanion: the defence bonus, then the fork: with a static → damage tolerance; without a static → damage boost, to solo-clear faster (TW, 2026-09-05).
  • Gear: start watching gear score for real. 1,000 is the first Ascension Trial rank’s requirement and the reason not to rush the story (Global, 2026-09-18/19).
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2-star; 1,000 for the Ascension Trial, which also drops shards.

Do not: dodge normal attacks. Every block is Fury for the whole party, a threat spike and a heal. And do not copy Korean dodge timing: on Global the hit lands about 24 frames after the circle fills, against about 8 on Korea, roughly three times the window (one creator’s frame count on his own footage, Global, 2026-09-18).

37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at level 37. This is where you close the set of four you will carry all of Season 1.
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40 (Global, 2026-09-17). The Asphel (PvP) board opens at 45; the PvE board was not in the test build.
  • Skills: the core ones towards 20. Past 10 they no longer depend on points: they depend on the four boards (+4), on Arcana cards and on soul bindings from rings and weapons (Global LST, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • The levelling specialisation you will undo later: one Global-client guide puts life absorb on Judgment, heal-over-time on Warding Strike and absorb on Punishment while levelling, then trades all of it for maximum damage at 45. Respec is free, so testing it is cheap (Global, 2026-09-19).
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20).
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100 (one source read 2,200); Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: sit at 14/14 Nightmare entries without spending. Two generate a day and anything over the cap is lost (Global, 2026-09-18/19). And clearing Zikel does not hand you the Colossus statue: buying it for 14,000 Nightmare points is a second step people forget.

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21), so the whole absorb-and-heal branch you levelled with converts into endgame damage and utility the day you arrive.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are not paid in points but in Stigma Shards, taking all four to 20 is roughly 300 shards (~75 each), and levelling to 45 while doing every side quest yields on the order of 20 (KR). The cost of resetting them on Global nobody has seen. That is why the four get chosen at 22 and then left alone.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes. On Korea they refund at 500 Kinah each and a whole branch for ~13,500 (KR, 2025-11), removed from the tips inwards. One low-quality Global source says the cost rises with each reset; with no second source, do not plan on it.

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source publishes a node map, so this is a priority order, not a route. Taiwanese order of 2026-09-05 with the Global adjustments of 2026-09-17/19.

  1. Accuracy, early and unconditionally: one missed Taunt sinks a group.
  2. Blue nodes for Punishment, then Judgment, then Pummel / Continuous Flurry (Taiwan, 2026-09-04).
  3. Flat Attack (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan).
  4. Attack and cooldown speed, favouring speed: Judgment is a static cast.
  5. Blue nodes for Warding Strike, then Annihilate.
  6. The defence bonus.
  7. The fork: with a static, damage tolerance; without one, damage boost (1.5% per node on Global against Korea’s 5%).
  8. Skip the crit-damage line: a Templar’s crit rate does not pay for it (Global, 2026-09-17). And the +50/+50 board-centre pieces no longer exist on Global.

Still contested or unconfirmed

The Templar levelling skill order rests on a single Global source, of medium quality, which names several skills only by their mouse slot (2026-09-21). The passives are worse: three sources, three different orders (Fury first, Blessing of Condemnation first, Fury → Impact Hit → Roar). This page publishes the majority order, not a consensus.

Nobody has seen the stigma reset cost on Global. Skill-point resets are free, but that is a different system. Plan the four from level 22.

Shield Smite’s MP specialisation reads the opposite way depending on the source. Two Korean and Taiwanese guides warn against taking the specialisation that raises the cost from 100 to 300 MP; the Inven database of 2026-09-22 says its level-8 specialisation removes the mana cost and returns 200 MP on hit. Check it in the client before you place it.

Fury’s party buff duration is unresolved: 10 s in the Korean database, 20 s according to one Global-build guide. The likely reconciliation is that the 20 s figure belongs to the separate “enhanced Fury” gear effect.

Which Korean patch state Global ships is unknown. NC said class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global and that patches apply to all of them at once, so the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 should be your launch kit, but nobody tested it: the test capped at level 37 with the Abyss locked. Korea has been in a run of near-weekly Templar hotfixes since 2026-08-26 that its own community computes as a net DPS loss.

The PvE Daevanion board was not in the test build. Its point sinks still exist in the Nightmare shop and in reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch. Do not plan Season 1 counting on it.
