Region: Korea · Version: Inven skill-simulator data pulled on 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: high on the numbers (this is the straight database dump), medium on the English names (working translations, not the official localisation).

All 39 Chanter skills (호법성, job 9) as Inven serves them: 12 Active, 4 Chain (the follow-ups that can only be cast after the skill that links them), 10 Passive and 13 Stigma. Values are rank 1 of 20; rank is bought with skill points, and for stigmas with Stigma Shards. For what to do with this kit (which mantra to run, how much off-healing is expected of you and the Spin Strike rework), see Chanter. How to read this table

The English names come from our own syntheses and the glossary, not from an official localisation: Global may well call them something else until the 09-30 Advanced Access (early access) confirms the real names. The English names and the short descriptions in the table are our own translation, not an official one; each skill’s original Korean text is one click away in its row. The numbers are from the September 2026 Korean build and Global launches on Season 1, so expect balance differences; on top of that, Global equips only 4 stigma slots, so these 13 stigmas are the full catalogue, not a build.
