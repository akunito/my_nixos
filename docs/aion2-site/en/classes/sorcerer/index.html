Sorcerer

Region: Korea and Taiwan · Version: up to 2026-09-15 · Reviewed: 2026-09-16 · Confidence: high. Short guide.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Sorcerer (마도성) is the spellbook fire and ice ranged nuker, “the closest thing Aion 2 has to a traditional glass-cannon mage”. Not a Templar or Cleric alternative: zero party demand, zero utility, pure personal damage.
  • One of the two hardest classes to play well, with Assassin (6.9/10). Unanimous consensus: not a beginner main.
  • Raw damage is fine (+2.9% over baseline, 5th of 9); leaderboard presence is not (C-tier, 83 of 800). That gap is the whole story: the kit is strong on a stationary target and falls apart when the boss moves.
  • Relevant as a matchup: A in the Abyss (KDA 11.46) and A in 1v1 (67.9%), but Templar beats it in both. A Templar, Gladiator or Assassin closing distance on a Sorcerer is a near-guaranteed kill: no gap closer, no escape, once melee connects it loses.
  • Ping-sensitive and gear-hungry; “the class that suffers the most gatekeeping in the whole game”.
  • In short: Sorcerer is for someone who wants big numbers and a long interlocking cooldown puzzle, and accepts dying to one mistake. Spiritmaster is the safer magic DPS for the same fantasy.
  • Global ships the post-March-25 Sorcerer (5 stigmas, Hibernation, the PvE/PvP split) and probably not the August 26 rebalance.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Two elements, two jobs: fire = sustained damage and cooldown recycling; ice = utility, CC and burst. In PvP, ice to lock and fire to kill.
  • The kit is a cooldown-reduction chain, not a spam rotation: “one ability reduces the cooldown of another”; it uses a much larger share of its bar than other classes. Every guide rediscovers the Firestorm → Hellfire loop.
  • It comes online late: abilities only reach their real form at ranks 12, 16 and 20; early leveling is “extremely tedious” until Blaze (불꽃 폭발) unlocks its lifesteal.
  • Survivability is the weak point (“lowest in the game”), with real tools (Steel Barrier, Arctic Armor, Hibernation, lifesteal) that do not change the outcome with a melee on top.
  • Best-in-class CC: “the most quantity, variety and power of CCs in the whole game”: slows, roots, freeze, pulls, polymorph, silence.
  • Movement is the enemy: ground-anchored casts and ground zones make a moving boss a direct DPS loss. The most repeated criticism.
  • Gear- and macro-dependent: the KR number-one parse came from a mouse macro, not manual play.

Build

Section titled “Build”
  • Leveling (1-45): single target with Flame Arrow (left click) → Flame Harpoon on cooldown (5 hits apply the fire mark efficiently) → Blaze on marked targets. Hellfire from level 30, not before; Freeze → Frost Burst as the early burst. AoE: Ice Chain for damage and Bitter Cold Wind to clump. Core actives to 7 and +1 from Daevanion to reach 8 (saves points). Blaze’s HP-absorb spec as soon as it exists (~600 per cast at level 30; near-eliminates potions). Passives: Robe of Earth (mana) and Robe of Flame first. All pre-20 regional quests before pushing the main quest: the accessories dominate early attack.
  • PvE, the canonical cooldown-loop build: Firestorm with +20% skill speed, -2 s Hellfire per fireball and +15% per fireball; Hellfire with +30% speed, ignore block and evasion, multi-hit and -15 s, without the fire-DoT node (it only pays on stationary targets); Flame Arrow that applies the mark and resets Blaze. Stigmas (5): Delayed Explosion and Element Enhancement are the two everyone agrees on; then Divine Burst (groggy break), Firewall, Cold Storm, Glacial Smite, with Steel Barrier or Arctic Armor as the defensive flex (Steel Barrier stops scaling: drop it late). 20 s cycle: Element Enhancement + Wish of Concentration → Delayed Explosion → three-stage charged Hellfire → Firestorm → weave Blaze and Flame Arrow until Delayed Explosion and Hellfire come back together. Ground skills only with the boss stationary. Never put Hellfire in a macro: it fires stage 1 only and loses the 40% per-stage bonus.
  • KR number-one endgame (August 2026, Chapter 1; the Arcana math almost certainly does not exist on Global): macro A (10 ms): Bitter Cold Wind + Blaze + Flame Harpoon + Wish of Concentration; macro B: Delayed Explosion + Hellfire + Winter Shackles; Element Enhancement, Firewall and Cold Storm manual. Assumed baseline: 114%+ combat speed and 42%+ cooldown reduction (the 5.54M parse came from 91.6% and 43.4%). At 20: Hellfire, Blaze, Wish of Concentration, then Flame Harpoon, Flame Arrow, Bitter Cold Wind. Manastones: 2 Heavy Strike, 3 Max Attack (Max Attack always beats Additional Attack). Only Robe of Flame matters for damage; the rest into resistances.
  • Stats: combat speed is near-mandatory and the stat that gates the endgame macro (~100% via rolls, buffs, +5% Daevanion nodes and “Time” stats). Deprioritize multi-hit (a fast-class stat). Precision and Constitution to 200, overflow into attack. Smite, attack %, damage % and move speed are the S rolls.
  • PvP: a second stigma page with Soul Freeze (fixed 75% silence, 5 s) and the Curse line (Curse Tree at rank 5 adds hits so a follow-up Firestorm does not break your own root); Linear Space (guaranteed-hit airborne AoE) a must. Polymorph as a primary plan, no: 45.5% base resistance, a coin flip. Loop: pre-buff → ice CC → Curse Tree or Soul Freeze → charged Hellfire → Firestorm to reset Hellfire → retreat when the buff window ends, because you are naked after it. In the Abyss you are A only when your group gives you space.

Mistakes

Section titled “Mistakes”
  • Macroing Hellfire. Using Blaze right after applying the mark when another mark-dependent skill is queued (Blaze consumes it). Hellfire’s DoT node. Steel Barrier into late game (a flat ~1,400 shield that does not scale). Leveling Flame Barrage while leveling (bosses rarely stagger before 40). Banking on polymorph without stacking chance. Spending groggy breakers in immune phases. Chasing multi-hit. Picking it on high ping. Expecting group demand. Assuming any KR build transfers.
