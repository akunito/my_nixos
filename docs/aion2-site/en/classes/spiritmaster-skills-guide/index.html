Spiritmaster: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to 2026-09-22, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. Two things make this class different from the rest of this series: the Korean balance patch of 2026-09-16 does not mention Spiritmaster at all, not even in the anti-macro cancel-delay changes, so what you read here is the current Korean kit with no pending correction; and the database ships no English name for any of the 38 skills, so every English name on this page is a working translation. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, stigma level cap 20, the Ancient Spirit enchant capped at +20 instead of +25, no Heroic gear).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients, and in this class it is the only thing that exists at all, because Inven serves no English name for a single Spiritmaster skill.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. Almost no skill changes its visible numbers as it ranks up: what grows is the damage coefficient, which the database does not publish. The passives are the exception and they change a lot.
  3. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one. Two Korean guides describe a different ladder, and that argument is in the Aside at the bottom.
  4. Tell a chain apart from a condition. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears after the skill that triggers it. A condition (Elemental Fusion needs 4 elements, Domination needs a spirit to have used its skill, Continuous Barrage needs groggy) is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met. This class runs on conditions far more than on chains.
  5. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in.
  6. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  7. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  8. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on this class that list is long: seven separate places where the live tooltip and the guides say different numbers. Check them yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. The database field name_en is empty for all 38 skills, so unlike the other classes in this series there is no Inven name to fall back on. The English names here come from the cited guides where a guide gives one, and are ours where none does. The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean client text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 3 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 38 in total. All of them require an orb (보주), with no exceptions.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Cold Shock (냉기 충격)

Section titled “Cold Shock (냉기 충격)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals water damage to a target within 20 m and restores 100 MP. What it is for: the left click, the mana engine and the weaving tool. You hold it down and cancel out of it with Flame Cremation (KR, 2026-01-15). The Global Launch Scale Test guide calls it mandatory at the level-37 cap even in a build that leaves most summons unlevelled, purely because it generates mana (Global, 2026-09-18). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% MP recovery / 1.5% HP absorb / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on an Earth Tremor hit, +2% attack, stacking up to 5. 16: on an Earth Tremor hit, 10% chance to strip one buff from the target. Data note: both late specialties name Earth Tremor, which is the second link of the chain, not the first. They pay out only if you finish the chain. Chains into: Vacuum Blast (link 1) and Earth Tremor (link 2).

Flame Cremation (화염 전소)

Section titled “Flame Cremation (화염 전소)”

Called Combustion, Flame Immolation, Flame Consumption and Flame Transfer across different guides. All of them are 화염 전소.

Kind: active · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m around the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals fire damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around a target 20 m away. Groggy gauge damage: 3. What it is for: the cancel tool. You tap it to cut the post-cast recovery of what you just pressed, which is the whole reason the rotation has a rhythm at all (KR, 2026-01-15). Its level-16 specialty adds a chain link, Call of Ash (재의 부름), that Korean guides call practically the main skill of the kit (KR, 2026-07-09 and 2026-02-10); the database gives that link no entry of its own. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% MP cost / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit / becomes usable while moving. 12: +20% skill speed. 16: adds the 2nd chain link, Call of Ash (재의 부름). Data note: the Korean endgame build takes this to 20 precisely for that link (KR, 2026-09-07), while the Global level-37 guide calls it skippable that early (Global, 2026-09-18). Those are not in conflict, they are two different stages.

Summon: Fire Spirit (소환: 불의 정령)

Section titled “Summon: Fire Spirit (소환: 불의 정령)”

Kind: active, summon · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: summons the Fire spirit. It charges a target within 20 m and uses a skill dealing fire damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m. After that it keeps fighting with basic attacks and uses its skill again with a 15% chance per landed attack. Selecting the summon icon dismisses it. Each skill level raises the spirit’s attack and defense by 2%. Groggy gauge damage: 7. Spirit skill recast: 3 s. What it is for: the highest-damage spirit and the main one since the July 2026 builds (KR, 2026-07-09 and 2026-07-13). Note what “summon” means here: the modern rotation throws spirits as disposable triggers, for the damage of throwing them and to feed the element stacks, not as pets you nurse. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -50% MP cost / +20% Fire spirit critical damage / +10% skill proc chance on the spirit’s hits. 12: on the spirit’s hit, 5% chance of a 3 s knockdown. 16: +20% to the spirit’s stats.

Summon: Water Spirit (소환: 물의 정령)

Section titled “Summon: Water Spirit (소환: 물의 정령)”

Kind: active, summon · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: summons the Water spirit. It uses a skill dealing water damage to a target within 20 m. After that it keeps fighting with basic attacks and, with a 15% chance per landed attack, uses its skill and restores 20 MP to the Spiritmaster. Each skill level raises the spirit’s attack and critical by 2%. Groggy gauge damage: 7. Spirit skill recast: 3 s. What it is for: mana sustain, and the one summon the Global playtest guide says is worth levelling at the level-37 cap (Global, 2026-09-18). It carries the single hardest execution rule in the class: summon it last, with about a second of spacing. Chained back-to-back behind another summon it silently drops its own skill, which costs you both the damage and the element stack (KR, 2026-01-15; 2025-12-12; 2026-03-29). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -50% MP cost / +10% Water spirit attack / +10% skill proc chance. 12: on the spirit’s hit, 5% chance of a 2 s bind. 16: +20% to the spirit’s stats.

Joint Strike: Curse (협공: 저주)

Section titled “Joint Strike: Curse (협공: 저주)”

Kind: active · Cost: 80 MP · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m around the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around a target 20 m away and applies Curse. Curse: damage over time every second for 5 s, and the target deals 10% less damage to the Spiritmaster. You strike jointly with your spirit: Fire, area damage; Water, damage; Earth, damage and -10% status resistance; Wind, area damage and damage over time; Ancient, area damage. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: the cheapest of your two mandatory debuffs and the only one that is not a stigma. The joint-attack line is the part guides skip: which spirit is out changes what this skill does, and the Earth variant is the only status-resist shred in the kit. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +2 s Curse duration / +50% multi-hit on hit / becomes usable while moving. 12: -2 s cooldown. 16: Joint Strike: Curse ignores block and evasion and lands as a critical. Data note: guides bank a crit-damage amplification of around 20% on this skill. The current Korean text has no such line: what it has is the damage over time and the 10% damage reduction against you, which is defensive. Unreconciled.

Continuous Barrage (연속 난사)

Section titled “Continuous Barrage (연속 난사)”

Kind: active, gated on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target in a groggy state within 20 m. What it is for: a free filler with no cost and no cooldown that only exists inside a groggy window. The current Korean endgame build skips it entirely (KR, 2026-09-07), and so does the Global level-37 guide, which also throws away its level-16 specialty: -1 s to every cooldown in the kit on hit. Nobody has tested whether that is the right call in a groggy-heavy dungeon. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% HP absorb / restores 120 MP / becomes usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns.

Summon: Earth Spirit (소환: 땅의 정령)

Section titled “Summon: Earth Spirit (소환: 땅의 정령)”

Kind: active, summon · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: summons the Earth spirit. It uses a skill dealing earth damage to a target within 20 m and increasing threat; if the target is a player, 50% chance of a 2 s taunt. After that it keeps fighting with basic attacks and, with a 10% chance per landed attack, uses its skill and increases threat. Each skill level raises the spirit’s defense and health by 2%. Groggy gauge damage: 7. Spirit skill recast: 3 s. What it is for: this is the solo tool, and the reason NCsoft’s own September team update sells Spiritmaster as a solo class (Global, 2026-09-19). A playtester swapped to it to tank a boss he could not otherwise solo, then swapped back to Fire for general levelling (Global, 2026-09-18). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -50% MP cost / +20% PvE and +10% PvP damage tolerance for the spirit / +10% skill proc chance. 12: on the spirit’s hit, 5% chance to heal the spirit for 5% of its health. 16: +20% to the spirit’s stats. Data note: the database says 50% taunt chance against players. A tooltip read on camera says 30% (region unknown, 2026-09-20). We go with the data and flag it.

Domination (공간 지배)

Section titled “Domination (공간 지배)”

Called Space Dominance, Spatial Domination and Dimensional Control across guides. All of them are 공간 지배.

Kind: active, gated on a spirit having used its skill · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m around the target · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: a skill that activates for a short time every time a spirit uses its skill. Deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around a target 20 m away and slows them by 40% for 3 s. Restores 100 MP. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: the other half of the mana economy. A summon costs 150 MP and this gives 100 back (KR, 2025-12-12), which is why the rotation stays solvent at all. Its damage is minor and it is the skill everyone quotes to prove that over-levelling is waste: 13,248 at specialty 16 against 13,392 at 20, a difference of 144 (KR, 2026-07-13). In PvP it is worth nothing below level 16, where the root lives (KR, 2026-02-10). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% slow effect / extra damage 3 s after the hit / +50% multi-hit chance. 12: pulls the targets in toward the centre. 16: on hit, 50% chance of a 3 s root. Data note: every synthesis says this class has no slow. This skill is a 40% slow for 3 s. What the syntheses mean is that it is not a slow you can press on demand, because it only exists in the instant after a spirit fires its own skill.

Summon: Wind Spirit (소환: 바람의 정령)

Section titled “Summon: Wind Spirit (소환: 바람의 정령)”

Kind: active, summon · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: summons the Wind spirit. It uses a skill dealing wind damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m around a target 20 m away and healing the Spiritmaster. After that it keeps fighting with basic attacks and, with a 10% chance per landed attack, uses its skill and heals the Spiritmaster for 1.5% of max health. Each skill level raises the spirit’s accuracy and critical by 2%. Groggy gauge damage: 7. Spirit skill recast: 3 s. What it is for: the one that gets dropped. Every build from July 2026 onward runs three of the four, and Wind is the cut, because a fourth summon stretches the cycle and lowers damage (KR, 2026-07-13). It is still the early-levelling pick for its lifesteal (KR, 2026-02-10). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -50% MP cost / +20% Wind spirit critical / +10% skill proc chance. 12: on the spirit’s attack, 5% chance of -30% attack speed for 10 s. 16: +20% to the spirit’s stats.

Soul’s Scream (영혼의 절규)

Section titled “Soul’s Scream (영혼의 절규)”

Kind: active · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 45 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and with a 70% chance applies fear for 5 s. Against NPCs the fear lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 15. What it is for: one of the two single-target fears, and one of exactly four fear sources in the whole kit. PvE builds skip it outright (KR, 2026-09-07; Global, 2026-09-18); PvP builds take it to 20 (KR, 2026-03-29). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +50% multi-hit on hit / 5 s root / becomes usable while moving. 12: -10 s cooldown. 16: +2 s fear duration.

Elemental Fusion (원소 융합)

Section titled “Elemental Fusion (원소 융합)”

Kind: active, gated on 4 elements · Cost: none · Cooldown: 3 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: every time a spirit uses a skill, the Spiritmaster gains an element. At 4 stacks you enter the 4-element state and can use Elemental Fusion. It can be used only in that state, and using it deletes the 4 elements immediately. Deals damage to a target within 20 m. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: the payoff of the whole class, mandatory at 20 in every build anyone has published, Korean or English. Read the condition carefully, because the most common misunderstanding in the corpus is here: the stacks come from spirit skill uses, not from Domination casts, and a fumbled Domination does not block the burst (KR, 2025-11-25). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): becomes an area skill / extra damage 3 s after the hit / +7% amplification on all elements on hit. 12: becomes a charge skill, up to +200% damage. 16: 25% chance to regain the 4 elements instantly. Data note: the Korean endgame build casts this uncharged and warns against the charge node, which was firing repeatedly instead of charging on the Korean live server in September 2026 (KR, 2026-09-07). That is the level-12 specialty in the current data. Unconfirmed on Global.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, air-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. It is one of only two skills in the whole kit whose visible numbers change with rank. Text: removes your stun, knockdown, air bind, grab, freeze and fear states and puts you in Tenacity (강인함) for 5 s. Tenacity: immunity to status ailments. What it is for: your only CC break, and the mandatory first step of Curse of Despair. On a class with the lowest health pool in the game and no gap closer, this button plus Command: Proxy is the entire defensive kit. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Tenacity, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance / on cast, restores 1000 stamina / on cast, restores 20% of max health. 12: +2 s Tenacity. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Curse of Despair (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

There are only three chain buttons in the kit, and they come from two skills. All three activate at 100%, so they always land. One database detail: chains have no specialties of their own, they display their parent skill’s. What you pick on Cold Shock applies to both of its links.

Vacuum Blast (진공 폭발)

Section titled “Vacuum Blast (진공 폭발)”

No English name in the database. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to a target within 20 m and restores 100 MP. What it is for: the second hit of the left click. Same mana return as the first. It is not a decision, it is the rhythm. Chained from: Cold Shock, position 1.

Earth Tremor (대지 진동)

Section titled “Earth Tremor (대지 진동)”

No English name in the database. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals earth damage to a target within 20 m and restores 150 MP. What it is for: the third hit, the largest single mana return in the kit, and the link that both of Cold Shock’s late specialties key off. If you cut the chain at Vacuum Blast you lose 150 MP, the stacking +2% attack buff and the buff strip, all three at once. Chained from: Cold Shock, position 2.

Curse of Despair (절망의 저주)

Section titled “Curse of Despair (절망의 저주)”

No English name in the database. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 10 m in the entry, 20 m in the text · Target: single Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m and with a 75% chance applies fear and root for 5 s. Against NPCs both land 100% of the time. What it is for: the same overlooked thing the Templar has, in a different shape. It is not a loose 2-minute skill: it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a control effect, which means the moment somebody stuns you in PvP they hand you a 75% fear plus root. It is the best escape the class owns, and it is unavailable until somebody attacks you with control first. Data note: the entry says 10 m, its own text says 20 m. We leave the contradiction as it stands. Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Elemental Strike (정령 타격)

Section titled “Elemental Strike (정령 타격)”

Text: your attack increases by 1% and your spirit’s attack by 1%. At rank 20, 20% and 20%. What it is for: the first passive in every build note anybody has written for this class, Korean, Taiwanese or Global. It is the only line in the kit that scales you and the spirit with one number, which matters because both halves of your damage are large. The Gale stigma multiplies it by 1.5 while Gale is up.

Mental Focus (정신 집중)

Section titled “Mental Focus (정신 집중)”

Text: your mental-type accuracy increases by 11% and your Smite by 0.3%. At rank 20, 30% and 6%. What it is for: the co-first pick, named alongside Elemental Strike in every source. The scaling explains it: Smite is a double-damage proc and the priority damage stat at endgame, and 0.3% to 6% is a twentyfold increase while the accuracy only triples.

Elemental Cohesion (원소 결집)

Section titled “Elemental Cohesion (원소 결집)”

Text: each time one of your skill attacks lands, your and your spirit’s critical damage amplification increases by 1.1% for 10 s, stacking up to 5 times. Internal cooldown: 1 s per spirit. At rank 20, 3% per stack. What it is for: third in the passive queue (KR, 2026-07-13; 2026-09-07). Fully stacked at rank 20 that is 15% crit damage amplification on both you and the spirit, which is why the stat guides tell you to stop buying crit-damage amplification on manastones and buy unconditional amplification instead (KR, 2026-03-29).

Spirit Regeneration (정령 회생)

Section titled “Spirit Regeneration (정령 회생)”

Text: on a landed attack, 11% chance to cut the recast limit of your summon skills by 1 s. When a summoned spirit is at 50% health or below, it recovers 12% of its maximum health. Internal cooldowns: 1 s for the recast cut, 30 s for the spirit heal. At rank 20, 30% and 50%. What it is for: uptime. It is the only thing in the kit that shortens summon cooldowns, and the advice from two Korean guides is not to max it but to take it to roughly 20 to 22 so its proc window lines up with the Gale and Ancient Spirit cycle (KR, 2026-07-13; 2026-09-07).

Spirit Descent (정령 강림)

Section titled “Spirit Descent (정령 강림)”

Text: for 10 s after summoning a spirit, each of your landed attacks has a 50% chance to deal extra damage. When you summon the Ancient Spirit, Spirit Descent lasts 30 s. What it is for: the reason the burst window is the Ancient Spirit’s window and not anything else. Ten seconds of a coin flip on every hit is a filler passive; thirty seconds of it, lined up with the Ancient Spirit, is the burst. The Ancient Spirit stigma’s level-10 specialty multiplies both its proc chance and its damage by 1.5 for the first 10 s.

Contract of Revival (회생의 계약)

Section titled “Combos”

(a) The chains the database defines

Section titled “(a) The chains the database defines”

There are only two chain routes in the whole Spiritmaster kit, and they cover three skills. Everything else the guides call a “combo” is a condition, a summon window or a macro priority, not a chain.

Trigger Link 1 Link 2 Activation
Cold Shock (냉기 충격) Vacuum Blast (진공 폭발) Earth Tremor (대지 진동) 100%
Shock Release (충격 해제) Curse of Despair (절망의 저주) 100%, gated on Shock Release having fired
Flame Cremation (화염 전소) Call of Ash (재의 부름), only with the level-16 specialty not stated

Four readings that come out of this and not out of any guide:

  • The left-click chain is your mana economy, not a damage trick: 100 + 100 + 150 MP per full sequence. A summon costs 150. Cutting the chain short to press something else is the difference between affording the next summon and not.
  • Cold Shock’s two best specialties both key off Earth Tremor, the second link: the stacking +2% attack to 5 stacks at level 12, and the 10% buff strip at level 16. A build that takes them and then habitually cuts the chain at link 1 collects neither.
  • Flame Cremation has no chain of its own in the data. Its level-16 specialty adds one, described as the second link, and Call of Ash has no entry in the database at all. That is the same shape as the Templar’s Threatening Smash: a chain link that exists only as a specialty line.
  • Curse of Despair is the only chain that triggers off a defensive skill. Do not put it in a macro expecting it to fire on its own: it only exists in the window after you break a control effect.

One warning from the macro mechanic itself: only links with near-100% activation deserve a macro slot. A 15% link more than halved a Taiwanese Cleric’s dummy DPS (TW, 2026-03-18). All of Spiritmaster’s are 100%, so there is no trap here.

(b) The four-element loop

Section titled “(b) The four-element loop”

This is the mechanic that defines the class, and it is a resource loop rather than a window like the Templar’s.

What the database says. Every time a spirit uses its own skill, you gain one element. At 4 elements you enter the 4-element state and Elemental Fusion becomes pressable; using it consumes all four instantly. The spirits fire their skills on a chance per landed basic attack, with a 3 s recast between them:

Spirit Skill proc chance per hit What the proc also does
Fire 15% area damage, highest output of the four
Water 15% restores 20 MP to you
Earth 10% increases threat, 50% chance of a 2 s taunt on a player
Wind 10% heals you for 1.5% of max health
Ancient after 7 basic attacks, 5 with the level-15 specialty area damage, and a 4-element charge in one

The most widespread misunderstanding in the whole corpus lives here: the stacks come from spirit skill uses, not from Domination casts (KR, 2025-11-25). Domination is a symptom of the loop running, not its cause: it lights up for a moment every time a spirit fires, which is also when you gain the element.

Two ordering rules that are not optional:

  1. Buff before you summon. Spirits copy your stats at the instant of summoning, so Blessing of Flame and Gale go up first, always. Buffing after the summon is the most named mistake in the corpus and it locks in a weaker spirit for the spirit’s whole life (KR, 2026-07-09; 2026-07-13; Global, 2026-09-18).
  2. Water last, with a gap. Summoning the Water spirit immediately behind another summon silently drops its own skill, which costs you the damage, the mana and one element (KR, 2026-01-15; 2025-12-12; 2026-03-29).

The Ancient Spirit is the window. Thirty seconds of uptime, during which Spirit Descent runs for 30 s instead of 10, its level-10 specialty multiplies that passive’s proc and damage by 1.5 for the first 10, and every one of its skill uses hands you a full 4-element charge on its own. When it ends, damage drops by roughly 30%, with a 12 to 14 s recall wait once cooldown reduction and Spirit Regeneration are running (region unknown, 2026-09-18).

(c) PvE rotations and macro chains

Section titled “(c) PvE rotations and macro chains”

The opener, which every source agrees on (KR and Global, 2026-07 through 2026-09-18): Blessing of Flame → Gale → Summon: Ancient Spirit → then start attacking. A Global playtester phrases it “buff, buff, buff, release spirit”. The order is not stylistic, it is the stat snapshot from section (b).

The body of the cycle:

The execution rule of the current Korean build (KR, 2026-09-07): confirm Curse and Corrosion are both applied, then cast a full-charge Assault, while Elemental Fusion is cast uncharged. That guide calls it the single most important point of the whole build. It is also a reversal of the July 2026 build, which cast Assault uncharged (KR, 2026-07-09), so read it as patch-driven and not as a stable rule.

Three of four spirits, not four. Wind is the cut, because a fourth summon stretches the cycle and lowers damage (KR, 2026-07-13). At the Global level-37 cap the advice is narrower still: only the Water spirit is worth levelling yet, Earth is kept as a situational aggro summon for solo bosses, and Fire and Wind are left unlevelled to save points (Global, 2026-09-18).

The macro shape (KR, 2026-03-29; 2026-09-07; TW, 2026-09-05): three buttons, not one row. Button 1 is Flame Cremation alone, button 2 is Cold Shock, button 3 is Elemental Fusion plus Curse plus Domination. And a warning specific to this class: external mouse-software macros measurably outperform the in-game macro tool here, because the in-game tool cannot chain the self-buff into the triple summon into the spirit procs. Expect a lower parse if you use the in-game one (KR, 2026-09-07).

Tab-target rather than action mode for macro rotations, because auto-attack fills the gaps and restores mana between casts (TW, 2026-09-05; Global, 2026-09-21).

Macro delays: 10 ms below 50 ms of ping, 40 to 50 ms above 80 to 100 (TW, 2026-09-05). A visible “shadow” trailing your character during the chain is the tell that the delay is tuned correctly, the same cue every other class uses.

Point cost, and why the 16 to 20 gap is free. Levels 2 to 10 cost 1, 2 and 4 points per level in three bands, 21 points to reach level 10, and from 11 to 20 a skill costs no skill points at all: those levels come from the Daevanion board, soul-bind lines and Arcana cards instead. That is the arithmetic behind the most repeated piece of advice for this class, which is that skill level buys Specialty slots, not damage. The measurement everyone quotes: Domination deals 13,248 at specialty 16 against 13,392 at 20, a difference of 144 (KR, 2026-07-13). Pushing a skill past the threshold it needed is called “a totally waste” (KR, 2026-09-07).

(d) PvP: what the class has and what it does not

Section titled “(d) PvP: what the class has and what it does not”

Start from the shape of the problem. No gap closer, no on-demand slow, the lowest health pool in the game and a long time to kill. You do not open a fight, you survive the opener and win the trade. In an Abyss zerg that is a strength; in a duel it is why the class sits at the bottom of the 1v1 tables.

The control the class actually owns, all of it from the database rather than the tier lists:

Skill Effect Chance against players
Curse of Despair (절망의 저주) fear and root, 5 s 75%, but only off a CC break
Soul’s Scream (영혼의 절규) fear, 5 s, single target 70%
Spell Block (마법 차단) seal, 5 s, single target 75%, 100% at stigma level 20
Scream of Fear (공포의 절규) fear, 5 s, up to 4 targets 50%, 70% at level 20
Fear (강습 공포) fear, 5 s, up to 4 targets 30%, 50% at level 20
Domination (공간 지배) 40% slow 3 s, and a 3 s root at level 16 50% for the root
Joint Strike: Corrosion with the Wind spirit out area stun not stated

Two of those lines contradict the received wisdom that the class has no control: Corrosion’s joint attack is an area stun with Wind out and an area knockdown with Fire out, and Domination is a slow. What is true is that neither is a button you can press on demand, because both depend on which spirit is up and on the spirit having just acted.

Command: Proxy is the defensive opener, not a panic button. Five seconds, the spirit within 25 m, half your incoming damage going to the spirit at rank 1. Press it when you see the burst start, the same way the Templar presses Double Armor on the opponent’s CC break. With its level-5 specialty it becomes 10 s, which is what turns it from a reaction into a plan.

Shock Release into Curse of Despair is the escape. It is also the only escape. Every time an opponent opens on you with a stun, they are handing you a 75% fear plus root at the end of it.

The PvP stigma set trades the damage stigmas for utility: Kaisinel’s Authority, Spell Steal and Absorption in place of Corrosion and Assault (KR, 2026-07-09), with Command: Proxy as the fixed defensive slot (unknown region, 2026-09-18). Kaisinel’s +20% status accuracy is what raises every number in the table above.

PvP specialty picks (KR, 2026-03-29; 2026-02-10): Curse takes its 3rd and 4th nodes rather than the PvE picks; Soul’s Scream goes to 20 with the root, the cooldown cut and the +2 s fear; and Domination must reach at least 16, because below that its 50% root does not exist and the skill has no PvP value at all.

Gear the defences first (KR, 2026-03-29): health, resistance, Smite resistance, PvP defence and Will, because this class cannot block or evade and is heavily control-vulnerable. Stop buying damage tolerance near the 80% cap, since Gale alone supplies a large slice of it.

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Mass area fear during a mechanics fight. It repositions the boss and the adds and can break the encounter outright (KR, 2025-11-29). This is the class’s version of the universal rule and it is the one that gets people removed from parties.
  • The Ancient Spirit’s summon timing. It is a 30 s burst window on a 1 min 30 cooldown; a macro will spend it on trash.
  • The Curse and Corrosion confirmation before Assault. The whole point of the execution rule is that you check both debuffs are live first. A macro cannot check.
  • Curse twice in a cycle. Its 10 s cooldown does not fit the roughly 15 s summon cycle while moving, and a macro will try anyway (KR, 2025-11-25).
  • Elemental Fusion’s charge node while it is bugged. On the Korean live server in September 2026 it fired repeatedly instead of charging (KR, 2026-09-07). Unconfirmed on Global; if it is present, the node stays out of the build, not just out of the macro.
  • The Water spirit’s spacing. The one-second gap before the last summon is manual by definition; a macro fires it back-to-back and eats its skill.
  • Several skills crammed onto one keybind. Dropped inputs, double-applied debuffs, and you lose the ability to read individual cooldowns (KR, 2025-11-25).
  • Command: Proxy and any reactive defensive. Five seconds of mitigation is worth exactly what your timing is worth.
  • Passives, which simply cannot be added.

What is still unresolved

The English names, more than on any other class. The database ships no English name for any of the 38 skills, so every name on this page is a working translation from a cited guide or ours. The same Korean skill appears in guides as Flame Cremation, Combustion, Flame Immolation and Flame Consumption. Check the whole list during Advanced Access from 30 September.

Corrosion, and this is the one to check first. The live client says +10% damage taken from the spirit specifically, for 20 s. Nearly every guide says +20% damage taken from all Spiritmaster sources, for about 10 s (KR, 2026-07-09; 2026-07-13; 2026-03-29). Different magnitude, different scope, different duration, on the class’s mandatory debuff. Nobody has reconciled them.

Fear’s own bonus: defense or attack. The live tooltip says your defense rises by up to 20% in proportion to targets hit. The synthesis says +20% attack power on hit (KR, 2026-07-09). In the current data the attack bonus is the level-5 specialty, which would explain the divergence cleanly, but that reconciliation is ours and unverified.

Command: Proxy’s numbers. The database reads 5 s, 25 m leash, 50/50 at rank 1 and 31/31 at rank 20. The March 2026 patch notes said 30% for 10 s; September tooltip reads said 50% for 4 to 6 s. And the rank-20 line means 38% of the hit disappears entirely, which nobody has verified in game.

Cold Shock’s chain: sequence or choice. The database gives Vacuum Blast position 1 and Earth Tremor position 2, which reads as a three-hit sequence exactly like the Templar’s left click. One reading of the guides has them as two alternatives picked by specialty. We publish the data.

The Earth spirit’s taunt against players: 50% in the database, 30% read off a tooltip on camera (region unknown, 2026-09-20).

The Ancient Spirit’s re-trigger: specialty or enchant. The database puts the 7-to-5 basic-attack step on the level-15 specialty. A Global creator demonstrated it on camera as a function of the stigma’s enchant level, 7 at +0 to +10, 5 at +15, 3 at +25 (Global, 2026-09-20). Two knobs or one, nobody says.

The specialty ladder. The live client shows exactly three tiers, 8, 12 and 16, five picks in total, on every active skill. Two Korean guides describe a fourth tier at 16 and three more at 20 (KR, 2026-09-07), and the round’s cross-class consensus leaves the 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 ladder explicitly unresolved.

Curse’s crit-damage amplification. Guides build around roughly 20% crit damage amplification from Curse. The current Korean text lists only the damage over time and the 10% reduction to the damage the target deals to you.

Two entries contradict their own text: Curse of Despair says 10 m in the entry and 20 m in the text, and Fear says 30 m in the entry and 20 m in the text. Left as they stand.

The Korean bug backlog. Assault’s reset mistiming, Elemental Fusion’s charge node firing uncontrolled, and the elemental cast windup NCsoft acknowledged as real and unfixed on the 8 September stream (KR, 2026-09-07; 2026-09-08). None of the three has been checked on the Global build, and the Launch Scale Test could not check them because it capped at level 37 with the arena and battlegrounds locked.
