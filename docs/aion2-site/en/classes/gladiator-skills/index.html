Region: Korea · Version: Inven skill-simulator data pulled on 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: high on the numbers (this is the straight database dump), medium on the English names (working translations, not the official localisation).

Values are rank 1 of 20; rank is bought with skill points, and for stigmas with Stigma Shards. For what to do with this kit (greatsword rotation, lifesteal, which stigmas fit Global’s 4 slots and the buff clash with Templar), see Gladiator.

How to read this table

The English names come from our own syntheses and the glossary, not from an official localisation: Global may well call them something else until the 09-30 Advanced Access (early access) confirms the real names. Only 18 of these 41 skills have an English name at all (44%): the Gladiator syntheses quote fewer Korean skill names than the Templar and Cleric ones, and we would rather leave a gap than invent a translation. Descriptions are left in Korean for the same reason. The numbers are from the September 2026 Korean build and Global launches on Season 1, so expect balance differences; on top of that, Global equips only 4 stigma slots, so these 14 stigmas are the full catalogue, not a build.
