Assassin

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Global Season 1 equips only 4 stigmas and caps skills at 20, so the Korean 5-slot ordering below no longer fits; every Global figure here comes from a level-37 test build, not from launch. Skill names largely transliterated from auto-captions. Short guide.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 40 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Assassin (살성, Scout line with Ranger) is the melee burst DPS: dual daggers, back-attack damage, one short buff window that carries most of the fight.
  • Global Season 1 caps skills and stigmas at 20, not 25, and equips only 4 stigmas, not 5 (the first slot with the level-22 Ascension quest, then 27, 32 and 37; Global LST 2026-09-17/19). That deletes every Chapter-1 level-25 specialization from day one, including Triniel’s Dagger’s +10% damage-taken debuff and Dark Blade Throw’s +30% skill speed, and it costs you a stigma slot against every KR guide.
  • In PvE it is number one in raw DPS at the top end (110.4 against the baseline, P90 of 50,000 characters) (TW, 2026-08) and at the same time B in practice, because that ceiling collapses without perfect back-attack uptime. It is not number one early. In the Global playtest, with all eight classes at level 37 and gear equalized, an Assassin hitting the dummy from behind parsed 334-343k against Chanter’s 564k, Templar’s 432k and Gladiator’s ~370k (Global, 2026-09-17, one creator’s test): the stats its burst scales on do not exist yet at that level.
  • For a Templar or Cleric player, it is the class you will duel constantly. In 1v1 arena it wins 70.5% (TW, 2026-07), the highest figure in that data. September leaderboards put Gladiator ahead of it, though: ~33% of the top solo-PvP players are Gladiators against Assassin’s ~20% (2026-09). In the Abyss it is the worst by KDA (7.23, C) because it gets focused. Its counter is not out-damaging it but surviving the burst window, and its defensive stat is evasion, not block, which is what makes anti-Assassin accuracy worth building on Templar.
  • Do not roll it on bad ping: two independent sources put it with Sorcerer as the classes to avoid on high latency; one quantifies up to -40% DPS.
  • Its whole build is one stat: cooldown reduction. Every other class targets 33-36%; Assassin is the stated exception and stacks it with no ceiling, because Elusive Clone can never reach full uptime.
  • Zero group utility: no raid buff, no heal, no shield. A Templar or Cleric is invited on role; an Assassin on parse.
  • As an alt it levels fine on the Global build: a creator who took all eight classes to 37 lists Assassin among the easy ones, alongside Chanter, Templar and Cleric (Global), and its gap closer (Sling Dagger) resets on kill like every other class’s, so pack clearing keeps up. It is still a fragile leveller with no self-heal outside lifesteal, and a great max-level duellist. That is a levelling verdict only: at cap it is still bad at open-world pack farming.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Three stacking pillars: crit rate, crit damage and back-attack amplification (백어택); that is why its base attack looks unremarkable and its crits do not.
  • The core loop is one buff: Elusive Clone (환영분신) removes Heart Gore’s (심장찌르기) cooldown for its duration; the rotation becomes “spam Heart Gore until the buff ends”. Inside the window Heart Gore rises from ~21% to ~50% of fight damage.
  • It is a front-loaded class, and endgame fights are short. A TW Assassin reports doing far more damage than the rest of the group in roughly the first 15 seconds and then falling off; with power creep pushing top-end fights to 16-20 seconds, that is why Assassin tops short parses while the sustained classes (Sorcerer, Ranger, Spiritmaster) out-damage it in the newest, longest raid (TW, 2026-09).
  • Positioning is the class: passives and the largest multipliers only fire from behind, and attacking from behind bypasses a monster’s block entirely.
  • Auto-attack cancelling (평캔) is mandatory: hold the basic attack and weave skills into its animation; it raises both damage and sustain, and the gap is described as “enormous”.
  • Macro-driven but not macro-solved: movement, the stagger breaker, the dodge and the buffs stay outside. “It simplifies the buttons, not the decision-making.”
  • Squishy with lifesteal instead of defense: recovery depends on landing hits, which tempts bad positioning.
  • Second-hardest class (7.0/10: rotation 8, survival 8, ceiling 9). Bad at open-world pack farming.
  • Dodge timing on Global is not KR’s. The boss’s attack lands about 24 frames after its telegraph circle fills, against about 8 frames on KR, roughly three times the reaction window (Global, one creator’s frame count from his own footage). For a class that has to stay behind a boss through its casts, that is the most useful mechanical difference in the build.

Build

Section titled “Build”
  • Leveling: Quick Slash, Beast’s Roar and Sigil Explosion to rank 12 first (two specializations plus a bonus effect; ranks 13-14 add only 2-3%); the surplus into passives. Heart Gore specialized at 16 (crit resets its cooldown and restores mana): the single most valuable node. Enchant early skills for HP absorb. Defensive HP passive over the offensive one early. Poison Coating: zero points (it only keeps a target in combat state). On Global, skip crit damage while levelling: do not enchant Shadow Strike’s crit-damage passive and do not take the Vasial crit-damage Daevanion line, because crit rate is near zero without buffs or scrolls at that stage. Take Rear Smite (behind-target plus flat PvE damage) first, then Determination (Global).
  • PvE: cooldown reduction with no ceiling → combat speed → multi-hit (S for fast classes) → crit → back damage → weapon damage. Arcana Mirror card on Illusion permanently (every other class pivots to Wisdom; Assassin does not). Sustained rotation: hold the basic attack with a macro containing only Beast’s Roar, and hold Heart Gore and Sigil Explosion separately; do not stack multiple entries into one macro slot (visible cancel skips). Burst window (Elusive Clone + Swift Contract): hold the basic, mash Heart Gore and Sigil, and the instant Heart Gore enters cooldown swap to holding left and right click together. Out of the macro always: the 20 m gap closer, the 10 m positional swap, the stagger breaker, the Q dodge and the proc-only teleport. Enable the ground indicator marking the boss’s true rear. Points: Heart Gore first, Beast’s Roar to 20, Quick Slash to 20. Stigmas, Global (4 slots): Throwing Shadow Blade, Swift Contract, Illusion Clone and Throwing Dagger, the loadout actually played at the level-37 cap, on ~15 stigma points (Global). Stigmas, KR (5 slots): Triniel’s Dagger to 10, everything else to 5, then Swift Contract and Elusive Clone together 10 → 15, Elusive Clone to 20 before Swift Contract, Triniel’s to 20 last.
  • PvP: do not sink 15 skill levels into Stealth, a detection item counters it outright. But do use it: since March 2026 its cooldown refreshes on player kills, which is exactly the solo-Abyss loop (open from stealth, kill, vanish, re-engage) that puts Assassin in the top tier for picking off isolated targets (Global, 2026-09; TW, 2026-09). Soul-engrave evasion first then agility (omnidirectional and doubles as CC avoidance); it is all or nothing. Combo: stun → back-attack skill → basic → third skill → back-attack again → Sigil Explosion. Disengage loop: Esc to drop the target → run to a far undamaged target → one light hit → forced Heart Gore crit → lifesteal heals you while you reposition. Shift for the speed boost then immediately V to fold wings: caught mid-flight with wings open, Shift is locked and you die.

Mistakes

Section titled “Mistakes”
  • Copying a KR guide from July on. Investing 15 levels in Stealth. Over-investing in Sigil Explosion (lower share than its reputation, and it clears a stacked debuff). Points in Poison Coating. Several skills in one macro slot. Storm Rampage on every groggy (its job is filling the gap while Elusive Clone is down). Optimizing the gold weapon handed out at the end of leveling (a throwaway). Half-committed evasion. Drinking a high-tier potion mid-stack (wipes Sigil stacks; known bug as of July 2026). Expecting to carry open-world farming. Botching the gap closer: “a strong Assassin is horrible to play against, a weak one chases people and does nothing”. Carrying KR percentage targets onto Global: the Global build cuts damage boost per point to 1.5% (KR 2.5%), cuts Daevanion node values and soulbind damage lines by roughly a third to a half, drops pet-sourced Smite (the client calls it Double Chance) from ~24% to a 3% cap on one board slot, and ships without the PvE Daevanion board, which was absent from the test build though its point sinks still appear in shop and reward text, so it is unconfirmed for launch. Flat weapon and armour stats rose to compensate, so the 33-36% cooldown band, the ~3,000/~5,700 accuracy figures and every other KR percentage in this guide are on a scale that does not exist there (Global). Copying a KR macro after 2026-09-16: that patch added a cancel delay to Beast’s Roar and Heart Gore, killing the “main cancel” technique on KR, and it is not known whether Global’s build carries it (KR, 2026-09-16).
