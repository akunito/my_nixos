Gladiator: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, enchant cap 20, no Heroic gear).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients. Of the 41 skills, the database gives an English name to only 18.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. On Gladiator almost nothing does: of the 12 actives, only Shock Release moves a visible number (1 min to 41 s). What grows is the damage coefficient, which the database does not publish. The passives are the exception: there everything scales.
  3. Skill points only buy levels 1 to 10, and they cost 21 in total (1-1-1-2-2-2-4-4-4). From 11 to 20 the entry reads 0 points: on Global those levels come from the Daevanion board and Arcana cards, not from points (Global, LST 2026-09-17/19).
  4. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  5. Tell a chain apart from a condition. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears after the skill that triggers it. A condition is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met. Gladiator lives off its conditions: Overhead Slam and Aerial Snare need a knockdown, Sword Aura Rampage needs groggy, Ankle Slice needs a weapon block and Rush Strike needs an emergency dodge.
  6. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in.
  7. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  8. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on Gladiator that list is long: names, durations, and even whether the party buff is a party buff. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. The English names in the entries are the ones Inven serves (name_en) where it serves any, and in the 23 skills where it gives none the translation is ours or comes from a Global guide. The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean client text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 5 chains, 10 passives, 14 stigmas, 41 in total. All of them require a greatsword (대검) except Rupture Strike and Wrathful Strike, which list no weapon.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Keen Strike (예리한 일격)

Section titled “Keen Strike (예리한 일격)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Restores 100 MP. What it is for: this is your left click and your mana battery while levelling. It is also a stopgap: once Rending Blow hits level 16 and starts refunding mana on every crit, Keen Strike retires as filler (Global, 2026-09-21). It stays as half of the animation cancel, which is played by holding the basic attack down alongside the macro. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% MP recovery / 2% HP absorb / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Ruinous Blow’s cooldown. 16: adds the 4th chain link, Reckless Strike (무모한 일격). Data note: our baseline synthesis attributes that level-12 cooldown cut to Rending Blow. The database says 파멸의 맹타, which is Ruinous Blow. We keep the data, and it changes the reading: it means the left click accelerates your buff metronome, not your filler. Chains into: Rupture Strike (link 1), Wrathful Strike (link 2) and, with the level-16 specialty, Reckless Strike (link 4).

Rending Blow (절단의 맹타)

Section titled “Rending Blow (절단의 맹타)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: 0.5 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the shortest cooldown in the whole kit, 0.5 s, and therefore the real filler. Its level-16 specialty, 50 MP per crit, is what defines the entire build: it solves the mana economy of a class that spends 120 per press, and it retires Keen Strike. Every source, Korean, Taiwanese and Global, takes it to 20; one of them leaves it as one of only two mandatory skills (KR, 2026-09-05). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% MP cost / +200 block while casting / becomes a skill usable while moving. 12: up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit. 16: on a critical hit, restores 50 MP. Chains into: Breaking Blow (link 1).

Leaping Slam (도약 찍기)

Section titled “Leaping Slam (도약 찍기)”

Inven gives no English name. The Global client calls it Leap Slam.

Kind: active · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: you leap onto an enemy within 20 m and deal damage. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your opening gap closer and, with the reset-on-kill specialty, the skill that makes Gladiator the best solo levelling class in the game: you kill, it resets, you leap to the next pack. The overlooked level-8 option is the other one: it puts Prepare for Battle on you for 3 s without touching Ruinous Blow, which is the only way to carry that buff before you commit to going in. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 3 s of Prepare for Battle on yourself / 50% chance to activate Ankle Slice on hit / resets the cooldown on a kill. 12: 3 s of +20% PvE and +10% PvP damage tolerance. 16: becomes an area skill and pulls. Version warning: the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 made this skill stop firing while you are rooted or bound (KR, 2026-09-16). The same applies to Ruinous Blow and Ankle Slice. Chains into: nothing.

Devastating Sword (유린의 검)

Section titled “Devastating Sword (유린의 검)”

Inven gives no English name. A Global client guide calls it Devastating Sword (Global, 2026-09-20).

Kind: active · Cost: 200 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 4 m and knocks it down for 3 s. Against a control-immune target the animation changes. Groggy gauge damage: 15. What it is for: this is your knockdown on demand, which means it is the manual key to Overhead Slam and Aerial Snare. Against a boss it is no such thing: “the animation changes” is the polite way of saying there is no knockdown there, and that is exactly why the whole class hangs off the Rage Burst stigma. Most builds leave it at 12 (Global, 2026-09-20). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 4% HP absorb, 15% against control-immune targets / the downward strike becomes area damage / 2 extra attacks. 12: +1 s knockdown. 16: ignores block and evasion, lands as multi-hit. Chains into: nothing.

Overhead Slam (내려찍기)

Section titled “Overhead Slam (내려찍기)”

Kind: active, gated on a knockdown · Cost: none · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a knocked-down target within 4 m and increases threat. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 7% chance per hit. What it is for: this is the class. Everything else on the bar exists to manufacture windows for it. The level-16 specialty removes the cooldown and is mandatory: without it the skill lights up, you press it once and it goes on the full 5 s, which means it does not work (KR/TW, 2026-07/08). That 7% against bosses is what makes Rage Burst a near-unanimous stigma. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 2% HP absorb / becomes an area skill / adds the 2nd chain link, Upward Strike (올려치기). 12: guaranteed crit on hit. 16: removes the cooldown. Data note: the database names Upward Strike but gives it no entry of its own, and that phantom link happens to be the exact unit Korea measures the class ceiling in: 95 to 100 Upward Strike taps per rotation. Chains into: with the level-8 specialty, Upward Strike (link 2).

Sword Aura Rampage (검기 난무)

Section titled “Sword Aura Rampage (검기 난무)”

Kind: active, gated on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target in the groggy state within 4 m. What it is for: groggy-window damage dump, no cost and no cooldown. Its level-16 specialty is one of the best in the kit on paper (-1 s to all your cooldowns on hit), but it only fires if the target can be staggered at all: against bosses that cannot, for example floors 8 and 9 of Deis Research, it never triggers and the investment is wasted (TW/KR, 2026-08). If that is your content, stop at 12. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% HP absorb / restores 120 MP / usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Chains into: nothing.

Ankle Slice (발목 베기)

Section titled “Ankle Slice (발목 베기)”

Inven gives no English name. A Global client guide calls it Ankle Strike.

Kind: active, gated on a weapon block · Cost: none · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies around the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: on a successful block, deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m around a target within 4 m and with a 50% chance roots them for 3 s. Against NPCs the root lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: it is the payoff for blocking and, with the +16 m specialty, your second 20 m gap closer. Watch the date: that range specialty did absolutely nothing until the Korean patch of 2026-09-16, which fixed it (KR, 2026-09-16). Any earlier guide recommending it was recommending an empty box. The other level-8 route, the 30% knockdown, is one more Overhead Slam opener. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 30% chance of a 3 s knockdown / adds the 2nd chain link, Ankle Break (발목 격파) / +16 m range, i.e. 20 m. 12: ignores block and evasion, lands as multi-hit. 16: on a successful block, 50% chance to reset its own cooldown. Version warning: since 2026-09-16 it also stops firing while you are rooted or bound. Chains into: with the level-8 specialty, Ankle Break (link 2).

Crushing Wave (분쇄 파동)

Section titled “Crushing Wave (분쇄 파동)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: 250 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 4 m in the entry, 5 m in the text · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies around you · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m around you. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: your sustain AoE and, with the level-12 specialty, a farming machine: a crit resets the cooldown, so against a pack it feeds itself and heals you while it spins. It is also the most expensive skill in the kit at 250 MP, so without Rending Blow’s mana node you cannot hold it. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 2% HP absorb / 50 MP on a critical hit / up to +20% damage the more targets you hit. 12: on a critical hit, resets the cooldown. 16: 50% chance to pull. Chains into: Rampaging Wave (link 1).

Rush Strike (돌진 일격)

Section titled “Rush Strike (돌진 일격)”

Inven gives no English name. The Global client calls it Rush Strike; Korean guides call it Charging Strike.

Kind: active, gated on the emergency dodge · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 10 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: after using Emergency Dodge, you move to a target within 10 m, deal damage and with a 50% chance knock it down for 3 s. Against NPCs the knockdown lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: a Korean guide singles this out as the reason Gladiator performs well in Arena: dodging stops being dead time and becomes a reconnect with a knockdown, and it restores stamina even when you are dodging a boss pattern (KR, 2025-11 to 2026-01). Specced up it is 20 m and a 75% knockdown. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): restores 300 stamina on hit / +10 m range / becomes a charge skill with area damage. 12: -5 s cooldown. 16: +25% knockdown chance. Chains into: nothing.

Aerial Snare (공중 결박)

Section titled “Aerial Snare (공중 결박)”

Inven gives no English name. The Global client calls it Aerial Bind.

Kind: active, gated on a knockdown · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 45 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a knocked-down target within 4 m and with a 50% chance air-binds it for 3 s. Against NPCs it lands 100% of the time. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 5% chance. What it is for: in PvP, a high-level Taiwanese player says landing this proc consistently is the CC that decides fights (TW, 2026-09-05). In PvE it is the first skill to fall off the list: it needs a knockdown like Overhead Slam, carries a 45 s cooldown and procs at 5% against a boss. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): multi-hit on hit / +25% air-bind chance / +100% damage against control-immune targets. 12: adds the 2nd chain link, Forced Fall (강제 낙하). 16: -50% cooldown. Data note: that 5% comes only from the database; no video mentions it, and it is half of Overhead Slam’s 7%. Nobody has verified it on the Global client. Chains into: with the level-12 specialty, Forced Fall (link 2).

Ruinous Blow (파멸의 맹타)

Section titled “Ruinous Blow (파멸의 맹타)”

The Global client calls it Luminous Blow.

Kind: active, charge skill · Cost: 250 MP · Cast: 0.3 s · Cooldown: 30 s · Range: 7.5 m · Target: single · Properties: charge skill, maximum 1 stage Rank 20: entry identical. Text: you charge a target within 7.5 m dealing damage, and you enter Prepare for Battle for 5 s. At full charge it damages up to 4 enemies. Prepare for Battle (전투 준비): +20% PvE damage amplification, +10% PvP, +100 crit and +15% status-effect accuracy. Groggy gauge damage: 15. What it is for: the metronome of the class. If that buff is down you are playing badly, and that is the number-one execution mistake in every source. It is also your third mandatory level-20 skill across three sources from three regions. In PvP the advice flips: take ignore block and evasion instead of extending the buff, because the fights do not last long enough (TW, 2026-07/08). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% skill speed / +12.5 m range, i.e. 20 m / usable while moving. 12: +5 s Prepare for Battle. 16: ignores block and evasion, lands as multi-hit. Data note: everybody quotes Prepare for Battle as a 10 s base buff. The Korean database says 5 s, and puts the other 5 s on the level-12 specialty. A third Global reading says 20 s (Global, 2026-09-20), which almost certainly already counts that specialty. We publish the database figure. Version warning: since 2026-09-16 it does not fire while you are rooted or bound. Chains into: nothing.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Called Defiance in several English guides.

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Properties: non-targeted · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, air-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. It is the only active in the kit whose visible numbers change with rank. Text: removes your stun, knockdown, air bind, grab, freeze and fear states and puts you in Steadfast (강인함) for 5 s. Steadfast: immunity to status ailments. What it is for: your CC break, and the mandatory first step of Wrath Burst. One Global client guide takes it all the way to 16 purely for the post-stun tankiness specialty (Global, 2026-09-21). Rebind it off the space bar on day one: mashing space to escape a stun summons your wings instead (Global, 2026-09-18). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Steadfast, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance / on cast, restores 1000 stamina / on cast, restores 20% of max HP. 12: +2 s Steadfast. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Wrath Burst (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

The five chains are buttons that only exist after the skill that triggers them, and all five activate at 100%, so they always land. One database detail: chains have no specialties of their own, they display their parent skill’s, with one exception we flag under Rampaging Wave. There are five further links the database names inside specialties but never gives an entry to: Reckless Strike (무모한 일격), Upward Strike (올려치기), Ankle Break (발목 격파), Forced Fall (강제 낙하) and Destruction Charge (파괴 돌진).

Rupture Strike (파열의 일격)

Section titled “Rupture Strike (파열의 일격)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Restores 100 MP. What it is for: the second hit of the left click, same mana as the first. It is not a decision, it is the rhythm. Chained from: Keen Strike, position 1.

Wrathful Strike (분노의 일격)

Section titled “Wrathful Strike (분노의 일격)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Restores 120 MP. What it is for: the third hit of the left click and the one that returns the most mana. The full sequence is 320 MP, which is what a little over two Rending Blows cost: until you own the mana-on-crit node, cutting this chain short is cutting your own economy. Chained from: Keen Strike, position 2.

Breaking Blow (격파의 맹타)

Section titled “Breaking Blow (격파의 맹타)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the other half of the real filler. It shares Rending Blow’s specialties, so the level-16 mana-on-crit node pays you twice per cycle, not once. It is the link the Korean animation cancel revolves around. Chained from: Rending Blow, position 1.

Rampaging Wave (광폭 파동)

Section titled “Rampaging Wave (광폭 파동)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 250 MP · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m in the entry, 5 m in the text · Target: up to 4 enemies around you · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m around you. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: the second spin of the AoE. With Crushing Wave’s level-12 specialty, every crit hands you back the whole pair and the pack turns into a loop. Data note: it is the only chain in the kit whose specialty list does not match its parent’s: here the level-8 lifesteal option reads “2% on a critical hit” while Crushing Wave’s reads simply “2%”. It could be a real distinction or a database typo; nobody has tested it. Chained from: Crushing Wave, position 1.

Wrath Burst (분노 폭발)

Section titled “Wrath Burst (분노 폭발)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours. Do not confuse it with the Rage Burst stigma (격노 폭발): two different skills with near-identical Korean names.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies around you · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: deals damage to enemies within 4 m and knocks them down for 3 s. What it is for: this is the most ignored thing in the kit. It is not a standalone skill: it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a CC, and what it gives you is a 3 s area knockdown, which is a free Overhead Slam window. Translated into PvP: whoever stuns you is opening your own damage window. None of our syntheses records it as a chain; the database does (Shock Release’s link_skill points here). Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Experienced Counter Strike (노련한 반격)

Section titled “Experienced Counter Strike (노련한 반격)”

Text: on a successful block, for 10 s your PvE damage amplification increases by 6.5% and your PvP by 3.25%. At rank 20, 35% and 17.5%. What it is for: first in the passive queue in almost every source, Korean, Taiwanese and Global, and the classic argument for putting a Gladiator in a raid. It scales 5.4x with rank, the largest multiplier the class has. Data note: there is a clash here that matters. Every guide describes it as a party buff that does not stack with a Templar’s Fury, and one Global source confirms that from the client (Global, 2026-09-20); our own Templar page publishes it that way. The Korean database text of 2026-09-22 says “자신의”, meaning you only. Either the text is incomplete or the party effect disappeared in the August rework and nobody has re-measured it. This is the highest-priority check on this page.

Murderous Burst (살기 파열)

Section titled “Murderous Burst (살기 파열)”

Text: every attack that lands gives you a stack of Bloodlust (살기) lasting 3 s. (b) The Overhead Slam loop and who opens the door

Section titled “(b) The Overhead Slam loop and who opens the door”

This is the mechanic that defines the entire class.

What the database says: Overhead Slam only hits knocked-down targets. Against a control-immune target, meaning any boss, it activates with a 7% chance per hit. The level-16 specialty removes its cooldown, and without it the skill lights up, you press it and it goes on the full 5 s. The Rage Burst stigma activates it for 10 s on hit, with no knockdown needed.

Who manufactures a knockdown by hand, with the chance and the cooldown:

Opener Knockdown Cooldown Notes
Devastating Sword (유린의 검) 3 s, guaranteed 20 s against control-immune targets only the animation changes
Wrath Burst (분노 폭발) 3 s area, guaranteed 2 min, 1 min 22 s at rank 20 only after breaking a CC
Wave of Wrath (분노의 파동), stigma 50% area, 75% at level 20 1 min plus 50 groggy gauge damage
Rush Strike (돌진 일격) 50%, 75% at level 16 20 s only after the emergency dodge
Ankle Slice (발목 베기) 30% with the level-8 specialty 10 s only after a weapon block
Assault Strike (강습 일격), stigma 30% area, 55% at level 20 2 min
Whirling Blades (칼날 화무), stigma 30% with Blade Toss at level 20 20 s the only ranged one

And here is the problem: against a boss, which is control-immune, none of those knockdowns land. That is why Rage Burst is near-unanimous in the Global guides, and why the class plays differently against a pack (where you open windows by hand constantly) and against a boss (where you have the 7% and Rage Burst’s 10 s, and nothing else).

The second conditional, Aerial Snare, is worse: it needs a knockdown all the same, costs a 45 s cooldown and procs at 5% against a boss, not 7%. It is the first skill to fall out of any PvE build.

How the ceiling is measured. Korea does not measure this class in DPS but in taps: Upward Strike hits per rotation. The 2026-08-12 patch broke the cancel and the dummy dropped to 74; the 2026-08-26 patch restored it and it rose to 97; the stated target is 95 to 100 (KR, 2026-08/09). If you are counting under 74, you are on the wrong chain.

The animation cancel, which is the real subject of this class.   • The delays are yours, not the creator’s you are copying. Every Taiwanese and Korean macro in this corpus is tuned to 170-200 ms ping and the authors say so explicitly. At European ping the chain behaves differently, and a Taiwanese creator with 1000 hours says everything felt dramatically cleaner on Global (Global, 2026-09-19).

(d) PvP combos and openers

Section titled “(d) PvP combos and openers”

The standard opener (TW, 2026-01): Leaping Slam → Ankle Slice to root before committing the rotation. The weave comes after that.

The combo that decides the fight (TW, 2026-09-05): Overhead Slam prioritised into Rending Blow, cancelled by holding Keen Strike and Overhead Slam together, and on top of that landing the Aerial Snare proc, which is the CC that wins duels. Remember the real number: 50% against a knocked-down player, 5% against a control-immune target.

The PvP rule most people break (KR, 2026-09-21): in PvP only two skills are safe inside a macro, Rending Blow and Overhead Slam. Everything else is hand-fired against the opponent’s state, and there are two exact timings:

  1. Rage Burst is held. If you release it into the opponent’s invulnerability, it does not detonate. You wait for it to drop.
  2. CC lands in a narrow window: after the opponent’s shock immunity ends and before the follow-up status immunity comes up.

Your escape and your do-not-die button:

  • Shock Release cleanses six states and gives you 5 s of Steadfast, and it leaves Wrath Burst on your bar, which is a free area knockdown.
  • Tenacity is 3 s in which you take no damage and no status, and it goes before the opponent’s big cast, not as a reaction.
  • Underneath, running by themselves: Will to Survive after you eat a CC, and Blood Absorption, which heals you harder the worse you are doing.

Specialty and stigma swaps for PvP (KR/TW, 2026-07 to 2026-09):

  • Ruinous Blow: ignore block and evasion instead of extending Prepare for Battle. The fights do not last long enough for the buff to pay off.
  • Stigmas: in come Tenacity (3 s immunity), Force Restraint (75% seal for 5 s), a health-reduction stigma and Crushing Charge if you have it. A Korean guide from November 2025 also slots Armor of Balance at level 5 in place of the block stigma, because blocking as a Gladiator leaves you open to status ailments. Global has stigma presets, so switching between PvE and PvP no longer means resetting anything (KR, 2026-09-21).
  • Build around damage to knocked-down targets. Gladiator hits harder into someone on the ground than in any other situation, and the whole CC chain exists to put them there.
  • What the opponent knows about you: Ranger guides carry a shock-resistance scroll specifically for the Gladiator matchup and disengage rather than trade once you land an airborne gap closer (TW/KR, 2026). If they leave before you arrive, that is deliberate.

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Rage Burst. It is the one stigma with a timing rule of its own: fired into the opponent’s invulnerability, it is lost. Held by hand, always.
  • Ruinous Blow. It is a charge skill, and although the database says it has a single stage, holding the macro costs you the full-charge area hit. The weightier reason is different: it is your buff metronome and it is pressed when Prepare for Battle drops, not when the macro feels like it.
  • Position-dependent gap closers: Leaping Slam, Rush Strike, Crushing Charge and Assault Strike. In PvE there is a Korean dummy test that found no DPS loss from macroing dashes (KR, 2026-08), so this is mainly a PvP and mechanics rule: you will launch yourself into a telegraph while mashing the button.
  • Root and seal skills: Ankle Slice and Force Restraint. A CC’s entire value is the instant you choose it.
  • Stigmas in general. Competitive Korean players fire them by hand, and adding a stigma row to the macro measurably lowers your hit count.
  • Focused Block and the emergency dodge. The invulnerability frames are manual, always.
  • Passives, which simply cannot go in.
  • More than one row. If you need two, split one into the game macro and one into the mouse macro.

What is still unresolved

Rage Burst’s Korean name. Our baseline synthesis calls it 경로 폭발 (Path Burst) and the database says 격노 폭발. The effect is identical (it activates Overhead Slam for 10 s), so this is a transcription mismatch between sources, not two skills. We publish the database name and keep the other as an alias; nobody has reconciled where the first one comes from.

Rage Burst’s cooldown. 45 s according to two Global sources with the client in hand (Global, 2026-09-20), 1 min 30 s according to the Korean database of 2026-09-22. That is the difference between two windows per fight and one.

Prepare for Battle’s duration. 5 s in the database, 10 s in two Global guides, 20 s in a third. The most likely reading is that 10 includes the level-12 specialty and 20 is a misreading, but nobody has measured it.

Whether Experienced Counter Strike is a party buff at all. Every guide says yes and that it clashes with a Templar’s Fury; one Global source confirms it from the client. The Korean text of 2026-09-22 says “자신의”, you only. This is the highest-priority day-one check, because whether it makes sense to run a Templar and a Gladiator in the same party of five hangs off it.

Whether Zekiel’s Blessing is still a party buff. Same shape of conflict: the database says you and up to four party members within 40 m, one Global source says self buff.

Aerial Snare’s 5%. It comes only from the database dump, with no video backing it, and it is half of Overhead Slam’s 7%.

Whether blocking on Global is an input at all. Four Global sources contradict each other: an active block of about 1.5 s, “melee get a block button”, a parry stigma as the only way to block, and “there is no button, block is a passive stat”. Half of Gladiator’s kit (Ankle Slice, Experienced Counter Strike, Armor of Protection, Focused Block) depends on the answer.

Which direction macro priority runs. The Global Launch Scale Test showed, on Templar, that the macro fires top down; a Korean Gladiator guide describes the opposite, that the ability lower in the list has higher priority. Both cannot be true and nobody has put them side by side.

Which cancel chain works on the Global client. Three different answers within a single month of Korean patches, and the 2026-09-16 patch added cancel delays to Templar and Assassin and left Gladiator untouched. The Korean forum reading is that this is groundwork for reverting Gladiator’s perfect cancel too. The build volatility is not over.

The size of the stigma pool. This round’s consensus says 13 stigmas per class; the Gladiator dump has 14. Nobody has checked which figure holds for Global.

Three internal contradictions in the database itself, unresolved: Force Restraint describes a seal (봉인) while its specialties talk about silence (침묵); Crushing Wave, Rampaging Wave and Wave of Wrath list 4 m range in the entry and 5 m in the text; and Crushing Charge lists 4 m range for a charge the text puts at 10 m.
