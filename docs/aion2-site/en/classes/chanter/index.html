Chanter

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The September Spin Strike rework is confirmed live in the Global build, so Global ships Season 1 content on current KR skill balance.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 39 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Chanter is the second support: a melee staff hybrid that buffs, off-heals and deals real damage. Not a Cleric substitute in progression and not a DPS class either: about 83% of a pure DPS’s output at endgame (KR/TW, August 2026). Early on it is the other way round: in the Global playtest (Global LST 2026-09-17/19), one creator’s one-minute dummy test with all eight classes at level 37 on equalised gear put Chanter first of the eight at 564,000, ahead of Templar (432,000) and Gladiator (~370,000).
  • If you want a support that fights, this is it. If you want the group’s safety net, that is Cleric. Every comparison lands on that split.
  • Party demand is guaranteed and the gear bar is lower than a DPS: a Chanter at 2,800-2,900 combat power gets into Fire Temple hard where a DPS needs 3,100-3,200, a KR/TW community norm from August 2026 that will not transfer to Global’s numbers. The rule itself does transfer: in Global, parties take tanks and supports below the listed combat-power requirement, but you still need about 5% of the boss’s damage to open the loot queue. A good fit for a casual schedule without whaling.
  • The hardest class to play well at endgame in the one source that scores difficulty (7.75/10: group responsibility 9, mastery 9), and macro-heavy in KR practice. Global playtest sources see only the easy half: one rates its difficulty 3 of 5, mid-pack, and another lists Chanter among the classes that levelled easily. Both are true: a low floor for getting invited and for levelling, a high ceiling for contributing in a real raid.
  • PvP: strong 1v1 (67.9%, A, tied with Sorcerer), mediocre in the Abyss zerg (B, KDA 9.74) because you are melee in a mass fight. For legion PvP, a solid small-scale and arena pick, weaker than Cleric at mass scale.
  • If you main Cleric, Chanter is the best alt: KR alt guides rate it top-tier once Undefeated hits 15-20, and it farms and solos far faster than Cleric. A playtest benchmark timed a full 1-to-cap run at 4 h 16 m on Chanter against 5 h 29 m on Ranger, 73 minutes, credited to constant cleave plus built-in healing removing the downtime between pulls.
  • Warning: this class is a patch punching bag. Four heavy reworks in two months; one KR creator published two full rebuilds a week apart and complained about the cadence on camera. Any build you read today is a snapshot.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Priest line with Cleric; staff (법봉), melee range, positioned with the party or near the front line. Namu warns: a completely different class from Aion 1’s Chanter. The Inven guide author’s one-line identity: “시너지랑 버프”, synergy and buffs, neither pure healing nor pure damage.
  • Mantras are the core: always-on toggles with no upkeep cost. Undefeated Mantra (불패의 진원: party damage amp, resistance, crit, accuracy, crit damage, heavy strike) and Sprint Mantra (질주의 진원: move speed and heal on hit). Undefeated does not stack with the Cleric’s Light of Protection. Undefeated at max is the class’s top-priority investment, unanimous across every KR guide.
  • Healing is real but secondary: 3 heals plus a lifesteal mantra vs the Cleric’s 5; zone and passive regeneration rather than instant emergency heals. No resurrection.
  • Where it solo-heals (KR/TW, mid-2026): ~90% of the Conquest and Expedition tier; exceptions Fire Temple hard and Citadel hard; Transcendence “50/50”; Sanctuary requires both Chanter and Cleric.
  • Damage identity: a proc chain around the Dark Crush (암격세) state, fed by the basic-attack chain and trigger skills. Auto-attack cancelling is the mechanical expression, driven in KR with a held macro.
  • The September Spin Strike rework is in the Global build. Two creators verified on their own Chanters during the playtest that Spin Strike is already the 20 m ranged AoE and a fourth Dark Crush trigger, not the old 4 m melee skill. That settles a bigger question: Global ships Season 1 content on current KR skill balance, not on a pre-Chapter-1 skill set. Expect the rework’s trade, better in AoE and mass PvP, worse in 1v1 and on bosses, from day one.
  • Stigma works differently in Global. The same 13 Stigma skills exist, but Global unlocks one registerable slot every five levels, at 22, 27, 32 and 37, instead of KR/TW’s flat “6 of 13 usable”, and the system only opens after the level-22 Ascension quest. That bites Chanter harder than most classes: two of your slots are permanent mantra toggles, so the staggered unlock delays the flexible slots, not the fixed ones. Two more Global-only changes hit the same plan: Wisdom Stones for skill levels no longer come from the feather and Monolith path but from the 60+ Sealed Dungeons and regional side quests, and Stigma shards can no longer be crafted (Shugo Games, Nightmare and Abyss points only). Getting Undefeated to max, the class’s top-priority investment, now runs through Sealed Dungeons.
  • Utility that survives every patch: Gale’s Power (질풍의 권능, +5% weapon and magic damage for the party, +200 accuracy and 10-20% cooldown reduction that shortens teammates’ cooldowns, verified live); Barrier Spell (결계의 주문, a party HP floor at 20% for 3-5 s and a heal on expiry, since March 2026); Fracturing Blow (-30% defense 5 s); Wave Strike’s node (-20% incoming boss damage for the party, near-permanent uptime); a dash that resets on kill and chains mob to mob: why it is the better farmer of the two supports.
  • Standing (KR/TW, August-September): A or B everywhere, never S and never bottom; 8th of 9 in P90 DPS (83.3%); “the biggest winner” of the March overhaul. Late September pulls in two directions. PvE up: a TW creator says Chanter took large offensive buffs and now takes 10-11% of raid damage against a real DPS’s 12% and a Cleric’s 8-9%, and a TW PvE tier list puts Cleric and Chanter together as S-tier must-haves, taken everywhere even under-geared. Solo PvP down: a leaderboard-representation tier list puts Chanter in C, arguing the flexible toolkit is its burden: no one focused role, constant situational decisions, less overwhelming than a specialist. In mass PvP the picture improved, since the reworked Spin Strike lets it support while keeping ranged options.

Build

Section titled “Build”
  • Leveling and AoE farming (KR, November 2025 to January 2026, closest to Global): basic chain (격파세) → Spin Strike on cooldown → weave the post-block strike; the heal (쾌유의 주문) to 12 first for its continuous-healing window. Single target: stun → knockdown → Dark Crush. Key specs: the 격파세 node that cuts 1 s off Spin Strike per hit; Charge Strike’s 50% chance to proc Heat Wave; Impact Strike converted to AoE plus its stun follow-up, which lets Impact and Dark Crush be cast twice in a row. Passives first: Attack Readiness (공격 준비), Blessing of Life (생명의 축복), Impact Hit (충격 적중), the crit spell (고치의 주문). Skip Cross Guard and the ~200-block passive. Stigmas: Undefeated and Sprint as permanent toggles; two damage slots solo, swapped to the AoE heal and Gale’s Power in a party. Mana is the early wall: MP specs over damage specs.
  • PvE (KR, mid-2026, the most detailed build): at 20 Break Strike, Dark Crush, Recuperation, Spin Strike; at 16 Rear Strike, Gale’s Charge, Wave Strike; at 12 Charge Strike, Impact Strike, Vibration Strike, Shock Release. Fixed stigmas Undefeated + Sprint + Gale; the 4th rotates among Block, Marcutan, Barrier Spell and Blocking Power by role. Gale’s Charge to 16 is the single most valuable level-16 pick in the class: it shortens Gale’s Power’s cooldown, which shortens every party member’s cooldowns; worth a personal DPS loss. Raid opener: cast Gale’s Power before entering the boss’s aggro range so its 20% reduction front-loads onto teammates’ openers; refresh Spin Strike and Marcutan at the 16-17 s mark of the 20 s window. Boss discipline: block or just-dodge only attacks that would hard-CC you; otherwise Shock Release and keep attacking. Content swaps: the cleanse on Recuperation for burn bosses; the stagger stigma mandatory on bosses whose one-shot needs staggering (Draupnir); the dodge stigma for Shattered Island’s second boss. KR, September 2026: Marcutan has dropped off the mandatory list, losing it costs a little proc uptime and damage, and the freed slot buys mechanic-skip utility in Sanctuary-tier content, which Global’s Season 1 does not have yet, so keep it in the rotating fourth slot at launch. The bug where a Break Strike spec broke Spin Strike’s cooldown is fixed, as is a second one where attack-speed gains made a Marcutan-chain skill get skipped; a new one replaced them, an attack-speed-piercing effect that stopped breaking the cap as of KR patch 122.6. Blessing of Protection was cut from a 5-minute duration and recast to 2 minutes in the 2026-09-16 KR patch, the only Chanter line in it. And the macro rule inverted: put fewer skills in a macro, because every extra slot reduces how often the others fire.

Mistakes

Section titled “Mistakes”
  • Expecting a Cleric: it cannot resurrect or rescue a collapsing raid. Undefeated next to Light of Protection. Splitting stigma investment (Undefeated to max first; the other three at 1 is a legitimate KR build). Cross Guard, the block passive or Binding Brand in PvE (worthless per six guides). Gale’s Charge in PvP: never. Blocking and dodging on reflex: the two biggest avoidable DPS losses after boss downtime.
