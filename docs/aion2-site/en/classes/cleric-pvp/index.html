Cleric in PvP

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. No dedicated Cleric PvP build video exists in the corpus: the build is reconstructed from the swap sections of PvE guides and two gank VODs. Class balance on Global is stated to match Korea and Taiwan exactly at launch, so the kit below carries over; the numbers around it (stigma slots, gear tiers, Daevanion percentages) do not.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 39 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Cleric is a group-PvP class, not a duelist. Every dataset and every opinion agrees: top-tier in co-op arena and mass Abyss fights, mid-tier in 1v1, and near-useless at killing anyone solo. If your PvP is “with the clan”, it fits. If it is “I want to win 1v1s and gank”, it does not.
  • You will almost never die, and almost never kill. The kit: 3 s of full immunity (Salvation), a CC break with 5 s of status immunity (Shock Release), party shields, cleanse and the game’s only resurrection. In exchange, about 150 damage per hit against a Templar draining 3,000 HP per slash.
  • The real complaint to know: healing earned no Abyss Points at the KR launch. Supporting your group correctly paid nothing; points came from kills. KR later retuned the criteria and there are six non-PvP point routes, but a Cleric’s siege medal income is still stated to be poor because contribution scoring favours damage.
  • Group responsibility the highest in the game, alongside Chanter: 9/10 on the one structured rating we have, mastery 8/10, rotation only 4/10. The complexity is in reading the fight, not in managing bags.
  • The damage rotation can be macroed. After the 2026-08-26 patch the in-game macro matches a hardware one. Standard practice: damage and buffs on the held macro; heals and Salvation manual.
  • None of this was testable in the September Launch Scale Test, and that is now confirmed. Cap 37, Arena and Battleground visible in the menu but locked, progress wiped at the end of Day 2: duelling was the only PvP within reach. What the test did settle is bigger than any of it: class balance on Global is stated to match Korea and Taiwan exactly at launch, so the kit and the swaps below are the real build. The first live check is Advanced Access (early access) from 09-30.
  • Global ships one Abyss map (Chaotic Lower Rashanta, level 45, item level 1,000) with 7 h/week of Abyss time for F2P. Abyss Points convert to stigma points, so PvP feeds your power even if PvP is not the reason you are here.
  • Two things will shape your first month more than build choice: Abyss access (Stigma Shards are the bottleneck: 75 to take one stigma to 20) and whether your server’s faction wins artifact sieges, which snowballs point income.
  • If you want a PvP main that also gets invited to everything, Cleric is the safest pick on the board: with Templar, the two “guaranteed party demand” classes at launch.

Two fresh lists from September 2026 revise the July table above and agree on the shape: Gladiator alone at S in 1v1, Cleric in the A/B band with Chanter and Templar, all three there for tankiness rather than kills.

What changes from PvE to PvP

Section titled “What changes from PvE to PvP”
  • Every groggy-gated skill becomes dead weight: players have no groggy gauge. Lightning Barrage (groggy only), Burst of Authority (groggy 50) and Earth’s Punishment’s groggy contribution are PvE-only. Drop them and reclaim the points.
  • The replacements are the CC and anti-heal package: Bind (75%, 10 s root, 4 targets, 1 min), Voice of Ruin (20 s DoT cutting the target’s received healing and potions by 60%), plus the PvP-only passives Recovery Block and Will to Survive.
  • Absolution flips value: KR rankers dropped it in PvE (72% → 46%) but in PvP it is mandatory as an instant heal plus 2-debuff cleanse on a 1:30 cooldown.
  • Specializations re-swap freely and several have a PvP branch: Judgment Lightning → -10% enemy combat speed; Mark of Weakening → stun; Chain of Pain → root; Holy Aura → -40% move speed; Condemnation at 16 → 20% root; Radiance of Recovery → +20% received healing.
  • Chain of Pain is worth more in PvP than PvE: its debuff is -5% PvE tolerance but -7.5% PvP.

What changed by patch

Section titled “What changed by patch”
  • Healing was cut 50% in the Abyss and arena (2026-03-25), framed as a buff to healers: longer fights make support relatively more valuable. The same patch separated PvP and PvE coefficients and lowered PvP damage generally; in July came another -30%. Net for Global: fights long enough for a support to matter.
  • Immunity chaining is dead. Since March, two immunity effects cannot be applied within 30 s; Salvation went from 5 s to 3 s in the same pass. Anything written before March 2026 about “Salvation then X” is void.
  • Reincarnating Touch (환생의 손길), the new March stigma: 5 min active, +10% defense, +5% tolerance, +10% healing, and revives you at 100% if you die while it is up; 8 min 25 cooldown. Two sources describe the staged version (level 5/10/15/20) as reviving an ally, not the caster. Unresolved, and unknown whether it ships on Global.
  • Stigma cap 20 or 25. KR raised it to 25 with Chapter 1; Global is inferred earlier. The level-25 PvP bonuses (Salvation +10% instant HP, Prayer of Amplification with personal PvP damage amp, Yustiel with a 20% max-HP shield) will not exist at launch.
  • The 2026-08-26 patch added a minimum cancel time: a KR Cleric’s live A/B test showed the in-game macro matching an external device. The same patch lowered the Cleric party damage buff slightly.

PvP gear tiers: Decanus → Centurion → Tribunus → High Commander, bought with Abyss Points and silver, gold and platinum medals; not purchasable with real money. At Global launch only the first two exist: Decanus/Archon (no medal) and Centurion (medal-gated), with only silver medals seen anywhere in the test build, so plan for a two-tier ladder in Season 1 and treat the four-tier version as the Korea/Taiwan picture. Every PvP set carries +40% damage to Abyss monsters and -20% everywhere else, which is why hybrid loadouts are a real decision. Set bonuses stack across different tiers (5 Centurion + 8 Tribunus beats a full Tribunus set), but that trick needs a third tier to exist, so it is Korea/Taiwan advice for now. Cleric stat targets: combat speed 60-70% (base ~42%, the slowest start in the game) and accuracy ~2,500 combined. Healer PvP priorities: max HP, healing boost, cast and move speed, and status resist, the stat that decides whether a CC chain lands.

The structural problem: siege medals

Artifact sieges pay medals by contribution and only to the winning faction, and contribution scoring favours damage. A Cleric main says it plainly: gold medals are scarce for him “specifically due to weak siege performance”, and as a workaround he buys the expensive Centurion pieces first to reach the next set-bonus threshold sooner. This and the launch complaint about points for healing are what most separate Cleric from Templar for a clan-PvP goal. The same logic runs one layer down: Abyss Points scale with your individual contribution to a kill, so a kill you take alone pays far more than one shared among dozens, and the mass fights where a Cleric is strongest are the worst place to farm points.

Builds

Section titled “Builds”

Note

Reconstructions from KR/TW sources. Stigma and skill level 20 (16 without Arcana cards). Global Season 1 equips four stigmas (Korea runs five, Taiwan six), so the five-slot bar below is one slot too long: drop Summon Revive outside sieges and run Salvation, Light of Protection, Voice of Ruin and Absolution. Four is what every hands-on report of the test build shows, though no official note states a count.

Abyss and mass PvP (the primary use)

Section titled “Abyss and mass PvP (the primary use)”

Stigmas: Salvation · Light of Protection to 15 minimum (swap for Yustiel’s Power with a Chanter: the damage halves do not stack, the resistance halves do; the KR “호치” combo) · Voice of Ruin to 15 for the -60% anti-heal, best-in-slot for mass PvP · Absolution · Summon Revive.

Dropped: Burst of Authority, Earth’s Punishment (its groggy is worthless vs players), Lightning Barrage. Passives to add: Recovery Block and Will to Survive, both PvP-only and skipped in every PvE build. Specializations to swap in: Judgment Lightning → -10% combat speed; Mark of Weakening → stun; Chain of Pain → root; Holy Aura → -40% move speed; Radiance of Recovery → +20% received healing.

Positioning: your entire advantage over Chanter is being ranged at 20 m in a fight with dozens of players; Chanter has to be in melee.

Solo PvP and ganking (“meat shield”)

Section titled “Solo PvP and ganking (“meat shield”)”

Stats: status resist, regeneration, survival passives, Constitution for HP, and accuracy pushed via soulbinds and Genus rolls. Skills seen in play: Shock Release (held for the dangerous chain), Holy Aura as damage, Salvation, Voice of Ruin, a root. Evasion is not recommended unless fully committed: a half-evasion build loses to everyone, and Ranger and Assassin have passives that make it work where Cleric does not. Honest framing from the same creator: a Cleric cannot kill a competent Templar, Gladiator or Assassin one-on-one; the build is “survive and annoy”.

1v1 arena (non-equalised)

Section titled “1v1 arena (non-equalised)”

No source gives a dedicated build. Reconstructed shape: maximum status resist plus Will to Survive (your losses come from CC chains, not damage), Voice of Ruin to beat the opponent’s potions and lifesteal, Bind to break a melee’s momentum, and Salvation held for their burst window. Unverified.

Battlefield (10v10 equalised)

Section titled “Battlefield (10v10 equalised)”

Gear is levelled: the one mode where the kit alone decides and your Abyss gear investment does not count. No Cleric-specific battleground guidance exists in any source.

PvP gear plan for Season 1

Section titled “PvP gear plan for Season 1”
  1. Out-of-season points first (Corridor, battleground, arenas, supply requests, Shugo Festa) before you have gear to farm kills.
  2. Full Decanus set minus weapon (~715k AP), then weapon → guard → accessories to +11 everywhere before pushing anything to +17.
  3. Mix tiers deliberately rather than completing one set.
  4. Crafted weapon over the PvP-shop weapon (better scaling, a fifth soulbind line).
  5. A hybrid loadout is legitimate: 8 PvP pieces in defensive slots plus one flexible PvE slot clears normal PvE, because past ~100% stacked damage boost a 15-40% single-source difference is only 5-10% real.
  6. Consumables: cheap drinks for the action power behind the Shift dodge, and shock resistance and mitigation scrolls hotkeyed; resistance before Gladiator, Assassin and Templar burst.
  7. Do not copy a Korean or Taiwanese Daevanion board. Global cut the node values: the PvP board’s damage bonus is 1.5% against 2.5% on Korea and Taiwan, crit 10 to 5, flat attack +5 to +3, defence 50 to 30, combat speed 2.5 to 1.5, cooldown reduction 2.5% to 1.5%, damage boost 5% to 1.5%, and the point cap is 45 against Taiwan’s 50. The cut is not uniform and base item stats were raised to compensate, but percentage stats are down, so any Korean percentage on this page may read lower on your character even with identical class balance. One Global report also has each point of divinity stat converting to about half the attack it does on Taiwan; that is a single source, so treat it as a flag, not a number.

Macros and settings

Section titled “Macros and settings”
  • Damage and set-and-forget buffs (Light of Protection, Prayer of Amplification, Voice of Ruin, Mark of Weakening) on the held macro; all heals and Salvation manual.
  • Never a charge skill (Thunderclap) in a macro.
  • Only steps at ~100% activation: a dummy test showed 2,826 vs 1,348 DPS between including and excluding a 15% step.
  • Start the delay at 50 ms and raise it for ping.
  • Turn off “attack-time main target lock” and rank hostile players above monsters in Tab priority.
  • 20-skill capacity; holding the macro does not block manual casts, it pauses.
  • Tune the delay to your ping, not to a number from a guide: ~50-60 ms at 200 ms to Taiwan, ~8-10 ms on a nearby server.
  • The damage macro that actually works: Earth’s Punishment (10 s DoT) → Condemnation (auto-crits under the DoT and resets its own cooldown on each crit) → Voice of Ruin → Mark of Weakening, held through the DoT window.
  • Play Cleric in tab-target (“Aion 1”) mode: the base attack’s level-12 passive cuts the cooldown of your heaviest skill, so weaving the auto-attack is real damage.
  • Settings are your anti-zerg kit. Global’s test build did not render distant players while flying and struggled to hold 60 FPS, which gets Clerics killed by attackers they never saw. Set Player Particle Effect Display to self and party, turn on Identical Player Appearance, lower Mass Combat Quality first when the Abyss stutters, keep max view distance far (threats arrive from above), set teleport-effect colour to dark, and export the UI preset once so you never redo the pass.

How it plays

Section titled “How it plays”

Cleric PvP is an attrition class in a game that spent 2026 making PvP more attritional. The moment-to-moment texture is visible in the two solo VODs in the corpus: both are largely about being crowd-controlled. Paralysis is “the one thing I can’t do anything against”; 7 s fears are received; the player repeatedly declines to spend Shock Release on the first CC because he is saving it for the chain that actually kills him. The paralysis is a godstone, not a class skill: two per weapon, roughly a 2-3% proc on a ~5 s internal cooldown, and it fires far more often for the fastest-attacking classes, which is to say for the Gladiators and Assassins already beating you.

In a group the picture inverts, and that inversion is the whole argument for playing it. Co-op arena is where Cleric ranks S, and the stated reason is as much matchmaking as power: teams without a Cleric simply lose more. In mass Abyss fights, being ranged at 20 m rather than melee is the concrete difference from Chanter. And it is a macro class: your damage runs itself off a held key while your hands are free for heals, Shift dodges and Salvation.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Bringing your PvE bar into the Abyss: Lightning Barrage and Burst of Authority do literally nothing.
  • Dropping Absolution because a PvE ranker guide said so.
  • Firing Shock Release on the first CC.
  • Assuming you can chain Salvation with another immunity.
  • Running Light of Protection next to a Chanter.
  • Queueing as the second Cleric. Duplicate support buffs do not stack and neither does the revive: a party takes exactly one Cleric, and the optimised five is Templar, Gladiator, Chanter, Cleric and one ranged DPS.
  • Standing in the middle of your own group in a mass fight. Hold the edges: stacked in the centre you get body-blocked and shoved out of position by allies’ dodge rolls, and Light of Regeneration is range-gated (an ally’s name greys out when they leave it).
  • Half-committing to evasion.
  • Macroing a charge skill.
  • Toggling your PvP flag in a Rift “just to look”: once set it cannot be turned off for ~1 h, and enemy guards force it on.
  • Dissolving Abyss gear for the 80% refund.
  • Skipping the Abyss because you dislike PvP: points convert into stigma power in PvE and PvP.
  • Building a pure healer.

Cleric HP vs other classes (one source gives 53k vs ~80k Assassin, contradicting “highest defensive stats”). The 09-08/09 Cleric pass is still uncovered, though the 09-16 Korean patch is now known: Radiance of Recovery’s second specialization went from +20% skill speed to +1 consecutive cast, and the Abyss 12-piece set bonus was raised on every tier (top tier 55%). New question: Abyss access is 7 h/week on the Global claim but 14 h/week on Taiwan live, so verify it in the first week.
