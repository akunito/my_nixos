Spiritmaster

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The loudest playtest complaint, that Spiritmaster was nerfed for Global, turned out to be an enchant threshold on the Ancient Spirit.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Spiritmaster (정령성, orbs) is the ranged DoT summoner of the caster pair with Sorcerer. It matters mostly as a class a group-mate might roll and one you will fight in the Abyss.
  • It ships to Global with a known bug list, but not the one the playtest complained about. A KR creator’s September guide ends: “I do not recommend playing Spirit Master right now, it has a lot of problems, bugs and errors”. The loudest Global report, that Spiritmaster had been nerfed for Global, was demonstrated on camera to be an enchant threshold instead: below +15 on the Ancient Spirit stigma, its Elemental Fusion re-trigger can fail to fire at all (the full scale is under How it works). NCsoft states class balance stays identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, applied to all three at once.
  • The most contradictory class in the whole corpus. The same weeks a 50,000-character parse puts it 3rd of 9 in PvE DPS (+6.6%), a leaderboard census puts it last, D-tier, 34 appearances of 800. Both August, both KR/TW. Read it as “good numbers on a dummy, contested numbers on moving bosses”: September parse commentary puts it above Assassin in the last two Korean raids because those fights reward sustained damage, and a Taiwanese tier list says the ranged classes keep attacking through boss mechanics and teleports with almost no downtime, while a leaderboard census still shows it appearing about a third as often as Sorcerer. Low representation, not proven low output.
  • The Abyss is its one undisputed S-tier: KDA 13.66, best of the 8 after the 30% nerf. In 1v1 it is bottom (64.6%, C): no CC, no gap closer, no slow, pure stand-and-trade. If your clan wants a mass-PvP zerg caster, this is it; if it wants duellists, no.
  • Lowest HP pool in the game, offset by Command: Proxy (대역, March 2026). Three September sources, one reading the tooltip in game, now put the redirect at 50% of your incoming damage for about 4-6 seconds, and it only works with the spirit within ~25 m. The 30% for 10 s in the March patch notes is the old value: this is burst mitigation, not a standing buff.
  • Difficulty ~5.5/10 (4th easiest), but three Korean creators call the rotation “a self-buff cycle, not a 1-2-3-4”, and one says outright: do not play it if you dislike cycle rotations.
  • “Best solo class” is not only aggregator marketing: NCsoft’s own September team update describes Spiritmaster as “built around solo-oriented play”, and four Global playtest creators repeat it (Global LST 2026-09-17/19). A September analyst still debunks the performance half: all solo content is trivial for every class and others beat it in Nightmare. What is real is the mechanism, the Earth spirit taunts (30% against players, +10% threat per hit against NPCs), and a playtester used it to tank a boss he could not otherwise solo.
  • Less risk than Sorcerer for the caster fantasy: tankier, more ping-forgiving, and needs less gear because damage comes from the spirits. The least played (KR population at 700+ power: Ranger ~5,000, Sorcerer ~3,200, Spiritmaster ~1,200).

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • Four elemental spirits and one Ancient Spirit (고대 정령); one active at a time (the Ancient can coexist with one elemental). Fire = aggressive and AoE with stagger; Water = mana and strong single target; Earth = threat and taunt (the solo survival tool); Wind = HP drain.
  • Spirits inherit your stats at the moment of summoning. So the self-buffs (Blessing of Flame 불길의 축복, Gale 정령의 가호) go before summoning, in PvE and PvP: the most repeated mechanical rule in the corpus.
  • Elemental Fusion (원소 융합) is the payoff skill, mandatory at 20 in every build; it unlocks after 4 summons (not 4 Domination casts, a widespread misunderstanding).
  • The Ancient Spirit’s Elemental Fusion re-trigger scales with the stigma’s enchant level, not with your skill level: 7 basic attacks at +0/+5/+10, 5 at +15, 3 at +25. Below +15 it can fail to fire at all. The Global playtest capped that enchant at +20 against Korea and Taiwan’s +25, so plan the opening weeks around a 5-attack cycle, not the 3-attack one the Korean guides assume.
  • Corrosion (부식) is the mandatory debuff stigma: +20% damage taken by the target from Spiritmaster sources, ~10 s. The often-quoted ~60% spirit / ~40% caster split is one creator’s July estimate; the only measurement anyone has posted, a September dungeon meter, ran the other way: 67k from the player against 44-45k from the spirit. Either way both halves are large, and the debuff applies to both.
  • It plays as an auto-attack-cancel weaver: hold left click (Cold Shock 냉기 충격), tap Flame Cremation (화염 전소) to cancel the recovery, weave skills into the gaps. Flame Cremation at 20 unlocks Reburn (재부름), “practically the main skill of the kit”.
  • Skill level buys specialty slots (4th at 16, three more at 20), not damage: Space Dominance deals 13,248 at spec 16 vs 13,392 at 20.
  • No real CC, no gap closer, no slow in PvP; so defensive investment is unusually efficient. Its one real PvP tool is the AoE fear (Soul’s Cry).
  • Launched weak and has been buffed incrementally since: agreed by four independent creators.

Build

Section titled “Build”
  • Leveling and solo: Earth Spirit for its taunt, the real solo tool (swapping to it cleared a solo boss that had killed the player on a damage spirit). Wind early for lifesteal, then a damage spirit as gear improves.
  • PvE endgame (KR, September 2026; the two bugs are Global-uncertain): Flame Cremation 20 (specs 2, 4, 5; unlocks Reburn); Fire Spirit 20 (2, 3, 5); Elemental Fusion 20 (2, 3, 5, cast uncharged); Cold Shock 16 (2, 3, 4); Water 16 (2, 5); Earth 16 (3, 5); Curse 16 or 20 (2, 3, 4); Domination 16 (2, 3). Passives: Elemental Strike (정령 타격) and Mental Focus (정신 집중) top → Elemental Cohesion → Spirit Regeneration (~20-22, to line up with Gale and the Ancient Spirit). Stigmas rotating in 5-6 slots: Blessing of Flame, Gale, Ancient Spirit, Corrosion, Assault, Absorption. Core rule: confirm Curse and Corrosion are both applied, then cast a full-charge Assault. Bug workarounds (KR): Assault to 19, not 20 for predictable reset timing; do not take Elemental Fusion’s 4th spec (it fires repeatedly instead of charging).
  • July 2026 PvE alternative (pre-bug, closer to Global): at 20 Cold Shock, Flame Cremation, Curse, Elemental Fusion, Shock Release; spirits at 16 with specs 2 and 3 (a 5th spec at 20 tested as near-worthless); Fire > Water > Earth, drop Wind. Rotation: Blessing of Flame → Gale → summon the Ancient Spirit → attack continuously → Corrosion → Assault to reset Curse and Corrosion, or fear before the Ancient summon so it inherits the +20% attack (parses slightly higher; the other is safer).
  • PvP: Curse specs 3 and 4; Soul’s Scream to 20 with 1, 4, 5; Domination to at least 16 for its 50% 3 s root (below 16 it has no PvP value). Gear defensively first: HP, resistance, Smite resistance, PvP defense, Will (the class cannot dodge or parry and is heavily CC-vulnerable); stop investing in damage resistance near the 80% cap (Gale already gives 45%) and move to Steel Wall. Manastones: PvP damage amp if obtainable, then HP. Needs a separate Arcana set and rolls; one KR creator’s split is 27:3 PvE:PvP and he advises casuals to build PvE only. PvP utility: Retreat, Spell Steal, Spell Block, Kaisinel’s Authority.
  • Stats and gear: the orb has the highest max-attack spread of any weapon, so Max Attack beats flat attack; the class naturally reaches the accuracy and crit caps (the Ancient gives +100 accuracy, Blessing of Flame +100/+100): spend the freed budget on Smite and Perfect. Manastones: unconditional damage amp (무피증) over crit damage or general damage amp, because it already carries ~10% crit amp (Blessing of Flame), 20% (Assault’s Curse) and ~20% damage amp (Corrosion) innately. Avoid frontal damage amp: your summons wander and boss damage comes from behind. Multi-hit is the wrong stat. Arcana: Grail, Scale and Bell for +4 Elemental Strike each; Parchment and Compass for the core actives; Scale may prefer Justice (Perfect) over Destruction. Pantheon: might and accuracy mandatory, then Destruction, Death, Wisdom, Freedom, Illusion, Time.
  • Macros: three buttons (Flame Cremation alone; Cold Shock; Elemental Fusion + Curse + Domination). External mouse macros outperform in-game ones here because in-game macros cannot chain self-buff → triple summon → spirit skill. Tab-target mode works better with macros because the auto-attack fills gaps and restores mana.
  • What the Global playtest changed (September 17-19, level cap 37): the kit itself is identical to Korea’s. What differs is everything around it: stigma slots unlock at 22, 27, 32 and 37 (four by the level cap, against the six the Korean endgame sets assume), the PvE Daevanion board was absent from the Global build while the PvP board stays (its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch), pets cap at level 3 instead of 5, the Ancient Spirit stigma caps at +20 instead of +25, and Season 1 ships without Heroic-tier gear. Arena and Battleground were locked, so nothing PvP was testable and every endgame number above is still Korea and Taiwan.

Mistakes

Section titled “Mistakes”
  • Summoning before buffing. Chaining the Water spirit right after another (its skill is dropped; always last, with a ~1 s gap). Thinking Elemental Fusion needs 4 Domination casts. Over-levelling past the threshold (Elemental Fusion at 22/24/25 is “a total waste”). Over-investing in Erosion (~5% of damage). Casting Curse twice per cycle. Several skills on one keybind. Chasing frontal damage or standing in the melee stack. Trusting displayed combat power (a server-number-one at 932 was doing less DPS than peers). Mass fear in a mechanics fight (it repositions the boss and adds). Picking it for “best solo class”.
