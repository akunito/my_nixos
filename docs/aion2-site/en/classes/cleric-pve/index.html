Cleric in PvE

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: KR/TW baseline + Global LST 2026-09-17/19 adjustments · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. Global Season 1 equips four stigmas, which rebuilds the dungeon loadout, and the Snowfield of Grief crystal rule is corrected; skill names are the English forms seen in the Global and Taiwan clients where known, otherwise working translations.

See also: the leveling plan, for what to spend points on band by band, and the skills guide, for all 39 skills with their specialties and chains.

Summary

Section titled “Summary”
  • Cleric is not a Western-MMO healer. The Korean community’s phrase is “힐은 거들뿐”: “healing is just a garnish”. The job: keep two damage debuffs permanently on the boss, hold the party’s damage buff, weave damage between heals, and save two panic cooldowns for scripted mechanics. A Cleric who only heals is playing it wrong by KR standards, not by taste.
  • It is the lowest-DPS class in the game, measured in Taiwan: 71.7 against a 100 baseline at the 90th percentile, -28.3%, last of 9. Every other class is within 10.1% of each other; Cleric sits alone below them. No build fixes that. Playtest reports from the Global build say it in plainer words: buffed since the Korean launch, still a support class, still the bottom of the meter, and there is no “DPS Cleric” build to find (GLOBAL, 2026-09-20, r/Aion2).
  • Even so it is A-tier by real leaderboard presence (101 of 800 top-100 slots across 4 PvE modes) and mandatory in top-end raids: the only class with mass resurrection.
  • Solo leveling and daily farming are the pain. If you want to farm mats, dailies and alts fast, Chanter is better; Cleric wins at mass PvP and hard raid progression. In 1v1 it survives almost anything but struggles to finish a kill, because the opponent simply leaves, which is why leaderboard-derived solo-PvP rankings put it mid-table rather than top. For a competitive legion-PvP goal, that split still favours Cleric.
  • Your whole rotation runs on one macro key. Hold it; the game cycles Mark of Weakening → Chain of Pain → Condemnation → Judgment Lightning by cooldown. Heals and the charge nuke stay manual. Normal in Korea, not an exploit. And on interface load: Cleric heals are self-centred 25 m AoEs; nobody gets clicked.
  • Since the 2026-08-26 patch (minimum cancel time) you do not need a hardware macro to compete: the in-game macro reaches the same damage and hit count.
  • Your damage is ~30% Condemnation. Level it and its enabler first; defer healing investment until max level.
  • The single build lever: ~34% cooldown reduction, which drops the Earth’s Punishment stigma from 30 s to 20 s so its debuff never falls off, which keeps Condemnation critting and resetting. Everything else hangs off that.
  • Two buttons decide raids: Salvation (3 s full immunity, 2 min 30 cooldown) and Yustiel’s Power (party +20% PvE damage tolerance, 10 s, 1 min). In every Cleric-PoV Sanctuary 4 clear the save is Salvation timed to a named mechanic, not healing throughput.
  • Global Season 1 will be weaker than anything on YouTube. Skills and stigmas cap at 20, not 25; no Heroic gear. Every KR guide from July 2026 on is built on level-25 stigma specs you cannot reach.

How it actually works

Section titled “How it actually works”

The damage engine is Chain of Pain → Condemnation. Condemnation only hits targets carrying Chain of Pain’s debuff, has a 3 s base recast and a specialization giving a 50% chance to reset its own cooldown on hit (the KR client tooltip as of 2026-09-22 says 75%, and puts it in the level-12 slot; see the skills guide), so it chains 5-6 casts per Chain of Pain window. It is ~30% of total damage.

Two debuffs are the party contribution. Mark of Weakening cuts the target’s PvE damage amp; Chain of Pain cuts its tolerance. Per the official patch notes their resistance reductions stack additively rather than overwriting. Keeping both up is, per the Korean sources, the actual job.

Earth’s Punishment is the burst enabler. It locks Condemnation into guaranteed crits for ~10 s at level 5, and with enough cooldown reduction its debuff becomes permanent.

Basic attacks carry the rotation. Left and right click are ~70% of skill usage; Earth’s Retribution restores 110 MP and Discharge 150, which is how the kit pays for 120-MP Judgment Lightning. They also build the groggy gauge.

Healing cannot crit. Healing Power Boost is nearly the only lever, and since 2026-03-25 it scales off attack power: attack is a healing stat.

Heals are self-centred 25 m AoEs. No manual target selection anywhere in the heal kit. Light of Healing picks the lowest-HP member on its own every 6 s; Light of Regeneration is a 30 s HoT you refresh; Radiance of Recovery is a half-second charge that heals everyone and cleanses one debuff at full charge.

Its two real cooldowns are mechanic answers, not resource management. Salvation gives 3 s of full immunity every 2 min 30; Yustiel’s Power gives the party 20% tolerance for 10 s every minute. In any Sanctuary clear from the Cleric’s seat, healing does not carry the hard moments: Salvation is spent on a named burst at 75% and Yustiel’s Power is pre-cast before a known pattern.

There is a hard number under “don’t build a pure healer”. You need roughly 5% of the damage dealt to a boss to open its loot queue at all. Tanks and supports do get invited below a party’s listed combat power (a 200k party will take a 150k Cleric), but nobody is carried past that 5%. The optimised five-player composition, for what it is worth, is Templar, Gladiator, Chanter, Cleric and one ranged damage dealer, to collect every distinct buff; the ordinary one is one tank, three damage, one support, and a second Cleric adds nothing because neither the buff nor the revive stacks.

What changed, and what English guides do not know

Section titled “What changed, and what English guides do not know”
  • Prayer of Amplification: dropped in November 2025 (its +20% attack is self-only), mandatory at 15 in January 2026 because its spec multiplies Sovereign’s Grace by 1.5 (whether that is party-wide or self-only is unresolved), and “a trap” by February. Testable with two accounts.
  • Absolution: from the number-one stigma in December 2025 (“do not skip”) to mode 0 in February (adoption 72% → 46%), because the active heal kit already suffices. Situational: dungeons with a mandatory cleanse, such as the Fire Temple burn DoTs.
  • Burst of Authority: mandatory in November because boss patterns required a forced groggy; 26% by February, “do not run it, it lost the slot race”. English guides from August and September still recommend it: they read a pre-rework meta.
  • Light of Protection was the biggest single change in Cleric history: from average level 7.2 and 52% adoption to average 16.8, mode 15 and 97% after gaining +20% party damage at level 15. It does not stack with a Chanter’s damage buff; the resistance halves do.
  • The 2026-03-25 overhaul: stigmas 4 → 5 with no restrictions; PvE and PvP coefficients separated; Abyss healing -50%; charge-stage bonus 20% → 40%; immunities not chainable within 30 s. For Cleric: Mark of Weakening casts while moving and healing scales off attack, the developers’ answer to “Cleric feels weak as gear improves”.
  • Chapter 1 (July 2026), level-25 specs: Salvation +10% instant HP, Light of Protection +5% party Smite, Yustiel +20% shield, Earth’s Punishment 10 s → 20 s duration (the KR client tooltip as of 2026-09-22 puts that +10 s in the level-20 slot, which Global can reach; see the skills guide). None of it exists on Global, which caps stigmas at 20.
  • The 2026-08-26 patch added a global minimum cancel time: it closed the gap between hardware and in-game macros, lowered the Cleric party damage buff slightly, and flipped the boss rotation: basic attack + Condemnation now beats Judgment Lightning + Condemnation, and one KR Cleric stopped using Judgment Lightning on bosses. Testable with the damage meter (Ctrl+X).
  • Earth’s Retribution: “weave it between every skill” vs “do not level it”. Rankers sit in between: average 16, most stop at 16, ~10% reach 20, and those who do not attack-cancel skip it.
  • One English source names a Cleric kit (“Divine Hammer”, “Radiant Ward”, “Spear of Radiance”) found nowhere else, including the database: fabricated or another game. Nothing from it is used.

Builds

Section titled “Builds”

Leveling 1-45 on Global

Section titled “Leveling 1-45 on Global”
  • Damage before heals. Both KR guides that revised themselves converged: Earth’s Retribution, Judgment Lightning, Lightning Barrage and Thunderclap first, then Condemnation; heals get points at max level.
  • Both basic attacks to 8 and specialized before anything else. Outsized early impact on clear speed even with everything else at 1.
  • Mark of Weakening and Lightning Barrage to their level-12 thresholds if investing further: the level-12 effects (CC, Vandanite proc) matter more than the level-8 damage.
  • Day-one gearing: sealed dungeons and strongholds, transmute the noble belt and revelation amulet at 45 for a free yellow weapon at ~1,000 gear score. No full clears: route to the next gear-score gate. First 100%-selection item: boots with movement speed (affects mount speed too). The Monolith favour-to-skill-point conversion does not exist on Global: skill points and Wisdom Shards come from sealed dungeons instead, and open-world feathers no longer grant them.
  • Do not socket manastones before 45.

Dungeon stigma loadout (4 slots at Global launch, 5 in KR)

Section titled “Dungeon stigma loadout (4 slots at Global launch, 5 in KR)”

Global Season 1, four slots (a Taiwanese rank-1 Cleric and the Launch Scale Test guide arrive at the same four): Light of Protection, Resurrection (Summon Revive), Earth’s Punishment to 20, and Noble Aura (Noble Energy) for its constant damage: a developer livestream confirmed Cleric keeps Noble Aura at release. Swap Noble Aura for Prayer of Amplification if your own testing shows more damage, or for Absolution / Authority of Life when the content demands healing. Level Resurrection to at least 10: below that its cast time makes it barely usable, and running without it is a kickable offence in Korean and Taiwanese groups.

Korean endgame, five slots, which is what every ranker statistic on this page describes: the locked three are Light of Protection to 15 (+20% party damage; swap out with a Chanter), Earth’s Punishment to 20, and Salvation (93-100% adoption; not a heal, a scheduled answer). The other two by content: Yustiel’s Power (the natural Light of Protection replacement with a Chanter), Summon Revive, Absolution where a cleanse is mandatory, Prayer of Amplification for solo and Nightmare, Burst of Authority only if your group misses groggy checks. Salvation falling out of the launch loadout is a consequence of four slots and of Season 1 having no Sanctuary-4-class raid to spend it on, not of it getting worse.

Dungeon skills and specializations

Section titled “Dungeon skills and specializations”
  • Radiance of Recovery / Radiant Recovery to 20 first (the highest-adoption active). Specs: the cleanse spec (“cleanse 2 debuffs” at max charge) is the pick two Taiwanese guides make. The +20% skill-speed spec the Korean rankers treat as mandatory was reworked into “+1 consecutive cast” by the Korean 2026-09-16 patch; whether Global ships the old spec or the new one is unknown, so check the tooltip before spending points.
  • Light of Healing to at least 16: the 16-spec cuts ~2 s of cast and brings the recast to ~1 s.
  • Condemnation to 20: 50% reset on hit (the KR client tooltip as of 2026-09-22 says 75%; see the skills guide) + multi-hit. This is your damage.
  • Chain of Pain to 16: the level-8 spec (+3 s) fits a second Condemnation into the window; the level-12 spec turns single-element resistance reduction into all-resistance.
  • Mark of Weakening to 12-16. Light of Regeneration to 16 (damage-chasing rankers stop at 16 to fund Condemnation). Earth’s Retribution to 8 or 16 depending on attack cancelling. Holy Aura cap 8. Lightning Barrage to 16 for the -1 s-to-all-cooldowns-on-hit effect, used to reset stigmas right after a groggy.
  • Specializations are freely swappable: re-check them per content type.

Passives

Section titled “Passives”

Sovereign’s Grace (the only pure damage passive) → Grace of the Earth → Warm Blessing, Healing Power Boost and Veil of Immortality round-robin (1 point, then 2, then 4) → Radiant Blessing and Prayer of Focus. Solo variant: Warm Blessing ahead of Healing Power Boost. Skip Recovery Block.

The macro (the real execution layer)

Section titled “The macro (the real execution layer)”
  • Editor: Settings (Esc) → Keys → General → Macros → right-click to create. Up to 20 skills; actives and stigmas only; three damage-bar options and the macro mirrors the active one.
  • Order matters: bottom-up so the debuffs land first: Mark of Weakening → Chain of Pain → Condemnation → Judgment Lightning. Hold the key; the game cycles by cooldown.
  • Out of the macro: Thunderclap (charge skill: macroed it fires one uncharged hit). Workaround: bind it as press-and-hold with no key-up event and it auto-fires at full charge.
  • Buff block: one key holding Earth’s Punishment + Light of Protection + Prayer of Amplification + Noble Energy. Light of Protection is a toggle.
  • Heals stay manual. They must be reactive.
  • Maximum-damage variant: pull Thunderclap, Light of Regeneration and Prayer of Amplification out of automation and time them by hand around boss invulnerability windows; the creator himself does not go that far, trading a little damage for comfort.
  • Delay: start at 50-60 ms and tune to your own ping; 8-10 ms is only for genuinely low-latency connections (a Taiwanese Cleric playing at ~200 ms uses 50-60). Never set it below your ping. Test in the Legion Hall with the meter (Ctrl+X) before a dungeon and re-tune every patch.
  • One KR guide argues against manual animation cancelling: small gain, high miss and interrupt risk, and the freed attention is better spent on boss patterns. The other school treats it as the core skill.

Raid (Sanctuary 4, Snowfield of Grief)

Section titled “Raid (Sanctuary 4, Snowfield of Grief)”

This is Korea’s current raid tier, not Global’s. Global Season 1 opens on a different 10-player Sanctuary, Abyssal Forge: Ludra, at around 2,700 gear score (Korean sources read ~2,800), so read what follows as how the class is played in a raid, not as a fight guide you will use at launch.

  • Salvation is a mechanic answer, not a heal. Named uses: the party-wide burst when accumulated warmth detonates in the ~75% breath phase, and the ~25% enrage window where managing your own freeze while dodging is too much. One Cleric holds it rather than using it on cooldown, saving it for the densest overlapping windows.
  • Yustiel’s Power is pre-cast, before known pulls and rear swings, not reactively.
  • Two different crystal mechanics, and confusing them wipes the raid. On the first boss at ~50%, colour means damage type: red is broken by physical damage, sky-blue by magic, so the Cleric takes blue. On the second and final bosses’ ~50% gate the rule is the opposite kind of thing: break purple crystals only. Hitting a blue one there damages the whole raid and stacks “Deltras’s Hatred” on the boss, raising its damage mitigation for the next pattern. On the second boss, melee at 12, 3, 6 and 9, ranged at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o’clock.
  • Temperature mechanic: at zero, full freeze deals 10% max HP every 5 s while immobile; standing near a warmer ally restores yours at no cost to them.
  • When a groggy precedes a phase transition that resets boss HP, stop damaging and bank cooldowns.
  • Whether an extra pillar phase is inserted depends on the raid’s damage pace: exactly the check a heal-only Cleric fails.
  • One more named Salvation window: the stab-then-whirlwind pattern drains the main tank’s temperature badly. If you have not eaten anything else yourself, that is the moment to spend the invulnerability on the tank rather than on you.
  • Heal through the pillar phase instead of panicking. Its follow-up hit is survivable for a Cleric- or Templar-geared character, so the marked players live if you keep healing, and call out anyone standing wrong instead of moving them yourself.
  • Stand at the edge of the formation, never stacked in the middle. Other players’ dodge-rolls body-block and displace you. Hold the edge, but close enough that nobody’s name greys out: that is Light of Regeneration’s range telling you they have dropped out of your heals.

Mistakes and traps

Section titled “Mistakes and traps”
  • Building a pure healer: the most repeated critique in Korea and in English.
  • Copying a KR guide from July 2026 on for Global day one.
  • Putting a charge skill in the macro.
  • Copying someone else’s macro delay without tuning it to your ping.
  • Macro spam without watching MP: it drains far faster than manual play.
  • Running Light of Protection alongside a Chanter’s damage buff.
  • Two godstones with the same effect.
  • Reading Combat Power as readiness: you can raise it while making the character worse at its job.
  • Judging manastone popularity from raw pick rates.
  • Over-enhancing gear you will replace.
  • Ignoring Abyss Points as a PvE player: they buy stigma shards, and stigmas are where a Cleric’s power is. On Global this is softer than in Korea, since the Shugo Festival shop (500 tokens) and the Nightmare shop also sell shards, one batch of 35 being about half of what a stigma needs to reach 20, but the Abyss is still the largest source.
  • Getting the Light of Regeneration cast interrupted: since February 2026, being hit or dodging during the cast locks the follow-up heal for the full 20 s.
  • Farming regular-tier bracelets.
  • Picking Cleric off a tier list. A main’s test: “if the class lost 20% of what makes it meta today, would you still log in tomorrow?”

Open questions

Section titled “Open questions”

Whether Prayer of Amplification’s multiplier is party-wide or self-only. Whether 34% cooldown reduction is reachable on Global without Heroic gear: harder than it looked, since Global cut the Daevanion node values (combat speed 2.5% to 1.5%, cooldown reduction 2.5% to 1.5%, damage boost 5% to 1.5%); if it is not reachable, the build premise changes. Whether Global ships Radiance of Recovery’s old “+20% skill speed” spec or Korea’s 2026-09-16 “+1 consecutive cast” rework. Whether the basic-attack-over-Judgment-Lightning finding holds on the Global build: two regions now agree it does on KR and TW. Whether the PvE Daevanion board returns at launch: it is absent from the Global Launch Scale Test build, its point sinks still exist in shop and reward text, and it is unconfirmed for the 10-05 launch. Whether the four-stigma-slot figure survives launch: the test build showed four, no official note states a count. What exactly “Reincarnating Touch” (the new March stigma in NCsoft’s press release) is versus Summon Revive. The 94.4% breakpoint. Nothing in English on Cleric in sieges. Whether support actions earn Abyss Points.
