Templar: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, enchant cap 20, no Heroic gear).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. Almost no skill changes its visible numbers as it ranks up: what grows is the damage coefficient, which the database does not publish.
  3. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  4. Tell a chain apart from a condition. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears after the skill that triggers it. A condition (Judgment needs a stun, Flash Rampage needs groggy) is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met.
  5. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in.
  6. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  7. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  8. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. The English names in the entries are the ones Inven serves (name_en), not NCsoft’s localisation, and in four cases (the chains) Inven gives none at all and the translation is ours. The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean client text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 4 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 39 in total. All of them require a long sword (장검) except Protective Strike and Annihilation, which list no weapon.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Fierce Strike (맹렬한 일격)

Section titled “Fierce Strike (맹렬한 일격)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Restores 100 MP. What it is for: this is your left click and your mana battery. It builds threat and keeps the blue bar alive while the right click drains it. It is also the entrance to the only three-link chain the class has. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% MP recovery / 2% HP absorb / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on hit, -1 s to Protective Strike’s cooldown. 16: adds the 4th chain link, Threatening Smash (위협의 맹타). Chains into: Decisive Strike (link 1), Desperate Strike (link 2) and, with the level-16 specialty, Threatening Smash (link 4).

Continuous Flurry (연속 난타)

Section titled “Continuous Flurry (연속 난타)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: 1 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Groggy gauge damage: 3. What it is for: the right click, the filler with the heaviest threat weight, and what holds aggro when you have no Taunt slotted. The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 raised its cancel delay, and the Korean community reading is literally “stop using the Continuous Flurry combo” (KR, 2026-09-17/22). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% MP cost / Punishing Strike absorbs 3% HP / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit. 12: on a Punishing Strike hit, -2 s to Punishment’s cooldown. 16: Punishing Strike activates one extra time. Chains into: Punishing Strike (link 1).

Capture (포획)

Section titled “Capture (포획)”

Kind: active · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 15 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m, pulls it in front of you and with 75% chance binds it for 2 s. Against NPCs the bind lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your most valuable PvP skill, and not for the damage: yanking an overextended enemy out of their line and into yours turns a 4v5 into an effective 5v4 before damage enters the equation (KR/Global, 2026-07 and 2026-09-20). In PvE it regathers scattered adds. The reset-on-kill specialty is what makes Templar a pack-to-pack farming machine. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): if the target is control-immune, you move to it instead / on hit, -200 accuracy on the target for 5 s / resets the cooldown on a kill. 12: shield worth 10% of your max HP. 16: 50% chance of a 3 s stun. Chains into: nothing. It is not a chain.

Shield Smite (방패 강타)

Section titled “Shield Smite (방패 강타)”

Kind: active · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies in front Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m. The target is stunned for 3 s with a 65% chance. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: three jobs at once. It opens 2 s of Judgment window (TW, 2026-09-13), it is your reliable stun against NPCs, and it is the proc route into Weakening Strike, which is what frees you from depending on block RNG. The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +20% PvE damage, the largest single buff in that patch (KR, 2026-09-16). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +1 s stun duration / removes the MP cost and restores 200 MP on hit / 50% chance on hit to activate Weakening Strike. 12: -2 s cooldown. 16: area stun. Chains into: nothing; it feeds Judgment by condition, not by chain.

Judgment (심판)

Section titled “Judgment (심판)”

Kind: active, gated on stun · Cost: none · Cooldown: 5 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a stunned or knocked-down target within 4 m and increases threat. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 7% chance on hit. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: the damage engine of the class, roughly 65% of total output before the August rework (KR, 2026-08-25). That 7% against bosses is unplayable on its own, so the entire class exists to manufacture windows for it. The full story is in Combos, section (b). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 2% HP absorb / turns into an area skill / extra damage on hit. 12: guaranteed critical on hit. 16: removes the cooldown. Data note: one creator claimed the cooldown-removal specialty sits at level 20 (single source, region unknown, 2026-09-20). The database puts it in the level-16 slot. We go with the data. Chains into: nothing.

Flash Rampage (섬광 난무)

Section titled “Flash Rampage (섬광 난무)”

Kind: active, gated on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target in a groggy state within 4 m and increases threat. What it is for: a groggy-window filler with no cost and no cooldown. Its level-16 specialty is one of the most underrated things in the kit: -1 s to all your cooldowns every time it lands. Taiwanese damage builds treat it as an early-game skill that falls off (TW, 2026-09-04), and its Arcana card is described the same way. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% HP absorb / restores 120 MP on hit / becomes usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Chains into: nothing.

Weakening Strike (쇠약의 맹타)

Section titled “Weakening Strike (쇠약의 맹타)”

Kind: active, gated on a weapon block · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant at rank 1 · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant Rank 20: here the entry does change, and oddly: the cooldown becomes 10 s and the target goes from “single” to “up to 4 enemies in front”. At rank 1 the entry says single target while its own text already talks about 4 enemies, so the rank-1 row contradicts itself. We leave it as it stands. Text: on a successful block, deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and applies Weakened (쇠약) for 5 s. Weakened: -25% defense. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: your single largest contribution to party damage. The -25% defense goes on before the burst, never after (KR, 2025-11-25). The -50%-healing-received specialty is Templar’s anti-heal tool in group PvP; a Taiwanese synthesis puts that number at -16% (TW, 2026-09-04) while the Korean database says -50%. Two different numbers for the same node, and nobody has reconciled them. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 30% chance of a 3 s stun / +3 s Weakened duration / -50% healing received while Weakened. 12: ignores block and evasion, lands as multi-hit. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on a successful block. Chains into: nothing; it fires off a block or off Shield Smite’s proc.

Protective Strike (비호의 일격)

Section titled “Protective Strike (비호의 일격)”

Also called Warding Strike in the Taiwanese client and in nearly every English guide.

Kind: active · Cost: 200 MP · Cooldown: 30 s · Range: 4.5 m in the entry, 5 m in the text · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies around you · Properties: non-targeted Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m around you. You recover health instantly and gain Warded (비호) for 10 s. Warded: +20% PvE damage tolerance, +10% PvP. What it is for: self-heal, mitigation and 2 s of Judgment window (TW, 2026-09-13), all on one 30 s button. The detail that makes it good in PvP is that the buff applies even if you hit nobody, which makes the +5 s duration specialty worth far more than it looks (KR, 2026-01-15). The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +10% PvE damage. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +5 s Warded / +100 block while Warded / heal-over-time while Warded. 12: the more targets you hit, the greater Warded’s PvE and PvP tolerance. 16: on use, the party gains +1 PvE damage tolerance and +5% PvP damage tolerance. Data note: that “+1 PvE tolerance” next to a “+5%” is what the literal Korean text says. It looks like a database error, most likely a lost percent sign. We do not correct it. Chains into: nothing.

Shield Charge (방패 돌격)

Section titled “Shield Charge (방패 돌격)”

Kind: active, gated on an emergency evade · Cost: none · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 10 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: after using the emergency evade, charge a target within 10 m and deal damage. The target is stunned for 3 s with a 50% chance. Against NPCs it lands 100% of the time. Groggy gauge damage: 20. What it is for: your second and last gap closer, and one of the four skills that open a Judgment window (KR, 2026-08-25). In PvP it goes to 20 precisely because Templar has nothing else to re-engage with: fully specced it reaches 20 m, 75% stun chance and a cooldown falling toward 10 s, which lets you re-enter roughly twice as often in an arena (KR, 2026-01-15). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): restores 300 stamina on hit / +10 m range / becomes a charge skill with an area attack when charged. 12: -10 s cooldown. 16: +25% stun chance. Chains into: nothing.

Annihilation (섬멸)

Section titled “Annihilation (섬멸)”

Kind: active, gated on stun · Cost: 200 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 4 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a stunned or knocked-down target within 4 m and with a 30% chance knocks it down for 3 s. Against NPCs the knockdown lands 100% of the time. Against control-immune targets it activates with a 5% chance. What it is for: the second conditional hit. Its +100%-damage-against-control-immune specialty is enormous, because “control-immune” is nearly every boss. The Korean rule of thumb is that if Judgment and Annihilation are both available you press Judgment: it procs at 7% against 5% and hits harder (KR, 2026-01-15). The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +10% PvE damage. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +50% multi-hit on hit / +20% knockdown chance / +100% damage against control-immune targets. 12: -10 s cooldown. 16: turns into an area skill. Chains into: nothing. Shield of Ruin resets its cooldown on hit (the stigma’s level-5 specialty).

Punishment (징벌)

Section titled “Punishment (징벌)”

Kind: active, charge skill · Cost: 200 MP · Cast: 0.5 s / 1 s / 1.5 s (3 stages) · Cooldown: 30 s · Range: 5 m · Target: up to 4 enemies around you · Properties: non-targeted, charge skill Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 5 m around you and you gain Executor (집행자) for 10 s. Executor: +200 accuracy, +10% PvE damage amplification and +5% PvP. Maximum 3 charge stages. Groggy gauge damage: 20 to 35. What it is for: the biggest hit in the kit and the opener of every PvE rotation. Measured in Taiwan on boosted gear: around 100,000 at full charge plus around 56,000 of damage over time (TW, 2026-09-04). It cannot go in a macro: the macro only fires the first stage, which leaves you with the weakest of the three hits (KR, 2026-01-15; independently confirmed, 2026-09-20). The 2026-09-16 patch gave it +10% PvE damage. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): absorbs 10% of the damage as health / damage over time on hit / +30% skill speed. 12: usable while moving. 16: ignores block and evasion, and lands as a critical. Chains into: nothing. Continuous Flurry, via Punishing Strike, cuts 2 s off its cooldown.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Called Defiance in several English guides.

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: self · Cast: instant · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, air-bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. It is one of only two skills in the whole kit whose visible numbers change with rank. Text: removes your stun, knockdown, air bind, grab, freeze and fear states and puts you in Steadfast (강인함) for 5 s. Steadfast: immunity to status ailments. What it is for: your CC break, and the mandatory first step of Seize Alive. Rebind it off the space bar on day one: mashing space to escape a stun summons your wings instead (Global, 2026-09-18). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Steadfast, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance / on cast, restores 1000 stamina / on cast, restores 20% of max HP. 12: +2 s Steadfast. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Seize Alive (link 1).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

The four chains are buttons that only exist after the skill that triggers them. The first three activate at 100%, so they always land. One database detail: chains have no specialties of their own, they display their parent skill’s. What you pick on Fierce Strike is what applies to both of its links.

Decisive Strike (회심의 일격)

Section titled “Decisive Strike (회심의 일격)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Restores 100 MP. What it is for: the second hit of the left click. Same mana return as the first. It is not a decision, it is the rhythm of the left click. Chained from: Fierce Strike, position 1.

Desperate Strike (필사의 일격)

Section titled “Desperate Strike (필사의 일격)”

Inven gives no English name. The translation is ours.

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Restores 120 MP. What it is for: the third hit of the left click and the one that gives the most mana back. If mana is tight early (2,700 to 3,000 without gear), finishing the whole chain instead of cutting it short is literally your economy. Chained from: Fierce Strike, position 2.

Punishing Strike (응징의 일격)

Section titled “Punishing Strike (응징의 일격)”

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: instant · Range: 4 m · Target: up to 4 enemies in front Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m and increases threat. Groggy gauge damage: 5. What it is for: the right-click link and the filler that pushes the most groggy. It is also what cuts Punishment’s cooldown once you take Continuous Flurry’s level-12 specialty. The 2026-09-16 patch touched three of its things at once: it raised its cancel delay, it made its 4th specialty cut Punishment’s recast when triggered via Continuous Flurry and not only via itself, and it changed the 5th from a guaranteed extra proc into extra damage on hit (KR, 2026-09-16). Chained from: Continuous Flurry, position 1.

Seize Alive (생포)

Section titled “Seize Alive (생포)”

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Range: 20 m · Target: single Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: deals damage to a target within 20 m. Pulls it in front of you, binds it for 3 s and with a 75% chance stuns it for 3 s. Against NPCs the stun lands 100% of the time. What it is for: this is the most overlooked thing in the kit. It is not a loose 2-minute skill: it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a control effect, which means that every time somebody stuns you in PvP they are handing you a 20 m grab with a 75% stun attached. None of our syntheses records it as a chain; the database does (Shock Release’s link_type points at it). Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Fury (격앙)

Section titled “Fury (격앙)”

Text: on a successful block, for 10 s you and your party members gain +11% PvE damage amplification and +5.5% PvP. At rank 20, +30% and +15%. What it is for: it is your entire group value in one line of text, and the reason dodging normal attacks is the classic beginner mistake: you switch off your own party buff. Watch one composition trap: it does not stack with a Gladiator’s Experience Counter Strike, it is the same buff (KR, 2026-09-10).

Roar of Insult (모욕의 포효)

Section titled “Roar of Insult (모욕의 포효)”

Text: your threat amplification increases by 100% and, on a successful block, you deal damage to the target and increase threat by a large amount. Internal cooldown: 5 s. What it is for: half of your aggro. The other half is hitting things. Taiwanese damage builds put it at around 15.5% frontal attack at level 20-21 after its buff (TW/region unknown, 2026-08-05).

Blessing of Condemnation (단죄의 가호)

Section titled “Blessing of Condemnation (단죄의 가호)”

Text: your critical increases by 100 and, on a successful attack, you gain Blessing of Condemnation for 10 s. Blessing of Condemnation: each time an attack lands, 50% chance to deal extra damage to the target. Internal cooldown: 1 s. At rank 20, +195 crit. What it is for: a Global-client guide puts it first in the passive queue while levelling, arguing that a tank’s sustain is already surplus and what is missing is crit (Global, 2026-09-21). That is a single medium-quality source; two earlier Korean sources and a third from this round keep Fury at the top.

Shield of Protection (비호의 방패)

Section titled “Shield of Protection (비호의 방패)”

Text: your block increases by 200 and you recover health on a successful block. Internal cooldown: 5 s. What it is for: the third leg of the block economy, alongside Fury and Roar of Insult. The Armor of Balance stigma multiplies its block and healing by 1.5 while it is up (level-15 specialty).

Iron-Wall Defense (철벽 방어)

Section titled “Iron-Wall Defense (철벽 방어)”

Text: your defense increases by 7% and your Iron Wall by 1%. At rank 20, +45% defense and +20% Iron Wall. What it is for: Iron Wall (철벽) is a proc that halves incoming damage and, unlike block, works from any angle (KR, 2026-01-15). A top-bracket Korean Templar built his whole PvP setup around it in January 2026, arguing that Iron Wall Penetration is not obtainable from manastones and so nobody carries it. That analysis predates the 2026-03-25 stat reweighting and Perfect Block, so treat it as historical. The Double Armor stigma multiplies this defense bonus by 1.5 while it is up.

Seal of Guardianship (수호의 인장)

Section titled “Seal of Guardianship (수호의 인장)”

Text: when your health is at 50% or below, creates a guardian seal that blocks damage for 30 s. Internal cooldown: 1 min. What it is for: an automatic safety net. The Protect Ally stigma multiplies its shield by 1.5 while it is up (level-10 specialty).

Shock Hit (충격 적중)

Section titled “Shock Hit (충격 적중)”

Text: your impact-type accuracy increases by 11.2% and your Smite by 0.3%. At rank 20, +34% and +6%. What it is for: the biggest damage passive of the class according to the damage builds (region unknown, 2026-08-05), and the scaling explains why: Smite is a double-damage proc and the priority damage stat at endgame. 0.3% sounds like nothing; 6% does not.

HP Boost (체력 강화)

Section titled “HP Boost (체력 강화)”

Text: your maximum health increases by 7.5% and the healing you receive by 6%. At rank 20, +55% and +25%. What it is for: the most aggressive scaling of any passive. It belongs in the second wave, once dungeons start.

Will to Survive (생존 의지)

Section titled “Will to Survive (생존 의지)”

Text: your status-ailment resistance increases by 17% and, when hit by stun, knockdown, air bind, freeze or fear, you gain +12% PvE and +6% PvP damage tolerance for 5 s. While in one of those states, each hit taken grants +10% status resistance for 5 s, stacking up to 10 times. Internal cooldowns: 1 min for the tolerance, 1 s for the resistance. At rank 20, +55% resistance and +50% / +25% tolerance. What it is for: anti-CC-chain insurance. Combos

Section titled “Combos”

(a) The chains the database defines

Section titled “(a) The chains the database defines”

There are only three chain routes in the whole Templar kit, and they cover four skills. Everything else the guides call a “combo” is a condition or a macro priority, not a chain.

Trigger Link 1 Link 2 Link 4 Activation
Fierce Strike (맹렬한 일격) Decisive Strike (회심의 일격) Desperate Strike (필사의 일격) Threatening Smash (위협의 맹타), only with the level-16 specialty 100%
Continuous Flurry (연속 난타) Punishing Strike (응징의 일격) with the level-16 specialty, Punishing Strike activates one extra time 100%
Shock Release (충격 해제) Seize Alive (생포) 100%, gated on Shock Release having fired

Three readings that come out of this and not out of any guide:

  • The left-click chain is your mana economy, not a damage trick: 100 + 100 + 120 MP per full sequence. Cutting it in half to press something else costs you 120 MP you will miss later.
  • Fierce Strike’s chain can have four links, not two. The fourth only exists if you take the level-16 specialty, and the database gives Threatening Smash no entry of its own.
  • Seize Alive is the only chain that triggers off a defensive skill. Do not put it in a macro expecting it to fire on its own: it only exists in the window after you break a control effect.

One warning from the macro mechanic itself: only links with near-100% activation deserve a macro slot. A 15% link more than halved a Taiwanese Cleric’s dummy DPS (TW, 2026-03-18). All three of Templar’s are 100%, so there is no trap here.

(b) The Judgment loop and its feeders

Section titled “(b) The Judgment loop and its feeders”

This is the mechanic that defines the class, and the one that has changed most.

What the database says: Judgment only hits stunned or knocked-down targets. Against a control-immune target, meaning any boss, it activates with a 7% chance per hit. Shield of Ruin (Doom Shield) activates Judgment on the target for 7 s on hit. Those are the only two numbers the database publishes about the window.

What the measurements say: the set of skills that opens the window is Shield Smite, Protective Strike (Warding Strike), Shield Charge and Shield of Ruin (KR, 2026-08-25). Measured live in Taiwan: Shield Smite gives 2 s, Warding Strike another 2 s, and Doom Shield acts as a reset adding 3 s while Shield Smite returns, which chained together yields roughly 4 s or more of continuous Judgment per cycle (TW, 2026-09-13).

The three window figures do not agree and nobody has reconciled them:

Source Region and date Judgment window
Patch-preview recap KR, 2026-08-25 3 s, and the creator corrects herself live
Inven database, Shield of Ruin rank 1 KR, 2026-09-22 7 s
Taiwanese player, measured in game TW, 2026-09-13 2 s + 2 s + 3 s, chainable

The loop as it is actually played (TW, 2026-09-13): Judgment → Shield Smite → Warding Strike → Doom Shield, looping as Shield Smite returns, on a mouse side button, while the right-click macro runs in parallel. A Korean one-minute test counts more than 230 Judgment casts (KR, 2026-09-02).

The 2026-09-16 cancel-delay warning, and it is the most important thing on this page. The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 increased the cancel delay on Continuous Flurry, Punishing Strike and Judgment, and the official notes say the change was aimed at players macro-cancelling into Judgment (KR, 2026-09-16). Consequences:

  • The animation-cancel figures in circulation (5.5M per rotation with cancelling against 4.9M without; 2.5M against 1.6M in a 4 s PvP window) were measured on 2026-09-13, three days before the patch (TW, 2026-09-13). They are historical.
  • The Korean forum reading is “stop using the Continuous Flurry and Fierce Strike rotation” (연심쓰지마), and the most upvoted threads compute current Templar DPS as below the pre-2026-08-26 baseline (KR, 2026-09-17/22).
  • Nobody has published a post-patch measurement, and nobody could take one in the Global Launch Scale Test because the cap was level 37. There is no new number to publish here, only the direction.
  • And the patch did raise damage: +20% PvE on Shield Smite, +10% PvE on Protective Strike, Annihilation and Punishment. Every increase is PvE only; PvP got the cut and nothing else.

(c) PvE rotations and macro chains

Section titled “(c) PvE rotations and macro chains”

The macro system rule: the in-game macro fires the first available skill going down the list, in strict priority order, skipping any whose condition is not met. This was confirmed on the Global client (Global, LST 2026-09-20) and settles the old argument about whether the order ran top-down or bottom-up: it is top-down. So conditional skills go above the filler or they never fire.

Dungeon macro chain (TW, 2026-09-13):

plus a separate macro on a side button running Judgment → Shield Smite → Warding Strike → Doom Shield in a loop. Opener: Punishment first, then into the Judgment spam.

PvX and open-world farming macro chain (TW, 2026-09-13):

Dashing into and out of packs resets Doom Shield and Capture, and that is exactly what makes Templar a pack-to-pack and Abyss-Corridor farming machine.

Prebuilt combos the client itself shows (Global, 2026-09-03): Judgment → Shield Smite → Warding Strike → Pummel; Doom Shield → Executing Blade → Punishment → Imperion Lord's Punishment; Capture → Debilitating Smash → Annihilate → Shield Rush. Buffs: Comrade in Arms → Second Skin. Situational: Taunt.

The three-skill variant (region unknown, 2026-09-20): only three skills in the macro, led by Banner of the Battlefield, which chains into Taunt and the following attacks and closes with the heavy finisher; you hold the macro key together with the basic-attack key to alternate between the two and sustain mana. Single source.

Right-click priority chain without a macro (KR, 2025-11-27), if you would rather play it by hand: Weakening Strike → Shield Smite → Annihilation → Continuous Flurry (no cooldown, the filler). Left click: Fierce Strike.

In a groggy window (KR, 2025-11-25): Weakening Strike (defense shred) → Sovereign's Punishment → burst. The order matters: the -25% defense goes on before the damage, not after.

Macro delays: the game ships 30 ms by default, and that is also what was seen on the Global client. Taiwan uses about 10 ms; a creator at 35 ms ping uses 10 and recommends 40-50 ms above 80-100 ms of ping. Tune to your real ping: input rate above your connection’s limit is wasted (TW/KR, 2026-09; Global, LST 2026-09-20). A hold-style macro wants a large delay, 50 to 100 ms, precisely because it is not meant to tap rapidly (KR, 2026-09-21/22).

Visual confirmation: a correctly executed cancel leaves a “shadow” or after-image of your character. Six independent creators use it as the signal that the delay is tuned; none of them knows whether it is an intended cue or a render artifact.

Point cost: taking three active skills to level 20 costs no passives; four costs 2 passives; five costs 6 (KR, single source).

(d) PvP combos and openers

Section titled “(d) PvP combos and openers”

The classic Korean opener (KR, 2026-01-15), the densest source that exists on Templar PvP:

The stun-overwrite rule, which is what makes the opener work: Capture’s stun lasts 3 s; Shield Smite’s, with its specialty, lasts 4 s. Landing Shield Smite while Capture’s stun is still running resets the clock to Shield Smite’s duration rather than adding to it. That is why they are thrown together and never in sequence: getting it wrong is a straight 25% cut to your whole damage window (KR, 2026-01-15).

Why Executioner’s Sword goes before Punishment: Punishment’s 3-stage charge is slow enough that the opponent will use their CC break before it lands, so you bank the guaranteed impact-state damage first with the fast animation (KR, 2026-01-15).

The defensive combo, which is the one that wins fights. Once the opener is spent and your control chain is broken, the Templar has roughly 5 seconds with no offensive cooldowns and simply has to not die. The timing rule is exact:

  1. You see the opponent press their CC break.
  2. At that instant, Double Armor: +30% PvP tolerance at base, +50% at level 20 with its specialty.
  3. On top, Protective Strike: another +10% PvP, and it applies even if you hit nobody.
  4. Combined, around 50% reduction through that 5 s window.
  5. Not earlier (you get Silenced with the defensive spent and evaporate) and not later (you die reacting).

Underneath, running by themselves: Pain Block (+5.5% PvP when hit, 20 s internal cooldown), Will to Survive (+6% PvP the instant a control effect lands on you) and Seal of Guardianship (automatic shield below 50% HP, 1 min internal cooldown).

The Taiwanese damage build’s PvP rotation (TW, 2026-09-04), meant for open world and rift rather than arena:

Capture, Executing Blade and Punishment’s charge, one line each:

  • Capture is the skill that wins group fights, and not through damage: pulling an overextended enemy into your line turns a 4v5 into a 5v4 before damage matters. Its 20 m cast is dodgeable by an alert opponent’s dash, and after that you have Shield Charge and nothing else (KR, 2026-01-15; 2026-07).
  • Executing Blade applies Exposed, which reduces block and evasion, and pushes the target’s skills onto cooldown (TW, 2026-09-04). Keep it manual, out of the macro, so you control when it lands.
  • Punishment’s charge never goes in a macro and never starts inside a short control window. It goes after Executing Blade, or as the opener in PvE where nobody is going to break your stun.

PvP-specific specialties (KR, 2026-01-15): Capture to 16 minimum for its 50% stun node, treated as mandatory; Shield Charge to 20 for the +10 m, the 75% stun and the cooldown drop toward 10 s; Shield Smite takes +1 s stun duration and the node that enables Weakening Strike, and deliberately skips the cast-time reduction, because Shield Smite roots you on cast and a mobile opponent simply walks out of 4 m; Judgment takes the guaranteed-crit node instead of the raw-damage one, because arena opponents stack crit resistance; Protective Strike takes the +5 s duration; Shock Release to a full 20 in a dedicated PvP build.

Two behaviour notes: do not pre-buff before engaging, because the buff cast telegraphs the fight; buff mid-combo instead, since buffs can be animation-cancelled into another skill (TW, 2026-09-13). And in PvP the macro has to shrink: a held macro fires blind to the opponent’s state, so Korean specialists automate two or three skills and keep the rest reactive (KR, 2026-09-21).

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Punishment. It is a 3-stage charge; the macro only fires the first stage, so you trade your biggest hit for the smallest of the three. It is the most repeated rule in the whole corpus and independently confirmed on the Global client.
  • Any forward dash, Shield Charge and Doom Shield included, in boss content. You will throw yourself into a telegraphed hit while mashing the button.
  • The party shields (Shield of Nezekan, Protect Ally). They are proactive mitigation and their entire value is in the moment you press them.
  • Block and Parry, and the dodge. Invulnerability frames are manual, always.
  • Any 20 m skill (Capture, Taunt, Sovereign’s Punishment, Seize, Assault Fierce Strike) if it would force you to reposition mid-chain. A Taiwanese guide excludes them explicitly so the macro never drags you out of position.
  • Heals and reactive defensives. Unanimous across all eight classes.
  • Passives, which simply cannot be added.
  • More than one row. Two or more rows reduce how many times each skill in the macro fires; if you need two, split one into the in-game macro and one into the mouse macro (KR, 2026-09-17/22).

What is still unresolved

The English names. Every one on this page is a working translation from Inven, from Taiwanese creators, or ours; NCsoft has published no Global localisation. The four chains have no English name at all in the database and their names here were invented by us from the Korean. Check them during Advanced Access from 30 September.

The Judgment window. Three incompatible figures: 3 s (KR, 2026-08-25), 7 s (Inven database, 2026-09-22) and 2 s + 2 s + 3 s chainable (TW, measured, 2026-09-13). This is the highest-priority day-one check.

The cancel-delay values. The Korean patch of 2026-09-16 raised the cancel delay on Continuous Flurry, Punishing Strike and Judgment, but the official notes give no coefficients and nobody has measured since. The size of the nerf is forum sentiment, not data.

Fury’s buff duration: 10 or 20 seconds. The database says 10 s. A Global-client guide describes a proc every second while blocking with a 20 s party buff (Global, 2026-09-21, medium quality). The likeliest reconciliation is that the 20 s figure is the separate “enhanced Fury” (강화격앙) gear effect Korean players complain about rather than the base passive, but nobody has confirmed it.

The stigma reset cost. Skill-point resets are free on Global, confirmed twice during the Launch Scale Test. The cost of resetting a stigma was observed by nobody, because the Abyss, the arenas and the battlegrounds were locked for the whole test.

Three mismatches between the database and the guides, unresolved: the Inven dump of 2026-09-22 still shows Taunt with flat threat and Noble Armor at 5 minutes, when the 2026-09-16 patch changed both; Weakening Strike’s anti-heal specialty reads -50% healing received in the Korean client and -16% in a Taiwanese synthesis; and Shield Smite’s level-8 specialty removes the mana cost in the current data, while Korean guides from 2025 warned about a node that raised it from 100 to 300.
