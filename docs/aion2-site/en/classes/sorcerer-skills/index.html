Sorcerer skills

Region: Korea · Version: Inven skill-simulator data pulled on 2026-09-22 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: high on the numbers (this is the straight database dump), medium on the English names (working translations, not the official localisation).

All 39 Sorcerer skills (마도성, job 7) as Inven serves them: 12 Active, 4 Chain (the follow-ups that can only be cast after the skill that links them), 10 Passive and 13 Stigma. Values are rank 1 of 20; rank is bought with skill points, and for stigmas with Stigma Shards. For what to do with this kit (fire and ice chains, the best crowd control in the game and cast cancelling), see Sorcerer.

How to read this table

The English names come from our own syntheses and the glossary, not from an official localisation: Global may well call them something else until the 09-30 Advanced Access (early access) confirms the real names. Only 19 of these 39 skills have an English name at all (49%): the Sorcerer syntheses quote fewer Korean skill names than the Templar and Cleric ones, and we would rather leave a gap than invent a translation. Descriptions are left in Korean for the same reason. The numbers are from the September 2026 Korean build and Global launches on Season 1, so expect balance differences; on top of that, Global equips only 4 stigma slots, so these 13 stigmas are the full catalogue, not a build.
