Cleric: every skill and every combo

Region: Korea (skill data), Taiwan and Global (practical use) · Version: Inven skill-simulator dump of 2026-09-22, KR/TW syntheses up to the 2026-09-16 patch, and the Global Launch Scale Test of 17-19 September 2026 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. The numbers in the skill entries are a direct dump of the Korean database and are reliable; what is “medium” is the naming and the combos half, which comes from creators and forums, not from NCsoft. NC has stated that class balance is identical across Korea, Taiwan and Global, so this kit is the kit you get; what changes is the Season 1 frame around it (4 stigmas, enchant cap 20, no Heroic gear).

How to use this page while playing

Section titled “How to use this page while playing”
  1. Looking for one specific skill, go to Skill reference and search on the Korean name: it is the only thing that does not change between clients. Cleric is also the class with the most aliases in circulation: the same skill shows up as Holy Aura, Divine Aura and Sacred Aura depending on who is writing.
  2. Every entry gives you rank 1 and what changes at rank 20. On Cleric a fair amount does change: the heals scale enormously and nearly every passive multiplies.
  3. Specialties are grouped by the level that unlocks them (8, 12, 16 on normal skills; 5, 10, 15, 20 on stigmas). At level 8 you pick one of three; at 12 and 16 the game offers only one.
  4. Tell a chain apart from a condition. A chain (연속기) is a new button that appears after the skill that triggers it. A condition (Condemnation needs Chain of Pain on the target, Lightning Barrage needs groggy) is not a chain: the skill sits on your bar permanently and only lands when the state is met.
  5. If you came here to build the macro, jump straight to Combos, section (c) for PvE and (e) for what must never go in. If you came to understand where your damage comes from, section (b).
  6. Every combat number carries a region and a date in brackets. If it carries none, it comes from the Korean database of 2026-09-22.
  7. Anything labelled single-source is exactly that: one person, uncorroborated. Do not build around it.
  8. The Aside at the bottom lists what is still unresolved, and on Cleric it is long: the database and the guides disagree on five points that hit the build directly. Check it yourself during Advanced Access from 30 September before trusting this page all the way.

Skill reference

Section titled “Skill reference”

Working translations from the KR client text; Global names may differ. The English names in the entries are the ones Inven serves (name_en), not NCsoft’s localisation. Where the name seen in the Global or Taiwanese client is known, it is given too: Debilitating Mark (Mark of Weakening), Chains of Torment (Chain of Pain), Healing Light (Light of Healing), Radiant Recovery (Radiance of Recovery), Resurrection (Summon Revive), Noble Aura (Noble Energy), Voice of Doom (Voice of Ruin), Imperion Lord’s Grace (Sovereign’s Grace), Earth’s Grace (Grace of the Earth), Healing Enhancement (Healing Power Boost) and Bolt (Thunderclap) (TW/Global, 2026-09-17/22). The Korean in brackets on first mention is the only stable handle. The descriptions are direct translations of the Korean client text: where the original is odd or contradicts itself, we say so instead of fixing it on our own authority.

Split: 12 actives, 4 chains, 10 passives, 13 stigmas, 39 in total. All 39 require a mace (전곤), without a single exception: unlike Templar, there are no weaponless skills here.

Actives

Section titled “Actives”

Earth’s Retribution (대지의 응보)

Section titled “Earth’s Retribution (대지의 응보)”

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: none · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single · Properties: usable while moving · Element: earth Rank 20: entry identical to rank 1. Text: deals earth damage to a target within 20 m and restores 110 MP. What it is for: this is your left click and half your mana economy. Basic attacks are roughly 70% of a Cleric’s skill usage (KR, 2025-11), and they are also what fills the groggy gauge. What makes it more than filler is what was measured in Korea and Taiwan in September: against bosses, plain basic attack paired with Condemnation out-damages weaving Judgment Lightning in (KR, 2026-08-27; TW, 2026-09-21). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +20% MP recovery / +20% Discharge chain activation chance / +50% multi-hit on hit. 12: on a Discharge hit, -7 s to Thunderclap’s cooldown. 16: ignores evasion and block. Chains into: Lightning (link 1, 100%) and Discharge (link 2, 30%). Build note: Korean rankers are split on it. “Weave it between every skill, it is the mana engine” against “do not level it at all, mana does not deplete with a maxed healing passive”. Top-100 data lands in the middle: average 15.9-16, most cap at 16, about 10% reach 20 (KR, 2026-01/02). English guides cap it at 8 for the mana rune.

Judgment Lightning (심판의 번개)

Section titled “Judgment Lightning (심판의 번개)”

Also Judgment Thunder in some Global-facing guides.

Kind: active, chain trigger · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: none · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target · Properties: usable while moving · Element: wind Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target 20 m away. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the classic right click and the most argued-about skill in the kit. No cooldown and 120 MP a cast, so it is what eats the blue bar the basic attacks fill. Korean rankers cap it at 20 (average 20.0, 77% at cap; KR, 2026-02) and call it the single most important damage skill. Two independent September measurements say the opposite against bosses (KR, 2026-08-27; TW, 2026-09-21), and one Korean Cleric stopped using it on bosses entirely, keeping it for trash. Nobody has measured it on the Global client. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -20% MP cost / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit / [Divine Punishment] on hit, -10% combat speed on the target for 5 s. 12: [Divine Punishment] on hit, 10% chance of a 2 s bind. 16: [Divine Punishment] activates one extra time. Chains into: Divine Punishment (link 1, 100%).

Mark of Weakening (약화의 낙인)

Section titled “Mark of Weakening (약화의 낙인)”

Called Debilitating Mark in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Kind: active · Cost: 100 MP · Cooldown: 10 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target 20 m away. For 10 s the enemies take -5% PvE damage amplification and -2.5% PvP, plus damage over time every 1 s. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: the first of your two permanent debuffs, and half your actual job in a party. Mark of Weakening cuts what the boss deals; Chain of Pain cuts what the boss withstands. Per the official Korean patch notes their reductions stack additively rather than overwriting. The Korean community’s phrase for this class, “힐은 거들뿐” (“healing is just a garnish”), is precisely about keeping these two at 100% uptime. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): removes the MP cost and restores 150 MP / -15% target defense while the damage-over-time runs / 20% chance of a 3 s stun. 12: an extra -5% PvE and -2.5% PvP amplification. 16: ignores evasion and block, and the damage-over-time interval drops 50%. Chains into: nothing.

Holy Aura (신성한 기운)

Section titled “Holy Aura (신성한 기운)”

Also Divine Aura and Sacred Aura depending on the guide. Same skill.

Kind: active, summon · Cost: none · Cooldown: 30 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: summons a holy aura near you that lasts 5 s. The aura cannot move and periodically deals earth damage to a target you designate within 20 m. What it is for: damage that keeps landing while you do something else, which in a class whose whole problem is not being able to heal and hit at once is worth more than it looks. It is the skill one Korean Cleric leaves inside the macro even when he pulls everything else out to manual (KR, 2026-08-27). Its level-8 -40% move-speed specialty is also one of your few crowd-control tools in PvP. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): -40% move speed for 3 s on up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the aura / becomes an area skill / +50% aura firing speed. 12: +3 s aura duration. 16: -10 s cooldown. Build note: cap it at 8. Of 100 Korean rankers, zero took it to 20 (KR, 2026-01). Chains into: nothing. The database lists it as “related” to all seven damage actives, but that is not a chain.

Chain of Pain (고통의 연쇄)

Section titled “Chain of Pain (고통의 연쇄)”

Called Chains of Torment in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Kind: active · Cost: 150 MP · Cooldown: 20 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target · Properties: usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals earth damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target 20 m away. For 10 s the enemies take -5% PvE damage tolerance and -7.5% PvP, plus damage over time every 1 s. Groggy gauge damage: 10. What it is for: it is the key to all of your damage, because Condemnation only lands on targets carrying this debuff. And it is one of very few things in the game worth more in PvP than in PvE: -7.5% PvP tolerance against -5% PvE. In a group fight, putting it on whatever your side is focusing is your single largest contribution. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): applies Slow (둔화) / +3 s effect duration / 20% chance of a 3 s knockdown. 12: an extra -10% damage tolerance. 16: ignores evasion and block, and the damage-over-time interval drops 50%. Build note: the level-8 +3 s is what fits one more Condemnation into the window; the level-12 turns the reduction into something the whole party notices. Take it to 16 (KR, 2025-11/2026-02). Chains into: nothing; it feeds Condemnation by condition, not by chain.

Lightning Barrage (벼락 난사)

Section titled “Lightning Barrage (벼락 난사)”

Kind: active, gated on groggy · Cost: none · Cooldown: none · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: single Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals damage to a target in a groggy state within 20 m. What it is for: a groggy-window filler with no cost and no cooldown, and the same hidden gem as on Templar: the level-16 specialty gives -1 s to all your cooldowns every time it lands, which is how you reset stigmas immediately after a groggy phase. In PvP it does not exist: players have no groggy gauge, so this skill and every point in it are dead weight the moment you enter the Abyss. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): 5% HP absorb / restores 120 MP / becomes usable while moving. 12: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. 16: on hit, -1 s to all cooldowns. Chains into: nothing.

Light of Regeneration (재생의 빛)

Section titled “Light of Regeneration (재생의 빛)”

Kind: active, heal · Cost: none · Cooldown: 30 s · Area: 25 m · Cast: instant · Target: you and up to 4 party members · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: restores health to you and party members within 25 m every 2 s for 30 s. What it is for: your background healing, and the reason Cleric does not have another healer’s UI-noise problem: you select nobody, it is a self-centred area. The price is that range rules: when an ally’s name greys out, they have dropped out of your heals (TW, 2026-09-22). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +5% defense while it heals / +50% healing below 50% HP / +5% received healing while it heals. 12: +5% move speed while it heals. 16: +5% PvE and +2.5% PvP damage tolerance while it heals. Version trap: since the Korean patch of 2026-02, taking a hit or dodging during its cast locks the follow-up heal out for the whole cooldown (KR, 2026-02). Chains into: nothing.

Condemnation (단죄)

Section titled “Condemnation (단죄)”

Kind: active, gated on Chain of Pain · Cost: none · Cooldown: 3 s · Range: 20 m · Cast: instant · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals earth damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target 20 m away that is under the effect of Chain of Pain. Groggy gauge damage: 7. What it is for: the damage engine of the class, roughly 30% of your total output (KR, community guide). No cost, a 3 s base cooldown, and a specialty that resets it on itself. A Korean creator describes it literally as “a machine gun” (KR, 2026-09-20). The full story is in Combos, section (b). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): restores 100 MP on hit / up to +12% damage the fewer targets you hit / becomes usable while moving. 12: on hit, 75% chance to reset its own cooldown. 16: guaranteed multi-hit on hit. Data note: that reset reads 50% in every Korean guide from 2025 and 2026 and 75% in the database of 2026-09-22. We go with the data and flag it. And the level-16 specialty appears in two PvP syntheses as “20% root”: the database says guaranteed multi-hit. Point note: take it to 20, not 16. Level 16 unlocks the third specialisation slot and level 20 lets all three be equipped at once (two below 20) (TW, 2026-09-22). Chains into: nothing.

Light of Healing (치유의 빛)

Section titled “Light of Healing (치유의 빛)”

Called Healing Light in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Kind: active, heal · Cost: 50 MP · Cooldown: 6 s · Area: 25 m · Cast: instant · Target: one party member · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: entry identical. Text: restores health to the lowest-HP party member within 25 m and to yourself. What it is for: your spam heal. The game picks the target; you only decide when. It is the skill every guide tells you to level early: to 16 as soon as possible as a repeatable heal (TW, 2026-09-21), and 97% of Korean rankers reach 16 (KR, 2026-01). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): +2 consecutive casts / +50% healing below 50% HP / 50% chance to remove 1 debuff. 12: +4% extra health restored. 16: -2 s cooldown. Data note: that level-16 specialty appears in the ranker guides as “cuts about 2 s and brings the recast to roughly 1 s”. The database says a 6 s base cooldown and -2 s, which is 4 s, not 1 s. Unreconciled. Chains into: nothing.

Radiance of Recovery (쾌유의 광휘)

Section titled “Radiance of Recovery (쾌유의 광휘)”

Called Radiant Recovery in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Kind: active, heal, charge skill (1 stage) · Cost: none · Cast: 0.5 s · Cooldown: 12 s · Area: 25 m · Target: you and up to 4 party members · Properties: non-targeted, charge, usable while moving, cleanse Rank 20: here the entry does change, enormously: the heal goes from 315-347 to 2613-2874. It is the largest rank scaling in the whole kit. Text: restores 315-347 health to you and party members within 25 m. At max charge the healing rises 50% and it removes 1 debuff. What it is for: the highest-adoption active in the class (average 20.3, 91% at cap) and the first skill to take to 20 (KR, 2026-01/02). Party heal, cleanse and panic button in one place, and for exactly that reason it is the one skill that must not be in a macro: a macro fires it at stage 0 and you lose the +50% and the cleanse. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): at max charge, remove up to 2 debuffs / +20% skill speed / -3 s cooldown. 12: at max charge, +20% received healing for 10 s. 16: at max charge, an extra 10% of max HP healed. Version warning: the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 changed that second specialty from “+20% skill speed” to “+1 consecutive cast”. The Inven dump of 2026-09-22 still shows the old one. Korean rankers treated the +20% as mandatory; two Taiwanese guides pick the cleanse instead. Read the tooltip before spending points. Chains into: nothing.

Thunderclap (벽력)

Section titled “Thunderclap (벽력)”

Called Bolt in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Kind: active, charge skill (3 stages) · Cost: none · Cast: 0.5 s / 1 s / 1.5 s · Cooldown: 45 s · Range: 20 m · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target 20 m away. Maximum 3 charge stages. Groggy gauge damage: 20 to 35. What it is for: your biggest single hit and, next to Burst of Authority, your biggest contribution to the groggy gauge. In the Global Launch Scale Test at level 37 it hit for 14,000-18,000 per landing, by far the top of the bar (Global, 2026-09-17). It cannot go in a macro: the macro fires stage 1 and you get the weakest of the three. There is a workaround and it is in section (e). Specialties: 8 (pick 1): at max charge, 30% chance of a 3 s stun / becomes usable while moving / +30% skill speed. 12: up to +20% damage the fewer targets you hit. 16: killing an enemy with Thunderclap resets its cooldown. Chains into: nothing.

Shock Release (충격 해제)

Section titled “Shock Release (충격 해제)”

Called Defiance or Deliverance in various English guides.

Kind: active, conditional chain trigger · Cost: none · Cooldown: 1 min · Target: yourself · Cast: instant · Properties: non-targeted · Condition: being stunned, knocked down, aerially bound, grabbed, frozen or feared Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 41 s. Text: clears your stun, knockdown, aerial bind, grab, freeze and fear states and grants Tenacity (강인함) for 5 s. Tenacity: immunity to status ailments. What it is for: your crowd-control break, and the single decision that most separates a good PvP Cleric from a bad one: Korean veterans deliberately eat the first crowd control and save it for the chain that actually kills them (KR, 2026-02). It is also the mandatory first step of Blessing of Regeneration, which is your mana reset. Specialties: 8 (pick 1): during Tenacity, +50% PvE and +25% PvP damage tolerance / on cast, restore 1000 stamina / on cast, restore 20% of max HP. 12: +2 s Tenacity. 16: 25% chance to reset the cooldown on cast. Chains into: Blessing of Regeneration (link 1, 100%).

Chains

Section titled “Chains”

The four chains are buttons that only exist after the skill that triggers them. Three activate at 100%; one, Discharge, at 30%, and that difference is the most important macro decision in the class (section (a)). One database detail: the chains have no specialties of their own, they show their parent skill’s. What you pick on Earth’s Retribution also applies to Lightning and Discharge.

Lightning (뇌전)

Section titled “Lightning (뇌전)”

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: none · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Element: wind Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to a target within 20 m and restores 110 MP. What it is for: second hit of the left click. Same mana as the first. It is not a decision, it is the rhythm of the left click. Chained from: Earth’s Retribution, position 1.

Discharge (방전)

Section titled “Discharge (방전)”

Kind: chain, 30% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: none · Range: 20 m · Target: single · Element: wind Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to a target within 20 m and restores 150 MP. What it is for: the link that gives back the most mana of the three, and the only one in the whole kit that does not always fire. In the chain-percentage method (section (a)) this is exactly the skill that comes out of the macro, and taking it out more than doubled a Taiwanese Cleric’s dummy DPS (TW, 2026-03-18). If you want it in, Earth’s Retribution’s level-8 specialty raises its chance by 20%. Data note: the database of 2026-09-22 says 30%. The creator who ran the measurement says he saw 15% base and 30% with a rune (TW, 2026-03-18). The two numbers do not line up; it could be an intervening patch, or he could have been reading the value with the specialty already taken. Chained from: Earth’s Retribution, position 2.

Divine Punishment (천벌)

Section titled “Divine Punishment (천벌)”

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: 120 MP · Cooldown: none · Range: 20 m · Target: up to 4 enemies within 4 m of the target Rank 20: entry identical. Text: deals wind damage to up to 4 enemies within 4 m of a target 20 m away. Groggy gauge damage: 2. What it is for: the right-click link. Note that it costs another 120 MP: the full right-click chain is 240 MP a sequence, which is where the reputation comes from that a Cleric macro drains the blue bar far faster than manual play (TW, 2026-07-05). Almost every interesting Judgment Lightning specialty actually acts on this link, not on the trigger. Chained from: Judgment Lightning, position 1.

Blessing of Regeneration (재생의 축복)

Section titled “Blessing of Regeneration (재생의 축복)”

Kind: chain, 100% activation · Cost: none · Cooldown: 2 min · Target: yourself · Properties: non-targeted, usable while moving Rank 20: the cooldown drops to 1 min 22 s. Text: restores your health and 40% of your mana. What it is for: this is the most ignored thing in the kit. It is not a stand-alone two-minute skill: it is Shock Release’s chain. It only appears after you break a crowd control, which means every time somebody stuns you in PvP they are handing you a 40% mana reset and a heal. It is what lets a Cleric win a long attrition fight. None of our syntheses records it as a chain; the database does (Shock Release’s link_type points at it). Chained from: Shock Release, position 1.

Passives

Section titled “Passives”

The ten passives have no specialties. What they do have is real rank scaling, and on Cleric it is more aggressive than on any other class: Healing Power Boost multiplies almost fivefold and Warm Blessing sixfold on mana. If you are wondering where to put points, this table is the answer.

Passive Rank 1 Rank 20
Warm Blessing (따뜻한 가호) +6% max HP, +7% max MP +25% / +45%
Sovereign’s Blessing (주신의 가호) +200 block, heal on a successful block no visible change
Sovereign’s Grace (주신의 은총) +100 crit, +0.2% Smite, bonus damage on hit +195 / +4%
Healing Power Boost (치유력 강화) +10% heal amplification, plus a bonus scaled by attack +48%
Veil of Immortality (불사의 장막) +2% defense, +901 crit resistance +40% / +920
Recovery Block (회복 차단) 7% on hit: -32% received healing on the target for 5 s -70%
Prayer of Focus (집중의 기도) below 50% HP: 30 s shield and +20% heal amplification no visible change
Grace of the Earth (대지의 은총) +3% critical damage amplification, +100 accuracy +12.5% / +100
Will to Survive (생존 의지) +17% status resist, +12% / +6% tolerance after control +55%, +50% / +25%
Radiant Blessing (찬란한 가호) 7% on hit: heals the party within 40 m no visible change

Priority order (KR, 2025-11/12): Sovereign’s Grace → Grace of the Earth → Warm Blessing / Healing Power Boost / Veil of Immortality round-robin (1 point each, then 2, then 4) → Radiant Blessing / Prayer of Focus. Solo, put Warm Blessing ahead of Healing Power Boost. Recovery Block for PvP only.

Sovereign’s Grace (주신의 은총)

Section titled “Sovereign’s Grace (주신의 은총)”

Called Imperion Lord’s Grace in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Text: your critical rises by 100 and your Smite by 0.2%, and every time you land an attack you deal extra damage to the target. Internal cooldown: 1 s. At rank 20, +195 critical and +4% Smite. What it is for: the only pure damage passive in the class and first in the queue. That 1 s internal cooldown, on a Cleric casting Condemnation every 3 s or less, means the extra damage lands essentially always. And the Smite scaling (a double-damage proc, the priority damage stat at endgame) from 0.2% to 4% is what keeps it at the top at high level.

Grace of the Earth (대지의 은총)

Section titled “Grace of the Earth (대지의 은총)”

Called Earth’s Grace in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Text: your critical damage amplification rises 3% and your accuracy by 100. At rank 20, +12.5% critical damage; the accuracy does not rise. What it is for: the second damage passive, and second in the queue in all three ranker lists. Note the detail: the +100 accuracy is flat and never scales, so everything you buy from rank 2 onward is critical damage.

Healing Power Boost (치유력 강화)

Section titled “Healing Power Boost (치유력 강화)”

Called Healing Enhancement in the Global and Taiwanese clients.

Stigmas

Section titled “Stigmas”

Stigmas run on a different scale: their specialties land at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, not 8/12/16, and they rank up with Stigma Shards, not skill points. Combos

Section titled “Combos”

(a) The chains the database defines

Section titled “(a) The chains the database defines”

There are only three chain routes in the whole Cleric kit, and they cover four skills. Everything else the guides call a “combo” is a condition or a macro priority, not a chain.

Trigger Link 1 Link 2 Activation
Earth’s Retribution (대지의 응보) Lightning (뇌전) Discharge (방전) 100% / 30%
Judgment Lightning (심판의 번개) Divine Punishment (천벌) with the level-16 specialty, Divine Punishment activates one extra time 100%
Shock Release (충격 해제) Blessing of Regeneration (재생의 축복) 100%, conditional on Shock Release having fired

Three readings that come out of this and out of no guide:

  • The left-click chain is your entire mana economy: 110 + 110 + 150 MP per full sequence. The right-click chain is the opposite, a spend of 120 + 120. The kit is designed so the left click pays for the right, which is why basic attacks are ~70% of the class’s skill usage.
  • Discharge is the only sub-100% link in the whole kit, and that is where the chain-percentage method comes in.
  • Blessing of Regeneration is the only chain that fires off a defensive. Do not put it in the macro expecting it to come out on its own: it only exists in the window after you break a crowd control.

The chain-percentage method, and this is the reference test for the whole game, run on a Cleric precisely: hover each skill in the K menu to read its chain-activation chance, and put only the links near 100% into the macro. Measured on a dummy with a damage meter: the rotation without the low-chance link gave 2,826 DPS; the same rotation with it, 1,348 (TW, 2026-03-18). More than double, and the only change is taking one skill out. The explanation is that a link that does not fire deals no damage but still consumes its macro slot.

Two caveats on that measurement: it is a single dummy run on one creator’s gear, not a controlled benchmark, so the magnitude is illustrative; and runes can raise a low chance (he saw 15% → 30%) without it becoming reliable. The database of 2026-09-22 gives Discharge a 30% base, not 15%, and Earth’s Retribution’s level-8 specialty adds another +20%. Nobody has re-measured with those numbers.

(b) The Condemnation engine

Section titled “(b) The Condemnation engine”

This is the mechanic that defines the class, and it is far cleaner than Templar’s Judgment loop because it does not run on luck.

What the database says: Condemnation only hits targets carrying the Chain of Pain effect. It has a 3 s base cooldown and no cost. Its level-12 specialty gives a 75% chance to reset its own cooldown on hit. The Earth’s Punishment stigma, at its level-5 specialty, makes Condemnation land as a guaranteed critical against anything carrying its mark.

What that means together: mark → guaranteed critical → reset → mark still up → guaranteed critical again. The loop sustains itself for as long as the mark lasts, which is why Koreans count five or six Condemnations per Chain of Pain window and one creator calls it “a machine gun” (KR, 2026-09-20). That one button is roughly 30% of your total damage (KR, community guide).

The arithmetic that decides your whole build:

Piece Base value With the level-20 specialty
Earth’s Punishment cooldown 30 s 30 s
Earth’s Punishment mark duration 10 s 20 s
Cooldown reduction needed so it never drops – ~34% (30 s → 20 s)

At ~34% cooldown reduction the gap disappears and the guaranteed critical never switches off. That is the class’s only threshold; everything else is downstream. Two warnings: Global cut the Daevanion nodes that give cooldown reduction (2.5% to 1.5% per node; Global, LST 2026-09-17/19), so reaching 34% in Season 1 is harder than it looked; and if the +10 s specialty sits at level 25 rather than 20, as one Global-facing guide says, the threshold is worthless because the duration stays at 10 s.

What sits around the loop:

  • The two debuffs are your job, not an extra. Mark of Weakening cuts the boss’s damage amplification; Chain of Pain cuts its tolerance. Per the official Korean patch notes their reductions stack additively rather than overwriting.
  • The healing sits on top of it, not instead of it. No Cleric heal needs target selection: they are all self-centred 25 m areas. That is why you can keep the macro held and drop a heal in the middle without dropping the rotation.
  • Heals cannot critically hit. The only lever is Healing Power Boost, and since 2026-03-25 it scales off attack power. Gearing for damage gears your healing.

(c) PvE rotations, the damage macro, and keeping the heals out of it

Section titled “(c) PvE rotations, the damage macro, and keeping the heals out of it”

Macro system rule: the in-game macro fires the first available skill going down the list, in strict priority order, skipping any whose condition is not met. Confirmed on the Global client (Global, LST 2026-09-20). So conditional skills go above the filler or they never come out.

The split that defines the class: damage goes in the macro, heals stay manual. It is the one rule every Korean, Taiwanese and Global source agrees on, including the ones that contradict each other on everything else. And it works because of one concrete mechanic:

Holding the macro key does not block the rest of your bar. The macro pauses by itself while you press another skill and resumes when you release (TW, 2026-03-18). In practice that means you hold the damage key down through the entire fight and press the heal on top of it, without letting go, without restarting the rotation and without losing a tick. It is literally what lets a Cleric be a reactive support and a macro class at the same time. The macro has to be held, not tapped: tapping it does not keep the cycle going (TW, 2026-07-05).

The real difference between them is not the order; it is that the Taiwanese one brings Voice of Ruin in for its damage over time and takes Judgment Lightning out, because by September 2026 the basic attack measures better against bosses.

Buff block, on a second key (Global, 2026-09-02): Earth's Punishment + Light of Protection + Prayer of Amplification + Noble Energy. Light of Protection is a toggle: turn it on once and it stays, so in the macro it is only there so you never forget to re-enable it.

The right-click debate against bosses. Two independent measurements, in two regions, reach the same conclusion: against bosses, basic attack + Condemnation (“평단”) out-damages Judgment Lightning + Condemnation (“심단”) (KR, 2026-08-27; TW, 2026-09-21). The Korean Cleric who measured it stopped using Judgment Lightning on bosses entirely and kept it for trash; the Taiwanese one dropped the Magic Arcana set’s 2-piece mana bonus from his build precisely because he no longer weaves Judgment. Against that, the 2026-01/02 ranker data caps it at 20 in 77% of cases. Nobody has measured it on the Global client. Test it yourself with Ctrl+X.

Maximum-DPS variant (KR, 2026-08-27): pull Thunderclap and Light of Regeneration out of automation and fire them by hand; for the absolute ceiling, also pull Prayer of Amplification so the macro cannot waste it during a boss invulnerability window. The creator himself does not go that far and keeps Prayer of Amplification inside, trading a little DPS for comfort. His live one-minute test with a hardware macro: ~5,000,000 damage, 130 Condemnation hits, 537 total hits; the same test on the in-game macro alone came out “almost the same” after the global-cooldown patch of 2026-08-26. You do not need a macro mouse to compete.

Macro delays: start at 50-60 ms and tune to your own ping; 8-10 ms only on genuinely low-latency connections. The Taiwanese creator who explains it plays at ~200 ms and uses 50-60 (TW, 2026-09-21). Never set it below your ping. A hold-style macro wants a large delay, 50 to 100 ms, precisely because it is not meant to tap rapidly (KR, 2026-09-21/22). The default seen on the Global client was 30 ms (Global, LST 2026-09-20).

Macro construction rules:

  • Capacity 20 skills; only actives and stigmas, never passives.
  • The macro mirrors the active bar: the game has three bar presets (1/2/3) and the macro reads whichever is live, so switching preset switches the whole rotation. On Global, presets save skill placement, skill level and the macro together.
  • Only what sits in the side column the macro reads goes in: put it there first.
  • One row only. Two or more reduce the number of times each skill in the macro fires. If you need two, split one into the in-game macro and one into the mouse macro (KR, 2026-09-17/22).
  • Watch the mana. The macro empties the blue bar noticeably faster than manual play: mana is what decides when you can hold the key and when you cannot (TW, 2026-07-05).
  • Test it in the Legion Hall with the meter (Ctrl+X) before taking it into a dungeon, and re-tune it every patch.

Where the points go, in short (KR, 2026-01/02; TW, 2026-09-21/22): Radiance of Recovery to 20 first · Condemnation to 20 (16 opens the third specialisation slot, 20 lets all three be equipped) · Light of Healing to 16 · Chain of Pain to 16 · Mark of Weakening to 12-16 · Light of Regeneration to 16 · Lightning Barrage to 16 for the -1 s to all cooldowns · Holy Aura capped at 8 · Earth’s Retribution to 8 or 16 depending on whether you animation-cancel.

And a hard number under all of it: you need roughly 5% of the damage dealt to the boss to open its loot queue at all (Global, 2026-09-20). A support does get invited below a party’s listed combat power, but nobody is carried past that 5%. It is the mechanical answer to “do not build a pure healer”.

(d) PvP: kiting, control, and the two presses that decide the fight

Section titled “(d) PvP: kiting, control, and the two presses that decide the fight”

The first thing is what comes off the bar. Anything gated on groggy does not exist in PvP, because players have no groggy gauge: out go Lightning Barrage and Burst of Authority, and Earth’s Punishment’s groggy contribution stops counting. In come Bind, Voice of Ruin and the two passives that only serve here, Recovery Block and Will to Survive.

The specialties change, and they are free to swap. All of these are in the database, and each is the PvP branch of a skill you pick differently in PvE:

Skill PvP specialty Level
Judgment Lightning [Divine Punishment] on hit, -10% combat speed for 5 s 8
Judgment Lightning [Divine Punishment] on hit, 10% chance of a 2 s bind 12
Mark of Weakening 20% chance of a 3 s stun 8
Chain of Pain applies Slow, or a 20% chance of a 3 s knockdown 8
Holy Aura -40% move speed on enemies within 4 m of the aura 8
Radiance of Recovery at max charge, +20% received healing for 10 s 12
Bind +25% root chance, that is 100% 15
Salvation grants the base effect to the lowest-HP party member 15

The shape of the fight: you are a 20 m caster with no gap closer at all. That is the fact that organises everything else. You do not open, you peel: hold your distance, put Chain of Pain on whatever your side is focusing (-7.5% PvP tolerance, your biggest contribution), keep Mark of Weakening up, Bind the melee that closed on you (75%, 100% with the level-15 node, 10 s and 12 s with the level-10), put Voice of Ruin on whoever you are trying to kill, and land Condemnation for whatever damage you have. Your full kiting kit is: Chain of Pain’s slow, Holy Aura’s -40% move speed, Divine Punishment’s -10% combat speed, Judgment Lightning’s 2 s bind and Bind’s 10 s. None of it kills anyone; all of it makes the person who does kill arrive in time.

The two presses:

  1. Shock Release is saved for the chain, not the first crowd control. Experienced Korean Clerics deliberately eat the “typical” stun and save the break for the sequence that actually kills them (KR, 2026-02). Spending it on the first proc is the classic mistake. As a bonus, breaking control opens Blessing of Regeneration: a heal plus 40% of your mana.
  2. Salvation is saved for the opponent’s burst window, never used on cooldown. 3 s of taking no damage and no status ailments. It cannot be chained with another invulnerability: since 2026-03-25 there is a 30 s lockout between invulnerability effects. And at level 15 it stops being yours: it gives the effect to the lowest-HP party member, which in a group fight is worth considerably more.

The anti-heal package, which is the only thing that lets you kill. Voice of Ruin removes 60% of received healing and of potion recovery for 20 s; its level-10 node also strips shields; Recovery Block takes between 32% (rank 1) and 70% (rank 20) at a 7% chance per hit. Against a Templar or against another Cleric that is literally the difference between a kill and a ten-minute draw.

Resurrection in PvP. It is your biggest card in sieges and battlegrounds, where revival stones are often disabled: 2 s cast (1 s with the level-5 node), 40 m, and 3 s of invulnerability after you pick the party up with the level-10, 5 s with the level-20. Those seconds are what stops the enemy re-killing the people you just revived. Never in a macro.

In PvP the macro has to shrink. A held macro fires blind to the opponent’s state: a control cast during an invulnerability is simply lost. Korean specialists automate two or three damage skills and leave the rest reactive (KR, 2026-09-21), and the one Cleric macro source that covers PvP flatly refuses to automate heals there (TW, 2026-09-21).

The limits, said plainly. Cleric survives almost everything and closes almost nothing: “excellent survivability but struggles to secure kills: you can survive, but the enemy can just run” (Global, 2026-09-20). In 1v1 it lands around 66% (B) in the only numeric data that exists, whose methodology is unstated and where all eight classes come out above 60%, so the ordering is the signal and not the value (KR/TW, 2026-07). An Assassin opening from stealth can delete or silence you before you react (TW, 2026-09-22). And arena rounds run up to 10 minutes, which means a healer can stall out a whole match by out-sustaining (TW, 2026-09-21). Where you are genuinely strong is mass PvP, because you are ranged: in large fights ranged classes scale and melee cannot reach (Global, 2026-09-20).

(e) What never goes in a macro

Section titled “(e) What never goes in a macro”
  • Thunderclap (Bolt). It is a 3-stage charge; the macro only fires the first, so you trade your biggest hit for the smallest of the three. It is the most repeated rule in the whole corpus. The workaround: bind it as a press-and-hold key with no key-up event and it auto-fires at full charge (KR, 2026-08-27).
  • Radiance of Recovery. Same reason, and worse: macro’d it comes out at charge 0, so you lose the +50% healing and the cleanse, which is what you carry it for.
  • Resurrection. A 2-second cast in the wrong second is a dead macro slot and a dead party.
  • Salvation and Shock Release. Their entire value is the exact instant you press them. A macro spends them on the first proc and leaves you naked for the one that matters.
  • Every heal, in PvP. Unanimous rule, and the one Cleric macro source that addresses PvP says it outright. In PvE the background healing can be automated (Light of Regeneration, Light of Healing, Radiance of Recovery on a separate key), but never the emergency ones.
  • The 30% link, Discharge, unless you have taken Earth’s Retribution’s level-8 chance node and measured it yourself. The reference test is 2,826 against 1,348 DPS (TW, 2026-03-18).
  • Passives, which simply cannot be added.
  • More than one row. Two or more rows reduce the number of times each skill in the macro fires.

What is still unresolved

The English names. The ones on this page are Inven’s, the ones seen in the Global and Taiwanese clients, and our own. The same skill appears as Holy Aura, Divine Aura and Sacred Aura across three sources. Check it during Advanced Access from 30 September.

Condemnation’s reset: 50% or 75%. Every Korean guide from 2025 and 2026 says 50%. The database of 2026-09-22 says 75%. It is the figure that decides how many Condemnations fit in a window, which is 30% of your damage.

Condemnation’s level-16 specialty. The database says guaranteed multi-hit; two PvP syntheses say “20% root”. That is not the same skill.

Earth’s Punishment’s duration: level 20 or level 25. The database puts the +10 s in the level-20 slot, reachable in Global Season 1. A Global-facing September guide says 25, which is not. Whether the ~34% cooldown-reduction target means anything at all, and with it the whole build, depends on this. It is the highest-priority day-one check.

Radiance of Recovery’s second specialty. The database of 2026-09-22 still says “+20% skill speed”; the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 changed it to “+1 consecutive cast”. Nobody knows which one Global ships.

Light of Protection against a Chanter. The Korean client text says the whole buff does not stack with [불패의 진언] and that only the higher skill level applies, with ties going to the Chanter. The Korean community reading is that only the damage-amplification half fails to stack and that the tolerance half does. Both cannot be true, and the difference decides whether a party with a Chanter wants your stigma.

Light of Healing’s level-16 specialty. The database says -2 s on a 6 s base cooldown, that is 4 s. The ranker write-ups describe it as “brings the recast to roughly 1 s”.

Resurrection’s useful threshold. The database puts the cast-time cut at level 5 and the 3 s of invulnerability at 10. A Taiwanese Cleric puts the cast-time cut at 10 and says it is not worth carrying below that.

Judgment Lightning against bosses. Measured worse than the basic attack in Korea (2026-08-27) and Taiwan (2026-09-21), but capped at 20 by 77% of Korean rankers (2026-02). No measurement on the Global client.

Whether Prayer of Amplification’s Sovereign’s Grace x1.5 is party-wide or self-only. Explicitly open since January 2026; nobody published the test.

The four stigma slots. Twelve Global Launch Scale Test sources say four, unlocking at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37. No NC note says anything.

Two typos in the Korean text. The rank-20 entries for Prayer of Amplification and Authority of Yustiel read “20초 동 동안”, with a duplicated character. We read them as a 20-second duration and do not correct the original.
