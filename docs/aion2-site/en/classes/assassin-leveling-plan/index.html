Assassin: what to level and when (1-45 and endgame)

Region: Korea, Taiwan and Global · Version: Inven Korean skill data of 2026-09-22 over the KR/TW syntheses to 2026-09-16 and the Global Launch Scale Test rules of 2026-09-17/19 · Reviewed: 2026-09-22 · Confidence: medium. This page is a point-spending checklist, not a combat guide: the kit, the Elusive Clone window and the macros live on the Assassin page. The frame rules (4 stigmas, skill cap 20, free resets) were measured on a test build capped at level 37, not on the launch client.

How to read this on launch day

Section titled “How to read this on launch day”
  1. Find your level band, work its list top to bottom, and do not read the others.
  2. Every figure carries its region and date in brackets. If it does not, it is a general rule with no source and says so.
  3. Skill points are free to reset on Global, so getting them wrong costs nothing.
  4. Stigma Shards are not free to reset and nobody has seen the cost screen: pick the four stigmas at level 22 and leave them alone.
  5. Positioning is the class. Missing the rear angle produces “the worst damage in the group on the same gear” on the Global build (Global, 2026-09-20), and the back-attack bonus was seen live at the level-37 cap: Throwing Dagger at ~4,000, +30% from behind (Global, 2026-09-17). Learn to turn the target at level 2, not at 45.
  6. The skill names on this page come from the Korean database and from one creator’s on-screen reading of the Global build, not from a verified skill-panel capture (Global, 2026-09-17). Check the name in your own client before you spend.
  7. Any Korean or Taiwanese build you find runs 5 or 6 stigmas: cut it to 4 before you copy it.

Level bands

Section titled “Level bands”

1 to 22: two chains, the target’s back and the Ascension bar

Section titled “1 to 22: two chains, the target’s back and the Ascension bar”
  • Skills, in this order: Quick Slash (빠른 베기), your mana battery and your permanent filler (1 s cooldown, 4 m, returns 100 mana per hit, chains at 100% into Soul-Severing Slash and Swift Slash), and Beast’s Roar (맹수의 포효), which also stamps 1 문양 (sigil mark) for 10 s and chains into Hind Kick and Swipe (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Third, the moment you have it: Heart Gore (심장 찌르기), 5 s cooldown, only activates on a critical hit, returns 100 mana. It is the button the whole class lives on (KR, 2025-11 to 2026-03).
  • Fourth: Sigil Explosion (문양 폭발), 10 s, 20 m, consumes the marks and stuns for 3 s. Use it, but do not over-invest: one meter-backed test calls it overvalued and points out that spending it wipes the back-attack-resist debuff the party had stacked (KR, 2026-03).
  • Passives: Heightened Sixth Sense (육감 극대화) first, the defensive one: +200 evasion, max HP scaling from 6% to 25% by rank, and a heal on dodge (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). It is what keeps you alive, because outside lifesteal this class has no self-heal. Then Rear Strike (배후 강타) and then Resolve (각오), in that order and for that reason (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • The passive you never touch: Poison Coating (독 바르기), zero points. It exists to keep a target in combat state, not to deal damage (KR, 2025-12 and 2025-11).
  • Stigmas: none. The first slot does not exist until 22.
  • Daevanion: the board opens around level 12-13 and is worth “roughly a third of your early power” (Global, 2026-09-19). Blue nodes first (active-skill level): on Global the orange cores lost between a third and a half of their value while a blue node still buys a whole skill level (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Gear: daggers, all disposable, nothing above +5 (Global LST, 2026-09-18/20). Belt and Amulet are the permanent exception.
  • Gate before you move on: level 22 has a red quest that blocks the main story until the world-progress bar is at 100%, filled with regional quests (J key) and sealed dungeons. One measured route reached 97.1% using only the stops that sit on the natural path, and took 1 h 10 min from 1 to 22 (Global, 2026-09-19).

Do not:

  • Learn to fight from the front. The back-attack multiplier is the entire argument for the class, and hitting from behind also bypasses a monster’s block outright, so the position buys accuracy as well as damage (KR, 2026-03 and 2026-08).
  • Spend anything on crit damage while levelling: do not enchant Assault Stance’s (강습 자세) crit-damage passive and do not take the Vasial crit-damage Daevanion line at 37, because crit rate is near zero without buffs or scrolls at that stage (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Put the 20 m gap-closer, the 10 m positional swap, the stagger-breaker, the dodge or the proc-only teleport-behind in a macro. All five stay out, always (KR, 2026-06).
  • Skip green side quests: each one is +1 skill point, +1 Daevanion point and +3 permanent passive stat points (Global, 2026-09-18).
  • Spend a key on a yellow cube: yellow cubes open for free (Global, 2026-09-20).
  • Believe the old reading that this class “dies from level 19”. A creator who took all eight classes to 37 on the Global test lists Assassin among the easy ones, alongside Chanter, Templar and Cleric (Global, 2026-09-17). You are still fragile, but not slow.

22 to 27: first stigma slot, first dungeon

Section titled “22 to 27: first stigma slot, first dungeon”
  • Skills: push Quick Slash, Beast’s Roar and Heart Gore towards 8 (13 points each, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22) and then place the specialisation: unlocking it and placing it are two separate steps in the UI.
  • Which level-8 specialisation to take while levelling (one per skill, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22):
    • Quick Slash: 생명력 1.5% 흡수 (lifesteal) if you are dying, otherwise 다단 히트 50% (multi-hit). The third option, +20% mana recovery, is redundant as soon as gear gives you max mana (KR, sponsored, 2026).
    • Beast’s Roar: 스킬 속도 20% (skill speed), the one that pays most under auto-attack cancelling.
    • Heart Gore: 생명력 1.5% 흡수 while levelling; at 45 it is traded for the crit option.
    • Surprise Attack (기습): 생명력 3% 흡수. Storm Rampage (폭풍 난무): 5%. Shadow Fall (그림자 낙하): 10%, the highest in the kit.
  • Stigma, slot 1. The sources give the final set, never the slot order. Two readings:
    • The Korean 5-slot reading (2026-06): Triniel’s Dagger (트리니엘의 비수) to 10 first. It is the CDR engine: +50% damage from behind and, at step 10, -10% cooldown on every skill (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
    • The levelling reading: Throw Shadow Blade (암검 투척), 30 s and 20 m, the shortest-cooldown stigma in the pool, with a 5 s slow and cooldown reset on kill at its step 10. For pulling packs and finishing while you level alone, it does more.
    • Whichever you take, stop it at 5 (4 shards) until you know your four. Effects only change at 5, 10, 15 and 20.
  • Daevanion: blue nodes on Heart Gore first, then Beast’s Roar and Quick Slash, the three that carry the measured damage (KR, 2026-03: Beast’s Roar ~37%, Heart Gore ~21%, Quick Slash ~16% of damage outside the buff window).
  • Gear: enhancement cap is still +5 green / +10 blue.
  • Gate before you move on: 700 gear score for 1-star Conquest (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: push one skill to 10 before the others are at 8; spread shards across four stigmas at once; copy a Korean 5-slot build.

27 to 32: second slot

Section titled “27 to 32: second slot”
  • Skills: the rest of the core actives to 8, and the main three towards 10, which is where the points run out.
  • Stigma, slot 2. Complete the cooldown pair with whichever of Triniel’s Dagger and Throw Shadow Blade you are missing. Both reset on kill at their higher steps (15 and 10 respectively), which is what makes pack clearing gapless (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Shock Release (충격 해제) to 8 if you are dying: it is your only hard defensive cooldown in the base kit (1 min, cleanses stun, knockdown, grab, freeze and fear, and grants 5 s of status immunity). Its level-8 specialisation adds 50% PvE and 25% PvP damage resistance for that window, or 20% of max HP restored on cast (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Daevanion: cooldown reduction, with no ceiling, then combat speed. This is the one class not asked to stop in the 33-36% band (TW, 2026-08).
  • Gear: blue to +10 at most.
  • Gate before you move on: 2-star Conquest, 1,400 gear score (one source read 1,500) (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: take Stealth into a dungeon. With four slots that is a slot thrown away.

32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate

Section titled “32 to 37: third slot and the second Ascension gate”
  • Ascension gate #2 at 32: another world-progress bar filled with exploration and side quests, then the red quest (Global, 2026-09-18). Side quests come back around 32-33 after being sparse since 21 (Global, 2026-09-18 and 09-20).
  • Stigma, slot 3: Elusive Clone (환영 분신). It is the whole loop: for 10 s it removes Heart Gore’s cooldown and adds 20% damage to every attack you land (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22). With it up, Heart Gore’s share of your damage goes from ~21% to ~50% (KR, 2026-03).
  • Skills: past this point you do not buy levels with points. Anything above 10 comes from the four boards (+4), from Arcana cards and from ring and weapon soul bindings (Global LST, 2026-09-18 and 09-20). What you are chasing with that is Heart Gore at 16: its level-16 specialisation is 치명타 적중 시 재시전 시간 초기화, a critical hit resets its own cooldown, and it is the single highest-value node in the class (KR, 2025-11 to 2026-03; confirmed in the Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Two more level-12 steps worth chasing: Quick Slash at 12 cuts 1 s off Sigil Explosion’s cooldown on hit, and Beast’s Roar at 12 cuts 1 s off Ambush’s. Both feed the same thing: fewer gaps (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  • Daevanion: keep going on cooldown and combat speed, then multi-hit and back damage. Skip the crit-damage line (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Janus pet, the special slot-4 line: Back damage boost, which is the Assassin-specific pick because the class has near-100% back uptime (Global, 2026-09-18). Pets cap at level 3 and need 105 kills.
  • Gate before you move on: 1,400 for 2-star; 1,000 for the first Ascension Trial rank, which also drops shards.

Do not: copy a Korean macro dated after 2026-09-16 without testing it. That patch added a cancel delay to Beast’s Roar and Heart Gore that killed the “main cancel” technique on Korea after three days of testing, and it is not known whether the Global build carries it (KR, 2026-09-18).

37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch

Section titled “37 to 45: fourth slot, fourth board and the last stretch”
  • Stigma, slot 4 at level 37: Swift Contract (신속의 계약), +20% combat speed for 10 s, staged with Elusive Clone in the Korean rotation (KR, 2026-06). That closes the set of four actually played at the level-37 cap on the Global test: Throw Shadow Blade, Swift Contract, Elusive Clone and Triniel’s Dagger, on ~15 stigma points (Global, 2026-09-17).
  • Fourth Daevanion board at level 40 (Global, 2026-09-17). The Asphel (PvP) board opens at 45; the PvE board was not in the test build.
  • Skills: the core ones towards 20 through boards, Arcana and soul bindings. Keep any gear piece that grants +levels to a skill you use instead of disenchanting it (KR, 2025-11).
  • The levelling specialisation you will undo later: every lifesteal option (Quick Slash 1.5%, Surprise Attack 3%, Storm Rampage 5%, Shadow Fall 10%) is traded for multi-hit, guaranteed crits and resets once you reach 45. The point respec is free, so testing it is cheap (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21).
  • Gear: at 45 the Season 1 ceiling is Unique; there is no Heroic rarity at launch (Global LST, 2026-09-17/20).
  • Gates: 3-star 2,100 (one source read 2,200); Nightmare, first boss Zikel ~2,400; Abyssal Forge: Ludra raid ~2,700 (Global LST, 2026-09).

Do not: expect endgame numbers at level 37. On the Global test, with all eight classes at 37 and gear equalised, an Assassin hitting the dummy from behind parsed 334-343k, fourth of five measured, against Chanter’s 564k and Templar’s 432k (Global, 2026-09-17). The #1 DPS figure from Korea and Taiwan is a top-end, top-decile measurement (110.4 against the baseline, P90 of 50,000 characters, TW, 2026-08): the stats your burst scales on do not exist yet at 37.

At 45: the reset question

Section titled “At 45: the reset question”

What to reset: skill points, without thinking about it. They are free on Global (Global, 2026-09-20 and 09-21). On Assassin the trade is specific: out goes all the lifesteal you survived on while levelling, in comes the damage package plus the points you had parked in utility.

What not to reset: the stigmas. They are not paid in points but in Stigma Shards, taking all four to 20 is about 300 shards (~75 each), and levelling to 45 while doing every side quest yields on the order of 20 (KR). The cost of resetting them on Global has not been seen by anybody. That is why the four are chosen at 22 and left alone.

The middle ground: Daevanion nodes. On Korea they refund at 500 Kinah each and a whole branch for ~13,500 (KR, 2025-11), and you remove them from the branch tips inwards.

The first ~45 Daevanion points

Section titled “The first ~45 Daevanion points”

No source in this corpus publishes an Assassin Daevanion route. There are exactly two hard data points, one of them negative, and the rest is the game’s general rule: this is a priority order with no class-specific source, not a route.

  1. Blue nodes on Heart Gore: steps 12 and 16 are out of reach of points, and 16 is the best node in the class (Inven KR database, 2026-09-22).
  2. Blue nodes on Beast’s Roar and Quick Slash, for the measured share of damage (KR, 2026-03).
  3. Cooldown reduction, without stopping at 33-36% the way every other class does (TW, 2026-08). More expensive on Global: the node went from 2.5% to 1.5%.
  4. Combat speed (1.5% per node on Global against 2.5% on Korea).
  5. Flat attack (+3 per node on Global, +5 on Korea and Taiwan).
  6. Blue nodes on Sigil Explosion and Surprise Attack.
  7. Skip the Vasial crit-damage line at 37: it is the only node a Global source names for Assassin, and it names it in order to avoid it (Global, 2026-09-17).

What is still disputed or unconfirmed

The Global names of Assassin skills are one creator’s on-screen reading, not a verified panel capture (Global, 2026-09-17), and this class has the weakest name coverage in the whole corpus: part of the Korean table is translated here, not attested. Before you spend shards, check the name and the tooltip in your own client.

Sling Dagger does not exist in this Korean data. A Taiwanese source names it as the Global gap closer that resets on kill (TW, 2026-09-22), and it matches none of the 40 skills in the Korean JSON. Either it is a renamed skill or a Global-only addition. Unresolved.

Stealth’s reset on player kills is, in this data, the level-20 specialisation (PC 처치 관여 시 재시전 시간 초기화, Inven KR database, 2026-09-22), while the Global and Taiwanese sources of 2026-09 describe it as base behaviour since the Korean patch of 2026-03-25. The difference is ~75 shards: if the database is right, the Abyss-picker loop is a whole stigma taken to cap, not a bonus. Read the tooltip before you commit.

There is no published Assassin Daevanion route. The only class-specific data point that exists is negative: do not take the Vasial crit-damage line at 37 (Global, 2026-09-17).

The anti-macro cancel delay from the Korean patch of 2026-09-16 is unverified on the Global build. If it carries over, every Korean macro guide in this corpus needs re-testing.

The cost of resetting stigmas on Global has not been seen by anybody. Resetting skill points is free, but it is a different system. Plan the four from level 22.

It is the most ping-sensitive class in the game. Three independent sources say so (Global, 2026-09-20; KR/TW, 2026-09-21; TW, 2026-09-22), and a Taiwanese ping list ranks it last under latency. The concrete figure, up to -40% DPS, is Korean and from 2026-05, and has not been re-measured. If your ping is bad, this plan does not fix it.

The accuracy target is still unresolved: ~3,000 (KR, 2026-02) against ~5,700 (KR, 2026-07), and neither figure transfers to Global, where the whole percentage scale is different (Global, 2026-09-18/19).
